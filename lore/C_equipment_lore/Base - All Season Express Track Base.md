# 🛡️ All Season Express Track Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 200 Power | 10 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Initiate  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 50,000 Gold20 Ornament Sugar Cookies20 Snowflake Sugar Cookies20 Toboggan Sugar Cookies

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> This base was made from a section of the beautifully-crafted all season train tracks developed specifically with the Holiday Express in mind. These extraordinary tracks magically change to suit the climate conditions of wherever the Holiday Express needs to go!
> This special section of track has been adapted to react when certain charms are equipped.
> Icy Form: When a Snowball Charm is equipped, this base ices over and provides a 100% chance for the Snowball Charm to roll up into its more powerful version.
> Melty Form: When a Small Power Charm, Power Charm, Super Power Charm, or Extreme Power Charm is equipped, this base heats up and provides a 10% chance to warm that charm up into its more powerful version.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The All Season Express Track Base can be purchased for 50,000 Gold, 20 Ornament Sugar Cookies, 20 Snowflake Sugar Cookies, and 20 Toboggan Sugar Cookies at Great Winter Hunt 2021.
