# Gargantuamouse

The Gargantuamouse is a breed of mouse found in the Fiery Warpath as part of the first three waves. It is known to drop Ancient Relic and Flameshard as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Gargantuamouse can be attracted by any Standard Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Gargantuamouse can be attracted when the Hunter's Streak is at 7 or higher in Fiery Warpath. Its attraction rate increases when the Streak gets higher.

The Hunter can use Gargantua Charm to remove all mice except the Gargantuamouse from the attraction pool when the required Streak is reached, thus guarantees the next attraction being the Gargantuamouse.

Draconic power type is very effective against this mouse. Arcane, Physical, Tactical, and Hydro are less effective against it.

When the Hunter fails to capture a Gargantuamouse, the following message will appear on the Hunter's Journal:

> Fearing that I was gaining the upper hand, the Warmonger released a Gargantuamouse, putting an end to my streak.


## History and Trivia
