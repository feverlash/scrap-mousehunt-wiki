# Chamber Cleaver Mouse

The Chamber Cleaver Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Bristle Woods Rift. It is the Rift counterpart of Realm Ripper Mouse.

## Preferred Cheese

The Chamber Cleaver Mouse has been found to be especially attracted to Runic String Cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.

## Hunting Strategy

Only Rift type traps are effective against Chamber Cleaver Mouse, all other power types are ineffective.

Chamber Cleaver Mouse can only be attracted upon clearing 20% of the loot counter in Gearworks, Ancient Lab and Runic Laboratory chambers.


## History and Trivia

- 31 May 2017: The Chamber Cleaver Mouse was introduced as part of the Bristle Woods Rift release.
