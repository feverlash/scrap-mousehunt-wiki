# Flame Archer Mouse

The Flame Archer Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Fiery Warpath location. It can be attracted and caught only during Wave Two. They are not known to drop any loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Flame Archer Mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## History and Trivia

- Flame Archer Mice were introduced on 22 March 2011 with the release of the Sandtail Desert region.
- Flame Archer Mice began dropping the Antique Blue Argyle Egg as loot, on 16 April 2011, for the Spring Egg Hunt 2011 event.
