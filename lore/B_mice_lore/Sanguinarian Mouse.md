# Sanguinarian Mouse

The Sanguinarian Mouse can be found in the Labyrinth and Zokor. It is known to drop Scholar Scrolls in Zokor.

## Preferred Cheese

The Sanguinarian Mouse prefers Glowing Gruyere in both the Labyrinth and Zokor, but can be attracted with Standard Cheeses.


## Hunting Strategy

Forgotten weapons are very effective against the Sanguinarian Mouse. Arcane weapons are of normal effectiveness.

- Labyrinth

> The Sanguinarian Mouse can be found in Plain, Superior, and Epic Scholar Hallways.

- Zokor

> The Sanguinarian Mouse can be found in the Neophyte Scholar Study, the Master Scholar Auditorium, and the Dark Library.


## Loot

- Zokor

> Labyrinth Theme Scrap II and Scholar Scrolls
