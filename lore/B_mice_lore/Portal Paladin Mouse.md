# Portal Paladin Mouse

The Portal Paladin Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Bristle Woods Rift. It is the Rift counterpart of Gate Guardian Mouse.

## Preferred Cheese

The Portal Paladin Mouse can be attracted with String Cheeses, but prefers Ancient String Cheese and Runic String Cheese in Guard Barracks of the Bristle Woods Rift.
