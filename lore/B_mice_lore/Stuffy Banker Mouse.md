## Hunting Strategy

Law power type is very effective against Stuffy Banker Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.

- In Claw Shot City, this mouse is available when the Hunter is working on a Thieves Gang Wanted Poster, Travelling Terror Wanted Poster, or Corrupt Cartel Wanted Poster.
- In Gnawnian Express Station, this mouse is always available.


## History and Trivia

- 25 June 2013: Stuffy Banker Mouse was released as part of the Claw Shot City, but couldn't be attracted yet.
- 27 June 2013: Stuffy Banker Mouse was made available with the Thieves Gang Wanted Poster.
- 26 September 2013: Stuffy Banker Mouse was added to Gnawnian Express Station.
