## Locations

Hunters can encounter the Vampire Mouse in the Catacombs, Forbidden Grove, and Mousoleum locations.


## Loot

The Vampire Mouse drops Greater Radioactive Blue Potions in the Mousoleum location.

The Vampire Mouse is not known to drop any loot in the Catacombs and Forbidden Grove locations.


## History and Trivia

- Despite its rarity and difficulty, the Vampire Mouse is worth much less than most Mousoleum breeds. MouseHunt developers have stated this was intentional so that hunters would consider it unappealing to catch.
- The Vampire Mouse's description references Nosferatu
- 28 July 2008: The Vampire Mouse is released.
- 17 April 2011: The Vampire Mouse began dropping the Antique Stripy Red Egg as loot in the Forbidden Grove as part of the Spring Egg Hunt 2011 event.
