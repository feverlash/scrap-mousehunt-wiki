## History and Trivia

- 9 December 2014: Borean Commander was released as part of the Great Winter Hunt 2014 event, but could not be attracted yet.
- 23 December 2014: Borean Commander became available as part of the third week of the Great Winter Hunt 2014.
- Catching the Borean Commander during the Great Winter Hunt 2014 event created the following entry in the Hunter's Journal:

> I caught the Borean Commander and caused her to flee to safety! Without leaders, the mice of the Crystal Citadel also flee to the fields, and what passes for peace returns again to Gnawnia. I now have until all this ice melts to try to catch the Stuck Snowball Mouse as many times as I can for prizes and loot!

- 13 December 2016: The points/gold for Borean Commander were changed.
