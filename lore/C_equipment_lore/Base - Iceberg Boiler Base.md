# 🛡️ Iceberg Boiler Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 300 Power | 6 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Lord/Lady  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 500,000 Gold

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> This base utilizes cutting-edge Steam Laser technology to boil solid ice in mere seconds. The design was introduced by Ronza as a powerful and efficient way to conquer the Iceberg!
> No longer will you need to worry about switching bases while digging to defeat Icewing. This is a "one base fits all" solution to your previous multi-base problem!
> 
> Increases the attraction rate of the Tunnel Rats mice
> Prevents any backwards movement from Brute mice
> Capturing Bomb Squad mice propels you forward 20 feet
> Repels Zealot mice
> Provides the same bonus as the Deep Freeze Base

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Iceberg Boiler Base can be purchased from the Trapsmith in Slushy Shoreline and Iceberg Trapsmith for 500,000 Gold.

It requires the Hunter to own Magnet Base, Spiked Base, Remote Detonator Base, Hearthstone Base, and Deep Freeze Base to purchase.

It cannot be refunded.
