## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Bionic Mouse can be attracted using the following Cheeses:

- Gnawnia region:

> Meadow - Standard Cheese
Town of Gnawnia - Standard Cheese and Gilded Cheese
Harbour - Standard Cheese
Mountain - Standard Cheese

- Burroughs region:

> Laboratory - Standard Cheese and Radioactive Blue Cheese
Town of Digby - Standard Cheese
Bazaar - Standard Cheese and Gilded Cheese


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Bionic Mouse.

## Loot

The Bionic Mouse drops loot in the following locations:

- Meadow

> Marble Cheese
Satchel of Gold (500 gold)

- Harbour and Mountain

> Brie Potion

- Laboratory

> Lab Research (1,000 points)

The Bionic Mouse is not known to drop any loot in the Bazaar, Town of Digby, or Town of Gnawnia locations.


## History and Trivia

- 25 June 2013: The Bionic Mouse gained a vulnerability to Law power type traps, with the release of Varmint Valley.
- 25 March 2014: The Cyborg Mouse was introduced as the Rift counterpart of the Bionic Mouse.
- 25 March 2014: The Bionic Mouse gained a vulnerability to Rift power type traps, with the release of the Rift Plane.
- 17 October 2018: The Bionic Mouse changed mouse groups from Indigenous Mice to Lab Experiments and no longer drops Radioactive Blue Cheese Potion.
