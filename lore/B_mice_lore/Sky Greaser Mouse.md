## Cheese Preference

The Sky Greaser Mouse can be attracted by using Standard Cheeses and Cloud Cheesecake.


## Hunting Strategy

All traps except Rift and Parental traps are effective against the Sky Greaser Mouse, all other power types are ineffective. The Sky Greaser Mouse can only be encountered on the Launch Pad.


## History and Trivia

- 08 September 2020: The Sky Greaser Mouse was introduced with the release of the Floating Islands.
