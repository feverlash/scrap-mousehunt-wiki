## Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Burglar Mouse.


## Power Type Weaknesses

The Burglar Mouse has a predominate weakness to Law weapons, but is found in locations were using a Law trap would be less effective against the other mice that Hunters will encounter.
It is recommended that Hunters use a trap that will be effective against the main mouse population in their hunting location.

## History and Trivia

- The nickname for the Burglar Mouse did indeed come from a certain notorious scammer. He was believed to be the first to try to repeatedly get honest players to part with their SUPER|brie+ for free on the forums.
- The Burglar Mouse was originally also available in the Town of Gnawnia, as well as several other locations.
- 23 May 2008: The Burglar Mouse is released
- 8 June 2010: The Burglar Mouse began to drop Gilded Cheese as loot with the release of MouseHunt version 3.0.
- 25 January 2011: The Burglar Mouse began to drop Flawless Orbs as loot.
- 19 April 2011: The Burglar Mouse dropped the Antique Scalloped Pink Egg as loot in the Bazaar as part of the Spring Egg Hunt 2011 event.
- 14 April 2011: The Burglar Mouse began dropping a bonus Burglar Egg as loot as part of the annual Spring Egg Hunt.
- 25 June 2013: The Burglar Mouse gained a vulnerability to Law power type traps, with the release of Varmint Valley.
- 25 March 2014: The Burglar Mouse gained a vulnerability to Rift power type traps with the introduction of the Rift Plane.
- 14 October 2014: The Burglar Mouse was given new art. The costumed version appeared during the Halloween 2014 event and the new uncostumed version appeared when the event ended on 4 November 2014.
