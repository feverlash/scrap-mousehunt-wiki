# Glitchpaw Mouse

The Glitchpaw Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Laboratory.
It was previously an Event Mouse, and used to be released in multiple locations whenever MouseHunt experiences unexpected downtime due to server issues. It was known to drop location specific loot when caught.

## History and Trivia

- When MouseHunt experiences unexpected downtime, the Glitchpaw Mouse is released as a treat for Hunters upon the game returning.
- When Hunters encounter issues accessing the game, the image of the Glitchpaw Mouse may appear along with an error message similar to:

> Uh oh! A Glitchpaw mouse sabotaged our code!
HitGrab developers have been notified of your problem.
Error Code:

- 1 April 2009: The Glitchpaw Mouse was briefly released for the first time. It could not be found in the Bristle Woods region, the Meditation Room nor the Pinnacle Chamber.
- 28 July 2009: The Glitchpaw Mouse dropped SUPER|brie+ as loot for the first time.
- 19 February 2010: The Glitchpaw Mouse was released.
- 13 July 2010: The Glitchpaw Mouse was released in all locations, dropping location specific loot and cheese.
- 14 January 2011: The Glitchpaw Mousewas released in all locations.
- 14-23 August 2011: The Glitchpaw Mouse was released after six hours of unscheduled maintenance.
- 7-12 March 2012: The Glitchpaw Mouse was available across nearly the entire Kingdom for the remainder of MouseHunt's Fourth Birthday Celebration.
- 15-19 February 2013: The Glitchpaw Mouse was released during the Year of the Snake Festival, due to unscheduled server downtime.
- 20 March 2013: The Glitchpaw Mouse was released for a few days after a planned server upgrade caused a nine hour downtime.
- 11 April 2013: The Glitchpaw Mouse was released after unplanned downtime interfered with the Spring Egg Hunt 2013 and was available for the remainder of the event along with +5 Luck bonus.
- 4 September 2013: The Glitchpaw Mouse was released for a few days due to unscheduled server downtime.
- 11-14 October 2013: The Glitchpaw Mouse was released due to unscheduled server downtime, making their first appearance in Claw Shot City.
- 13 December 2013: The Glitchpaw Mouse was released due to unscheduled server downtime to fix tournament glitches during the Great Winter Hunt 2013.
- 7 March 2014: The Glitchpaw Mouse was released due to unscheduled server downtime during MouseHunt's Sixth Birthday.
- 13 April 2014: The Glitchpaw Mouse was released due to unplanned server downtime.
- 21-29 October 2014: The Glitchpaw Mouse was released due to intermittent server downtime.
- 4-5 March 2015: The Glitchpaw Mouse was released due to intermittent server downtime.
- 3 September 2015: The Glitchpaw Mouse was released due to unplanned server downtime.
- 27 May 2016: The Glitchpaw Mouse was released due to unplanned server downtime.
- 15 November 2016: The Glitchpaw Mouse was released due to unplanned server downtime. It began visiting Fort Rox and stopped visiting the Fiery Warpath.
- 5 April 2017 The Glitchpaw Mouse was released due to problems caused after planned server downtime.
- 31 May 2017: The Glitchpaw Mouse was released due to problems with the launch of the Bristle Woods Rift.
- 21 July 2019: The Glitchpaw Mouse was released due to unplanned server downtime.
- 26 September 2019: The Glitchpaw Mouse was released due to unplanned server downtime.
- 18 August 2020: The Glitchpaw Mouse was released due to unplanned server downtime.
- 23 February 2021: The Glitchpaw Mouse was released due to unplanned server downtime.
- 11 August 2021: The Glitchpaw Mouse was released due to unplanned server downtime.
- 27 September 2022: The Glitchpaw Mouse was released for approximately 17 hours due to unplanned server downtime.
- 19 March 2024: The Glitchpaw Mouse became a permanent addition to the Laboratory on the start of the Spring Egg Hunt 2023 event. Its points and gold were adjusted to be appropriate for their new difficulties:
Points: 1,200 -> 2,404
Gold: 1,200 -> 2,404
