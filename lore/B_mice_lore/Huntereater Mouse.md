## Cheese Preference

The Huntereater Mouse can only be attracted by using Diamond Cheese, and to a lesser extent, Gemstone Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Huntereater Mouse can only be caught using Forgotten power type.


## History and Trivia

- 12 May 2015: The Huntereater Mouse was released as part of the Fungal Cavern location.
