# Ethereal Guardian Mouse

The Ethereal Guardian Mouse can be found in Zokor. It is known to drop Scholar Scrolls.

## Preferred Cheese

The Ethereal Guardian Mouse prefers Glowing Gruyere can be attracted with Standard Cheeses.


## Hunting Strategy

Forgotten weapons are very effective against the Ethereal Guardian Mouse. Arcane weapons are of normal effectiveness.

The Ethereal Guardian Mouse can be found in the Neophyte Scholar Study, the Master Scholar Auditorium, and the Dark Library.


## Loot

- Labyrinth Theme Scrap II and Scholar Scrolls


## History and Trivia

> 9 September 2015: The Ethereal Guardian Mouse was introduced as part of the Hollow Heights release.
