# Penguin Mouse

The Penguin Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Rodentia region. It can be attracted and caught only during the Winter Season. They are not known to drop any loot

## Preferred Cheese

Penguin Mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Strong Hydro weapons such as Ancient Spear Gun and Heat Bath are recommended in order to catch this mouse. Alternatively, hunters may equip their best Tactical trap.
