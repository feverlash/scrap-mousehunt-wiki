## Cheese Preference

Classroom Keener Mouse is only attracted to Apprentice Ambert.


## Hunting Strategy

The Classroom Keener Mouse can only be attracted while enrolled in Shadow Sciences or during the Shadow phase of the Final Exam.

Shadow power type is very effective against Classroom Keener Mouse.
Arcane power type is effective against Classroom Keener Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
