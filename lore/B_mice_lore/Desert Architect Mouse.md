# Desert Architect Mouse

The Desert Architect Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Sandtail Desert region. They are known to drop Limestone Brick, Coconut Timber and Shard of Glass as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Desert Architect mice have been found especially attracted to SUPER|Brie+ cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.


## History and Trivia

- Desert Architect Mice were introduced on 2 June 2011 with the release of the Muridae Market location.
- On 25 June 2013, they gained a vulnerability to Law power type traps, with the release of Varmint Valley.
