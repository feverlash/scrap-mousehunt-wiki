## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The HitGrab Rockin' Horse is a Limited Edition weapon and can no longer be acquired by Hunters. It could be purchased for 158,500 gold from Ronza's Traveling Shoppe during the Ronza's Rockin' Return event in late 2009. It could be refunded for 28,530 gold at the same location.
