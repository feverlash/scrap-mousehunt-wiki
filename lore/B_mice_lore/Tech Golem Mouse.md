# Tech Golem Mouse

The Tech Golem Mouse can be found in the Labyrinth and Zokor. It is known to drop Tech Power Cores in Zokor.

## Preferred Cheese

The Tech Golem Mouse prefers Glowing Gruyere in both the Labyrinth and Zokor, but can be attracted with Standard Cheeses.


## Hunting Strategy

Forgotten weapons are very effective against the Tech Golem Mouse. Arcane weapons are of normal effectiveness.

- Labyrinth

> The Tech Golem Mouse can be found in Superior, and Epic Tech Hallways.

- Zokor

> The Tech Golem Mouse can be found in the Tech Research Center, and the Manaforge.


## Loot

- Zokor

> Labyrinth Theme Scrap I and Tech Power Cores
