# Meteorite Golem Mouse

The Meteorite Golem is a breed of mouse found in Fort Rox. They are known to drop Bloodstone, Howlite, and Meteorite Pieces as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Meteorite Golem Mouse have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Arcane traps are very effective against the Meteorite Golem Mouse. Shadow traps are of normal effectiveness.
The Meteorite Golem Mouse can only be encountered during the Midnight, Pitch, Utter Darkness, and First Light phases at Nighttime in Fort Rox.


## History and Trivia

- 3 November 2016: The Meteorite Golem Mouse was release as a part of Fort Rox update.
