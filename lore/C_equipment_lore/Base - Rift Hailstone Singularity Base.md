# 🛡️ Rift Hailstone Singularity Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 490 Power | 5 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Lord/Lady  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 0

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> The mysterious festive magic contained within the Hailstones at the Great Winter Taiga have been known to exhibit some strange, fantastical properties under certain extraordinary conditions. This base demonstrates what happens when a massive number of Hailstones are combined and compressed into one another. The result is a Hailstone singularity of infinite density and infinite holiday cheer. The overwhelming gravitational force unleashed by this base is so strong that it can bend the very fabric of spacetime in the Valour Rift, allowing MouseHunters to slingshot themselves up the tower at unprecedented speeds. However, this inescapable gravitational force has the side effect of pulling all the tower's loot past its event horizon and into the singularity where it is lost forever.
> This base adds +1 step per catch in the Valour Rift but it consumes all loot drops while climbing the tower.
> In addition, this base has the same properties as the Prestige Base which causes it to grow in power as the hunter reaches new heights:
> +20 Power Boost per floor reached during Ultimate Umbra
> +2 Luck Boost per Total Eclipse floor reached during Ultimate Umbra
> These boosts are based on highest floor reached and are applied permanently as you reach greater heights during Ultimate Umbra.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
This base could only be obtained during Great Winter Hunt 2025 event.

After sending 50 Golems, the Hunter could claim this base from the Reward Track.
