## 📜 Konsep Lore & Aturan Permainan

# Wisdom

Knowledge of hunts past and those yet to come....


## Overview

Wisdom is a hidden stat item that determines a Hunter's Title and their percentage progress towards the next title. It can be gained by catching Mice, claiming Adventure Book rewards, and reading Tomes of Wisdom.

Unlike Points, Wisdom cannot be lost. Every hunt, regardless of the outcome, will grant at least 15 (friend hunt / trap check) or 25 (self-initiated hunt) base Wisdom.

In addition, the ratio of Points to Wisdom varies from mouse to mouse. This means that certain Mice with lower Points compared to other similar breeds may in fact have higher Wisdom values, making them more beneficial to target for players wishing to advance their Hunter's Title. Conversely, it is a common misconception that targeting Mice with high Points will be the fastest way of progressing towards the next title.
