# Sunken Banshee Mouse

The Sunken Banshee Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Sunken City. It is known to drop Barnacles and Sand Dollars as loot.

## Cheese Preference

The Sunken Banshee Mouse has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Sunken Banshee Mouse is susceptible only to Hydro weapons. It can be found in the Haunted Shipwreck zone while on a Dive in the Sunken City.


## Event Participation


### Spring Egg Hunt

- The Sunken Banshee Mouse drops the Barnacle Egg and the Deep, Deep, Deep Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

The Sunken Banshee Mouse was released 2 July 2014 as part of the Sunken City content.
