# Clumsy Cupbearer Mouse

Clumsy Cupbearer Mouse is a breed of mouse found on the Great Hall Floor in Bountiful Beanstalk.

## Cheese Preference

Clumsy Cupbearer Mouse is only attracted to Standard Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Clumsy Cupbearer Mouse can only be attracted after planting a Tall Vine to the Great Hall Floor.

Physical power type is effective against Clumsy Cupbearer Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.


## Loot

Clumsy Cupbearer Mouse can drop:

- Golden Goose Egg
