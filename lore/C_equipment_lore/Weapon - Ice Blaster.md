# ⚔️ Ice Blaster

> **Classification**: Hydro Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 3,800 Power | 12 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Grandmaster  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 189,000

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> The Ice Blaster is a mounted snow cannon complete with an ice ball hopper built in for auto-reloading action! The hopper also doubles as an ice generator as it uses a combination moisture vaporator and "Absolute Below Zero" brand coolant to create additional ammunition as required.
> On rare occasion, the ice balls fired have a chance to absorb the joy released by the hunter when catching a mouse, forging a Snowball Charm on the spot!
> Obtained from cannon parts dropped off of the S.N.O.W. Golem during the Great Winter Snowball Fight of 2013.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Ice Blaster is a Limited Edition weapon and can no longer be acquired by Hunters. It could only be bought from the Trapsmith in the Festive Snow Fort for 189,000 gold, 1 Cannon Bits and 40 Ice Coins during the Great Winter Snowball Fight event. It cannot be refunded.

The Ice Blaster (if unintentionally smashed) can be reassembled from Ice Blaster Parts.
