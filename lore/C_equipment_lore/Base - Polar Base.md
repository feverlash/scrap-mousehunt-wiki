# 🛡️ Polar Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 200 Power | 4 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Journeyman  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 59,550

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> This base is made from magical glacial ice that seems to absorb and freeze the humidity in the air, mysteriously retaining its shape. This physics-defying ice is imported by Ronza the merchant, perhaps making it the most expensive cube of water you'll ever own!

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Polar Base was available for purchase for 59,550 gold from Ronza's Traveling Shoppe during her New Year's Giveaway and MouseHunt's Birthday Giveaway visits. It refunded for 10,719 gold at the same location.

It was also available as one of the many Gifts dropped by the Elf Mouse and the Abominable Snow Mouse mice during the Great Winter Hunt in 2008, and as one of the three gifts inside the Fancy Birthday Gifts dropped by the Birthday Mouse during MouseHunt's 1st Birthday.
