# Sarcophamouse

The Sarcophamouse is a breed of mouse that can be found in the Sand Crypts, and is known to drop Aleth Essences, Ber Essences and Lunaria Petals as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Sarcophamouse have been found to be only attracted to Graveblossom Camembert.


## Hunting Strategy

The Sarcophamouse is a member of the Sand Dunes mouse group. As such all power types except Shadow weapons are ineffective against it.


## History and Trivia

- Sarcophamouse were released on 14 November 2012, as part of the Living Garden update.
