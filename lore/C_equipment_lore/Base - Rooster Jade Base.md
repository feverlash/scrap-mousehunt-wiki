# 🛡️ Rooster Jade Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 350 Power | 10 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Grandmaster  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 0

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Trustworthy, responsible and punctual when sounding the horn, the Fiery Rooster is represented in this Jade Base carving.
> Those who completed the 2017 Lunar New Year adventure were awarded with this beautifully carved Rooster Jade Base!

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Rooster Jade Base was a reward for completing the Celebrate Lunar New Year adventure during the Year of the Rooster event.
