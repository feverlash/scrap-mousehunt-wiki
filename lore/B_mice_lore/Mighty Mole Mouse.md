# Mighty Mole Mouse

The Mighty Mole Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Gnawnia Rift location. They are known to drop Riftiago Potions as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Mighty Mole mice have been found to be especially attracted to String Cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.
