## History and Trivia

- 7 March 2013: The Force Fighter Yellow was first released for the MouseHunt's Fifth Birthday event.
- During MouseHunt's Fifth Birthday, the Force Fighter Yellow could not be attracted when the Limited Edition Ultra MegaMouser MechaBot Trap was equipped.
- The appearance and description of Force Fighter Yellow, like all of the Force Fighters, is a play on the popular children's show Mighty Morphin Power Rangers. Force Fighter Yellow is also based on the character Tuxedo Mask from the Japanese animated show Sailor Moon, and the character Cloud Strife from the Japanese RPG Final Fantasy VII.
