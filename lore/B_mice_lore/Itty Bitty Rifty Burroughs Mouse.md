# Itty Bitty Rifty Burroughs Mouse

The Itty Bitty Rifty Burroughs Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Burroughs Rift. It is not known to drop any loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Itty Bitty Rifty Burroughs Mouse is only attracted to Terre Ricotta Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Itty Bitty Rifty Burroughs Mouse can be attracted when the Mist Meter is in Yellow and Green Zone.

Rift power type is effective against Itty Bitty Rifty Burroughs Mouse while all others are less effective.


## History and Trivia

- 9 September 2014: The Itty Bitty Rifty Burroughs Mouse was released as Itty Bitty Rifty Borroughs as part of the Burroughs Rift location.
- 11 September 2014: The mouse name was corrected from Itty Bitty Rifty Borroughs.
