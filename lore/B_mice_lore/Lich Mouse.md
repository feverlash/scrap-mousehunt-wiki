## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Lich Mouse is attracted only to Runic Cheese.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Lich Mouse.

## Locations

Hunters can encounter the Lich Mouse only in the Acolyte Realm location.


## Loot

The Lich Mouse is not known to drop any loot in the Acolyte Realm location.


## History and Trivia

- 6 March 2009: The Lich Mouse is released.
- 19 April 2011: The Lich Mouse began dropping the Antique Scalloped Pink Egg as loot during the Spring Egg Hunt 2011 event.
- 4 April 2012: The Lich Mouse began dropping the Lich Egg as part of the annual Spring Egg Hunt event.
