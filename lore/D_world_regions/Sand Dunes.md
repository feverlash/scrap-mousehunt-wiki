# 📍 Sand Dunes

> **Wilayah / Region**: Sandtail Desert  
> **Pangkat Minimal**: Baron/Baroness  
> **Fasilitas Lokal**: Cheese ShoppeTrapsmithCharm ShoppeGeneral Store

## 🌍 Gambaran & Atmosfer Lingkungan

Sand Dunes is a hunting location in Sandtail Desert region.

Living Garden, Lost City, Sand Dunes, Twisted Garden, Cursed City, and Sand Crypts are 6 connected hunting locations, forming the Living Garden complex.

They require the minimum rank of Baron/Baroness and the Living Garden Key to access.

Sand Dunes can only be traveled to when the Hunter's map is in Normal state.
