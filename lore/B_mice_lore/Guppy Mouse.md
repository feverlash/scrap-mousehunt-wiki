# Guppy Mouse

The Guppy Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Sunken City. It is known to drop Mouse Scales and Sand Dollars as loot.

## Cheese Preference

The Guppy Mouse has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Guppy Mouse is susceptible only to Hydro weapons. It can be found in the Mermouse Den and School of Mice zones while on a Dive in the Sunken City.


## Event Participation

- The Guppy Mouse drops the Mouse Scale Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

The Guppy Mouse was released 2 July 2014 as part of the Sunken City content.
