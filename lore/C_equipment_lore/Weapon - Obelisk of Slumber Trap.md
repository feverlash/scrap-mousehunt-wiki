# ⚔️ Obelisk of Slumber Trap

> **Classification**: Arcane Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 2,100 Power | 0 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Legendary  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 316,350 Gold

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> This arcane staff is slightly tingly to the touch and stands as a bright beacon designed to mesmerize mice that lurk in darkness. Its mysterious glow and refreshing minty smell gently coax unruly Forgotten mice into a deep sleep.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Obelisk of Slumber Trap can be purchased for 316,350 Gold from Trapsmith in:

- Catacombs
- Forbidden Grove
- Acolyte Realm
- Balack's Cove
- Fort Rox

It refunds for 56,943 gold at the same locations.

The Obelisk of Slumber Trap (if unintentionally smashed) can be reassembled from Obelisk Parts.
