## Hunting Strategy

Desperado Mouse is only available when the Hunter has cleared all Accomplices in a Thieves Gang Wanted Poster.
Law power type is very effective against this mouse. All other types are ineffective.


## History and Trivia

- 25 June 2013: Desperado Mouse was released as part of the Claw Shot City, but couldn't be attracted yet.
- 27 June 2013: Desperado Mouse was made available with the Thieves Gang Wanted Poster.
