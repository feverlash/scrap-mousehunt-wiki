## History and Trivia

- 9 December 2014: Glacia Ice Fist was introduced as part of the Great Winter Hunt 2014 event.

> Building the Ice Tower Level 1 and then catching 40 mice created a message in the Hunter's Journal:
I must take care, Glacia Ice Fist approaches! I must defeat her to complete the first stage of the icy invasion!
Catching Glacia Ice Fist before the first week of the Great Winter Hunt 2014 ends unlocks the Week 1 Bonus Zone and creates an entry in the Hunter's Journal:
I caught Glacia Ice Fist and chased her out of the Crystal Citadel! The next icy general has yet to show their face, so I have some time to spend catching the Stuck Snowball Mouse for prizes and loot!

- 13 December 2016: The points for Glacia Ice Fist were changed.
