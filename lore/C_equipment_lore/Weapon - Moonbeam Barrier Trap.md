# ⚔️ Moonbeam Barrier Trap

> **Classification**: Shadow Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 4,000 Power | 15 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Baron  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 2,500,000

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> A mystical trap that peers into the souls of the mice that approach it through its ruby lense. When detecting one whose spirit aligns with shadow energies, it opens up its trap door and seals them away in its trap dimension.
> Since it closely resembles a segment of a fort wall at Fort Rox, this trap immediately opens up when it detects a Battering Ram Mouse coming at it. Too many lenses have been destroyed during initial testing so it no longer takes any risks.
> This trap has a 100% catch rate against the Battering Ram Mouse.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Moonbeam Barrier Trap can be purchased for 2,500,000 Gold at Ronza's Traveling Shoppe 2019.
