# Night Shift Materials Manager Mouse

The Night Shift Materials Manager is a breed of mouse found in Fort Rox. They are known to drop Fool's Gold, Howlite, Meteorite Pieces and Tower Mana as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Night Shift Materials Manager Mouse have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Shadow traps are very effective against the Night Shift Materials Manager Mouse. Arcane traps are of normal effectiveness.
The Night Shift Materials Manager Mouse can only be encountered during the Twilight, Midnight, Pitch, and Utter Darkness phases at Nighttime in Fort Rox.

- 3 November 2016: The Night Shift Materials Manager Mouse was release as a part of Fort Rox update.
