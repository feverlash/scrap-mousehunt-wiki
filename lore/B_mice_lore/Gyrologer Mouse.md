# Gyrologer Mouse

The Gyrologer Mouse is a breed of mouse found on the Floating Islands, on a Tactical Island or a Tactical Castle.

## Cheese Preference

The Gyrologer Mouse is attracted to Cloud Cheesecake and Standard Cheese.


## Loot


### Floating Islands's Island Based Loot

When found on a Low Altitude Island or a High Altitude Island, depending on the buffs present, the Gyrologer Mouse can drop the following loot:


| Item | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| Sky Glass | Always drops Boosted by Sky Glass Formations and Loot Caches |
| Sky Ore | Always drops Boosted by Sky Ore Deposit and Loot Caches |
| Cloud Curd | Drops when a Cloud Curd Bonus is present Can be boosted by Loot Caches |
| Corsair's Curd | Drops when a Pirate Den is present Can be boosted by Loot Caches |



## Hunting Strategy

Only Tactical traps are effective against the Gyrologer Mouse, all other power types are ineffective. The Gyrologer Mouse can only be encountered on a Tactical Island or a Tactical Castle.


## History and Trivia

- 08 September 2020: The Gyrologer Mouse was introduced with the release of the Floating Islands.
