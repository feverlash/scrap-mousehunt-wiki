## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Keeper Mouse is attracted to Radioactive Blue and Ancient cheese.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Keeper Mouse.

## Locations

Hunters can encounter the Keeper Mouse only in the Catacombs location.


## Loot

The Keeper Mouse drops the following loot in the Catacombs location:

- Ancient Potion
- Coal
- Corrupted Radioactive Blue Potion
- Keeper's Candle
- Plankrun's Journal Cover
