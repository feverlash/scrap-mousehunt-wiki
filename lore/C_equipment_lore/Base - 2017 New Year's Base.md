# 🛡️ 2017 New Year's Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 200 Power | 10 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Novice  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 200,000

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Happy New Year! This party-powered base celebrates 2017 all year long!
> During all of 2017, while this base is equipped, mice have a chance to drop 2017 Charms when caught. The charm-generating powers of this base will cease to work on January 1, 2018.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The 2017 New Year's Base is a Limited Edition base that was only available during the Great Winter Hunt 2016 event. It could be purchased from the Great Winter Hunt 2016 General Store in exchange for 200,000 gold (spent) and 50 Deployed Festive New Year's Fireworks (not spent).
