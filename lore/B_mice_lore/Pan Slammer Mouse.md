## History and Trivia

- 15 April 2014: The Pan Slammer Mouse was released as part of the Spring Egg Hunt 2014 celebration.
- The description for the Pan Slammer Mouse has changed since its release. The final line of its description was removed and read as follows:

> Some mice just want to watch the world yearn.
