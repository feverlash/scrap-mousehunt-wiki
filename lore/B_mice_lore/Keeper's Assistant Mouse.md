## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Keeper's Assistant Mouse is attracted to Radioactive Blue and Ancient cheese.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Keeper's Assistant Mouse.

## Locations

Hunters can encounter the Keeper's Assistant Mouse only in the Catacombs location.


## Loot

The Keeper's Assistant Mouse drops the following loot in the Catacombs location:

- Ancient Potion


## History and Trivia

- 6 March 2009: The Keeper's Assistant Mouse was released.
- 16 April 2011: The Keeper's Assistant Mouse began dropping the Antique Blue Argyle Egg as loot as part of the Spring Egg Hunt 2011 event.
