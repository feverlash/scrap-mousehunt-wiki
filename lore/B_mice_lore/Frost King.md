| This once warm and cheery fellow has always enjoyed the festive season... perhaps a little too much. As a youngster, he would look forward to the holidays all year long but when they finally arrived, all he could think of was how soon they would be gone again. Over time, the warmth that he once felt for this magical season was replaced by an icy cold sliver in his heart that grew and grew until it eventually consumed him, transforming him completely from head to toe.<br>Driven mad by obsession, he became convinced that he could capture the magic of the holidays and keep it forever simply by freezing the festive season in place. And so he set to work on a plan to usher in an infinite winter to the Kingdom of Gnawnia.<br>By harnessing the power of his Magical Holiday Scarf, the self-proclaimed Frost King conjured a fortress made of Reinforced Ice and constructed the nefarious Frost Globes that would allow him to turn this luscious landscape into a frozen prison from which the festive season could never escape! |  |  |  |
