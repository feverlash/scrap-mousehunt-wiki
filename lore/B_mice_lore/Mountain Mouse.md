## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Mountain Mouse can only be attracted with Chedd-Ore Cheese.

Chedd-Ore Cheese is obtained by smashing boulders in the Mountain location.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Mountain Mouse.

## Locations

Hunters can encounter the Mountain Mouse only in the Mountain location.


## Loot

The Mountain Mouse drops Faceted Sugar and Iced Curds as loot which are used to craft Abominable Asiago Cheese.


## History and Trivia

- 7 November 2017: The Mountain Mouse was introduced as part of the Mountain overhaul.
- 17 October 2018: The Mountain Mouse changed mouse groups from Indigenous Mice to Mountain Mice.
