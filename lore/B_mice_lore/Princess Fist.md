# Princess Fist

The Princess Fist is a breed of mouse found in the General zones of the Iceberg. It is known to drop Bottled Cold Fusion, Hunting Research, Satchel of Gold, Sticky Charms, and Wax Charms as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Princess Fist has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## History and Trivia

- 24 May 2012: The Princess Fist was released as a part of the Iceberg.
- The description is a reference to a comic book hero Iron Man.
- 1 June 2012: The Princess Fist began to drop additional loot.
