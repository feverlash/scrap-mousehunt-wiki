## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

In the Forbidden Grove location, the Gate Guardian Mouse will be attracted when hunting with Ancient Cheese
In the Acolyte Realm location, the Gate Guardian Mouse will be attracted when hunting with Radioactive Blue cheese and (to a lesser degree) Ancient and Runic cheeses.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Gate Guardian Mouse.
