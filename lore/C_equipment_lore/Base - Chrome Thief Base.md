# 🛡️ Chrome Thief Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 1250 Power | 25 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Archduke  
> **Acquisition Cost**: #Obtained Via

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> This remarkable base was originally designed by a group of clever yet morally-dubious hunters who pooled their knowledge and resources to create a base that could properly pillage the city of Zokor.
> Some of them preferred a slow and quiet approach whereas others just wanted to smash and grab. After a lengthy debate, they finally decided: "Why not both?" And thus, the first truly switchable base was created.
> However, the original design blueprints that contained the secrets to this base's extraordinary effects was lost to the Trapsmiths of Gnawnia... until now! It turns out that the villainous Captain Crook discovered and then stole these lost blueprints during her raid of Zokor so that she could make her own pirated version. Now that the design secrets have been recovered, the Trapsmith at the Cerulean Skyport is able to forge this new, stronger, and shinier version using the miraculous combination of Cerulean Chrome that has been charged with the energy from Adorned Empyrean Jewels!
> Upon entering Zokor, hunters will be able to switch this base to one of the following modes:
> Stealth Mode:
> Provides a 50% chance to not lose Stealth.
> Heist Mode:
> Doubles Stealth lost but also doubles Fealty, Tech, and Scholar district loot gained.
> This base does not affect loot drops in the Minotaur's Lair.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Chrome Thief Base can be purchased from the Trapsmith in Cerulean Skyport for:

- 5,000,000 Gold
- 20 Chrome Thief Blueprint Scrap
- 200 Cerulean Chrome Ingot
- 3 Adorned Empyrean Jewel

It requires the Hunter to own the Chrome Base and complete the Liberate the Cerulean Skyport from Captain Crook adventure to purchase.

It cannot be refunded.
