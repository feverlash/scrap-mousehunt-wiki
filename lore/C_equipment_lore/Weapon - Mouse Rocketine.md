# ⚔️ Mouse Rocketine

> **Classification**: Physical Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 700 Power | 3 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Apprentice  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 22,400 Gold

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Off with their heads! This super-charged, rocket-powered version of a guillotine is the perfect way to hold all those criminal mice accountable for stealing your precious cheese.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Mouse Rocketine can be purchased for 22,400 Gold from the Trapsmith in the following locations:

- Town of Gnawnia
- Windmill
- Harbour
- Mountain
- King's Gauntlet

It can be refunded for 4,035 Gold at the same locations.
