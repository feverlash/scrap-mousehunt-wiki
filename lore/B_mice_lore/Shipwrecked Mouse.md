# Shipwrecked Mouse

The Shipwrecked Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Cape Clawed and S.S. Huntington IV. They are known to drop Flawless Orb as loot in both locations, and the Sextant in S.S. Huntington IV.

## Preferred Cheese

Shipwrecked mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## History and Trivia

- On 25 January 2011 they began to drop Flawless Orb as loot.
- Shipwrecked mice began dropping the Antique Green Plaid Egg as loot, in Cape Clawed on 14 April 2011, for the Spring Egg Hunt 2011 event.
