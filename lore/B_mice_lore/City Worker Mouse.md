City Worker Mouse is a mouse found in Sunken City. It can drop Oxygen Canister as loot.

## Hunting Strategy

City Worker Mouse is only available when Docked in Sunken City.

It can be attracted with Standard Cheese and Fishy Fromage.

Hydro is the only power type working against this mouse.
