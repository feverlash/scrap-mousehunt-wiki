# Theurgy Warden Mouse

The Theurgy Warden Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Fiery Warpath location. It can be attracted and caught only during Wave Four. They are known to drop Flameshards and Simple Orbs.

## Preferred Cheese

Theurgy Warden Mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## History and Trivia

- Theurgy Warden Mice were introduced on 22 March 2011 with the release of the Sandtail Desert region.
- They began dropping the Antique Scalloped Pink Egg as loot, on 19 April 2011, for the Spring Egg Hunt 2011 event.
