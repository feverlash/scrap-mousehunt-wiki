# 🛡️ Compass Magnet Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 100 Power | 5 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Duke/Duchess  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 0

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> This base is trimmed with brass and encased in glass and embedded within is a unique compass that behaves a bit differently from your run-of-the-mill Labyrinth Compass. While the Compass Magnet Base cannot prevent Dead End Clues like the Labyrinth Base, it can aid by extracting these unwanted clues from your actual Labyrinth Compass and absorbing them into the one trapped under glass.
> Using this base inside of the Labyrinth has a 30% chance of removing a Dead End Clue from your Labyrinth Compass after every catch, even within intersections!

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
Compass Magnet Base can only be obtained from opening Season 9 Treasure Chests. These chests are labeled (Season 9) in their names.

The chance of a chest containing this base increases with every chest opened.

The 30th Season 9 chest is guaranteed to contain the Compass Magnet Base if it is not yet owned by the Hunter.
