## Cheese Preference

Slipstream the Virtuoso of Vapours is only attracted to Aurora Bocconcini.


## Hunting Strategy

The Slipstream the Virtuoso of Vapours can only be attracted during an Atmospheric shipment.

Law power type is very effective against Slipstream the Virtuoso of Vapours.
All other types are ineffective.
