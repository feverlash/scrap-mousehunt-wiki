# 🛡️ Treasure Seeker Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 150 Power | 10 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Duke  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 25,000 Gold10 Ornament Sugar Cookies15 Snowflake Sugar Cookies40 Nutcracker Sugar Cookies

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> A wonder of Christmas contained under a pane of glass and wrapped in tinsel and ribbon. When gazed upon, this base looks deep inside a person's heart and shows them the wondrous life that could be theirs if they changed their ways.
> Using this base inside of the Labyrinth has a chance of preventing a Dead End Clue from confusing your Labyrinth Compass during an unsuccessful hunt.
> This base also has a chance to change any non-Dead End clue found into a Treasury Clue.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Treasure Seeker Base can be purchased for 25,000 Gold, 10 Ornament Sugar Cookies, 15 Snowflake Sugar Cookies and 40 Nutcracker Sugar Cookies at Great Winter Hunt 2019.
