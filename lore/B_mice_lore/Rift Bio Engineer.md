# Rift Bio Engineer

The Rift Bio Engineer is a breed of mouse found in the Burroughs Rift. It is known to drop Mist Canisters as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Rift Bio Engineer can be attracted using any Standard String Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Rift Bio Engineer can be attracted when the Mist Meter is in No Mist Zone.

Rift power type is effective against Rift Bio Engineer while all others are less effective.


## History and Trivia

- 9 September 2014: The Rift Bio Engineer was released as part of the Burroughs Rift location.
