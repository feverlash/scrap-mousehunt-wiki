# ⚔️ 500 Pound Spiked Crusher

> **Classification**: Physical Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 350 Power | 1 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Novice  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 3,000 Gold

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> A 500 pound weight with sharp spikes on the bottom precariously hangs by a thin rope, eagerly awaiting a mouse to wander below.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The 500 Pound Spiked Crusher can be purchased from the Trapsmith in Meadow and Town of Gnawnia for 3,000 Gold.

It can be refunded for 540 Gold at the same locations.
