# Ancient of the Deep

The Ancient of the Deep is a breed of mouse found in the Sunken City. It is known to drop Brined Curd, Ultimate Charm, Ultimate Luck Charm, and Ultimate Power Charm as loot.

## Cheese Preference

The Ancient of the Deep has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Ancient of the Deep is susceptible only to Hydro weapons. It can be found in the Lair of the Ancients and Monster Trench zones while on a Dive in the Sunken City


## Event Participation


### Spring Egg Hunt

- The Ancient of the Deep drops the Deep, Deep, Deep Egg and the Underwater Predator Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

The Ancient of the Deep was released 2 July 2014 as part of the Sunken City content.
