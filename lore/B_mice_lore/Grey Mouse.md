# Grey Mouse

> "Grey" redirects here. For other uses, see Grey (disambiguation).

## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Grey Mouse prefers Standard Cheese, but will avoid traps baited with White Cheddar Cheese.

In the Mountain location, the Grey Mouse will not be encountered when hunting with White Cheddar Cheese, Chedd-Ore Cheese, or Abominable Asiago.
In the Windmill location, the Grey Mouse will not be encountered when hunting with White Cheddar Cheese or Grilled Cheese.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Grey Mouse.

## Loot

The Grey Mouse drops various loot in the following locations:

- Meadow and Town of Gnawnia

> Marble Cheese

- Windmill

> Packet of Flour

The Grey Mouse is not known to drop any loot in the Harbour, King's Arms, or Mountain locations.


## History and Trivia

- 7 March 2008: The Grey Mouse was introduced with the release of MouseHunt.
- 25 June 2013: The Grey Mouse gained a vulnerability to Law power type traps, with the release of Varmint Valley.
- 25 March 2014: The Greyrun Mouse was introduced as the Rift counterpart of the Grey Mouse.
- 25 March 2014: The Grey Mouse gained a vulnerability to Rift power type traps, with the introduction of the Rift Plane.
- 2 September 2014: The Grey Mouse began dropping Packets of Flour at the Windmill.
