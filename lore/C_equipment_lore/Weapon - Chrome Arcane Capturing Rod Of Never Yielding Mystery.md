# ⚔️ Chrome Arcane Capturing Rod Of Never Yielding Mystery

> **Classification**: Arcane Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 5,000 Power | 20 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Legendary  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 1,900,000

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> This highly unstable trap has been introduced to chrome! Its constant state of metamorphosis mixes and swirls the chrome with the unpredictable power of the Onyx stone. The constant movement and flow prevents the chrome from hardening, almost giving the Onyx stone access to liquid metal limbs for added power!

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Chrome Arcane Capturing Rod Of Never Yielding Mystery is a Limited Edition weapon and could be purchased from the trapsmith in Ronza's Traveling Shoppe 2016. It costs 1,900,000 gold. It cannot be refunded.
