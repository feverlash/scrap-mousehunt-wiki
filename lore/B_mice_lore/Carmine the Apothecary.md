# Carmine the Apothecary

The Carmine the Apothecary is a breed of mouse that can be found in the Living Garden.

After capturing the Carmine the Apothecary in the Living Garden, the hunter is transferred to a different version of the location, the Twisted Garden. This also will allow travel to the alternate version of the Lost City, the Cursed City, and the alternate version of the Sand Dunes, the Sand Crypts. If the Hunter hunts with an incorrect cheese at any time, the Hunter will be returned to the normal versions of these locations.

## Preferred Cheese

The Carmine the Apothecary is only attracted to Duskshade Camembert.


## Hunting Strategy

The Carmine the Apothecary is a member of the Living Garden mouse group. As such all power types except Hydro weapons are ineffective against it.


## History and Trivia

- 14 November 2012: The Carmine the Apothecary was released as a part of the Living Garden update.
- The in-game description for the Carmine the Apothecary changes after the Hunter first catches her.
