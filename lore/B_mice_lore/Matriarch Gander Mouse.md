| This prolific writer is something of a mystery even to her fellow authors. No one has ever been able to determine definitively whether or not she is in fact a fictional character herself. Some literary scholars have theorized that she is both an author and a character and that she simply imagined herself into existence one day... although this theory is not very popular due its profound existential suggestions. Regardless of the ambiguous nature of her existence, she has proven herself to be a talented author and a compelling character so she and her trusty gander sidekick were must-haves for the Mythweaver's roster of literary talent.
