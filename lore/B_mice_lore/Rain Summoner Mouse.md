## Preferred Cheese

The Rain Summoner Mouse has been found to be especially attracted to Rainy Cheese cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.

## Hunting Strategy

Only Shadow, Arcane and Draconic type traps are effective against the Rain Summoner Mouse, all other power types are ineffective.

The Rain Summoner Mouse is only available between 35%-79% rain intensities in Moussu Picchu.


## History and Trivia
