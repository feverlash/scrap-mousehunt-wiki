## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Master of the Cheese Belt Mouse is attracted only to Glutter Cheese.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Master of the Cheese Belt Mouse.

## Locations

Hunters can encounter the Master of the Cheese Belt Mouse only in the Meditation Room location.


## Loot

The Master of the Cheese Belt Mouse drops the following loot in the Meditation Room:

- Master Belt Shard
- Satchel of Gold (1,000 gold)


## History and Trivia

- 26 November 2008: The Master of the Cheese Belt Mouse was released.
- 10 February 2011: The Master of the Cheese Belt Mouse began to drop the Ox Mask as loot in the Meditation Room as part of the Heart of the Rabbit Party.
