# 🛡️ Tribal Kaboom Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 200 Power | 0 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Legendary  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 172,000

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> A base made of tribal party crackers packed full of Tribal Isles loot. Those tribal mice can't resist this New Year's party tradition, literally jumping into your base to pop these poppers!
> In their excitement, they have the chance to pop open and drop some extra loot when caught with this base.
> The Tribal Kaboom base will increase drops for Seashells, Savoury Vegetables, Delicious Stones and all Seeds in the Tribal Isles!

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Tribal Kaboom Base can only be bought from the Extreme Toboggan Challenge Trapsmith during Great Winter Hunt 2015. It cost 172,000 gold and could not be refunded.
