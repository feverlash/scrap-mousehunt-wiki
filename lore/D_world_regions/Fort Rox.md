# 📍 Fort Rox

> **Wilayah / Region**: Varmint Valley  
> **Pangkat Minimal**: Baron/Baroness  
> **Fasilitas Lokal**: Cheese ShoppeTrapsmithCharm ShoppeGeneral StoreCartographer

## 🌍 Gambaran & Atmosfer Lingkungan

Fort Rox is a hunting location in Varmint Valley region.

It requires the minimum Rank of Baron/Baroness and the Fort Rox Battle Summons to access.

There are 5 defensive features in this area:

- The Wall determines the Hunter's survivability during Night and Dawn.
- The Ballista improves the catch rate against Weremice and Nightmancer.
- The Cannon improves the catch rate against Cosmic Critter and Nightfire.
- The Moat reduces damage to the Wall.
- The Mage Tower provides various effects to hunting and progressing in this area.

There are 4 phases in this area:

- During Day, the Hunter can collect Meteorite Piece, repair Wall and upgrade Fort.
- During Night, the Hunter can collect Howlite and Bloodstone to upgrade Fort when return to Day.
- During Dawn, the Hunter can collect Dawn Dust to purchase Map Piece for the next area as well as to access a harder challenge in Fort Rox.
- During Heart of the Meteor, the Hunter can hunt for Meteoric Core Fragments, which is required to purchase a new Law weapon in the Trapsmith.
