## Preferred Cheese

Spirit Fox mice have been found to be especially attracted to String Cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.


## Hunting Strategy

The Spirit Fox Mouse is weak to Rift Traps. It can only be attracted by String Cheese.

The Spirit Fox Mouse is only available when rage in the Crazed Clearing section in Whisker Woods Rift is between 25-49.


## Event Participation


### Spring Egg Hunt

- The Spirit Fox Mouse drops the Enlarged Rift Egg and the Rift Mist Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

- Spirit Fox mice were introduced on 28 January 2015, with the introduction of the Whisker Woods Rift.
- Spirit Fox mice are the Rift version of Foxy mice.
