# Charm Shoppe

Enhance your bait with magical charms that can boost the stats of your trap!
Using these charms along with your bait can give your trap boosted stats or special abilities.
Charms are consumed each time a piece of bait is used.

A Charm Shoppe is a shop which sells charms. Several regions include a permanent Charm Shoppe, and Events also tend to have a temporary Charm Shoppe. While the wording of the charm shoppe implies that charms are consumed on an encounter, there are some exceptions to the rule; read the "Consumed Upon" stat for a particular charm to see when it will be consumed.
