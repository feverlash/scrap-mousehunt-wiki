# Portable Generator Mouse

The Portable Generator Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Burroughs Rift. It is known to drop Mist Canisters and The Red Button as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Portable Generator mice have been found to be especially attracted to Magical String, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.


## Hunting Strategy

Portable Generator mice are weak to Rift Traps. They can only be attracted by Magical String while hunting at 0 Mist.


## Event Participation


### Spring Egg Hunt

- The Portable Generator Mouse drops the Enlarged Rift Egg and the Rift Mist Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

- Portable Generator mice were introduced on 09 September 2014, with the introduction of the Burroughs Rift.
