# Lovely Sports Mouse

The Lovely Sports Mouse is part of the Indigenous Mice found in Tournament Hall.
It was previously an Event Mouse that was found during the annual Valentine's Day or Lunar New Year events.

## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Lovely Sports Mouse is attracted only to Runny Cheese.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Lovely Sports Mouse.

## History and Trivia

- 30 January 2014: The Lovely Sports Mouse was released as part of the Year of the Horse Festival event.
- 19 March 2024: The Lovely Sports Mouse became a permanent addition to the Tournament Hall on the start of the Spring Egg Hunt 2023 event. Its points and gold were adjusted to be appropriate for their new difficulties:
Points: 1,850 -> 950
Gold: 450 -> 750
