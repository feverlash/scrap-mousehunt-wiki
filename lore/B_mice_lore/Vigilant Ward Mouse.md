# Vigilant Ward Mouse

The Vigilant Ward Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Bristle Woods Rift. It is the Rift counterpart of Gargoyle Mouse.

## Preferred Cheese

The Vigilant Ward Mouse has been found to be especially attracted to Ancient String Cheese and Runic String Cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.

Only Rift type traps are very effective against Vigilant Ward Mouse, all other power types are ineffective.
