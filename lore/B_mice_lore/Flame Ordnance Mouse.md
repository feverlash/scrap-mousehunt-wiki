# Flame Ordnance Mouse

The Flame Ordnance Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Fiery Warpath location. It can be attracted and caught only during Wave Three. They are not known to drop any loot. It is the only mouse belonging to The Marching Flame Artillery sub-group, and unlike the rest of the sub-groups, has no associated charms.

## Preferred Cheese

Flame Ordnance Mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Event Participation

- The Flame Ordnance Mouse drops the Warpath Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

- Flame Ordnance Mice were introduced on 22 March 2011 with the release of the Sandtail Desert region.
- Flame Ordnance Mice began dropping the Antique Wavy Purple Egg as loot, on 18 April 2011, for the Spring Egg Hunt 2011 event.
- During the Spring Egg Hunt 2011, Flame Ordnance mice also dropped a bonus Warpath Egg as loot for hunters who had obtained the Rocket Rabbit Mallet. They dropped the same loot the following year during the Spring Egg Hunt 2012 event for hunters who had obtained the Eggscavator.
