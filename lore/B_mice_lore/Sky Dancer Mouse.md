## Cheese Preference

- On an Arcane Island or an Arcane Keep, the Sky Dancer Mouse is only attracted to Cloud Cheesecake.
- In an Arcane Cauldron, the Sky Dancer Mouse is only attracted to Extra Rich Cloud Cheesecake.


## Hunting Strategy

Only Arcane traps are effective against the Sky Dancer Mouse, all other power types are ineffective. The Sky Dancer Mouse can only be encountered on an Arcane Island, an Arcane Keep, or an Arcane Cauldron.
