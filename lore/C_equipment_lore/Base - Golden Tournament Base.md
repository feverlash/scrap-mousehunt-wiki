# 🛡️ Golden Tournament Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 500 Power | 8 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Lord/Lady  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 200 Tournament Token

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> The Golden Tournament Base is a reward that is renowned as a symbol of a seasoned tournament competitor. Only the most dedicated of hunters dare set the goal of obtaining this opulent base.
> When armed alongside a Champion Charm, in addition to the charm's +3 luck, this base glows with an extra +4 luck, for a total of 15 luck!

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Golden Tournament Base can be purchased from the Trapsmith in Tournament Hall for 200 Tournament Token.

It requires the Hunter to own the Silver Tournament Base to purchase.

It cannot be refunded.
