# 🛡️ Prestige Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 490 Power | 5 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Archduke/Archduchess  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 12,170,000 gold2 Core of the Eclipse

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> A slab of the foundation from the Valour Rift Gauntlet Tower. It shares a bond with the original structure and grows stronger the further you progress into the tower. The bond is so strong that it persists through and outside of the Rift Plane, in all other areas of the Kingdom.
> This base will grow in power as the hunter reaches new heights:
> +20 Power Boost per floor reached during Ultimate Umbra
> +2 Luck Boost per Total Eclipse floor reached during Ultimate Umbra
> These boosts are based on highest floor reached and are applied permanently as you reach greater heights during Ultimate Umbra.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Prestige Base can be purchased from the Trapsmith in Valour Rift for:

- 12,170,000 gold
- 2 Core of the Eclipse

The Hunter must have completed the following adventures in order to purchase the base:

- Capture the Eclipse Mouse
- Defeat Twisted Carmine
- Capture the Goliath Field Mouse
- Defeat the Menace of the Rift
- Capture the Monstrous Black Widow
- Capture the Grand Master of the Dojo
- Capture the Absolute Acolyte

It cannot be refunded.
