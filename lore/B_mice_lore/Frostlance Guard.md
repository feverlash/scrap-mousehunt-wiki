# Frostlance Guard

The Frostlance Guard is a breed of mouse found in the later zones of the Iceberg: Icewing's Lair and Hidden Depths. It is known to drop Hunting Research, Satchel of Gold, Sticky Charms, and Wax Charms as loot.
