# Big Bad Behemoth Burroughs Mouse

The Big Bad Behemoth Burroughs Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Burroughs Rift. It is known to drop Rift Circuitry and Burroughs Rift Theme Scrap III as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Big Bad Behemoth Burroughs Mouse is only attracted to Terre Ricotta Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Big Bad Behemoth Burroughs Mouse can only be attracted when the Mist Meter is in Red Zone.

Rift power type is effective against Big Bad Behemoth Burroughs Mouse while all others are less effective.


## History and Trivia

- 9 September 2014: The Big Bad Behemoth Burroughs Mouse was released as part of the Burroughs Rift location.
