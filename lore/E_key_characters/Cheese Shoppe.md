# Cheese Shoppe

> Welcome to the Cheese Shoppe! Using better cheese attracts mice to your trap more often, so be sure to buy the best cheese you can afford, without exceeding the gold you earn from hunting. Want to save your gold and achieve the best attraction rate around? Buy SUPERlbrie+, and experience perks in attraction, crafting, and exclusive mice!

A Cheese Shoppe is a type of shop that offers various types of cheese for Hunters. Cheese is necessary for attracting mice to a trap setup..
