# ⚔️ C.L.A.W. Machine Trap

> **Classification**: Law Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 5500 Power | 18 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Count  
> **Acquisition Cost**: #Obtained Via

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> This creative contraption is Ronza's greatest contribution to the field of anti-pirate tech: the Confiscation and Legal Apprehension of Wrongdoers (C.L.A.W.) Machine! This highly sophisticated mechanical grappling device is specifically calibrated to locate and extract hidden stolen and prohibited items from Sky Pirates, Sky Raiders, and any other lawless mice who may be carrying corsair contraband.
> This marvellous machine DOUBLES loot drops of Corsair's Curds and Sky Pirate Seals.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The C.L.A.W. Machine Trap can be purchased from the Trapsmith in Cerulean Skyport for:

- 5,000,000 gold
- 25 Cerulean Chrome Ingot

It requires the Hunter to complete the Liberate the Cerulean Skyport from Captain Crook adventure to purchase.

The C.L.A.W. Machine Trap can initially be purchased from Ronza's Shoppe for:
