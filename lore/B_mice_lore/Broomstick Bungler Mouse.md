## Cheese Preference

Broomstick Bungler Mouse is only attracted to Standard Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Broomstick Bungler Mouse can only be attracted while enrolled in Arcane Arts or during the Arcane phase of the Final Exam.

Arcane power type is very effective against Broomstick Bungler Mouse.
Shadow power type is effective against Broomstick Bungler Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
