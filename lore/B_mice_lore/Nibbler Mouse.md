# Nibbler Mouse

The Nibbler Mouse is a breed of mouse found in various locations in Gnawnia, Valour, Whisker Woods, Burroughs, Furoma, Tribal Isles and Rodentia .

## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Nibbler Mouse is attracted only to SUPER|brie+.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Nibbler Mouse.

The Nibbler Mouse drops Ancient Relics in all locations where it can be found.


## History and Trivia

- 13 January 2009: The Nibbler Mouse was released as a thank you to the MouseHunt community. It took its name from The Nibbler, the now-defunct community run MouseHunt newspaper.
- 2 September 2009: The Nibbler Mouse began appearing in the Cape Clawed location.
- June 2010: The Nibbler Mouse was temporarily removed from the Cape Clawed location in June-July 2010 and reappeared in August 2010.
- Unknown date: The Nibbler Mouse was originally available in the Harbour and Lagoon but relocated from those locations to the Bazaar and S.S. Huntington IV.
- 14 April 2011: The Nibbler Mouse began dropping a bonus Nibbler Egg as loot as part of the Spring Egg Hunt 2011 event.
- 29 August 2012: The Nibbler Mouse began dropping Ancient Relic as loot.
- 25 June 2013: The Nibbler Mouse gained a vulnerability to Law power type traps, with the release of Varmint Valley.
- 25 March 2014: The Nibbler Mouse gained a vulnerability to Rift power type traps, with the introduction of the Rift Plane.
- 5 October 2021: The Gold for Nibbler Mouse was increased as part of the Town of Gnawnia and Windmill gameplay update.
