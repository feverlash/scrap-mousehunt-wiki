## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Vaporior Mouse is only attracted to Hot Queso or Flamin' Queso.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Vaporior Mouse.


## Hunting Strategy

Catching a Vaporior Mouse while building pressure in Queso Geyser increases the amount of pressure by 40 kPa.

Only Draconic traps are effective against the Vaporior Mouse, all other power types are ineffective. The Vaporior Mouse can only be encountered while building pressure in Queso Geyser, after a cork has been constructed.

Dragonbane Charm, Super Dragonbane Charm, Extreme Dragonbane Charm, and Ultimate Dragonbane Charm provide a power bonus against the Vaporior Mouse.
