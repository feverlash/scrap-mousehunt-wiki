# Bruticle Mouse

The Bruticle Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Rodentia region. It can be attracted and caught only during the Winter Season.

## Preferred Cheese

Bruticle Mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Strong Hydro weapons such as Ancient Spear Gun and Heat Bath are recommended in order to catch this mouse. Alternatively, hunters may equip their best Tactical trap.


## History and Trivia

- Bruticle Mice were introduced on 17 August 2010 with the release of the Seasonal Garden location.
- On 30 March 2011 they began to drop the Sandtail Call to Arms for the first time, following the release of the Fiery Warpath location on 22 March 2011.
- On 29 July 2015 they stopped dropping the Sandtail Call to Arms as loot with the introduction of the Adventure Book.
