## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Acolyte Mouse is attracted only to Runic Cheese.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Acolyte Mouse.

## Locations

Hunters can encounter the Acolyte Mouse only in the Acolyte Realm location.


## Loot

The Acolyte Mouse drops the following loot in the Acolyte Realm location:

- Mysterious Box
- Ancient Relic


## History and Trivia

- The Acolyte Mouse was first discovered after a glitch occurred while the developers were clearing out the account of a known scammer.

- On the Dojo header image, the two mice standing guard are Acolyte Mice.

- Acolyte Mice caught before 25 March 2009 did not drop Mysterious Boxes.

- On 30 September 2009, the Acolyte Mouse began to drop Frozen Scrolls alongside Mysterious Boxes. Hunters with previously unopened Mysterious Boxes were credited a Frozen Scroll, while those with a Frozen Scroll were given another Mysterious Box.

- In MouseHunt 3.0, the Acolyte Mouse no longer drops a Frozen Scroll. Instead the Mysterious Box now always gives a Frozen Scroll plus one random item.

- The Acolyte Mouse began dropping Ancient Relics as loot on 29 August 2012.
