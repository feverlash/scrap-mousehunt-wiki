## Cheese Preference

Bitter Grammarian is only attracted to Final Draft Derby Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Bitter Grammarian is only available when the stage is writing.

Forgotten power type is very effective against Bitter Grammarian.
All other types are ineffective.


## Loot

Bitter Grammarian can drop:

- Ancient Relic
- Condensed Creativity
- Flawless Orb

With Leather Bound Cover, the following loot is added to the pool:

- Cleverness Clam
- Crop Coin
- Inspiration Ink
- Mythical Mulch
- Pond Penny
- Papyrus Seed
- Parable Papyrus
