## Cheese Preference

Crusty Crab Mouse is only attracted to Standard Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Crusty Crab Mouse can only be attracted while on the shore or the rapids before the waterfall in Epilogue Falls.

Hydro power type is effective against Crusty Crab Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
