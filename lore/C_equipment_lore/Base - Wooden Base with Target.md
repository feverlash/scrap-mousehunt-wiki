# 🛡️ Wooden Base with Target

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 75 Power | 0 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Novice  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 850 Gold

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> A wooden base with a target pad to increase the accuracy of your weapon.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Wooden Base with Target can be purchased from the Trapsmith in the following locations:

- Meadow
- Town of Gnawnia
- Windmill
- Harbour
- Mountain

It can be refunded for 155 Gold at the same locations.
