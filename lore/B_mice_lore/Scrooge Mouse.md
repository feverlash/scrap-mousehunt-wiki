## History and Trivia

- The Scrooge Mouse's name, appearance, and description reference the Ebenezer Scrooge character from A Christmas Carol.
- 3 December 2009: The Scrooge Mouse was released as part of the Great Winter Hunt '09 event.
- 23 December 2009: The Scrooge Mouse began dropping Twelve Liters of Egg Nog as loot.
- 15 December 2010: TheScrooge Mouse dropped Heart of Ice as loot. The original notification about the re-release announced that the Scrooge Mouse dropped a Frozen Heart as loot; it was subsequently renamed Heart of Ice during a game update on the same day.
- 13 December 2016: The points/gold for Scrooge Mouse were changed.
