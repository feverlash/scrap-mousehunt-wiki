> "Magic" redirects here. For other uses, see Magic (disambiguation).

## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Magic Mouse is attracted only to SUPER|brie+.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Magic Mouse.

## History and Trivia

- 30 June 2010: The Magic Mouse was released as part of the Windmill update.
- 12 January 2011: The Magic Mouse could be found at Ronza's Traveling Shoppe where it dropped Luck Charm and Rockforth Cheese as loot.
- 14 April 2011: The Magic Mouse began dropping a bonus Magic Egg as loot as part of the Spring Egg Hunt 2011 event.
- 25 June 2013: The Magic Mouse gained a vulnerability to Law power type traps, with the release of Varmint Valley.
- 25 March 2014: The Magic Mouse gained a vulnerability to Rift power type traps, with the introduction of the Rift Plane.
- 5 October 2021: The Gold for Magic Mouse was increased as part of the Town of Gnawnia and Windmill gameplay update.
