# Snow Bowler Mouse

The Snow Bowler Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Slushy Shoreline and the Brutal Bulwark, Bombing Run, and The Mad Depths zones of the Iceberg location. They are known to drop War Scraps as loot in the Slushy Shoreline, and Hunting Research, Satchel of Gold, Sticky Charms, and Wax Charms in the Iceberg location. Additionally, they are known to drop Frozen Sealed Bottle in the Brutal Bulwark zone of the Iceberg.

## Preferred Cheese

The Snow Bowler Mouse has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

When a Snow Bowler Mouse is encountered in the Iceberg, the hunter will lose progress regardless of whether the mouse is caught or missed, unless the Iceberg Boiler Base, Spiked Base, or Ultimate Iceberg Base is armed.


## History and Trivia

- 7 May 2012:: The Snow Bowler Mouse was released in Slushy Shoreline. It was found to drop War Supplies as loot until 14 May 2012.
- Between 14 May 2012 and 24 May 2012: The Snow Bowler Mouse dropped Interlocking Hull Fragments.
- 1 June 2012: The Snow Bowler Mouse began to drop loot in the Iceberg.
- 18 June 2012: The Snow Bowler Mouse began to drop Frozen Sealed Bottle.
