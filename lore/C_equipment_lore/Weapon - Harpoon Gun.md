# ⚔️ Harpoon Gun

> **Classification**: Hydro Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 3,200 Power | 0 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Legendary  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 450,000 Gold

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Firing an oversized 200 pound spiked projectile makes for an extremely intimidating trap.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Harpoon Gun can be purchased for 450,000 Gold from the Trapsmith in:

- S.S. Huntington IV
- Cape Clawed
- Elub Shore
- Dracano
- Toxic Spill
- King's Gauntlet
- Slushy Shoreline
- Iceberg

It can be refunded for 81,000 Gold at the same locations.

The Harpoon Gun, if smashed, can be reassembled from Launcher Parts.
