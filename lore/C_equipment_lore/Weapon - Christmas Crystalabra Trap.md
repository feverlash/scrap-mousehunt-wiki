# ⚔️ Christmas Crystalabra Trap

> **Classification**: Rift Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 1,200 Power | 4 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Count/Countess  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 150,000 Gold1 Nugget of Nougat

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> A golden Christmas candelabra modified to hold powerful Rift crystals, this trap is a great holiday decoration that also doubles as a fully functional Rift trap. Plus, it looks great on the dinner table!
> The Christmas Crystalabra is in tune with the unknown energies of the Rift. Whenever a mouse caught by this trap drops Calcified Rift Mist, the crystals will hum and discharge energy creating one additional Calcified Rift Mist!

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Christmas Crystalabra Trap could be purchased from the Great Winter Hunt 2017 Trapsmith for:

- 150,000 Gold
- 1 Nugget of Nougat

It cannot be refunded.
