## Cheese Preference

Stubby Scrap Scavenger Mouse is only attracted to Sky Pirate Swiss or Sky Raider Romano.


## Hunting Strategy

The Stubby Scrap Scavenger Mouse can only be attracted while docked.

Law power type is effective against Stubby Scrap Scavenger Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
