## Preferred Cheese

Cursed mice have been found to be only attracted to Dewthief Camembert.


## Hunting Strategy

The Cursed mouse is a member of the Lost City mouse group. As such all power types except Arcane weapons are ineffective against it. During the Lost Curse Minigame, the Cursed mouse will not be attracted for the first three hunts after the successful use of a Searcher charm.


## History and Trivia

- 14 November 2012: The Cursed Mouse was released as a part of the Living Garden update.
