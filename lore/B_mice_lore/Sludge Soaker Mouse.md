# Sludge Soaker Mouse

The Sludge Soaker Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Toxic Spill, for hunters of rank Lord/Lady and above.

## Preferred Cheese

Sludge Soaker mice are attracted to Magical Rancid Radioactive Blue at every spill level and to Rancid Radioactive Blue when spill level is for Lord/Lady and above.


## Hunting Strategy

Hydro type traps such as Oasis Water Node Trap or Phantasmic Oasis Trap are effective against the Sludge Soaker Mouse.
