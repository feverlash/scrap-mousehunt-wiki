# 🛡️ Rainbow Diamond Refractor Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 3,500 Power | 40 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Viceroy  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 35,000,000 gold225 Mythical Rainbow Diamond

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> This awe-inspiring, breathtakingly beautiful base represents the pinnacle of energy refraction technology in Gnawnia. After rigorously studying all the previous refractor base iterations, the Trapsmith at the Conclusion Cliffs has finally perfected this extraordinary technology by incorporating the incomparably powerful Mythical Rainbow Diamonds into its design. These miraculous minerals are able to bend, split, and concentrate trap energy in profoundly powerful ways and when combined in large quantities, they become more than the sum of their parts. All of the transformative power that has fueled the Mythweaver's nefarious efforts throughout the Folklore Forest has finally been captured, harnessed, and given a noble purpose with this monumental piece of mouse-catching equipment.
> In addition to its extraordinary mouse-catching power, this miracle of modern science has the following special effects:
> Floating Islands: Chance to add +1 to area-specific loot drops
> Bountiful Beanstalk: Doubles Fabled Fertilizer loot drops
> School of Sorcery: Adds +1 Spell Force
> Draconic Depths: Doubles Dragonhide Sliver loot drops (Replicart™ compatible!)
> Afterword Acres: Provides a chance to prevent the Blight from regrowing (stacks with the Rainbow Quill)
> Epilogue Falls: Provides a chance to prevent getting pushed back by the current after failing to catch (stacks with the Rainbow Quill)
> Conclusion Cliffs: Provides a chance to not lose a Postscript hunt after failing to catch (stacks with the Rainbow Quill)
> Everywhere: Provides a chance to roll up armed Ultimate Ancient Charms into Rainbow Charms
> Everywhere: Provides a chance for any tier of armed Rainbow Charm to roll up into their next, more powerful version

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Rainbow Diamond Refractor Base can be purchased from the Trapsmith in Conclusion Cliffs for:

- 35,000,000 gold
- 225 Mythical Rainbow Diamond

It requires the Hunter to own the Dragon's Breath Opal Refractor Base and complete the Conclude the Mythweaver's Story adventure to purchase.

It cannot be refunded.
