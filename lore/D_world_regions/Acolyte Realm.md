# 📍 Acolyte Realm

> **Wilayah / Region**: Bristle Woods  
> **Pangkat Minimal**: Recruit  
> **Fasilitas Lokal**: Cheese ShoppeTrapsmithCharm ShoppeGeneral Store

## 🌍 Gambaran & Atmosfer Lingkungan

Acolyte Realm is a hunting location in Bristle Woods region.
It requires the rank of Legendary and the Keeper's Candle to access.
It cannot be manually traveled to. Instead, the Hunter is automatically transferred here upon catching a Realm Ripper in Forbidden Grove.

The main goal in this location is to catch an Acolyte. To do so, the Hunter needs to use Runic with a Forgotten setup.
