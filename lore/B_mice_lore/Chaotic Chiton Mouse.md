## Cheese Preference

Chaotic Chiton Mouse is only attracted to Symbolic Sirene.


## Hunting Strategy

The Chaotic Chiton Mouse can only be attracted while on the rapids before the waterfall in Epilogue Falls.

Hydro power type is effective against Chaotic Chiton Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
