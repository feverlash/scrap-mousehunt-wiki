# Octomermaid Mouse

The Octomermaid Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Sunken City. It is known to drop Mouse Scales and Sand Dollars as loot.

## Cheese Preference

The Octomermaid Mouse has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Octomermaid Mouse is susceptible only to Hydro weapons. It can be found in the Mermouse Den and Lost Ruins zones while on a Dive in the Sunken City.


## Event Participation


### Spring Egg Hunt

- The Octomermaid Mouse drops the Deep, Deep, Deep Egg and the Mouse Scale Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

- The Octomermaid Mouse was released 2 July 2014 as part of the Sunken City content.
- The Octomermaid Mouse's description contains references to the Ursula character from The Little Mermaid
