# Mystic King Mouse

The Mystic King Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Rodentia region. It is known to drop Mystic Curd, Mystic Crystal, Torn Mystic Page and Hunting Research.

## Preferred Cheese

Mystic King mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The weaknesses of the Mystic King mouse differ slightly from most others in the Wizard's Pieces group. Tactical weapons are very effective against it, while all others are ineffective. It is only available to attract after a Mystic Queen mouse has been caught.

Upon capturing a Mystic King mouse, the hunter is automatically transported back to the Seasonal Garden unless they are using either Checkmate Cheese or a Stalemate Charm. By using either Checkmate Cheese or a Stalemate Charm, a hunter is able to remain in Zugzwang's Tower and attempt to capture additional mice, including the Chess Master.

Upon capturing a Mystic King mouse with either Checkmate Cheese or a Stalemate Charm armed, the mouse population changes. If Checkmate Cheese is armed, the available mice will be the Chess Master, Mystic Rook and whichever Technic mice have been unlocked. If Standard Cheese is armed, the available mice will be the Mystic Pawn and whichever Technic mice have been unlocked.


## Event Participation


### Spring Egg Hunt

- The Mystic King Mouse drops the Mystic King Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

- Catching the Mystic King Mouse became one requirement for traveling to the Fiery Warpath when it was released on 22 March 2011. Hunters who had already caught this mouse did not have to catch another one.
- Mystic King mice began dropping the Antique Scalloped Pink Egg as loot, on 19 April 2011, for the Spring Egg Hunt 2011 event.
- 29 July 2012: Catching the Mystic King Mouse is no longer a requirement for gaining the Sandtail Call to Arms
