## Cheese Preference

The Devious Gentleman Mouse is primarily attracted to Cloud Cheesecake, however it also can be attracted to Standard Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Only Law traps are effective against the Devious Gentleman Mouse, all other power types are ineffective. The Devious Gentleman Mouse can only be encountered on a Law Island.


## Loot


### Floating Islands's Island Based Loot

When found on a Low Altitude Island or a High Altitude Island, depending on the buffs present, the Devious Gentleman Mouse can drop the following loot:
