## Cheese Preference

Uncoordinated Cauldron Carrier Mouse is only attracted to Standard Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Uncoordinated Cauldron Carrier Mouse can only be attracted while enrolled in Shadow Sciences or during the Shadow phase of the Final Exam.

Shadow power type is very effective against Uncoordinated Cauldron Carrier Mouse.
Arcane power type is effective against Uncoordinated Cauldron Carrier Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
