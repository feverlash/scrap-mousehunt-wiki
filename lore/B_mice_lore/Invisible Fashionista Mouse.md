## Cheese Preference

Invisible Fashionista Mouse is only attracted to Apprentice Ambert.


## Hunting Strategy

The Invisible Fashionista Mouse can only be attracted while enrolled in Arcane Arts or during the Arcane phase of the Final Exam.

Arcane power type is very effective against Invisible Fashionista Mouse.
Shadow power type is effective against Invisible Fashionista Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
