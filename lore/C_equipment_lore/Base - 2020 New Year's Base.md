# 🛡️ 2020 New Year's Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 220 Power | 10 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Novice  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 200,000 Gold30 Firework Sugar Cookies

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Happy New Year! This party-powered base celebrates 2020 all year long!
> During all of 2020, while this base is equipped, mice have a chance to drop 2020 Charms when caught. The charm-generating powers of this base will cease to work on January 1, 2021.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The 2020 New Year's Base could be obtained during the Great Winter Hunt 2019 event.
