# 🛡️ Attuned Enerchi Induction Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 500 Power | 10 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Grand Duke/Grand Duchess  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 351,000 Gold1 Rift Circuitry1 Rift Blossom Branch

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> A base in tune with nature and one with its surroundings. An inanimate object coming closer to oneness and enlightenment. Having attuned with nature, this base is more open to the natural Enerchi flowing through the Rift, allowing it to generate more Enerchi than before! As an added bonus, its increased strength has the chance to coax extra Battery Fragments to drop out of captured mice pockets.
> This base will generate 3 Enerchi whenever a mouse drops some as loot or when some is generated from an Enerchi Charm. It will also generate 1 Battery Fragment whenever a mouse drops some as loot.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Attuned Enerchi Induction Base can be purchased from the Trapsmith in Furoma Rift for:

- 351,000 Gold
- 1 Rift Circuitry
- 1 Rift Blossom Branch

It requires the Hunter to own the Enerchi Induction Base to purchase.

It cannot be refunded.
