## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Sylvan Mouse is attracted only to the following cheeses:

- Whisker Woods

> Calm Clearing - Standard Cheese
Great Gnarled Tree - Standard Cheese

- Tribal Isles

> Cape Clawed - Standard Cheese and Crunchy Cheese
Derr Dunes - Standard Cheese, except Gouda Cheese and SUPER|brie+


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Sylvan Mouse.

## Loot

The Sylvan Mouse is not known to drop any loot.


## History and Trivia

- 31 March 2009: The Sylvan Mouse was released with the introduction of the Calm Clearing.
- 17 April 2011: The Sylvan Mouse began dropping the Antique Stripy Red Egg as loot in the Great Gnarled Tree as part of the Spring Egg Hunt 2011 event.
- 25 June 2013: The Sylvan Mouse gained a vulnerability to Law traps, with the release of Varmint Valley.
- The Sylvan Plushie collectible is based on the Sylvan Mouse.
- 17 January 2023: The Sylvan Mouse was removed from the attraction pool in the Jungle of Dread.
