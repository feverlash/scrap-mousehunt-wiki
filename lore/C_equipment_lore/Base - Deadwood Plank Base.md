# 🛡️ Deadwood Plank Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 350 Power | 10 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Initiate  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 0

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> A base made of poisonous Deadwood, a type of lumber gathered from the aged coffins and housings of truly horrific and evil individuals. The residual spiritual energy caught within the wood make it a powerful material to unleash on the unsuspecting.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Deadwood Plank Base was obtained by defeating 2 Ghostships during the 2018 Halloween event.
