# Zombot Unipire Mouse

The Zombot Unipire Mouse is a breed of Event mouse that can be found in the Mousoleum and Catacombs when hunting with Undead Emmental cheese. It is known to drop a variety of loot. It can also be found during the annual Halloween event.

## Hunting Strategy

The Zombot Unipire Mouse is a typical member of the Event Mice group. All power types are of normal effectiveness against it with exception of Parental, which is ineffective.

The Zombot Unipire Mouse is known to drop Brain Bits in any location when hunting with the limited edition Brain Extractor or Maniacal Brain Extractor weapons, or Skello-ton base. Hunting with Brain or Super Brain Charms will increase the chance of attracting a Zombot Unipire Mouse.


## History and Trivia

- The name of the Zombot Unipire Mouse is a portmanteau word made out of Zombie, Robot, Unicorn and Vampire.
- 20 October 2010: The Zombot Unipire Mouse was introduced as part of the Halloween Trick AND Treat event.
- 25 June 2013: The Zombot Unipire Mouse gained a vulnerability to Law power type traps with the release of Varmint Valley.
- 25 March 2014: The Zombot Unipire gained a vulnerability to Rift power type traps with the release of the Rift Plane.
- 9 September 2014: The Zombot Unipire the Third was released as the Rift counterpart of the Zombot Unipire Mouse.
