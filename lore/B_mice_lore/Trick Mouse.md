## History and Trivia

- 20 October 2010: The Trick Mouse was introduced with the Halloween Trick AND Treat event.
- When the Trick Mouse was released in 2010, it was only weak against Arcane, Forgotten, Physical and Shadow type traps.
- When the Trick Mouse was released in 2011, Draconic, Hydro and Tactical type weapons became effective against it.
- 13 October 2021: Gold was changed for the Master the Magic of the Boiling Cauldrons event.
