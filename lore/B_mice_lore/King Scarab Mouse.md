# King Scarab Mouse

The King Scarab Mouse is a breed of mouse that can be found in the Sand Crypts. They are known to drop Ancient Relics, Scarab Chests and Living Garden Theme Scrap 3 as loot.

## Hunting Strategy

King Scarab Mouse can only be attracted with Graveblossom Camembert and Shattering Charm armed.

King Scarab Mouse is a member of the Sand Dunes Mice group. As such all power types except Shadow are ineffective against it.

Salting is recommended before trying to catch King Scarab Mouse.


## History and Trivia

- 14 January 2013: The King Scarab Mouse was released as part of the Living Garden expansion.
