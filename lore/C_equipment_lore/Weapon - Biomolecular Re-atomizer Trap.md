# ⚔️ Biomolecular Re-atomizer Trap

> **Classification**: Rift Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 1,500 Power | 0 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Count/Countess  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 5,000,000 Gold

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> A highly-advanced particle beam powered by the photosynthesis from a blanket of nutrient rich, artificial bio-moss. This beam de-atomizes mice and re-atomizes them into additional Rift Circuitry! It requires an imprint scan of Rift Circuitry in order to complete the process so the mouse will have to be in possession of at least one to atomize a new one.
> This trap will create an additional Rift Circuitry when a mouse drops one as loot.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Biomolecular Re-atomizer Trap is a Limited Edition weapon and could be purchased from the trapsmith in Ronza's Traveling Shoppe 2016 for 5,000,000 Gold.

It cannot be refunded.
