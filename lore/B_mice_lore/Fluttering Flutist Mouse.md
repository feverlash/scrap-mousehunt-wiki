## Preferred Cheese

The Fluttering Flutist Mouse has been found to be especially attracted to Windy Cheese cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.

## Hunting Strategy

Only Shadow, Arcane and Draconic type traps are effective against the Fluttering Flutist Mouse, all other power types are ineffective.

The Fluttering Flutist Mouse is only available between 35%-79% wind intensities in Moussu Picchu.


## History and Trivia
