# Spiky Devil Mouse

The Spiky Devil Mouse is a breed of mouse that can be found in the Sand Dunes, and is known to drop Ber Essences and Duskshade Petals as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Spiky Devil mice have been found to be only attracted to Dewthief Camembert.


## Hunting Strategy

The Spiky Devil mouse is a member of the Sand Dunes mouse group. As such all power types except Shadow weapons are ineffective against it.


## History and Trivia

- Spiky Devil mice were released on 14 November 2012, as part of the Living Garden update.
