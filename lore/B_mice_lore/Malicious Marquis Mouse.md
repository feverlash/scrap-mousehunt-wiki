## Cheese Preference

Malicious Marquis Mouse is only attracted to Lavish Beanster Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Malicious Marquis Mouse can only be attracted after planting a Tall Vine to the Great Hall Floor.

Physical power type is effective against Malicious Marquis Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.


## Loot

Malicious Marquis Mouse can drop:

- Ancient Relic
- Golden Goose Egg
