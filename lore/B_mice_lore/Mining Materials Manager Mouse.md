# Mining Materials Manager Mouse

The Mining Materials Manager is a breed of mouse found in Fort Rox. They are known to drop Fool's Gold, Meteorite Pieces and Tower Mana as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

While Mining Materials Manager mice can be attracted by standard cheese, they have a distinct preference for SUPER|brie+.


## Hunting Strategy

Law type traps are very effective against the Mining Materials Manager Mouse. Arcane and Shadow power types are less effective.
The Mining Materials Manager Mouse can only be encountered during the daytime phase in Fort Rox.
