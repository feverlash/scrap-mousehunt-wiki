## Cheese Preference

Architeuthulhu of the Abyss is only attracted to Stormy Clamembert Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Hydro power type is very effective against Architeuthulhu of the Abyss.
All other types are ineffective.


## Loot

Architeuthulhu of the Abyss can drop:

- Ancient Relic
- Condensed Creativity
- Flawless Orb

Additionally, it can drop the following loot depending on Chum status:

- Pond Penny (when Brainstorm Chum off)
- Inspiration Ink (when Brainstorm Chum on)


## History and Trivia
