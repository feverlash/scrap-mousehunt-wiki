# Derr Chieftain Mouse

The Derr Chieftain Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Derr Dunes.it is known to drop Ancient Mangled Blueprint and Red Pepper Seed as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Derr Chieftain Mouse has been found to be especially attracted to Crunchy cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.


## History and Trivia

- 21 July 2009: The Derr Chieftain Mouse was released with the introduction of the Tribal Isles region.
- 14 April 2011: The Derr Chieftain Mouse began dropping a bonus Derr Egg as loot during the Spring Egg Hunt 2011 event.
