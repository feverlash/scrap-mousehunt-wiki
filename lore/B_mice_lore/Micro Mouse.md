# Micro Mouse

The Micro Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Gnawnia Rift location. They are known to drop Riftiago Potions and Ionized Salt as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Micro mice have been found to be especially attracted to Brie String and Magical String Cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.
