## Preferred Cheese

The Monsoon Maker Mouse has been found to be especially attracted to Rainy Cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.

## Hunting Strategy

Only Shadow, Arcane and Draconic type traps are effective against the Monsoon Maker Mouse, all other power types are ineffective.

The Monsoon Maker Mouse is only available between 35%-99% rain intensities in Moussu Picchu.


## History and Trivia

- 6 September 2017: The Monsoon Maker Mouse was introduced as part of the Moussu Picchu release.
