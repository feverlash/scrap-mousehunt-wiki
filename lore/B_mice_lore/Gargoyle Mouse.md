## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Gargoyle Mouse is attracted only to Ancient cheese.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Gargoyle Mouse.

Hunters can encounter the Gargoyle Mouse only in the Forbidden Grove location.

## History and Trivia

- 4 March 2009: The Gargoyle Mouse is released with the introduction of the Forbidden Grove location.
