## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Corky the Collector Mouse is only attracted to Medium Queso, Hot Queso or Flamin' Queso.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Corky the Collector Mouse.


## Hunting Strategy

Only Draconic traps are effective against the Corky the Collector Mouse, all other power types are ineffective. The Corky the Collector Mouse can only be encountered in Queso Geyser while gathering Cork Bark.

Dragonbane Charm, Super Dragonbane Charm, Extreme Dragonbane Charm, and Ultimate Dragonbane Charm provide a power bonus against the Corky the Collector Mouse.


## History and Trivia

- 30 April 2019: The Corky the Collector Mouse was introduced, with the release of the Queso Geyser area.
- 30 March 2021: The Corky the Collector Mouse started dropping Corky, The Collector Egg during the Spring Egg Hunt 2021.
