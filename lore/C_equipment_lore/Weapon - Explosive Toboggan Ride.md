# ⚔️ Explosive Toboggan Ride

> **Classification**: Hydro Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 2,700 Power | 16 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Master  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 10,000 goldFestive FuseHeart of IceHoliday ExplosivesIciclesPacked SnowSled

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> This toboggan provides a unique ride where mice are sent sky high! The airborne journey is exciting, although the landing is a little rough.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Explosive Toboggan Ride could be purchased at the Festive Comet Trapsmith for 10,000 gold and the following items found during The Great Winter Hunt 2010:

- Festive Fuse
- Heart of Ice
- Holiday Explosives
- Icicles
- Packed Snow
- Sled

It could not be refunded.

The Explosive Toboggan Ride (if unintentionally smashed) can be reassembled from Toboggan Ride Parts.
