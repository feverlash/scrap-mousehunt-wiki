## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Mummy Mouse is attracted to Moon Cheese, Radioactive Blue Cheese, Undead Emmental and Undead String Emmental in the Catacombs and Mousoleum locations.

The Mummy Mouse will not be attracted to Ancient Cheese in the Catacombs location.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Mummy Mouse.

## Locations

Hunters can encounter the Mummy Mouse in the Catacombs and Mousoleum


## Loot

The Mummy Mouse drops Stale Cheese in the Catacombs location.

The Mummy Mouse is not known to drop any loot in the Mousoleum location.


## History and Trivia

- 21 July 2008: The Mummy Mouse was released.
- An image of the Mummy Mouse can be seen on the Mousoleum header image.
- The description for the Mummy Mouse has changed. The original description read:

> Hunters' lore describes these mice as completely invulnerable. These undead mice starve to enslave and take rule over the land they inhabit. A hunter will certainly need to be well prepared in order to face this enemy.
