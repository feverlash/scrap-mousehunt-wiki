# Wereminer Mouse

The Wereminer is a breed of mouse found in Fort Rox. They are known to drop Howlite and Meteorite Pieces as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Wereminer Mouse have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Shadow traps are very effective against the Wereminer Mouse. Arcane traps are of normal effectiveness.
The Wereminer Mouse mice can only be encountered during the Twilight, Midnight, Pitch, and Utter Darkness phases at Nighttime in Fort Rox.
