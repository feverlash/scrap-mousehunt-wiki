# 🛡️ Magnet Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 250 Power | 5 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Lord/Lady  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 48,500 Gold3 Bottled Cold Fusion3 Wire Spool3 Frosty Metal1 Heating Oil

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> The magnetic power of this base exerts a constant pull on iron tools and equipment, which translates into an increased chance of attracting Tunnel Rats. Interestingly, this effect is just subtle enough that the mice never consciously notice it. Besides, even if they did know why they encountered these bases so often, they would never be able to understand how they work.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Magnet Base can be purchased from Trapsmith in Slushy Shoreline and Iceberg for:

- 48,500 Gold
- 3 Bottled Cold Fusion
- 3 Wire Spool
- 3 Frosty Metal
- 1 Heating Oil

It cannot be refunded.
