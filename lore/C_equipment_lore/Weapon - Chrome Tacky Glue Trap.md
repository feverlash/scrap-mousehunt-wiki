# ⚔️ Chrome Tacky Glue Trap

> **Classification**: Physical Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 70 Power | 20 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Novice  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 158,000

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Simple and minimalistic, but far from subtle. This modified Tacky Glue Trap features a key ingredient - pure, liquified chrome - to give it a flashy look and a bonus to its luck.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Chrome Tacky Glue Trap is a Limited Edition weapon and can no longer be acquired by Hunters. It was available for purchase for 158,000 gold from Ronza's Traveling Shoppe during her January-February 2012 visit. It refunded for 28,000 gold at the same location.
