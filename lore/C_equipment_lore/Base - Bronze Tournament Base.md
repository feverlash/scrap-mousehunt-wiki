# 🛡️ Bronze Tournament Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 300 Power | 5 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Journeyman/Journeywoman  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 25 Tournament Token

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> The Bronze Tournament Base is a reward for hunters who have shown they are capable of competing in hunting tournaments.
> When armed alongside a Champion Charm, in addition to the charm's +3 luck, this base glows with an extra +2 luck, for a total of 10 luck!

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Bronze Tournament Base can be purchased from the Trapsmith in Tournament Hall for 25 Tournament Token.

It cannot be refunded.
