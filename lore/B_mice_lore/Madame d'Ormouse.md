## Cheese Preference

Madame d'Ormouse is only attracted to Standard Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Madame d'Ormouse is only available when the stage is Not writing.

Forgotten power type is effective against Madame d'Ormouse.
All other types are ineffective.


## Loot

Madame d'Ormouse can drop:

- Draft Derby Curd

With Leather Bound Cover, the following loot is added to the pool:

- Cleverness Clam
- Crop Coin
- Ingenuity Grub
- Inspiration Ink
- Mythical Mulch
- Pond Penny
- Papyrus Seed
- Parable Papyrus
