# Speedy Mouse

The Speedy Mouse is a breed of mouse found in various locations in Gnawnia, and in King's Arms.

## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Speedy Mouse is attracted only to Brie Cheese, Gouda Cheese, and SUPER|brie+.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Speedy Mouse.


## Power Type Effectiveness


| Very Effective | Effective | Less Effective |
| --- | --- | --- |
| None | All except Parental | None |



## Loot

The Speedy Mouse drops Packets of Flour in the Windmill location.

The Speedy Mouse is not know to drop any loot in the Town of Gnawnia, King's Arms, or Tournament Hall locations.


## History and Trivia

- 30 June 2010: The Speedy Mouse was released with the introduction of the Windmill location.
- 25 June 2013: The Speedy Mouse gained a vulnerability to Law power type traps, with the release of Varmint Valley.
- 19 September 2013: The Speedy Mouse gained a taste for Brie and Gouda cheeses during the Gnawnia population shift, although SUPER|brie+ still remains the most effective.
- 25 March 2014: The Speedy Mouse gained a vulnerability to Rift power type traps, with the introduction of the Rift Plane region.
- 2 September 2014: The Speedy Mouse began dropping Packets of Flour as loot in the Windmill.
- 5 October 2021: The Gold for Speedy Mouse was increased as part of the Town of Gnawnia and Windmill gameplay updates.
