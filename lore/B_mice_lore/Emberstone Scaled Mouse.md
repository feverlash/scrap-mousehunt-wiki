## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Emberstone Scaled Mouse is only attracted to Wildfire Queso.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Emberstone Scaled Mouse.


## Hunting Strategy

Catching an Emberstone Scaled Mouse while building pressure in Queso Geyser increases the amount of pressure by 1,500 kPa.

Only Draconic traps are effective against the Emberstone Scaled Mouse, all other power types are ineffective. The Emberstone Scaled Mouse can only be encountered while building pressure in Queso Geyser, after a cork has been constructed.

Dragonbane Charm, Super Dragonbane Charm, Extreme Dragonbane Charm, and Ultimate Dragonbane Charm provide a power bonus against the Emberstone Scaled Mouse.
