# Silvertail Mouse

The Silvertail Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Gnawnia and Valour regions. It is known to drop Simple Orb in each location and Packet of Flour at the Windmill..

## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Silvertail Mouse has been found to be especially attracted to SUPER|brie+ cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Silvertail Mouse.

Hunters may arm the Prospector's Charm in the Mountain location to improve the attraction rate of the Silvertail Mouse, Diamond Mouse, and Gold Mouse.

## History and Trivia

- 30 June 2010: The Silvertail Mouse was released with the introduction of the Windmill.
- 12 January 2011: The Silvertail Mouse was found at Ronza's Traveling Shoppe where it dropped Attraction Charm and Rockforth cheese as loot.
- 25 January 2011: The Silvertail Mouse began to drop Simple Orb as loot.
- 25 June 2013: The Silvertail Mouse gained a vulnerability to Law power type traps with the release of Varmint Valley.
- 25 March 2014: The Silvertail Mouse gained a vulnerability to Rift power type traps with the release of the Rift Plane.
- 2 September 2014: The Silvertail Mouse began dropping Packet of Flour at the Windmill.
- 17 October 2018: The Silvertail Mouse changed mouse groups from Indigenous Mice to Mountain Mice.
- 5 October 2021: The Gold for Silvertail Mouse was increased as part of the Town of Gnawnia and Windmill gameplay updates.
