# Raw Diamond Mouse

"Raw Diamond" redirects here. For other uses, see Raw Diamond (item).

The Raw Diamond Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Gnawnia Rift and Furoma Rift. It is not known to drop any loot.

## Preferred Cheese

In Gnawnia Rift, the Raw Diamond Mouse has been found to be especially attracted to Riftiago cheese, although Swiss String, Brie String, and Magical String cheese will also suffice.

In Furoma Rift, the Raw Diamond Mouse is only attracted to String Cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.
