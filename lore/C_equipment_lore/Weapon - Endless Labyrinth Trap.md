# ⚔️ Endless Labyrinth Trap

> **Classification**: Forgotten Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 10,000 Power | 9 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Duke/Duchess  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 6,980,000 Gold1 Powercore Hammer1 Infused Plate1 Sacred Script150 Scholar Scroll150 Tech Power Core150 Plate of Fealty

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Mice who try to steal cheese from this powerful trap are transported into a smaller, more twisted version of the Labyrinth. Only mice powerful or lucky enough manage to escape from this trap before it locks them within. The rest will live the remainder of their natural lives forgotten within this endless maze.
> Due to the nature of this trap, the King has expressedly forbidden hunters from nesting these traps into other Endless Labyrinth traps, as infinitely shrinking things to beyond the smallest known particle might have cataclysmic consequences on the natural order of the universe...

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Endless Labyrinth Trap can be purchased from Trapsmith in Labyrinth and Zokor for:

- 6,980,000 Gold
- 1 Powercore Hammer
- 1 Infused Plate
- 1 Sacred Script
- 150 Scholar Scroll
- 150 Tech Power Core
- 150 Plate of Fealty

It cannot be refunded.

The Endless Labyrinth Trap (if unintentionally smashed) can be reassembled from Endless Labyrinth Trap Parts.
