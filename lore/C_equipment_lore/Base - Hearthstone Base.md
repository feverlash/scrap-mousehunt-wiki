# 🛡️ Hearthstone Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 200 Power | 2 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Lord/Lady  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 147,500 Gold10 Bottled Cold Fusion7 Wire Spool4 Frosty Metal4 Heating Oil

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Zealot mice find the pleasant warmth of this base to be incredibly repulsive, and avoid it like the plague. Actually, the plague doesn't bother them that much at all. The warm comfort of this base repulses them so greatly that there are simply no words to describe their disgust.
> Aside from strongly averting Zealot mice, it makes an excellent hand warmer in the frozen depths!

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Hearthstone Base can be purchased from Trapsmith in Slushy Shoreline and Iceberg for:

- 147,500 Gold
- 10 Bottled Cold Fusion
- 7 Wire Spool
- 4 Frosty Metal
- 4 Heating Oil

It cannot be refunded.
