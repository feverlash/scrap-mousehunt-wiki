## Cheese Preference

Barbaric Barnacle Mouse is only attracted to SUPER|brie+.


## Hunting Strategy

The Barbaric Barnacle Mouse can only be attracted while on the shore or the rapids before the waterfall in Epilogue Falls.

Hydro power type is effective against Barbaric Barnacle Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
