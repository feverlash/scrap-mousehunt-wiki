## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Wave Racer Mouse is only attracted to Runny Cheese


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Wave Racer Mouse.


## Power Type Weaknesses

The Wave Racer Mouse is weak to all Power Types except Parental.
