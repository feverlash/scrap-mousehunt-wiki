# 🛡️ Spellbook Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 500 Power | 7 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Lord/Lady  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 1,296,000 Gold1 Master Bindings3 Torn Technic Page3 Torn Mystic Page

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> The bindings on this spell book glow with runic magic. When you touch it, the pages seem to be moving, ever so slightly. Magic this strong can only be bound by a powerful wizard, and even then, it strives for release. Luckily for you, the spells trapped within this book will try to lure in mice in to break the binding... put a trap on it, and you're in business.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Spellbook Base can be purchased from the Trapsmith in Seasonal Garden, Zugzwang's Tower, and Crystal Library for:

- 1,296,000 Gold
- 1 Master Bindings
- 3 Torn Technic Page
- 3 Torn Mystic Page

It cannot be refunded.
