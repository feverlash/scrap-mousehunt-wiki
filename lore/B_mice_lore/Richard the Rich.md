# Richard the Rich

The Richard the Rich is a breed of mouse found on the Floating Islands, on any island with 2 Loot Caches present and active.

## Cheese Preference

The Richard the Rich is attracted to Cloud Cheesecake.


## Loot


### Floating Islands's Island Based Loot

When found on a Low Altitude Island or a High Altitude Island, depending on the buffs present, the Richard the Rich can drop the following loot:


| Item | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| Sky Glass | Always drops Boosted by Sky Glass Formations and Loot Caches |
| Sky Ore | Always drops Boosted by Sky Ore Deposit and Loot Caches |
| Cloud Curd | Drops when a Cloud Curd Bonus is present Can be boosted by Loot Caches |
| Corsair's Curd | Drops when a Pirate Den is present Can be boosted by Loot Caches |


Richard the Rich also drops Storm Cells outside of the Sky Palace, if Rocket Boosters have been attached on the Hunter's Dirigible. It also has a chance to drop the following loot:

- High Altitude Treasure Trove
- Low Altitude Treasure Trove
- Richard's Sky Yacht Airship Sail
- Richard's Sky Yacht Airship Balloon
- Richard's Sky Yacht Airship Hull


## Hunting Strategy

All powertypes except Rift and Parental are effective against the Richard the Rich. The Richard the Rich can only be encountered on an island with 2 Loot Caches present and active.


## History and Trivia

- 08 September 2020: The Richard the Rich was introduced with the release of the Floating Islands.
