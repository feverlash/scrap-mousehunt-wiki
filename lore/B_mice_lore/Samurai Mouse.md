## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Samurai Mouse is attracted mainly to Standard Cheese in the Training Grounds and Dojo locations.

In the Dojo location, the Samurai Mouse will also be attracted when hunting with Maki Cheese.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Samurai Mouse.

## Locations

Hunters can encounter the Samurai Mouse in the Training Grounds and Dojo locations.


## Loot

The Samurai Mouse drops the following loot:

- Training Grounds

> Combat Research (1,250 points)
Satchel of Gold (500 gold)

- Dojo

> Combat Research (1,250 points)
Satchel of Gold (500 gold)

The Samurai Mouse will drop Luscious Lumber in the Training Grounds location when Hunters are working the Zurreal Trap Research Assignment.
6 Luscious Lumber are required to construct the Zurreal's Folly weapon.


## History and Trivia

- 10 October 2008: The Samurai Mouse was released in the Training Grounds.
- 27 November 2008: The Samurai Mouse's habitat expanded to the Dojo.
- 11 December 2008: The Samurai Mouse began to drop Satchel of Gold (500 gold) as loot.
- 23 August 2011: The Samurai Mouse began to drop Luscious Lumber as loot in the Training Grounds for Hunters working a Zurreal Trap Research Assignment..
