# 🛡️ Chrome Tribal Vegetation Kaboom Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 1250 Power | 25 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Archduke  
> **Acquisition Cost**: #Obtained Via

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Long ago in the exotic Tribal Isles there existed two different magical bases whose designs were both lost to the Trapsmiths of Gnawnia... until now! It turns out that the villainous Captain Crook discovered and then stole these lost blueprints during her raid of the Tribal Isles so that she could make her own pirated versions. However, she got the blueprints for each all mixed up and couldn't tell them apart so she combined them into something new that has the effects of both!
> Now that the design secrets have been recovered, the Trapsmith at the Cerulean Skyport is able to forge this new, stronger, and shinier version using the miraculous combination of Cerulean Chrome that has been charged with the energy from Adorned Empyrean Jewels!
> This base has the special effects of both the Tribal Kaboom Base and the Vegetation Base when hunting in the Tribal Isles:
> • Increases quantity range for drops of Seashells, Savoury Vegetables, Delicious Stones, and all Seeds
> • Spawns a pepper that matches whatever seed a mouse drops

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Chrome Tribal Vegetation Kaboom Base can be purchased from the Trapsmith in Cerulean Skyport for:

- 5,000,000 Gold
- 20 Chrome Tribal Vegetation Kaboom Blueprint Scrap
- 200 Cerulean Chrome Ingot
- 3 Adorned Empyrean Jewel

It requires the Hunter to own the Chrome Base and complete the Liberate the Cerulean Skyport from Captain Crook adventure to purchase.

It cannot be refunded.
