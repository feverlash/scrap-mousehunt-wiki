## Cheese Preference

Teleporting Truant Mouse is only attracted to Master Mimolette.


## Hunting Strategy

The Teleporting Truant Mouse can only be attracted while enrolled in Shadow Sciences or during the Shadow phase of the Final Exam.

Shadow power type is very effective against Teleporting Truant Mouse.
Arcane power type is effective against Teleporting Truant Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
