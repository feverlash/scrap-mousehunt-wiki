# Crate Camo Mouse

The Crate Camo Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Gnawnian Express Station. It can only be attracted and caught during the Supply Depot phase. It is known to drop Iron Pellets and Royal Supply Crates as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Crate Camo Mouse has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Law type traps such as S.L.A.C. are very effective against the Crate Camo Mouse.


## Event Participation


### Spring Egg Hunt

- The Crate Camo Mouse drops the Crate Egg and Gnawnian Express Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.
