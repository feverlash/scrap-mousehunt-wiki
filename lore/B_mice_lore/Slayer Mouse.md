# Slayer Mouse

> "Slayer" redirects here. For other uses, see Slayer (disambiguation).

The Slayer Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Tribal Isles region. They are known to drop Yellow Pepper Seed in Nerg Plains.

## Preferred Cheese

Slayer mice have been found to be especially attracted to Gumbo cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.
