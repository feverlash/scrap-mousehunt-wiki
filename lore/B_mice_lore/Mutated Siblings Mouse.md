# Mutated Siblings Mouse

The Mutated Siblings Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Toxic Spill with spill level for hunters of rank Hero to Duke/Duchess.

## Preferred Cheese

Mutated Siblings mice are attracted to Magical Rancid Radioactive Blue at every spill level and to Rancid Radioactive Blue when spill level is for Hero to Duke/Duchess.


## Hunting Strategy

Hydro type traps such as Oasis Water Node Trap or Phantasmic Oasis Trap are effective against the Mutated Siblings Mouse.


## Event Participation

- The Mutated Siblings Mouse drops the Toxic Spill Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

- Released on 21 January 2014 as part of the Toxic Spill content.
