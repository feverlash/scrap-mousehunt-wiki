## Cheese Preference

Illustrious Illusionist Mouse is only attracted to Master Mimolette.


## Hunting Strategy

The Illustrious Illusionist Mouse can only be attracted while enrolled in Arcane Arts or during the Arcane phase of the Final Exam.

Arcane power type is very effective against Illustrious Illusionist Mouse.
Shadow power type is effective against Illustrious Illusionist Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
