# 🛡️ Extra Sweet Cupcake Birthday Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 300 Power | 8 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Journeyman  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 0

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> A delicious batch of birthday cupcakes baked and put under a tough sheet of thermal glass. The cupcakes are kept fresh and warm inside of this impregnable container, emitting a sugary glow of irresistible deliciousness.
> On top of that, this base contains a special surprise secretly added by the Head Trapsmith Chef: Cupcakes that happen to pop off the base during any scuffles turn into Cupcake Charms! Also, with the included lifetime warranty, all damaged cupcakes are covered under an immediate replacement plan, so your base is never missing one of its colourful cupcakes!
> A flavour first savoured during MouseHunt's 8th birthday!
> This base has a rare chance to drop an Extra Sweet Cupcake Charm when a mouse is caught!

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
This base can be obtained during the annual MouseHunt Birthday event. The requirements for obtaining this base may change from year to year.
