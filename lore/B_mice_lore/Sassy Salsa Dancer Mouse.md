## Cheese Preference

Sassy Salsa Dancer Mouse is only attracted to Standard Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Sassy Salsa Dancer Mouse can only be attracted after planting a Medium Vine to the Ballroom Floor.

Physical power type is effective against Sassy Salsa Dancer Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.


## Loot

Sassy Salsa Dancer Mouse can drop:

- Golden Harp String
- Royal Ruby Bean
