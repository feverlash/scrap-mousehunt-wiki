## Cheese Preference

Enchanted Chess Club Champion Mouse is only attracted to Apprentice Ambert.


## Hunting Strategy

The Enchanted Chess Club Champion Mouse can only be attracted while enrolled in Arcane Arts or during the Arcane phase of the Final Exam.

Arcane power type is very effective against Enchanted Chess Club Champion Mouse.
Shadow power type is effective against Enchanted Chess Club Champion Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
