# King Grub Mouse

The King Grub Mouse is a breed of mouse that can be found in the Sand Crypts, and is known to drop Dol Essences, Est Essences, Fel Essences and Lunaria Petals as loot.

## Hunting Strategy

Normally, King Grub Mouse can only be attracted with Graveblossom Camembert and Grub Scent Charm armed.
It can also be attracted without Grub Scent Charm if Smelly Sodium Base is armed and salt level is at 50.

King Grub Mouse is a member of the Sand Dunes Mice group. As such all power types except Shadow are ineffective against it.

Salting is recommended before trying to catch King Grub Mouse.
