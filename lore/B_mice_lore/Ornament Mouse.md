# Ornament Mouse

> "Ornament" redirects here. For other uses, see Glass Mouse Ornament.

The Ornament Mouse is an Event Mouse found during the annual Great Winter Hunt event.

## History and Trivia

- 3 December 2009: The Ornament Mouse is introduced as part of the Great Winter Hunt '09 event.
- 9 December 2009: The Ornament Mouse began dropping Gingerbread Base Blueprints as loot.
- 16 December 2009: The Ornament Mouse began dropping the Five Ornaments as loot.
- 13 December 2016: The points/gold for Ornament Mouse were changed.
