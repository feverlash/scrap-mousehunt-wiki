# ⚔️ Zugzwang's Ultimate Move

> **Classification**: Tactical Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 4,500 Power | 20 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Lord  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 1,400,000

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> An elegant and well-defended chess trap. A specific arrangement of finely crafted chess pieces, setup in a perfect formation that is incredibly difficult to overcome. This was designed after one of the last brilliant moves played out by Zugzwang himself.
> This trap has a chance to trigger its special effect and instantly outwit and capture a mouse as long as your Tower Amplifier has some charge within the Seasonal Garden and Zugzwang's Tower.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
Zugzwang's Ultimate Move is a Limited Edition weapon and could be purchased from the trapsmith in Ronza's Traveling Shoppe 2016. It costs 1,400,000 gold. It cannot be refunded.
