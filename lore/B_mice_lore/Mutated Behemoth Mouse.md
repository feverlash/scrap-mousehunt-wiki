# Mutated Behemoth Mouse

The Mutated Behemoth Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Toxic Spill, for hunters of rank Archduke/Archduchess and above.

## Preferred Cheese

Mutated Behemoth mice are especially attracted to Rancid Radioactive Blue cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.


## Hunting Strategy

Hydro type traps are effective against the Mutated Behemoth Mouse.


## Event Participation
