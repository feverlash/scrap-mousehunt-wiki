# Tidal Fisher Mouse

The Tidal Fisher Mouse is a breed of mouse found in Balack's Cove during low tide. They are not known to drop any loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Tidal Fisher Mouse is only attracted to Vanilla Stilton cheese.


## History and Trivia

- 21 September 2010: Tidal Fisher Mouse was released to replace the Riptide mouse, whose role had changed, during low tide.
- 17 January 2023: Tidal Fisher Mouse began to drop Empowered Anchor Charm and Vanilla Bean as loot.
