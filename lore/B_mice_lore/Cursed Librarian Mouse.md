# Cursed Librarian Mouse

The Cursed Librarian Mouse is a breed of mouse that can be found in the Cursed City, and is known to drop Ber Essences, Cynd Essences, Dol Essences, Est Essences, and Plumepearl Herbs as loot..

## Preferred Cheese

The Cursed Librarian Mouse has been found to be only attracted to Graveblossom Camembert.


## Hunting Strategy

The Cursed Librarian Mouse is a member of the Lost City mouse group. As such all power types except Arcane weapons are ineffective against it.


## History and Trivia

- 14 November 2012: The Cursed Librarian Mouse was released as a part of the Living Garden update.
