The Overcaster Mouse is a breed of mouse found on the Floating Islands, on a Shadow Island or a Shadow Stronghold.

## Cheese Preference

The Overcaster Mouse is attracted to Cloud Cheesecake and Standard Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Only Shadow traps are effective against the Overcaster Mouse, all other power types are ineffective. The Overcaster Mouse can only be encountered on a Shadow Island or a Shadow Sanctuary.


## Loot


### Floating Islands's Island Based Loot

When found on a Low Altitude Island or a High Altitude Island, depending on the buffs present, the Overcaster Mouse can drop the following loot:
