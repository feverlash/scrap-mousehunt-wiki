## History and Trivia

- 8 April 2011: The Eggsplosive Scientist Mouse was a special mouse painted live during Feedback Friday. Viewers were asked for feedback at certain stages of creation.
- 14 April 2011: The Eggsplosive Scientist Mouse was released as part of the Spring Egg Hunt 2011 event.
- The Eggsplosive Scientist Mouse description contains a play on Albert Einstein's name.
