# 🛡️ Sheep Jade Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 325 Power | 10 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Grandmaster  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 550,000

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Horns of strength, beard of majesty, eyes of tenderness: this carved Jade Base represents the gentle, yet strong, spirit of the sheep.
> Only hunters who have helped Carl complete a full cruise in 2015 and saved up sufficient Cruise Bux will be able to have their Jade Base carved into this attractive and fantastic base!

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Sheep Jade Base was available for purchase for 550,000 gold, 1 Jade Base, and 1000 Cruise Bux from Calamity Carl's Cruise Trapsmith during the Year of the Sheep Festival once the Captain's Coat was obtained. It could not be refunded.
