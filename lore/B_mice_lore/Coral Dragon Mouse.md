# Coral Dragon Mouse

The Coral Dragon Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Sunken City. It is known to drop Damaged Coral Fragments and Sand Dollarsas loot.

## Cheese Preference

The Coral Dragon Mouse has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Coral Dragon Mouse is susceptible only to Hydro weapons. It can be found in the Coral Garden and Coral Castle zones while on a Dive in the Sunken City.


## Event Participation


### Spring Egg Hunt

- The Coral Dragon Mouse drops the Coral Egg and the Deep, Deep, Deep Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

The Coral Dragon Mouse was released 2 July 2014 as part of the Sunken City content.
