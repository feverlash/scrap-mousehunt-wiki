## Cheese Preference

Violent Violinist Mouse is only attracted to Lavish Beanster Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Violent Violinist Mouse can only be attracted after planting a Medium Vine to the Ballroom Floor.

Physical power type is effective against Violent Violinist Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.


## Loot

Violent Violinist Mouse can drop:

- Ancient Relic
- Golden Harp String
- Royal Ruby Bean
