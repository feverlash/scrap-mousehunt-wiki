## Cheese Preference

Greedy Stone Grappler Mouse is only attracted to Sky Pirate Swiss or Sky Raider Romano.


## Hunting Strategy

The Greedy Stone Grappler Mouse can only be attracted during a Mining shipment.

Law power type is effective against Greedy Stone Grappler Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
