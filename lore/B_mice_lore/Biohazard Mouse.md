# Biohazard Mouse

The Biohazard Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Toxic Spill, for hunters of rank Count/Countess and above.

## Preferred Cheese

Biohazard mice are attracted to Magical Rancid Radioactive Blue at every spill level and to Rancid Radioactive Blue when spill level is for Count/Countess and above.


## Hunting Strategy

Hydro type traps such as Oasis Water Node Trap or Phantasmic Oasis Trap are effective against the Biohazard Mouse.


## Event Participation

- The Biohazard Mouse drops the Toxic Spill Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

- Released on 21 January 2014 as part of the Toxic Spill content.
