## History and Trivia

- 15 December 2015: The Black Diamond Racer Mouse was introduced as part of the Great Winter Hunt 2015.
- During the Extreme Toboggan Challenge, the Black Diamond Racer Mouse was caught as a Bonus Hunt and did not consume cheese or charms.
- 13 December 2016: The points/gold for Black Diamond Racer Mouse were changed.
