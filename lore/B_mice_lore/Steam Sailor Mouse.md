## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Steam Sailor Mouse is only attracted to Mild Queso, Medium Queso, Hot Queso or Flamin' Queso.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Steam Sailor Mouse.


## Hunting Strategy

Catching a Steam Sailor Mouse while building pressure in Queso Geyser increases the amount of pressure by 5 kPa.

Only Draconic traps are effective against the Steam Sailor Mouse, all other power types are ineffective. The Steam Sailor Mouse can only be encountered while building pressure in Queso Geyser, after a cork has been constructed.

Dragonbane Charm, Super Dragonbane Charm, Extreme Dragonbane Charm, and Ultimate Dragonbane Charm provide a power bonus against the Steam Sailor Mouse.
