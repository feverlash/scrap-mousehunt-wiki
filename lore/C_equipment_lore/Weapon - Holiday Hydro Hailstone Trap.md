# ⚔️ Holiday Hydro Hailstone Trap

> **Classification**: Hydro Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 3,500 Power | 20 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Knight  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 0

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> A hydro-powered holiday tree equipped with patented IceVeins™ and Let-It-Snow® (L.I.S.) technology! The specialized tubing running within its branches provide a steady stream of ice-cold water that freezes on command, allowing an endless supply of icicles and iceballs to form. These icicles are then utilized by the L.I.S. system to hurl the frozen water at the mice at incredible speeds! Ouch!

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Holiday Hydro Hailstone Trap is a Limited Edition weapon not sold by any Trapsmith.

Hunters who traveled 40,000 meters during the Great Winter Hunt 2016 were awarded with this trap. Hunters must claim this trap from the event HUD for it to be added to their inventory.
