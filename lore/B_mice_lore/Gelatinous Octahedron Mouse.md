# Gelatinous Octahedron Mouse

The Gelatinous Octahedron Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Toxic Spill with spill level for hunters of rank Knight to Duke/Duchess.

## Hunting Strategy

Gelatinous Octahedron Mouse can be attracted with Rancid Radioactive Blue when spill level is from Knight to Duke/Duchess.
Magical Rancid Radioactive Blue can attract it in all spill levels.

Only Hydro power type is effective against Gelatinous Octahedron Mouse.
