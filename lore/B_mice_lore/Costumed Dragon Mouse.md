## History and Trivia

- 23 January 2012: The Costumed Dragon Mouse was released as part of the Year of the Dragon Festival event.
- The amount of points (2,888) and gold (888) awarded on catching a Costumed Dragon Mouse reflects the belief of the number eight being considered "lucky" in Chinese culture; a sequence of a "lucky" number is considered even more favorable!
- The description for the Costumed Dragon Mouse originally contained a sentence about charm usage:

> Use Dragonbreath Charms to attract this most ferocious beast; it's the only fireworks display that can stun them long enough to be captured!
