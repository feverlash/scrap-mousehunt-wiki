# Mimic Mouse

The Mimic Mouse can be found in the Labyrinth and Zokor. It is known to drop Plates of Fealty, Scholar Scrolls, and Tech Power Cores in Zokor.

## Preferred Cheese

The Mimic Mouse prefers Glowing Gruyere in both the Labyrinth and Zokor, but can be attracted with Standard Cheeses.


## Hunting Strategy

Forgotten weapons are very effective against the Mimic Mouse. Arcane weapons are of normal effectiveness.

- Labyrinth

> The Mimic Mouse can be found in Plain and Superior Treasury Hallways.

- Zokor

> The Mimic Mouse can be found in the Treasure Room and the Treasure Vault.


## Loot

- Zokor

> Labyrinth Theme Scrap IV, Plates of Fealty, Scholar Scrolls, and Tech Power Cores
