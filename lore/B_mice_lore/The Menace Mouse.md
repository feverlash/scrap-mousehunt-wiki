# The Menace Mouse

The Menace Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Toxic Spill, for hunters of rank Grand Duke/Grand Duchess and above.

## Preferred Cheese

The Menace Mouse can be attracted at every spill level when the Hunter arms Magical Rancid Radioactive Blue.
It can only be attracted at Grand Duke/Grand Duchess spill level and above when the Hunter arms Rancid Radioactive Blue.


## Hunting Strategy

Hydro type traps are effective against The Menace Mouse.


## History and Trivia

- Released on 12 February 2014 as part of the second Toxic Spill content.
- The description of The Menace Mouse contains references to the Hulk comic book character.
