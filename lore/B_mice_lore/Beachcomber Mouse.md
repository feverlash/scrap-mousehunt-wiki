## Cheese Preference

Beachcomber Mouse can be attracted with any Standard Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Hydro power type is effective against Beachcomber Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.


## Loot

Beachcomber Mouse can drop:

- Ingenuity Grub
