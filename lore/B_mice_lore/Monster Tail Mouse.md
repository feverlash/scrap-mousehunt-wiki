# Monster Tail Mouse

The Monster Tail Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Toxic Spill with spill level for hunters of rank Hero to Duke/Duchess.

## Preferred Cheese

Monster Tail mice are attracted to Magical Rancid Radioactive Blue and Rancid Radioactive Blue when spill level is for Hero to Duke/Duchess.


## Hunting Strategy

Hydro type traps such as Oasis Water Node Trap or Phantasmic Oasis Trap are effective against the Monster Tail Mouse.
