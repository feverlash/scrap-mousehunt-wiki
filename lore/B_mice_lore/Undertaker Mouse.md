## Hunting Strategy

Undertaker Mouse is only available when the Hunter has cleared all Accomplices in a Dirt Dealers Wanted Poster.
Law power type is very effective against this mouse. All other types are ineffective.


## History and Trivia

- 25 June 2013: Undertaker Mouse was released as part of the Claw Shot City, but couldn't be attracted yet.
- 3 July 2013: Undertaker Mouse was made available with the Dirt Dealers Wanted Poster.
