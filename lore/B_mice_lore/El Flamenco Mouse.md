## History and Trivia

- 4 March 2014: The El Flamenco Mouse was released as part of MouseHunt's Sixth Birthday event.
- During MouseHunt's Sixth Birthday, the El Flamenco Mouse could only be encountered after clearing the first nine mice of a Conga Line.

> On the first capture of this mouse, an additional message is left in the Hunter's Journal:
I defeated El Flamenco in a dizzying dance battle!
To commorate his first defeat, I earned a special gift from the King: A DLU+ Gift Box!
On subsequent encounters, this message changes to:
El Flamenco got served!
His conga line dissolves in confusion.
Additionally, after every catch, a new Conga Line is started with this message:
A conga line approaches!
Grim their eyes, heavy their feet,
a lockstep whirlwind of defeat.
