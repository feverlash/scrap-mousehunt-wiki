## Cheese Preference

Arcane Master Sorcerer is attracted to Standard Cheese, Apprentice Ambert, and Master Mimolette.


## Hunting Strategy

The Arcane Master Sorcerer can only be attracted at the end of each course level while enrolled in Arcane Arts.

Arcane power type is very effective against Arcane Master Sorcerer.
Shadow power type is effective against Arcane Master Sorcerer.
All other types are ineffective.
