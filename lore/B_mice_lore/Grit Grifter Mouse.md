## Cheese Preference

Grit Grifter Mouse can be attracted with any Standard Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Grit Grifter Mouse is available when 0-2 plant is growing.

Tactical power type is effective against Grit Grifter Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.


## Loot

Grit Grifter Mouse can drop:

- Papyrus Seed
