# Spectre Mouse

The Spectre Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Acolyte Realm and the Forbidden Grove. It is not known to drop any loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Spectre Mouse has been found to be especially attracted to Ancient and Runic cheeses.


## Hunting Strategy

Arcane traps are very effective against the Spectre Mouse. Forgotten and Shadow traps are of normal effectivness.
