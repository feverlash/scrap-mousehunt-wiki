# Defender Mouse

The Defender Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Tribal Isles region. They are known to drop Yellow Pepper Seed in Nerg Plains.

Defender mice have been found to be especially attracted to Gumbo cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.
