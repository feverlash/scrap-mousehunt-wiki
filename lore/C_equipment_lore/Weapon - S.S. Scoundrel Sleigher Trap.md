# ⚔️ S.S. Scoundrel Sleigher Trap

> **Classification**: Hydro Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 4,500 Power | 18 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Initiate  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 64,000 Gold40 Snowflake Sugar Cookies10 Nutcracker Sugar Cookies

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> This majestic and magical sleigh soars silently through the cold, winter skies waiting to strike. Its ominous sleigh bells sound off right before it swoops into action, grabbing unsuspecting mice, and hauling them off on the ride of their lives! Their last ride, to be precise. The Scoundrel Sleigher is specially designed for sleighing (and slaying) pirates.
> This trap has a chance to instantly capture any pirate mouse.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The S.S. Scoundrel Sleigher Trap can be purchased for 64,000 Gold, 40 Snowflake Sugar Cookies and 10 Nutcracker Sugar Cookies at Great Winter Hunt 2020.
