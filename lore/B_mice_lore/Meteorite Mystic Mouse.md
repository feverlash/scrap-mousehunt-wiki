# Meteorite Mystic Mouse

The Meteorite Mystic is a breed of mouse found in Fort Rox. They are known to drop Bloodstone, Howlite, and Meteorite Pieces as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Meteorite Mystic Mouse have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Arcane traps are very effective against the Meteorite Mystic Mouse. Shadow traps are of normal effectiveness.
The Meteorite Mystic Mouse can only be encountered during the Pitch, Utter Darkness, and First Light phases at Nighttime in Fort Rox.
