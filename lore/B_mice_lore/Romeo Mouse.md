# Romeo Mouse

The Romeo Mouse is part of the Indigenous Mice found in Town of Gnawnia.
It was previously an Event Mouse that was found during the annual Valentine's Day or Lunar New Year events.

## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Romeo Mouse is attracted only to Gilded Cheese.
