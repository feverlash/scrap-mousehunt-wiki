# Beast Tamer Mouse

The Beast Tamer Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Tribal Isles region. They are known to drop Savoury Vegetables in Nerg Plains.

## Preferred Cheese

Beast Tamer mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Beast Tamer is a typical member of The Nerg Tribe group. As such Tactical weapons are very effective against it, and all other power types are ineffective.
