# Lightning Rod Mouse

The Lightning Rod Mouse is a breed of mouse found in Meadow, and in Tournament Hall.

| Never known for its qualities of better judgement, the Lightning Rod mouse will wait in fields for days on end, waving a fork at the sky and waiting for lightning. As Hunters know, waiting is hungry work, and so catching one of these mice is as easy as spying its fork and waiting for its tummy to rumble. Hunters don't know why the Lightning Rod mouse wants to be electrified, but common sense dictates that they should be avoided when a storm is brewing. |  |  |  |

## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Lightning Rod Mouse is attracted only to Standard Cheese.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Lightning Rod Mouse.


## Power Type Effectiveness


| Very Effective | Effective | Less Effective |
| --- | --- | --- |
| None | All except Parental | None |



## Loot

The Lightning Rod Mouse drops Marble Cheese in the Meadow location, but is not known to drop any loot in the Tournament Hall location.
