# Eel Mouse

The Eel Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Sunken City. It is known to drop Barnacles and Sand Dollars as loot.

## Cheese Preference

The Eel Mouse has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Eel Mouse is susceptible only to Hydro weapons. It can be found in the Deep Oxygen Stream, Haunted Shipwreck, and Murky Depths zones while on a Dive in the Sunken City.


## Event Participation


### Spring Egg Hunt

- The Eel Mouse drops the Barnacle Egg, the Deep, Deep, Deep Egg, the Bubbling Egg, the Oxygen Egg, and the Salty Sea Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

The Eel Mouse was released 2 July 2014 as part of the Sunken City content.
