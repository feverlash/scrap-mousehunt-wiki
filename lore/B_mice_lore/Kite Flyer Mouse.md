# Kite Flyer Mouse

The Kite Flyer Mouse is a breed of mouse found on the Floating Islands. It can be encountered on any Low Altitude Island with either Cloud Cheesecake or Standard Cheese, and on any High Altitude Island when using Standard Cheese

## Cheese Preference

The Kite Flyer Mouse is attracted to Cloud Cheesecake and Standard Cheese.


## Loot


### Floating Islands's Island Based Loot

Depending on the buffs present, the Kite Flyer Mouse can drop the following loot:

| --- | --- |

## Hunting Strategy

All types except Rift and parental are effective against the Kite Flyer Mouse.
