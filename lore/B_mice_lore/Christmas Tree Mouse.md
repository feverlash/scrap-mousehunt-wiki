## History and Trivia

- 3 December 2009: The Christmas Tree Mouse was released for the first time as part of the Great Winter Hunt '09 event and dropped Icing Sugar as loot.
- 12 December 2009: The Christmas Tree Mouse began to drop both Gingerbread Base Blueprints and Gingerbread House Plans in the Festive Comet.
- 14 December 2009: The Christmas Tree Mouse began to drop Three Santa Hats in the Festive Comet.
- 22 December 2009: The Christmas Tree Mouse began to drop Eleven Coloured Lights in the Festive Comet.
- 23 December 2009: The Christmas Tree Mouse began to drop Twelve Liters of Egg Nog in the Festive Comet.
- 2 January 2010: The Christmas Tree Mouse was released into the Harbour for a period of time to make up for a glitch and give players a chance at another set of Gingerbread Base Blueprints or Gingerbread House Plans. The glitch prevented players from collecting a second set of Gingerbread Base Blueprints or Gingerbread House Plans after failing to craft the item. It also dropped Frozen Fromage.
- 13 December 2016: The points/gold for Christmas Tree Mouse were changed.
