# Stonework Warrior Mouse

The Stonework Warrior Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Jungle of Dread. They are known to drop the Engraved Solid Stone Slab, Fire Salt and Runic Potions. They are also known to drop Runestones, but only while hunters are in the process of completing the Zurreal Trap Research Assignment.

## Preferred Cheese

Stonework Warrior mice have been found to be especially attracted to Magical Havarti cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.


## Hunting Strategy

Shadow type traps such as the Clockapult of Time are very effective against these mice. All traps with other power types, including Arcane type traps such as the Obelisk of Slumber, are ineffective against these mice.


## History and Trivia

- During the Spring Egg Hunt 2011, Stonework Warrior mice began dropping a bonus Jungle of Dread Egg as loot for hunters who had obtained the Rocket Rabbit Mallet. They dropped the same loot the following year during the Spring Egg Hunt 2012 event for hunters who had obtained the Eggscavator.
- On 23 August 2011 they began to drop Runestones as loot.
- 29 July 2015: The Stonework Warrior Mouse stopped dropping Laboratory Map Piece.
