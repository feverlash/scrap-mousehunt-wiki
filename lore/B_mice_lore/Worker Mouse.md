## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Worker Mouse is attracted only to SUPER|brie+ in the Training Grounds location.
The Worker Mouse is attracted to Maki Cheese and SUPER|brie+ in the Dojo location.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Worker Mouse.

## Locations

Hunters can encounter the Worker Mouse only in the Training Grounds and Dojo locations.


## Loot

The Worker Mouse drops Ambush Trap Blueprints and Splintered Wood as loot in the Dojo location.
The Ambush Trap Blueprints are required to construct the Ambush weapon.

The Worker Mouse is not known to drop any loot in the Training Grounds location.


## History and Trivia

- 22 December 2008: The Worker Mouse is released.
- 12 January 2011: The Worker Mouse appears in Ronza's Traveling Shoppe where it drops Gauntlet Potion Tier 2, Greater Radioactive Blue Potions, Luck Charm and Rockforth cheese as loot.
- 14 April 2011: The Worker Mouse begins dropping the Antique Green Plaid Egg as loot in the Training Grounds as part of the Spring Egg Hunt 2011 event.
