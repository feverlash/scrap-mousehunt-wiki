## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Itty-Bitty Burroughs Mouse is attracted only to Limelight Cheese.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Itty-Bitty Burroughs Mouse.

Arming the Mining Charm provides a power boost against Digby Dirt Dwellers and increases the chance of encountering the Itty-Bitty Burroughs Mouse.

## Locations

Hunters can encounter the Itty-Bitty Burroughs Mouse only in the Town of Digby.

The Itty-Bitty Burroughs Mouse drops Living Shards as loot.
