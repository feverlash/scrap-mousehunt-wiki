# ⚔️ Scarlet Ember Root Trap

> **Classification**: Forgotten Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 6,000 Power | 7 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Count/Countess  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 900,000 Gold1 Ember Root100 Nachore

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Thought to be lost long ago, this fully-grown Ember Root is an extremely powerful tree that protected the Queso Canyon from intruders long ago. Its attractive vines of golden leaves would lure those seeking valuables to their untimely demise.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Scarlet Ember Root Trap can be purchased from the Trapsmith in Queso River, Prickly Plains, and Cantera Quarry for:

- 900,000 Gold
- 1 Ember Root
- 100 Nachore

It cannot be refunded.
