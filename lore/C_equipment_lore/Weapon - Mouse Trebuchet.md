# ⚔️ Mouse Trebuchet

> **Classification**: Physical Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 750 Power | 1 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Apprentice  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 11,000 Gold

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> For those so inclined to not so much harm a mouse, but rather relocate it a few miles away there is the wooden mouse trebuchet. When triggered this trap catapults the mouse at over 500 miles per hour up to a range of 7 miles.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Mouse Trebuchet can be purchased for 11,000 Gold from the Trapsmith in the following locations:

- Meadow
- Town of Gnawnia
- Windmill
- Harbour

It can be refunded for 1,980 Gold at the same locations.
