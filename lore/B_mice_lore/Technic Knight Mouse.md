# Technic Knight Mouse

The Technic Knight Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Rodentia region. They are known to drop the Crystal Library Card.

## Preferred Cheese

Technic Knight mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Technic Knight mouse is a typical member of the Wizard's Pieces group, and as such, Tactical weapons are of normal effectiveness against it, while all others are ineffective. They are only available to attract when 8 Technic Pawn mice have been caught.


## History and Trivia

- Technic Knight mice began to drop the Crystal Library Card on 9 August 2011 with the release of the Crystal Library location.
