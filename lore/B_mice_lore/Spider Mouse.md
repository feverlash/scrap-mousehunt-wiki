# Spider Mouse

> "Spider" redirects here. For other uses, see Terrifying Spider Trap.

The Spider Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Catacombs and the Forbidden Grove. It is known to drop Radioactive Blue cheese in Catacombs.

## Preferred Cheese

The Spider Mouse has been found to be especially attracted to Ancient and Radioactive Blue cheeses.


## Hunting Strategy

Arcane traps are very effective against the Spider Mouse. Forgotten and Shadow traps are of normal effectiveness.


## History and Trivia

- 4 February 2009: The Spider Mouse was released.
- 12 January 2011: The Spider Mouse could be found at Ronza's Traveling Shoppe for the first time.
