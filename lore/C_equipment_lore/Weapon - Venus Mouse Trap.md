# ⚔️ Venus Mouse Trap

> **Classification**: Tactical Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 1,900 Power | 5 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Master  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 220,000 Gold

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> The carnivorous plants that grow near the Lagoon of Whisker Woods are often harvested by Trapsmiths and grown to supernatural size by unnatural means. The result is a highly effective and well-camouflaged mouse adversary.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Venus Mouse Trap can be purchased for 220,000 Gold from the Trapsmith at the following locations:

- Great Gnarled Tree
- Lagoon
- Bazaar
- Training Grounds
- Dojo
- Meditation Room
- Pinnacle Chamber
- Cape Clawed
- Nerg Plains
- King's Gauntlet

It can be refunded for 39,600 Gold at the same locations.

The Venus Mouse Trap (if unintentionally smashed) can be reassembled from Venus Mouse Trap Husk.
