# ⚔️ Veiled Vine Trap

> **Classification**: Tactical Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 3,500 Power | 20 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Grandmaster  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 1,150,000

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Rare and exotic! The Veiled Vine Trap consists of a unique subspecies of the flora used in the Venus Mouse Trap. Disguised to resemble a common mousehole, the trap provides the dangerous illusion of a "safe place" to eat that fresh, new found piece of cheese.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Veiled Vine Trap is a Limited Edition weapon and can no longer be acquired by Hunters. It was available to be purchased for 1,150,000 gold from Ronza's Traveling Shoppe during Ronza's May 2014 visit. It could be refunded for 207,000 gold at the same location.
