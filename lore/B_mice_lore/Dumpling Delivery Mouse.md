# Dumpling Delivery Mouse

The Dumpling Delivery Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Furoma Rift. It is the Rift counterpart of Dumpling Chef Mouse.

## Preferred Cheese

The Dumpling Delivery Mouse has been found to be especially attracted to String Cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.


## Hunting Strategy

Only Rift type traps are effective against Dumpling Delivery Mouse, all other power types are ineffective.


## History and Trivia

- 18 May 2016: Dumpling Delivery Mouse was released with the release of Furoma Rift.
