# Hope Mouse

The Hope Mouse is a rare breed of mouse found in the Bazaar and Training Grounds.
The Hope Mouse used to be released on several occasions in multiple locations to increase awareness about disasters around the world.

## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Hope Mouse is attracted to Gilded and Standard Cheese in the Training Grounds, but only Standard Cheese in the Bazaar.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Hope Mouse.

## History and Trivia

- 14 January 2010: The Hope Mouse was first released as the developers' response to the earthquake in Haiti.
Original description included additional two paragraphs that were later removed:

> Events unfolding in Haiti have sprung this mouse into action. We will be donating five cents to the Mission of Hope in Haiti for the first 200,000 of these mice caught. By sounding your horn you will help us reach our goal of raising $10,000.
We greatly encourage all MouseHunters to find a charity they are comfortable with and get involved.
When it was released, it could be found in various locations throughout the kingdom (Bazaar, Calm Clearing, Cape Clawed, Dojo, Great Gnarled Tree, Harbour, Laboratory, Meadow, Mountain, S.S. Huntington II, Town of Digby, and Training Grounds), but was later confined to just Training Grounds.

- Between 26 August and 21 September 2010: The Hope Mouse was released in various locations again, in response to the flooding in Pakistan. Its description has been changed, with the following paragraph added:

> Recent events in Pakistan have sprung this mouse into action! Many organizations, such as the Red Cross and World Vision, are currently helping out. Please consider giving them your support.
During that time the Hope Mouse could be found in Bazaar, Calm Clearing, Cape Clawed, Dojo, Great Gnarled Tree, Laboratory, Town of Gnawnia, Training Grounds, and S.S. Huntington II.

- 19 January 2011: The Hope Mouse sprang back into action, in response to flooding in several countries; the following paragraph was added to its description:

> Recent flooding in Australia, Brazil, Philippines and Sri Lanka have sprung this mouse into action! Many organizations, such as the Queensland Government, are currently helping out. Please consider giving them your support.

- 11 March 2011: The description changed in response to an earthquake and tsunami in Japan and the coastal areas across the Pacific basin. The following sentence was added:

> Recent natural disasters occurring in Japan and other parts of the world have sprung this mouse into action!
During that time the Hope Mouse could be found in a variety of locations including Calm Clearing, Cape Clawed, Dojo, Mountain, Town of Digby, Town of Gnawnia, Training Grounds, S.S. Huntington II and Windmill.

- 13 November 2013: After Typhoon Yolanda hit the Philippines, a note was temporarily added to its description:

> Join the discussionto see how you can help out too!

- 19 March 2024: The Hope Mouse was updated as part of the Valentine's Day event retirement. It's description was also updated to include the sentence:

> He can be found scrounging for supplies in the Training Grounds and the Bazaar to give to those in need.
