# ⚔️ Festive Forgotten Fir Trap

> **Classification**: Forgotten Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 7,000 Power | 10 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Lord  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 0

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Placed inside of the perfect holiday vessel, the holiday spirit within this tree has been infused with the maximum amount of festive fuel to fight off the humbug. The whole holiday tree bends to the will of the spirit of joy and cheer to thwart those who dare to steal the cheese placed right under it.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Festive Forgotten Fir Trap is a Limited Edition weapon and can no longer be acquired by Hunters. It is not sold by any Trapsmith.

Hunters who traveled 130,000 meters during the Great Winter Hunt 2016 were awarded with this trap. Hunters must claim this trap from the event HUD for it to be added to their inventory.
