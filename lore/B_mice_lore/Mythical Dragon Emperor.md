| After losing his tenure at the School of Sorcery, the relentless and resourceful Mythweaver came to realize that fantasy alone would not be enough to finally defeat the pesky humans and their persistent MouseHunters. He realized that he would need something much bigger and more terrifying this time. He needed something that struck to the very core of human fear, that burrowed deep into the psyche of MouseHunters and awakened some primal terror that has remained dormant since the time of their most ancient ancestors. He needed something so great and terrible that it would instantly remind the humans of their lowly place in the hierarchy of species.<br>After much searching, he eventually discovered a book much older and more primitive than the folktales he had been rewriting thus far. This book described a very different world with very different masters from a time long before the age of humans. A time that was ruled by fear and ferociousness, where only the mightiest and most ruthless survived and thrived; a time when mercy, compassion, and civilization were concepts yet to be discovered. T'was the time of dragons!<br>The Mythweaver studied the Tale of the First Dragons extensively and learned a most shocking and fortuitous discovery: the age of dragons had not ended, it was merely awaiting the beginning of its next chapter! And so, with quill in hand, inhibitions unfettered, and delusions of grandeur abound, he set upon that most villainous deed of penning the next chapter himself.<br>He followed the directions spelled out in the book and it led him deep below the Folklore Forest to a place that time forgot: the Draconic Depths. Deep within these coarse, cacophonous caverns, the Mythweaver discovered the hidden home and true origin of all the dragons in Gnawnia. Every great dragon that he had studied from the world above could trace its origins back to these dark and dangerous depths. From the furious flames of Kalor'ignis to the frigid ferocity of Icewing and all the way back to the great Dragon mouse itself. All of these legendary beasts once called these depths their home.<br>Here the Mythweaver had everything he needed to amass the greatest and most terrible army of ancient monsters with which to lay waste to the human world above. So he used every ounce of his fabled magic to transform himself into the mightiest dragon of them all and named himself their Emperor. Now he intends to lead this legendary legion up to the surface and dispose of the human Kingdom of Gnawnia once and for all. That is, unless the strongest and bravest MouseHunters can face their fears and descend into these dreadful depths to put a stop to this heinous plan before it takes flight! |  |  |  |

## Cheese Preference

Mythical Dragon Emperor is only attracted to Elemental Emmental.


## Hunting Strategy

The Mythical Dragon Emperor can only be attracted while hunting in an Elemental Dragons' Den.

Only Draconic power types are effective against Mythical Dragon Emperor.
All other types are ineffective.
