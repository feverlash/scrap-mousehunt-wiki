## Hunting Strategy

Law power type is very effective against Bartender Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.

- In Claw Shot City, this mouse is available when the Hunter is working on a Dirt Dealers Wanted Poster or Corrupt Cartel Wanted Poster.
- In Gnawnian Express Station, this mouse is always available.


## History and Trivia

- 25 June 2013: Bartender Mouse was released as part of the Claw Shot City, but couldn't be attracted yet.
- 3 July 2013: Bartender Mouse was made available with the Dirt Dealers Wanted Poster.
