# Dawn Guardian Mouse

The Dawn Guardian is a breed of mouse found in Fort Rox. They are known to drop Dawn Dust as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Dawn Guardian mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Arcane traps are very effective against the Dawn Guardian. Shadow traps are of normal effectiveness.
Dawn Guardian mice can only be encountered during the dawn phase in Fort Rox.


## History and Trivia

- 3 November 2016: The Dawn Guardian Mouse was released as a part of the Fort Rox content.
