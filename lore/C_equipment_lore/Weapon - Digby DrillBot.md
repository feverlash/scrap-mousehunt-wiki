# ⚔️ Digby DrillBot

> **Classification**: Physical Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 3,600 Power | 5 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Master  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 256,000 Gold

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> The scientists of Digby used their knowledge of tunnel digging technology to create the ultimate mouse adversary. Using its two massive high-speed drills this titan can easily destroy any mouse put in its path.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Digby DrillBot can be purchased from the Trapsmith in Town of Digby for 256,000 Gold.

It can be refunded for 46,080 Gold at the same location.

The Digby DrillBot can also be assembled from Digby DrillBot Parts if obtained from loot, or reassembled from the same if unintentionally smashed.
