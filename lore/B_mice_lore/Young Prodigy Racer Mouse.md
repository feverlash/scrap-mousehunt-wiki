## History and Trivia

- 15 December 2015: The Young Prodigy Racer Mouse was introduced as part of the Great Winter Hunt 2015.
- During the Extreme Toboggan Challenge, the Young Prodigy Racer Mouse was caught as a Bonus Hunt and did not consume cheese or charms.
- 13 December 2016: The points/gold for Young Prodigy Racer Mouse were changed at the beginning of Great Winter Hunt 2016 event:
Points: 1,700 -> 5,000
Gold: 1,700 -> 900
- 6 December 2022: The points for Young Prodigy Racer Mouse was adjusted at the beginning of Great Winter Hunt 2022 event:
Points: 5,000 -> 3,000
