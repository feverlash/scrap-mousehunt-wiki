## Floor Type Info

- The Shade of the Eclipse Mouse can only be encountered after entering the Valour Rift Tower with the Ultimate Umbra augmentation inactive.
- The Shade of the Eclipse Mouse can be found on Eclipse floors.
Catching the Shade of the Eclipse Mouse will extend the duration the Hunter has in the Valour Rift tower - this boost will depend on the Hunter's Siphon level.
- The Shade of the Eclipse Mouse has a Minimum Floor of 8.
