# Supply Hoarder Mouse

The Supply Hoarder Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Gnawnian Express Station. It can only be attracted and caught during the Supply Depot phase during a Supply Rush. They are known to drop Iron Pellets and Royal Supply Crates as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Supply Hoarder mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Law type traps such as S.L.A.C. are very effective against the Supply Hoarder Mouse. This mouse can only be caught during a Supply Rush, which requires the use of a Supply Schedule Charm to first catch a Warehouse Manager Mouse. However, as any other hunter in the Hunting Team riding the same train can trigger the supply rush, it is possible to catch this mouse without using any charms. During a Supply Rush, the Supply Schedule Charm provides no additional advantage, and use of a different charm may make it easier to catch a Supply Hoarder.


## Event Participation

- The Supply Hoarder Mouse drops the Gnawnian Express Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.
