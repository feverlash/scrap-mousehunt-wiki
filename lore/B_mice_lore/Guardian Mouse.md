# Guardian Mouse

> "Guardian" redirects here. For other uses, see Guardian (disambiguation).

The Guardian Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Tribal Isles region. They are known to drop Red Pepper Seed in Derr Dunes.

## Preferred Cheese

Guardian mice have been found to be especially attracted to Crunchy cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.
