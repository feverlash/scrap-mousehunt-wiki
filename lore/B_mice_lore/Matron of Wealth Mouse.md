# Matron of Wealth Mouse

The Matron of Wealth Mouse can be found in Zokor. It is known to drop Plates of Fealty, Scholar Scrolls, and Tech Power Cores in Zokor.

## Preferred Cheese

The Matron of Wealth Mouse prefers Glowing Gruyere, but can be attracted with Standard Cheeses.


## Hunting Strategy

Forgotten weapons are very effective against the Matron of Wealth Mouse. Arcane weapons are of normal effectiveness.

The Matron of Wealth Mouse can be found in the Treasure Room and the Treasure Vault.


## Loot

- Labyrinth Theme Scrap IV, Plates of Fealty, Scholar Scrolls, and Tech Power Cores


## History and Trivia

> 9 September 2015: The Matron of Wealth Mouse was introduced as part of the Hollow Heights release.
