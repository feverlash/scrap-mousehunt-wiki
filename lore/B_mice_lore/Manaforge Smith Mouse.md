## Hunting Strategy

Manaforge Smith Mouse is available in Manaforge district in Zokor after 20 mice has been caught. It can only be caught once per trip there.

Manaforge Smith Mouse can be attracted with Standard Cheese and Glowing Gruyere.

Forgotten power type is very effective against the this mouse. Arcane is effective.


## Loot

- Ancient Relic
- Powercore Hammer
- Tech Power Core
- Climbing Grapnel of Tech.
