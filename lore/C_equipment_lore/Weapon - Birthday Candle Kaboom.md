# ⚔️ Birthday Candle Kaboom

> **Classification**: Physical Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 2,400 Power | 14 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Journeyman  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 289,000

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> These aren't your normal everyday decorative candles, this becomes apparent as soon as you light one. Candles don't normally spark, hiss and burn up their wick as fast as a mouse eating string cheese. Just a hint: don't put these candles on a friend's cake unless you want them wearing it!

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Birthday Candle Kaboom is a Limited Edition weapon and can no longer be acquired by Hunters.

It could only be purchased at the Birthday Party Trapsmith for 289,000 gold and required Hunters to have the following items found during the Birthday Party event:

- Birthday Explosives
- Fuse
- Red Wax
