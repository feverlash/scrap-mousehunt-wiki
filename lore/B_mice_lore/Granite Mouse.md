## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Granite Mouse is attracted mainly to Standard Cheese.

In the Mountain location, the Granite Mouse is not attracted to Chedd-Ore Cheese or Abominable Asiago Cheese.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Granite Mouse.

## History and Trivia

- 31 March 2009: The Granite Mouse began dropping the Whisker Woods Clearing Map Piece in the Harbour, giving access to the Calm Clearing.
- 25 June 2013: The Granite Mouse gained a vulnerability to Law power type traps, with the release of Varmint Valley.
- 25 March 2014: The Shard Centurion Mouse was introduced as the Rift counterpart of the Granite Mouse.
- 25 March 2014: The Granite Mouse gained a vulnerability to Rift power type traps, with the release of the Rift Plane.
- 29 July 2015: The Whisker Woods Clearing Map Piece is renamed Calm Clearing Map Piece.
- 11 April 2017: The Granite Mouse stopped dropping the Calm Clearing Map Piece when the Harbour was updated.
- 17 October 2018: The Granite Mouse changed mouse groups from Indigenous Mice to Mountain Mice and was removed from the Laboratory.
