## Cheese Preference

Data Devourer Mouse is only attracted to Master Mimolette.


## Hunting Strategy

The Data Devourer Mouse can only be attracted during the Shadow phase of the Final Exam.

Shadow power type is very effective against Data Devourer Mouse.
Arcane power type is effective against Data Devourer Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
