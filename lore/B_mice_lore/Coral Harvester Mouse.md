# Coral Harvester Mouse

The Coral Harvester Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Sunken City. It is known to drop Damaged Coral Fragments and Sand Dollars as loot.

## Cheese Preference

The Coral Harvester Mouse has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Coral Harvester Mouse is susceptible only to Hydro weapons.It can be found in the Coral Garden zone while on a Dive in the Sunken City.


## Event Participation


### Spring Egg Hunt

- The Coral Harvester Mouse drops the Coral Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

The Coral Harvester Mouse was released 2 July 2014 as part of the Sunken City content.
