## Cheese Preference

Supremia Magnificus is only attracted to Elemental Emmental.


## Hunting Strategy

The Supremia Magnificus can only be attracted while hunting in an Elemental Dragons' Den.

Only Draconic power types are effective against Supremia Magnificus.
All other types are ineffective.
