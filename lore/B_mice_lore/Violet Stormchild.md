## Preferred Cheese

The Violet Stormchild has been found to be especially attracted to Dragonvine Cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.

## Hunting Strategy

Only Draconic type traps are effective against the Violet Stormchild, all other power types are ineffective.

Dragonbane Charms, Super Dragonbane Charms and Extreme Dragonbane Charms provide a power bonus against the Violet Stormchild.

The Violet Stormchild is only available when either wind or rain intensities are between 0%-34% in Moussu Picchu.


## History and Trivia

- 6 September 2017: The Violet Stormchild was introduced as part of the Moussu Picchu release.
