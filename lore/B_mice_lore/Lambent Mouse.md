# Lambent Mouse

The Lambent Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Burroughs Rift. It is known to drop Polluted Parmesan Potions as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Lambent Mouse is only attracted to Terre Ricotta Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Lambent Mouse can only be attracted when the Mist Meter is in Green Zone.

Rift power type is effective against Lambent Mouse while all others are less effective.


## History and Trivia

- 9 September 2014: The Lambent Mouse was released as part of the Burroughs Rift location.
