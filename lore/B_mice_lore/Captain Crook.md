## Cheese Preference

Captain Crook is attracted to Aurora Bocconcini but can also be encountered at a lower rate with Sky Pirate Swiss or Sky Raider Romano.


## Hunting Strategy

The Captain Crook can only be attracted while intercepting one of Captain Crook raids.

Physical, Shadow, Tactical, Arcane, Forgotten, Hydro, Draconic, Law power types are effective against Captain Crook.
All other types are ineffective.
