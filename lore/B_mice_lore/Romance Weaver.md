## Cheese Preference

Romance Weaver is attracted to Plotting Paneer or Storied Saga or Deus Ex Maconnais.


## Hunting Strategy

The Romance Weaver can only be attracted while writing a postscript to a story.

Only Law power types are effective against Romance Weaver.
All other types are ineffective.
