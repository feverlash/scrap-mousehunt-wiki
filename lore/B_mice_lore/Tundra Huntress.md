## History and Trivia

- 9 December 2014: Tundra Huntress was released as part of the Great Winter Hunt 2014 event, but could not be attracted yet.
- 15 December 2014: Tundra Huntress became available as part of the second week of the Great Winter Hunt 2014.
- 13 December 2016: The points/gold for Tundra Huntress were changed.
