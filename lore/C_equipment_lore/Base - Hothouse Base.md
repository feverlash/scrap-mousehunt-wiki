# 🛡️ Hothouse Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 250 Power | 6 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Baron/Baroness  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 300 Ice Coin

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> A lovely flowerbed of poinsettias. Kept fresh inside a fully sealed and functional hothouse! Perfect for keeping these flowers growing even out of season. Perfect for celebrating the Great Winter Hunt all year round!
> Don't you wish you could just smell them?
> The Hothouse base will double petal and herb drops from mice in the Living Garden, Lost City, Sand Dunes, and their twisted counterparts!

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Hothouse Base was available for purchase for 300 Ice Coin after defeating the Borean Commander at the Festive Ice Fortress during the Great Winter Iceball Brawl.
