## Locations

Hunters can encounter the Fairy Mouse in the Great Gnarled Tree and Lagoon locations.

Hunters must capture the Fairy Mouse in the Great Gnarled Tree in order to obtain the Map of the Lagoon and gain access to the Lagoon location.


## Loot

The Fairy Mouse drops the Map of the Lagoon in the Great Gnarled Tree location.

The Fairy Mouse is not known to drop any loot in the Lagoon location.


## History and Trivia

- 9 April 2009: The Fairy Mouse was released with the introduction of the Great Gnarled Tree.
- 22 April 2009: The Fairy Mouse begins dropping Map of the Lagoon as loot, and begins appearing in the Lagoon.
- 25 June 2013: The Fairy Mouse gained a vulnerability to Law traps with the release of Varmint Valley.
- 29 July 2015: The strength of the Fairy Mouse was reduced and it's chance of encounter was increased.
