# ⚔️ Mouse Hot Tub

> **Classification**: Physical Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 70 Power | 2 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Novice  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 600 Gold

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Instead of harming a mouse, give it soothing place to relax before relocating it to a location far from the Kingdom.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
If the Hunter choose a different weapon from the King when they first start playing, they can purchase the Mouse Hot Tub from the Trapsmith in Town of Gnawnia for 600 Gold.

It can be refunded for 110 Gold at the same location.
