## Preferred Cheese

The Bilged Boatswain Mouse has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Hunters must have an active Pirate Bounty to attract this mouse.

Hunters must capture a Pirate Mouse to obtain a pirate bounty. When Hunters begin the search for the mice listed on the crew manifest provided by the Pirate Mouse, the 5 Pirate Crew mice are added to the mouse population of the Harbour. Capturing all 3 mice listed on the crew manifest will reward the player with booty from the pirate bounty.


## Event Participation


### Spring Egg Hunt

- The Bilged Boatswain Mouse drops the Pirate Crew Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.
