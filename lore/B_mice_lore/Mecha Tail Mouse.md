# Mecha Tail Mouse

The Mecha Tail Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Burroughs Rift. It is not known to drop any loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Mecha Tail Mouse is only attracted to Polluted Parmesan Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Mecha Tail Mouse can be attracted when the Mist Meter is in Yellow and Green Zone.

Rift power type is effective against Mecha Tail Mouse while all others are less effective.


## History and Trivia

- 9 September 2014: The Mecha Tail Mouse was released as part of the Burroughs Rift location.
