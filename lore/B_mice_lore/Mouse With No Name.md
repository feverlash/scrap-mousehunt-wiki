# Mouse With No Name

The Mouse With No Name is a breed of mouse found in the Gnawnian Express Station. It can only be attracted and caught during the Raider River phase, where they are known to drop Copper Beads as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Mouse With No Name has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Law type traps such as S.L.A.C. are very effective against the Mouse With No Name. They are more attracted when the correct charm (Roof Rack, Door Guard, or Greasy Glob) for the current wave is armed, and do not visit during Trap Checks.


## Event Participation

- The Mouse With No Name drops the Gnawnian Express Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

- Released on 26 September 2013 as part of the Gnawnian Express Station content.
- The name of this mouse is a reference to the "Man With No Name" character played by Clint Eastwood in a series Sergio Leone Spaghetti Westerns.
