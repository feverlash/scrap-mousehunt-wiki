# Student of the Chi Claw Mouse

The Student of the Chi Claw Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Furoma Rift. It is the Rift counterpart of Student of the Cheese Claw Mouse.

## Preferred Cheese

The Student of the Chi Claw Mouse has been found to be especially attracted to Maki String cheese, but can be attracted with other String Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Only Rift type traps are very effective against Student of the Chi Claw Mouse, all other power types are ineffective.


## History and Trivia

- 18 May 2016: Student of the Chi Claw Mouse was released with the release of Furoma Rift.
