## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Wicked Witch of Whisker Woods Mouse is attracted mainly to Standard Cheese.

In the Great Gnarled Tree location, the Wicked Witch of Whisker Woods Mouse will also be attracted when hunting with Gnarled Cheese.
In the Lagoon location, the Wicked Witch of Whisker Woods Mouse will not be attracted when hunting with Gnarled Cheese or Wicked Gnarly Cheese.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Wicked Witch of Whisker Woods Mouse.

## Locations

Hunters can encounter the Wicked Witch of Whisker Woods Mouse in the Great Gnarled Tree and Lagoon locations.


## Loot

The Wicked Witch of Whisker Woods Mouse is not known to drop any loot.


## History and Trivia

- 9 April 2009: The Wicked Witch of Whisker Woods Mouse was released with the introduction of the Great Gnarled Tree.
- 14 April 2011: The Wicked Witch of Whisker Woods Mouse began dropping a bonus Whisker Woods Egg as loot as part of the annual Spring Egg Hunt.
- 25 June 2013: The Wicked Witch of Whisker Woods Mouse gained a vulnerability to Law traps with the release of Varmint Valley.
- The Whisker Woods Wicked Hat collectible is based on the hat worn by the Wicked Witch of Whisker Woods Mouse.
