## Cheese Preference

Mythical Master Sorcerer is attracted to Standard Cheese, Apprentice Ambert, and Master Mimolette.


## Hunting Strategy

The Mythical Master Sorcerer can only be attracted at the end of each course level while enrolled in Final Exam.

Arcane and Shadow power type is very effective against Mythical Master Sorcerer.
All other types are ineffective.
