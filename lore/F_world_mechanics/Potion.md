# Potion


## Overview

Potions are used to convert one or more types of item into a different type. Hunters can find their Potions in their inventory.
Potions are used by Hunters to acquire various Cheeses that are occasionally necessary for attracting specific mice in specific locations. The same is true for Charms.
Certain Potions may also provide various options of conversion that affects the input cost amount and total amount of cheese converted.


## Conversion Strategy

Hunters should be aware that some cheeses that can be created using Potions, may also be obtained in other ways that may require more or less gold.
Hunters should also take into consideration their Hunter's Title when using Potions, as larger quantities of the created cheese can sometimes be obtained in an easier method when a Hunter increases in rank.


## Potion Categories

Potions are separated into various Potion Categories, usually based on where the item is primarily used in the Kingdom.
These categories are:

- Afterword Acres
