# ⚔️ Legendary KingBot

> **Classification**: Physical Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 20,000 Power | 42 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Viceroy  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 20,000,000 gold3 Golden Goose

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Using the new and improved Chivalric Code++ (CC++) programming language in combination with the latest advancements in A.I. (Aristocratic Intelligence), comes the cutting edge in Physical mouse trap technology: the Legendary KingBot!
> This model improves upon the statistics of its predecessor, the Charming PrinceBot, in every way by employing the most sophisticated nobility algorithms ever conceived. It was designed with a Worthiness Factor so high that when confronted with the industry standard Sword-in-the-Stone unit test, it not only removed the sword with ease but completely obliterated the stone in the process!

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Legendary KingBot can be purchased from the Trapsmith in Bountiful Beanstalk for:

- 20,000,000 gold
- 3 Golden Goose

It requires the Hunter to own the Charming PrinceBot and complete the Slay the Mythical Giant King adventure to purchase.

It cannot be refunded.
