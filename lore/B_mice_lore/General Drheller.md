# General Drheller

General Drheller is a breed of mouse found in the General zones of the Iceberg. It is known to drop a variety of loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The General Drheller has no specific preference for any kind of cheese, though will appear more frequently when traps are baited with SUPER|brie+.


## Hunting Strategy

Failing to capture a General Drheller will send the Hunter back 6 feet into the previous zone.
As the General Drheller is the rarest mouse among the Icewing's Generals, Hunters targeting this mouse might want to do General Cycling.


## History and Trivia

- The General Drheller was based on a design which won a Grand Prize in an "Official Design a Mouse Art Contest" that was held via the Discussion Boards.
- 24 May 2012: The General Drheller was released as a part of the Iceberg.
- 1 June 2012: The General Drheller began to drop additional loot.
- 29 August 2012: The General Drheller began dropping Ancient Relic as loot.
