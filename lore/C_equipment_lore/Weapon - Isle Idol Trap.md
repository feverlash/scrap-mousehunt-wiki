# ⚔️ Isle Idol Trap

> **Classification**: Physical Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 5,050 Power | 7 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Legendary  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 1,250,000

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Any intrepid mouse explorers investigating the cheese on this trap will have their work cut out for them. They had better make sure that they have steady paws, a carefully-weighed bag of sand, and really swift legs just in case something goes wrong!The power of the rolling boulder on this trap is hefty enough to knock double Delicious Stones out of the pockets of any Derr Dunes mice slow enough to be caught in its path.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Isle Idol Trap and its Skins can be obtained from opening the Isle Idol Box Set, which was available to be purchased for 1,250,000 gold from Ronza's Traveling Shoppe during Ronza's March 2013 visit. The box could be refunded for 750,000 gold at the same location.
