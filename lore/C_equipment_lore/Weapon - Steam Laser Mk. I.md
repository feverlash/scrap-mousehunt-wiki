# ⚔️ Steam Laser Mk. I

> **Classification**: Hydro Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 4,200 Power | 14 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Lord/Lady  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 345,000 Gold4 Wire Spool4 Heating Oil4 Frosty Metal

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Like a fog machine mixed with a laser lightshow, the Steam Laser Mark I is both effective and awesome. Even mice not directly struck by the Steam Laser Mark I's beam stop in their tracks, engrossed in the incredible lightshow before them.This combination of hot and hydro works especially well on the Slushy Shoreline and in the Iceberg.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Steam Laser Mk. I can be purchased from the Trapsmith in Slushy Shoreline and Iceberg for:

- 345,000 Gold
- 4 Wire Spool
- 4 Heating Oil
- 4 Frosty Metal

It can be refunded at the same locations for 62,100 Gold.

The Steam Laser Mk. I (if unintentionally smashed) can be reassembled from Steam Laser Mk. I Parts.
