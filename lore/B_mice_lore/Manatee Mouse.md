## Cheese Preference

The Manatee Mouse has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Manatee Mouse is susceptible only to Hydro weapons. It can be found in the Oxygen Stream, Rocky Outcrop, Shallow Shoals, and Sea Floor zones while on a Dive in the Sunken City.


## Event Participation


### Spring Egg Hunt

- The Manatee Mouse drops the Barnacle Egg, the Bubbling Egg, the Oxygen Egg, and the Salty Sea Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

The Manatee Mouse was released 2 July 2014 as part of the Sunken City content.
