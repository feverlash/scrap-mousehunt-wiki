# Mystic Bishop Mouse

The Mystic Bishop Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Rodentia region. They are known to drop the Crystal Library Card.

## Preferred Cheese

Mystic Bishop mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Mystic Bishop mouse is a typical member of the Wizard's Pieces group, and as such, Tactical weapons are of normal effectiveness against it, while all others are ineffective. They are only available to attract after 2 Mystic Knight mice have been caught.


## History and Trivia

- Mystic Bishop mice began to drop the Crystal Library Card on 9 August 2011 with the release of the Crystal Library location.
