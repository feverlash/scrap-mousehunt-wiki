## Cheese Preference

Arcana Overachiever Mouse is only attracted to Apprentice Ambert.


## Hunting Strategy

The Arcana Overachiever Mouse can only be attracted while enrolled in Arcane Arts or during the Arcane phase of the Final Exam.

Arcane power type is very effective against Arcana Overachiever Mouse.
Shadow power type is effective against Arcana Overachiever Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
