# Draconic Warden Mouse

The Draconic Warden Mouse is a breed of mouse found in Dracano. They are known to drop Coconut Milk, Fire Salt and Dragon Ember.

## Preferred Cheese

Draconic Warden mice have been found to be particularly attracted to Inferno Havarti, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.
