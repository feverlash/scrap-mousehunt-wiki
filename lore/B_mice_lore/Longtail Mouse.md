# Longtail Mouse

The Longtail Mouse is a breed of mouse found in various locations in Gnawnia, and in King's Arms.

## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Longtail Mouse is attracted to Standard Cheese.

In the Town of Gnawnia, the Longtail Mouse can be attracted when hunting with Gilded Cheese.
In the Windmill location, the Longtail Mouse will not be attracted when hunting with Grilled Cheese.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Longtail Mouse.

## Loot

The Longtail Mouse drops Ancient Relic and Swiss Cheese in all locations, and additionally Packets of Flour in the Windmill location.


## History and Trivia

- 8 June 2010: The Longtail Mouse was introduced with the rollout of MouseHunt v.3 (which was codenamed Longtail).
- 25 June 2013: The Longtail Mouse gained a vulnerability to Law power type traps, with the release of Varmint Valley.
- 19 September 2013: The effect of SUPER|brie+ on Longtail Mouse attraction when compared to Standard Cheese became more pronounced during the Gnawnia population shift.
- 25 March 2014: The Longtail Mouse gained a vulnerability to Rift power type traps, with the introduction of the Rift Plane.
- 2 September 2014: The Longtail Mouse began dropping Packets of Flour at the Windmill.
- 23 June 2015: The Longtail Mouse began dropping Ancient Relics with the start of Season 3 of Treasure Hunting.
