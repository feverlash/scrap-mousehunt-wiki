## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Stone Cutter Mouse is attracted only to Limelight Cheese.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Stone Cutter Mouse.

Arming the Mining Charm provides a power boost against Digby Dirt Dwellers.
