A reanimated mouse created to collect the corpses of intruders, salvage what pieces they can, and return these raw materials back to the mages that created him.<br>The fuming bowl of unknown magical contents keeps this corpse animated and prevents any further deterioration of the vessel. - Zokor: The Reanimated Carver Mouse can be found in all Districts and the Lair of the Minotaur.
