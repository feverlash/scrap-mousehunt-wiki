| What this miniscule mouse lacks in size, it more than makes up for in brute strength, bravado, and showmanship! ## Cheese Preference

Mighty Mite Mouse can be attracted with any Standard Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Mighty Mite Mouse is available when 3 plants are growing.

Tactical power type is effective against Mighty Mite Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.


## Loot

Mighty Mite Mouse can drop:

- Condensed Creativity
- Crop Coin
