## Cheese Preference

Killer Krill Mouse is only attracted to Symbolic Sirene.


## Hunting Strategy

The Killer Krill Mouse can only be attracted while on the rapids before the waterfall in Epilogue Falls.

Hydro power type is effective against Killer Krill Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
