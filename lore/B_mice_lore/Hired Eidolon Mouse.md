# Hired Eidolon Mouse

The Hired Eidolon Mouse can be found in the Labyrinth and Zokor. It is known to drop Plates of Fealty, Scholar Scrolls, and Tech Power Cores in Zokor.

## Preferred Cheese

The Hired Eidolon Mouse prefers Glowing Gruyere in both the Labyrinth and Zokor, but can be attracted with Standard Cheeses.


## Hunting Strategy

Forgotten weapons are very effective against the Hired Eidolon Mouse. Arcane weapons are of normal effectiveness.

- Labyrinth

> The Hired Eidolon Mouse can be found in Plain and Superior Treasury Hallways.

- Zokor

> The Hired Eidolon Mouse can be found in the Treasure Room and the Treasure Vault.


## Loot

- Zokor

> Labyrinth Theme Scrap IV, Plates of Fealty, Scholar Scrolls, and Tech Power Cores
