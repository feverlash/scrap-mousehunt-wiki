## Cheese Preference

Featherlight Mouse is only attracted to Master Mimolette.


## Hunting Strategy

The Featherlight Mouse can only be attracted while enrolled in Arcane Arts or during the Arcane phase of the Final Exam.

Arcane power type is very effective against Featherlight Mouse.
Shadow power type is effective against Featherlight Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
