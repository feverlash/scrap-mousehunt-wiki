# Grubling Mouse

The Grubling Mouse is a breed of mouse that can be found in the Sand Dunes, and are known to drop Cynd Essences and Duskshade Petals as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Grubling mice have been found to be only attracted to Dewthief Camembert.


## Hunting Strategy

The Grubling mouse is a member of the Sand Dunes mouse group. As such all power types except Shadow weapons are ineffective against it.

It can only be attracted during the Grubling Stampede Minigame, if the hunter has Grubling Chow Charms armed.


## History and Trivia

- Grubling mice were released on 14 November 2012, as part of the Living Garden update.
