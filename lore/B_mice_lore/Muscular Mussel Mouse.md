## Cheese Preference

Muscular Mussel Mouse is only attracted to Symbolic Sirene.


## Hunting Strategy

The Muscular Mussel Mouse can only be attracted while on the rapids before the waterfall in Epilogue Falls.

Hydro power type is effective against Muscular Mussel Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
