# Sand Pilgrim

The Sand Pilgrim is a breed of mouse that can be found in the Sand Dunes, and is known to drop Aleth Essences and Duskshade Petals as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Sand Pilgrim mice have been found to be only attracted to Dewthief Camembert.


## Hunting Strategy

The Sand Pilgrim mouse is a member of the Sand Dunes mouse group. As such all power types except Shadow weapons are ineffective against it.


## History and Trivia

- Sand Pilgrim mice were released on 14 November 2012, as part of the Living Garden update.
