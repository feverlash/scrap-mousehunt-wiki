# ⚔️ Steam AugerBot 3000

> **Classification**: Hydro Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 7,000 Power | 20 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Lord/Lady  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 0

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Behold the Steam AugerBot 3000! The latest technology in the field of yuletide logistics. Featuring not one but TWO diamond tipped augers capable of moving immense amounts of ice!
> When hunting at the Iceberg, this trap gains a luck bonus and has a chance of doubling your distance traveled.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Steam AugerBot 3000 could only be obtained during Great Winter Hunt 2024 event.

After sending 50 Golems, the Hunter could claim this weapon from the Reward Track.
