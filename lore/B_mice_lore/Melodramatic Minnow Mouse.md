## Cheese Preference

Melodramatic Minnow Mouse is only attracted to Grubbeen Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Hydro power type is effective against Melodramatic Minnow Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.


## Loot

Melodramatic Minnow Mouse can drop:

- Condensed Creativity

Additionally, it can drop the following loot depending on Chum status:

- Pond Penny (when Brainstorm Chum off)
- Cleverness Clam (when Brainstorm Chum on)
