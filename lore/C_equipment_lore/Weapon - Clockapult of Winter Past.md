# ⚔️ Clockapult of Winter Past

> **Classification**: Shadow Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 2,700 Power | 10 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Legendary  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 1,800,000 Gold

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> This more festive version of the Clockapult of Time sends caught mice into the not-so-distant past where they learn a valuable lesson that gives mice a change of heart.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Clockapult of Winter Past is not sold by any Trapsmith. It can only be crafted given the proper ingredients.
