## 🌍 Gambaran & Atmosfer Lingkungan

Table of Contents is a hunting location in Folklore Forest region.

Foreword Farm, Prologue Pond, and Table of Contents are 3 connected hunting locations.

They require the minimum rank of Archduke/Archduchess and the Legendary Looking Glass to access.

Gnawbel Prize for Literature earned by hunting here can be used to unlock several Upgrades in those 3 locations, as well as purchase new Weapons in the Trapsmith.
