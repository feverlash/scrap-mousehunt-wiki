# ⚔️ Gingerbread House Surprise

> **Classification**: Tactical Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 2,200 Power | 8 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Journeyman  
> **Acquisition Cost**: See currentGreat Winter Hunt event

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Warmth and the smell of baking cheese biscuits draw mice into this charming little gumdrop house, where they will find a present, waiting just for them. The mice unwrap the present, to find, in place of the cheese biscuits, a mouse-sized parachute. Once the mouse puts on the parachute, a burst of reverse Santa magic shoots it out the chimney, into the air to a land far away. Being flung into the air isn't what the mouse had on its list, but it's the thought that counts.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
- From 2015: The Gingerbread House Surprise was available for 75,000 gold and 2 Advent Chocolates from the Trapsmith at Great Winter Hunt event location.
- 2014: The Gingerbread House Surprise was not available during the Great Winter Hunt.
- 2013: The Gingerbread House Surprise was available for purchase for 75,000 gold and 50 Ice Coins during the Great Winter Snowball Fight.

Prior to the Great Winter Hunt event held in 2011, the Gingerbread House Surprise could only be crafted given the proper ingredients.
