# RR-8 Mouse

The RR-8 Mouse can be found in the Labyrinth and Zokor. It is known to drop Tech Power Cores in Zokor.

## Preferred Cheese

The RR-8 Mouse prefers Glowing Gruyere in both the Labyrinth and Zokor, but can be attracted with Standard Cheeses.


## Hunting Strategy

Forgotten weapons are very effective against the RR-8 Mouse. Arcane weapons are of normal effectiveness.

- Labyrinth

> The RR-8 Mouse can be found in Plain, Superior, and Epic Tech Hallways.

- Zokor

> The RR-8 Mouse can be found in the Tech Foundry Outskirts, the Tech Research Center, and the Manaforge.


## Loot

- Zokor

> Labyrinth Theme Scrap I and Tech Power Cores
