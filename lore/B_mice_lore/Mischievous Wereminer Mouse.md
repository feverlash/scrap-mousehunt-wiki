## Preferred Cheese

Mischievous Wereminer Mouse can be attracted by any Standard Cheese as well as Crescent Cheese and Moon Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Mischievous Wereminer Mouse is available during 4 sub-phases of Night:

- Twilight
- Midnight
- Pitch
- Utter Darkness

Shadow power type is very effective against this mouse. Arcane type is of normal effectiveness.
All other types are ineffective.


## History and Trivia

- 3 November 2016: Mischievous Wereminer Mouse was release as a part of Fort Rox location.
- 15 November 2022: Mischievous Wereminer Mouse started dropping Moon Cheese with the New Dawn balance update to Fort Rox.
