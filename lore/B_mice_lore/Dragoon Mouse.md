## Preferred Cheese

The Dragoon Mouse has been found to be especially attracted to Dragonvine Cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.

## Hunting Strategy

Only Draconic type traps are effective against the Dragoon Mouse, all other power types are ineffective.

Dragonbane Charms, Super Dragonbane Charms and Extreme Dragonbane Charms provide a power bonus against the Dragoon Mouse.

The Dragoon Mouse is only available when both wind and rain intensities are at least between 80%-99% in Moussu Picchu.


## History and Trivia
