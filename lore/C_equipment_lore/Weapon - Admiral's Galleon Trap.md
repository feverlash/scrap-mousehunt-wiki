# ⚔️ Admiral's Galleon Trap

> **Classification**: Shadow Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 2,500 Power | 15 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Lord  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 0

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> A massive haunted galleon once commanded by Admiral Arrrgh himself. It strikes fear in the souls of all nether-nautical ghostship captains and mice alike. Sitting idly by, hovering between this world and the next, it waits, invisibly, for an unsuspecting mouse to step aboard the base. Once awoken, it materializes and begins to lift into the sky with its new member of the crew.
> Of course they always have the option to jump...
> The Admiral's Galleon Trap has the spooktacular ability to randomly raid mice for up to 5,000 bonus Cursed Gold!

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Admiral's Galleon Trap is an Limited Edition weapon and can no longer be acquired by Hunters. It was not sold by any Trapsmith. It could only be obtained by defeating 1 Ghostship during the 2018 Halloween event.
