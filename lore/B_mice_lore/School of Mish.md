# School of Mish

The School of Mish are a breed of mouse found in the Sunken City. They are known to drop Mouse Scales and Sand Dollars as loot.

## Cheese Preference

The School of Mish have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The School of Mish are susceptible only to Hydro weapons. They can be found in the School of Mice zone while on a Dive in the Sunken City.


## Event Participation

- The School of Mish drops the Mouse Scale Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

The School of Mish were released 2 July 2014 as part of the Sunken City content.
