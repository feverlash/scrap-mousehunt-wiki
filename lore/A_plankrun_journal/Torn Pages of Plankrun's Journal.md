# Torn Pages of Plankrun's Journal

> "Journal" redirects here. For other uses, see Hunter's Journal.

The Torn Pages of Plankrun’s Journal are obtained as loot from certain breeds of mice. They are placed in the Plankrun Inventory tab of a hunter's inventory. As of now, the Pages have no known use, outside of being collector's items.

The pages are found grouped together as two sides of a single page. For example, if a hunter captures a mouse carrying the Dojo pages, the hunter will have both sides of that page. Similarly, covers include both the inside and outside of the respective cover.


## Description

During your travels you may find pages from the fabled MouseHunter Plankrun. The stories of this first-ever MouseHunter have been whispered over campfires throughout the years. Unfortunately, all that is known about the fate of this hunter is what can be deciphered from the Torn Pages of Plankrun's Journal, often found scattered across the Kingdom.
