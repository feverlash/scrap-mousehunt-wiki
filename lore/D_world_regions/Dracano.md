# 📍 Dracano

> **Wilayah / Region**: Tribal Isles  
> **Pangkat Minimal**: Knight  
> **Fasilitas Lokal**: Cheese ShoppeTrapsmithCharm ShoppeGeneral Store

## 🌍 Gambaran & Atmosfer Lingkungan

Dracano is a hunting location in Tribal Isles region.

It requires a minimum Rank of Knight and the Ocean Navigation Kit to access.

Inferno Havarti Cheese, which is required to attract mice here, can be crafted using various loot from Elub Shore, Nerg Plains, Derr Dunes, and Jungle of Dread.

The main goal in this location is to catch Dragon Mouse to loot a Dragon's Chest which can be opened to obtain the Zugzwang's Scarf.
