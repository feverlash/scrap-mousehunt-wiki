## Cheese Preference

Three'amat the Mother of Dragons is only attracted to Elemental Emmental.


## Hunting Strategy

The Three'amat the Mother of Dragons can only be attracted while hunting in an Elemental Dragons' Den.

Only Draconic power types are effective against Three'amat the Mother of Dragons.
All other types are ineffective.
