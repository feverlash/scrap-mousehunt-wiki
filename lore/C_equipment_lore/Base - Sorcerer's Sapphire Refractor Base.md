# 🛡️ Sorcerer's Sapphire Refractor Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 2,500 Power | 30 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Viceroy  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 21,000,000 Gold9,000 Sorcerer's Sapphire

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Taking what they learned from the two previous refractor bases, the Trapsmith at the School of Sorcery has created an extraordinary successor that combines the scientific principles of mousetrap energy refraction with the boundless powers of magic! It was made by fusing multiple Sorcerer's Sapphires together into one giant, intricately sculpted piece that is more than the sum of its parts. The enchanted energies harnessed and focussed through this gigantic, gorgeous gemstone imbue it with phenomenal mouse-catching power.
> In addition to its extraordinary mouse-catching power, this miracle of modern science has the following special effects:
> Floating Islands: Chance to add +1 to area-specific loot drops
> Bountiful Beanstalk: Doubles Fabled Fertilizer loot drops
> School of Sorcery: Adds +1 Spell Force

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Sorcerer's Sapphire Refractor Base can be purchased from the Trapsmith in School of Sorcery for:

- 21,000,000 Gold
- 9,000 Sorcerer's Sapphire

It requires the Hunter to own the Royal Ruby Refractor Base and complete the Become a Master Sorcerer adventure to purchase.

It cannot be refunded.
