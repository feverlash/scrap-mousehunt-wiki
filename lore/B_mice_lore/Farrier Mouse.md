## Hunting Strategy

Law power type is very effective against Farrier Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.

- In Claw Shot City, this mouse is available when the Hunter is working on a Thugs Gang Wanted Poster or Dirt Dealers Wanted Poster.
- In Gnawnian Express Station, this mouse is always available.


## History and Trivia
