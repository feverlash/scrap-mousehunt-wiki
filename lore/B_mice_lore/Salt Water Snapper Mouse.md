# Salt Water Snapper Mouse

The Salt Water Snapper Mouse is a breed of mouse found aboard the S.S. Huntington IV. It is not known to drop any loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Salt Water Snapper Mouse has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## History and Trivia

- 4 June 2009: The Salt Water Snapper Mouse was released with the introduction of the Rodentia region.
- 14 April 2011: The Salt Water Snapper Mouse began dropping the Antique Green Plaid Egg as loot as part of the Spring Egg Hunt 2011 event.
