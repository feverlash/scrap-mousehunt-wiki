# 🛡️ Cupcake Birthday Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 300 Power | 7 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Initiate  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 70,000

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Specially selected cupcakes, hand-picked by the Kingdom's best pastry chefs to ensure they are the most flavourful and fragrant. These cupcakes are then individually installed by the King's personal Trapsmith! A couple dozen of these delectable desserts are decorated and perfectly arranged on a titanium baking sheet for stability. Once baked, they come out deliciously dangerous!
> On top of that, this base contains a special surprise secretly added by the Head Trapsmith Chef: Cupcakes that happen to pop off the base during any scuffles turn into Cupcake Charms! Also, with the included lifetime warranty, all damaged cupcakes are covered under an immediate replacement plan, so your base is never missing one of its colourful cupcakes!
> A flavour first savoured during MouseHunt's 7th birthday!
> This base has a rare chance to drop a Cupcake Charm when a mouse is caught!

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Cupcake Birthday Base can be obtained during the annual MouseHunt Birthday event.
