## History and Trivia

- Pocketwatch Mice were introduced on 9 August 2011 with the release of the Crystal Library location.
- During the weekend event starting 26 August 2011, the Pocketwatch mouse dropped the Acrostic Research Assignment envelope as loot, which could then be opened to start the Acrostic Research Assignment.
- During the Spring Egg Hunt 2012 event, Pocketwatch mice began dropping a bonus Pocketwatch Egg as loot for hunters who had obtained the Eggscavator.
