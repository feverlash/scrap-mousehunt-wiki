# 🛡️ Dragonhide Sliver Forge Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 1,800 Power | 18 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Viceroy  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 18,000,000 gold9,000 Dragon Ashes5,000 Dragon's Breath Opal

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> By harnessing the intense heat of compressed Dragon Ashes, this miraculous base has the extraordinary ability to forge additional Dragonhide Slivers whenever a mouse drops them as loot.
> This base will double Dragonhide Sliver loot drops (Draconic Depths: Replicart™ compatible).

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Dragonhide Sliver Forge Base can be purchased from the Trapsmith in Draconic Depths for:

- 18,000,000 gold
- 9,000 Dragon Ashes
- 5,000 Dragon's Breath Opal

It requires the Hunter to complete the Vanquish the Mythical Dragon Emperor adventure to purchase.

It cannot be refunded.
