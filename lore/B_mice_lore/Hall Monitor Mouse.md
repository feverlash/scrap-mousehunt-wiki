## Cheese Preference

Hall Monitor Mouse is only attracted to Standard Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Hall Monitor Mouse can only be attracted in the Hallway while not being enrolled in any Courses.

Arcane and Shadow power type is very effective against Hall Monitor Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
