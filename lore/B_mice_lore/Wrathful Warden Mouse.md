## Cheese Preference

Wrathful Warden Mouse is only attracted to Royal Beanster Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Wrathful Warden Mouse can only be attracted after planting a Short Vine to the Dungeon Floor.

Physical power type is very effective against Wrathful Warden Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.


## Loot

Wrathful Warden Mouse can drop:

- Ancient Relic
- Lavish Lapis Bean
