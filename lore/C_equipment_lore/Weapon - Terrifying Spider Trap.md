> **Classification**: Shadow Weapon

> **Title Requirement**: Lord

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Dozens, no hundreds of tiny spiders must have created this huge cocoon, but why? Perhaps equipping it in the Haunted Terrortories or in the Seasonal Garden during Fall will reveal the reason.ORAUGH! That is a huge spider! That must have been what the massive cocoon was for! The sheer terror this incredible spider inflicts on surprised mice adds a fantastic +10 luck to your trap.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Terrifying Spider Trap is a Limited Edition weapon and can no longer be acquired by Hunters. It was not sold by any Trapsmith. It was only obtainable as loot by catching a Swamp Thang mouse during the Halloween 2012 event.
