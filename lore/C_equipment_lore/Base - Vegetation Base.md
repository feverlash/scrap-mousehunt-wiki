# 🛡️ Vegetation Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 250 Power | 5 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Legendary  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 0

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> The fertile, arable soil in this base has a magical effect when seeds land upon its surface. While hunting in the Tribal Isles, any pepper seed that drops from a mouse onto this base will instantly cause a pepper to bloom without destroying the seed!
> Use this base in the Elub Shore, Nerg Plains, or Derr Dunes to find a pepper that matches whatever seed a mouse drops.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Vegetation Base is a Limited Edition base not sold by any Trapsmith. It may only be obtained from opening a Season 7 Treasure Chest.

Each Season 7 Relic Hunter Treasure Chest has a chance of containing the Vegetation Base. The more Season 7 chests you open, the greater your chance of finding the Limited Edition base will be!
There are two Limited Edition bases in Season 7, After opening 29 chests, you're guaranteed to either 
1) find both bases in your 30th (if it is a rare chest) if you have not already found them
2) find the first base in your 30th and the second base in your 31st (if it is a normal chest) if you have not already found them.
