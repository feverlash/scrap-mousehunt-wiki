## Cheese Preference

Tranquilia Protecticus is only attracted to Elemental Emmental.


## Hunting Strategy

The Tranquilia Protecticus can only be attracted while hunting on the Surface.

Only Draconic power types are effective against Tranquilia Protecticus.
All other types are ineffective.
