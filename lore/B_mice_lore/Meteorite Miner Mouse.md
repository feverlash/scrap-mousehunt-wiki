# Meteorite Miner Mouse

The Meteorite Miner is a breed of mouse found in Fort Rox. They are known to drop Meteorite Pieces as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Meteorite Miner Mouse mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Law type traps are very effective against the Meteorite Miner Mouse. Arcane and Shadow power types are less effective.
The Meteorite Miner Mouse can only be encountered during the daytime phase in Fort Rox.

- 3 November 2016: The Meteorite Miner Mouse was release as a part of Fort Rox update.
