## Cheese Preference

Pesky Pleco Mouse is only attracted to Allegory Anari.


## Hunting Strategy

The Pesky Pleco Mouse can only be attracted while on the rapids before the waterfall in Epilogue Falls.

Hydro power type is effective against Pesky Pleco Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
