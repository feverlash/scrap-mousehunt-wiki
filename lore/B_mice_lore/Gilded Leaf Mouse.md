## Preferred Cheese

Gilded Leaf mice have been found to be especially attracted to Magical String Cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.


## Hunting Strategy

Gilded Leaf mice are weak to Rift Traps. They can only be attracted by Magical String Cheese.


## Event Participation


### Spring Egg Hunt

- The Gilded Leaf Mouse drops the Enlarged Rift Egg and the Gilded Leaf Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

- Gilded Leaf mice were introduced on 28 January 2015, with the introduction of the Whisker Woods Rift.
- Gilded Leaf mice are the Rift version of Goldleaf mice.
