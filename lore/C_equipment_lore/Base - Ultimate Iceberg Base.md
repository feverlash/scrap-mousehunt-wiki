# 🛡️ Ultimate Iceberg Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 200 Power | 5 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Lord/Lady  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 190,000 Gold

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> The ultimate base to traverse the Iceberg! No longer will you need to worry about switching bases while digging to defeat Icewing. This is a "one base fits all" solution to your previous multi-base problem!
> Increases the attraction rate of the Tunnel Rats mice
>  Prevents any backwards movement from Brute mice
> Capturing Bomb Squad mice propels you forward 20 feet
> Repels Zealot mice
> Provides the same bonus as the Deep Freeze Base

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Ultimate Iceberg Base could be purchased for 190,000 Gold from Ronza's Traveling Shoppe 2017.

It cannot be refunded.
