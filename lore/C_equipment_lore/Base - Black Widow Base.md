# 🛡️ Black Widow Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 100 Power | 10 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Novice  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 0

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> A tightly woven web of widow silk wrapped around itself, sticky enough that it can grab some bonus loot from the Black Widow Mouse when caught.
> Using this base will reward bonus loot when a Black Widow Mouse is caught.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Black Widow Base is a Limited Edition base not sold by any Trapsmith. It may only be obtained from opening a Season 6 Treasure Chest.

Each Season 6 Relic Hunter Treasure Chest has a chance of containing the Black Widow Base. The more Season 6 chests you open, the greater your chance of finding the Limited Edition base will be!
There are two Limited Edition bases in Season 6, After opening 29 chests, you're guaranteed to either 
1) find both bases in your 30th (if it is a rare chest) if you have not already found them
2) find the first base in your 30th and the second base in your 31st (if it is a normal chest) if you have not already found them.
