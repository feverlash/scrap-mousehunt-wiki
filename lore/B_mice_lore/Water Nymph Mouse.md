# Water Nymph Mouse

The Water Nymph Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Lagoon and on the S.S. Huntington IV. It is not known to drop any loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Water Nymph Mouse in the Lagoon has been found to be especially attracted to Gnarled and Wicked Gnarly cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice. It has no specific preference for any kind of cheese on the S.S. Huntington IV.


## Hunting Strategy

The Water Nymph Mouse is particularly weak to Hydro type traps.


## History and Trivia

- 22 April 2009: The Water Nymph Mouse is released with the introduction of the Lagoon location.
- 4 June 2009: The Water Nymph Mouse begins appearing aboard the S.S. Huntington IV with the introduction of the Rodentia region..
- Upon receiving a small handful of complaints from concerned parents about some perceived sexual content of the art, the images of the Water Nymph, Troll and Goblin mice were edited. The Water Nymph was deemed a little too risqué, so it was given a water sports bra and a few strategically placed water splashes.
