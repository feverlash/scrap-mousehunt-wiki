## Cheese Preference

Magnatius Majestica is only attracted to Elemental Emmental.


## Hunting Strategy

The Magnatius Majestica can only be attracted while hunting in an Elemental Dragons' Den.

Only Draconic power types are effective against Magnatius Majestica.
All other types are ineffective.
