# ⚔️ Dimensional Chest Trap

> **Classification**: Tactical Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 5,200 Power | 21 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Baron/Baroness

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> This immaculate chest is the last thing from this world that a greedy mouse will see. If an opponent ventures too closely the chest will spring to life and be upon them, opening itself to another dimension and pulling the mouse within.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Dimensional Chest Trap can be purchased from the Trapsmith in Muridae Market for:

- 1,200,000 Gold
- 175 Flameshard

It requires a Rebuild Trapsmith to purchase.

It can be refunded for 216,000 Gold at the same location.
