## History and Trivia

- 2 March 2010: The Terrible Twos Mouse was introduced as part of the MouseHunt Birthday II event.
- 5 March 2010: The Terrible Twos Mouse began to drop the Chocolate Birthday Cake Base as loot in the Meadow.
- 4 March 2014: The Terrible Twos Mouse gained a weakness to all power types, and no longer needed the use of a Parental trap or Nanny Charm to be attracted.
- 4 March 2014: During MouseHunt's Sixth Birthday capturing a Terrible Twos Mouse left an additional message in the Hunter's Journal:

> As the Terrible Twos Mouse approached my trap, I configured my trap to give the destructive mouse a time-out. I think it'll learn its lesson.
