# Malignus Vilestrom Mouse

This page contains information about recently released content.Content on this page may be incomplete or change rapidly as more information becomes available.

Malignus Vilestrom Mouse is a breed of mouse that will be found in Draconic Depths. It was released early for the area's pre-release event during Ronza's 2024 visit.
All information below is based off its cheese preference and hunting strategy during the visit.

## Cheese Preference

Malignus Vilestrom Mouse is only attracted to Poisonous Provolone.


## Hunting Strategy

Only Draconic power types are effective against Malignus Vilestrom Mouse.


## History and Trivia

- 30 July 2024: Malignus Vilestrom Mouse was released as part of a pre-release adventure of the Draconic Depths location.
- 13 August 2024: Originally weak to Hydro and attracted by Rancid Radioactive Blue Cheese, Malignus Vilestrom Mouse moved to Draconic Depths as part of the official release of the location.
