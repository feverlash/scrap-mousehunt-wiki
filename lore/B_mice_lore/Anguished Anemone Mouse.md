## Cheese Preference

Anguished Anemone Mouse is only attracted to Metaphor Manchego.


## Hunting Strategy

The Anguished Anemone Mouse can only be attracted while on the rapids before the waterfall in Epilogue Falls.

Hydro power type is effective against Anguished Anemone Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
