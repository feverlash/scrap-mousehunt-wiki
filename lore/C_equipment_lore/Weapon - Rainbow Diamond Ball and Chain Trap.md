# ⚔️ Rainbow Diamond Ball and Chain Trap

> **Classification**: Law Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 10000 Power | 30 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Viceroy  
> **Acquisition Cost**: #Obtained Via

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Despite its inexplicable ability to hover, this gigantic rainbow diamond is believed to have infinite density therefore making it the perfect trap for keeping a criminal mouse from scurrying away. By compressing a large amount of Mythical Rainbow Diamonds together into one massive ball and then attaching a shiny metal clamp and chain to it, the Trapsmith at the Conclusion Cliffs has managed to build a remarkably simple yet remarkably effective means of enforcing the law in Gnawnia.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Rainbow Diamond Ball and Chain Trap can be purchased from the Trapsmith in Conclusion Cliffs for:

- 10,000,000 gold
- 100 Mythical Rainbow Diamond

It requires the Hunter to complete the Conclude the Mythweaver's Story adventure to purchase.

It cannot be refunded.
