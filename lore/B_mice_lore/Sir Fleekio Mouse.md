# Sir Fleekio Mouse

The Sir Fleekio Mouse can be found in Zokor. It is known to drop Plates of Fealty and various Orbs.

## Preferred Cheese

The Sir Fleekio Mouse prefers Glowing Gruyere, but can be attracted with Standard Cheeses.


## Hunting Strategy

Forgotten weapons are very effective against the Sir Fleekio Mouse. Arcane weapons are of normal effectiveness.

The Sir Fleekio Mouse can be found in the Inner Fealty Temple and the Templar's Sanctum.


## Loot

- Divine Orb, Flawed Orb, Flawless Orb, Labyrinth Theme Scrap III, Plates of Fealty, and Simple Orb


## History and Trivia

> 9 September 2015: The Sir Fleekio Mouse was introduced as part of the Hollow Heights release.
The Sir Fleekio Mouse's name is a play on the word Fleek.
