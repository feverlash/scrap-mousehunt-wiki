## Cheese Preference

Constructively Critical Artist Mouse is only attracted to Master Mimolette.


## Hunting Strategy

The Constructively Critical Artist Mouse can only be attracted while enrolled in Arcane Arts or during the Arcane phase of the Final Exam.

Arcane power type is very effective against Constructively Critical Artist Mouse.
Shadow power type is effective against Constructively Critical Artist Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
