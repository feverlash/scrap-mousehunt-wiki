# Iceblade Mouse

The Iceblade Mouse is a breed of mouse found in The Mad Depths zone of the Iceberg. It is known to drop Hunting Research, Satchel of Gold, Sticky Charms, and Wax Charms as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Iceblade Mouse has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The encounter rate for the Iceblade Mouse can be decreased by arming the Hearthstone Base, Iceberg Boiler Base, or Ultimate Iceberg Base.


## History and Trivia

- 24 May 2012: The Iceblade Mouse was released as a part of the Iceberg.
- 1 June 2012: The Iceblade Mouse began to drop loot.
