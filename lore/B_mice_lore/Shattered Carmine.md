# Shattered Carmine

The Shattered Carmine is a breed of mouse that can be found in the Twisted Garden, and is known to drop Ancient Relics, Shattered Chests, and Living Garden Theme Scrap 1 as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Shattered Carmine is only attracted to Lunaria Camembert.


## Hunting Strategy

The Shattered Carmine can only be attracted with a Shattering Charm armed.

The Shattered Carmine is a member of the Living Garden mouse group. As such all power types except Hydro weapons are ineffective against it.


## History and Trivia

- 14 January 2013: The Shattered Carmine was released as a part of the Living Garden expansion.
