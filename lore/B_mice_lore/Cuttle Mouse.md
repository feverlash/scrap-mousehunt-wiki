## Cheese Preference

The Cuttle Mouse has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Cuttle Mouse is susceptible only to Hydro weapons. It can be found in the Coral Reef, Oxygen Stream, Sea Floor, and Shallow Shoals zones while on a Dive in the Sunken City.


## Event Participation


### Spring Egg Hunt

- The Cuttle Mouse drops the Bubbling Egg, the Coral Egg, the Oxygen Egg, and the Salty Sea Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

The Cuttle Mouse was released 2 July 2014 as part of the Sunken City content.
