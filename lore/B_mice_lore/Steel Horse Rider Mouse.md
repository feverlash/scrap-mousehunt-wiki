# Steel Horse Rider Mouse

The Steel Horse Rider Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Gnawnian Express Station. It can only be attracted and caught during the Raider River phase, where they are known to drop Copper Beads as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Steel Horse Rider mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Law type traps such as S.L.A.C. are very effective against the Steel Horse Rider Mouse. They are more attracted when the correct charm (Roof Rack, Door Guard, or Greasy Glob) for the current wave is armed, and do not visit during Trap Checks.


## Event Participation

- The Steel Horse Rider Mouse drops the Gnawnian Express Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

- Released on 26 September 2013 as part of the Gnawnian Express Station content.
- The name and description of the Steel Horse Rider Mouse are references to the song Wanted Dead Or Alive by the band Bon Jovi
