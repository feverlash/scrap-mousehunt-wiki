# ⚔️ Interdimensional Crossbow

> **Classification**: Shadow Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 4,500 Power | 18 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Baron/Baroness  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 5,360,000 Gold10 Silver Bolt25 Bloodstone100 Howlite

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> These finely-tuned crossbows create an interdimensional network between all other Interdimensional Crossbow Traps in existence.
> In synchronization with each other, once one of the traps detects a mouse, each of the other traps opens a portal to the offending location so that they may aid by opening fire upon the same target at once!
> It makes you wonder why all traps aren't made this way!

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Interdimensional Crossbow can be purchased from the Trapsmith in Fort Rox for:

- 5,360,000 Gold
- 10 Silver Bolt
- 25 Bloodstone
- 100 Howlite

It cannot be refunded.

The Interdimensional Crossbow (if unintentionally smashed) can be reassembled from Interdimensional Crossbow Trap Parts.
