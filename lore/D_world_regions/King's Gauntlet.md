# 📍 King's Gauntlet

> **Wilayah / Region**: Valour  
> **Pangkat Minimal**: Hero  
> **Fasilitas Lokal**: Cheese ShoppeTrapsmithGeneral StoreCartographer

## 🌍 Gambaran & Atmosfer Lingkungan

King's Gauntlet is a hunting location in Valour region.
It requires the minimum rank of Hero and the King's Gauntlet Floorplan to access.

Mice here can be divided into 8 sub-groups, or tiers, each requires a specific type of Gauntlet cheese and drops the Potion to brew the next tier cheese.
