## Preferred Cheese

The Wind Watcher Mouse has been found to be especially attracted to Windy Cheese cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.

## Hunting Strategy

Only Shadow, Arcane and Draconic type traps are effective against the Wind Watcher Mouse, all other power types are ineffective.

The Wind Watcher Mouse is only available between 0%-34% wind intensities in Moussu Picchu.


## History and Trivia
