## Preferred Cheese

The Decrepit Tentacle Terror prefers Glowing Gruyere, but can be attracted with Standard Cheeses.


## Hunting Strategy

Shadow and Forgotten weapons are very effective against the Decrepit Tentacle Terror. Arcane weapons are of normal effectiveness.

The Decrepit Tentacle Terror can be found in the Lair of the Minotaur and is the only mouse in Zokor that is weak to Shadow weapons..


## History and Trivia

> 9 September 2015: The Decrepit Tentacle Terror was introduced as part of the Hollow Heights release.
