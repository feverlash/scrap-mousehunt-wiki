The Thundering Watcher Mouse has been found to be especially attracted to Dragonvine Cheese cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.

## Hunting Strategy

Only Draconic type traps are effective against the Thundering Watcher Mouse, all other power types are ineffective.

Dragonbane Charms, Super Dragonbane Charms and Extreme Dragonbane Charms provide a power bonus against the Thundering Watcher Mouse.

The Thundering Watcher Mouse is only available when both wind and rain intensities are at least between 35%-99% in Moussu Picchu.
