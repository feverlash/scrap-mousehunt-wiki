# Werehauler Mouse

The Werehauler is a breed of mouse found in Fort Rox. They are known to drop Howlite and Meteorite Pieces as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Werehauler Mouse have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Shadow traps are very effective against the Werehauler Mouse. Arcane traps are of normal effectiveness.
The Werehauler Mouse can only be encountered during the Twilight, Midnight, Pitch, and Utter Darkness phases at Nighttime in Fort Rox.
