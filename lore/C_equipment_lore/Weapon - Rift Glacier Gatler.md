## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Win every snowball fight across dimensions with the Rift Glacial Gatler! The most high-tech snowball-blasting machine there is!
> Now, don't let the number of barrels fool you! These are state-of-the-art, A.I.-driven, Rocket-Propelled Mouse-Tracking Barrels! Quite the modern marvel in snowball fighting and mouse catching fusion technology!
> Once approached, the turrets hum to life and launch from the gatler platform, using an A.I.-powered tracking technology derived from attack and dispersal patterns learned from thousands of snowball fights. Each turret can independently predict a mouse's path and fire at its anticipated position. If the prediction is off for any given turret, the 3 others pick up the slack and learn from its mistakes!
> The concentrated atmosphere of the Rift adds to the already tried, tested and true mist-condensing technology, providing the chance to create a Rift Snowball Charm upon successfully capturing a mouse.
