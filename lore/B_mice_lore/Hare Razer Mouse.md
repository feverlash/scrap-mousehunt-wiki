## History and Trivia

- 14 April 2011: The Hare Razer Mouse was released as part of the Spring Egg Hunt 2011 celebration.
- The Hare Razer Mouse's name is a play on the phrase "Hair Raiser".
- The Hare Razer Mouse's description has changed twice since its release.

> Original version
While he's known as Hare Razer to his minions, he likes to refer to himself as Mr. Fluffy. This is likely to be one of the most insane mice you'll ever meet (most figure it's due to his diet consisting almost entirely of chemically treated candies), however during his rare bouts of lucidity, he manages to run a very effective cartel, smuggling prized hunter items out in tiny delicious but near impenetrable chocolate eggs. If you manage to acquire his mallet the "Rocket Rabbit" you might just have the key to unlocking these eggs.
