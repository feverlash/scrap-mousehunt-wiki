## Cheese Preference

The Cloud Miner Mouse is only attracted to SUPER|brie+.


## Hunting Strategy

All traps except Rift and Parental traps are effective against the Cloud Miner Mouse, all other power types are ineffective. The Cloud Miner Mouse can only be encountered on the Launch Pad.


## History and Trivia

- 08 September 2020: The Cloud Miner Mouse was introduced with the release of the Floating Islands.
