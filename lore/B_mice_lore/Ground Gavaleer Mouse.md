# Ground Gavaleer Mouse

The Ground Gavaleer Mouse is a breed of mouse found on the Floating Islands, on a Physical Island or a Physical Palisade.

## Cheese Preference

The Ground Gavaleer Mouse is attracted to Cloud Cheesecake.


## Hunting Strategy

Only Physical traps are effective against the Ground Gavaleer Mouse, all other power types are ineffective. The Ground Gavaleer Mouse can only be encountered on a Physical Island or a Physical Palisade.


## Loot


### Floating Islands's Island Based Loot

When found on a Low Altitude Island or a High Altitude Island, depending on the buffs present, the Ground Gavaleer Mouse can drop the following loot:


| Item | Condition |
| --- | --- |

| Sky Ore | Always drops Boosted by Sky Ore Deposit and Loot Caches |

| Corsair's Curd | Drops when a Pirate Den is present Can be boosted by Loot Caches |
