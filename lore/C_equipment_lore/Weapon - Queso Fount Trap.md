# ⚔️ Queso Fount Trap

> **Classification**: Hydro Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 11,000 Power | 25 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Count/Countess  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 12,000,000 Gold500 Solidified Amber Queso75 Nachore3 Queso Thermal Spring

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> A bubbling fount of molten queso which is superheated faster than it can vaporize. The surface tension is so high that even the slightest vibration can trigger an explosion! The specially crafted spouts focus the eruption towards the placement of the cheese on the trap hoping to cover any mice that wander too close.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Queso Fount Trap can be purchased from the Trapsmith in Queso Geyser for:

- 12,000,000 Gold
- 500 Solidified Amber Queso
- 75 Nachore
- 3 Queso Thermal Spring

It requires that the Hunter has completed the Slay the Geyser Dragon adventure prior to purchase.

It cannot be refunded.
