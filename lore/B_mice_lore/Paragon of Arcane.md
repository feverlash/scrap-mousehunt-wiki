## Island Info

- To encounter the Paragon of Arcane, the Hunter must pick an Arcane Keep on the Sky Map.
- After catching the Paragon of Arcane, the Arcane Keep will not be available on subsequent Sky Maps until ​the Hunter has defeated all 4 Sky Wardens.


## Loot

- Depending on the buff present, the Paragon of Arcane will drop the corresponding High Altitude Loot:


| Buff | High Altitude Loot |

## History and Trivia
