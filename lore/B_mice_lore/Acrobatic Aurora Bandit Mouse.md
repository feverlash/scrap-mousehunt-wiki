## Cheese Preference

Acrobatic Aurora Bandit Mouse is only attracted to Sky Pirate Swiss or Sky Raider Romano.


## Hunting Strategy

The Acrobatic Aurora Bandit Mouse can only be attracted during a Spice shipment.

Law power type is effective against Acrobatic Aurora Bandit Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
