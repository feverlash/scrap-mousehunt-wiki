# 🛡️ Clockwork Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 800 Power | 13 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Grand Duke/Grand Duchess  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 8,400,000 Gold25 Clockwork Cog1 Timesplit Rune

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Clicking gears, zinging sprockets and a low hum can be heard when this base whirls to life. A timeless timepiece meticulously hand-crafted and truly a proud addition to any hunter's collection.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Clockwork Base can be purchased from the Trapsmith in Bristle Woods Rift for:

- 8,400,000 Gold
- 25 Clockwork Cog
- 1 Timesplit Rune

It requires the Hunter to own a Fissure Base to purchase.

It cannot be refunded.
