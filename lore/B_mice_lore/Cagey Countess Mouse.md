## Cheese Preference

Cagey Countess Mouse is only attracted to Beanster Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Cagey Countess Mouse can only be attracted after planting a Tall Vine to the Great Hall Floor.

Physical power type is effective against Cagey Countess Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.


## Loot

Cagey Countess Mouse can drop:

- Golden Goose Egg
