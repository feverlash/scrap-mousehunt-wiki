## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Rockstar Mouse is attracted only to Rockforth Cheese.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Rockstar Mouse.

More information on how to locate and catch the Rockstar Mouse can be found on the current Ronza location page.

## History and Trivia

- Unknown Date after March 2008: The Rockstar Mouse replaced the iTunes Mouse which was released in March 2008 before it permanently retired a few days later.
- 14 August 2009: The Rockstar Mouse was released, using the same MouseHunt ID number (16) and a variation of the description of the iTunes Mouse. The Rockstar Mouse dropped Ronza's Satchel and SUPER|brie+. The color of the Satchel (blue, green, or red) was specific to the location in which the Rockstar Mouse was caught, with those from areas accessible only to higher ranks containing more valuable rewards.
- 12 January 2011: The Rockstar Mouse appeared for the first time in Ronza's Traveling Shoppe where it dropped Attraction Charm, Luck Charm, and Power Charm as loot.
