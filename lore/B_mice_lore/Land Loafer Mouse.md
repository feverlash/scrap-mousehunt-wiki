| Seed collecting on the Foreword Farm can be tough, back-breaking work and it takes a mouse with real gumption to get the job done day in and day out. This mouse, on the other hand, is the exception to that rule. The Land Loafer is a professional layabout, well-versed in the art of finding just the right mound to lean on and just the right angle to tip its hat to block out the sun for a not-so-well-deserved nap. Lackadaisical as it may be, this clever mouse still manages to get the job done each day and somehow finds a way to collect even more Papyrus Seeds than many of its co-workers with only a fraction of the effort. |  |  |  |

## Cheese Preference

Land Loafer Mouse can be attracted with any Standard Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Land Loafer Mouse is only available when no plant is growing.

Tactical power type is effective against Land Loafer Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.


## Loot

Land Loafer Mouse can drop:

- Papyrus Seed
