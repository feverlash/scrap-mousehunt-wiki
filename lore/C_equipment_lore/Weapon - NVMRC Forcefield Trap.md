# ⚔️ NVMRC Forcefield Trap

> **Classification**: Physical Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 2,350 Power | 12 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Journeyman/Journeywoman  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 180,000 Gold

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> This trap generates a force field tough enough to hold even the strongest mice within. Once trapped, the mouse is harmlessly incapacitated with 100% organic sleeping gas and relocated to a mouse reservation.

- Town of Gnawnia
- Windmill

- Calm Clearing
- Great Gnarled Tree
