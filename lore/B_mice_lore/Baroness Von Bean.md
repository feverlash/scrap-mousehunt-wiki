## Cheese Preference

Baroness Von Bean is only attracted to Beanster Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Baroness Von Bean can only be attracted after planting a Tall Vine to the Great Hall Floor.

Physical power type is effective against Baroness Von Bean.
All other types are ineffective.


## Loot

Baroness Von Bean can drop:

- Golden Goose Egg
