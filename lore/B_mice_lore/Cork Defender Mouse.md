## Cheese Preference

The Cork Defender Mouse is only attracted to Mild Queso, Medium Queso, Hot Queso or Flamin' Queso.


## Hunting Strategy

Only Draconic traps are effective against the Cork Defender Mouse, all other power types are ineffective. The Cork Defender Mouse can only be encountered in Queso Geyser while gathering Cork Bark.

Dragonbane Charm, Super Dragonbane Charm, Extreme Dragonbane Charm, and Ultimate Dragonbane Charm provide a power bonus against the Cork Defender Mouse.


## History and Trivia

- 30 April 2019: The Cork Defender Mouse was introduced, with the release of the Queso Geyser area.
