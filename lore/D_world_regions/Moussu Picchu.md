# 📍 Moussu Picchu

> **Wilayah / Region**: Hollow Heights  
> **Pangkat Minimal**: Recruit  
> **Fasilitas Lokal**: Cheese ShoppeTrapsmithCharm ShoppeGeneral StoreCartographer

## 🌍 Gambaran & Atmosfer Lingkungan

Moussu Picchu is a hunting location in Hollow Heights region.
It requires the rank of Grand Duke/Grand Duchess and the Moussu Picchu Climbing Route to access.

In this location, the Weather determines the attraction pool when using Windy, Rainy, and Dragonvine cheeses.

Wind Mice drop Arcanevine. Rain Mice drop Shadowvine. Those 2 loot can be combined to make Dragonvine.
Dragonvine Mice drop Dragon Scale needed to purchase most shop items.
