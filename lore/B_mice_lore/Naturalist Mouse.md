## Preferred Cheese

Naturalist mice have been found to be especially attracted to String Cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice. They also will not appear unless a Gnarled Charm is armed.


## Hunting Strategy

The Naturalist Mouse is weak to Rift Traps. The Naturalist Mouse can only be attracted by String Cheese and Gnarled Charm. Catching the Naturalist Mouse will award 6 Rage towards the Gigantic Gnarled Tree section of the Whisker Woods Rift.

The Naturalist Mouse is available at any rage in Whisker Woods Rift.


## Event Participation


### Spring Egg Hunt

- The Naturalist Mouse drops the Enlarged Rift Egg and the Naturalist Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

- Naturalist mice were introduced on 28 January 2015, with the introduction of the Whisker Woods Rift.
- Naturalist mice are the Rift version of Curious Chemist mice.
