## Cheese Preference

Ancient Wisdom Keeper is only attracted to Symbolic Sirene.


## Hunting Strategy

The Ancient Wisdom Keeper can only be attracted in the hidden grotto of Epilogue Falls.

Hydro power type is effective against Ancient Wisdom Keeper.
All other types are ineffective.
