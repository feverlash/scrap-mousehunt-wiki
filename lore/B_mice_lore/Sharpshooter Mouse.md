# Sharpshooter Mouse

The Sharpshooter Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Gnawnian Express Station. It can only be attracted and caught during the Raider River phase, where they are known to drop Copper Beads as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Sharpshooter mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Law type traps such as S.L.A.C. are very effective against the Sharpshooter Mouse. They are more attracted when the correct charm (Roof Rack, Door Guard, or Greasy Glob) for the current wave is armed, and do not visit during Trap Checks.


## Event Participation


### Spring Egg Hunt

- The Sharpshooter Mouse drops the Gnawnian Express Egg and Sharpshooter Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.
