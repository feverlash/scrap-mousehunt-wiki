# Spring Familiar Mouse

The Spring Familiar Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Rodentia region. It can be attracted and caught only during the Spring Season.

## Preferred Cheese

Spring Familiar mice have been found especially attracted to SUPER|Brie+ cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.


## Hunting Strategy

Strong Physical weapons are recommended in order to catch this mouse. Alternatively, hunters may equip their best Tactical trap.


## History and Trivia

- Spring Familiar Mice were introduced on 17 August 2010 with the release of the Seasonal Garden location.
- Spring Familiar Mice began dropping Sandtail Call to Arms on 22 March 2011 with the release of the Fiery Warpath location.
- On 29 July 2015 they stopped dropping the Sandtail Call to Arms as loot with the introduction of the Adventure Book.
