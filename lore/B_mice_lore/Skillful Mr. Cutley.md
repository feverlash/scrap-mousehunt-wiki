## Cheese Preference

Skillful Mr. Cutley is only attracted to Storied Saga.


## Hunting Strategy

The Skillful Mr. Cutley can only be attracted while writing a story.

Only Law power types are effective against Skillful Mr. Cutley.
All other types are ineffective.
