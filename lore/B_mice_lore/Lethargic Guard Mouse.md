## Cheese Preference

Lethargic Guard Mouse is only attracted to Beanster Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Lethargic Guard Mouse can only be attracted after planting a Short Vine to the Dungeon Floor.

Physical power type is effective against Lethargic Guard Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.


## Loot

Lethargic Guard Mouse can drop:

- Lavish Lapis Bean
