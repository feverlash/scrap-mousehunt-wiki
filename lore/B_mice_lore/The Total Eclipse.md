## Floor Type Info

- The Total Eclipse can only be encountered after entering the Valour Rift Tower with the Ultimate Umbra augmentation active.
- The Total Eclipse can be found on Eclipse floors.
Catching The Total Eclipse will extend the duration the Hunter has in the Valour Rift tower - this boost will depend on the Hunter's Siphon level.
- The Total Eclipse has a Minimum Floor of 8.
