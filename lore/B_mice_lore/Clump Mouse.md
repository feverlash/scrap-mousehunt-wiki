# Clump Mouse

The Clump Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Burroughs Rift. It is known to drop Polluted Parmesan Potions as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Clump Mouse is only attracted to Terre Ricotta Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Clump Mouse can be attracted when the Mist Meter is in Yellow and Green Zone.

Rift power type is effective against Clump Mouse while all others are less effective.
