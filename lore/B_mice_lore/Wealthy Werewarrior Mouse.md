# Wealthy Werewarrior Mouse

The Wealthy Werewarrior is a breed of mouse found in Fort Rox. They are known to drop Bloodstone, Flawless Orbs, Fool's Gold, Meteorite Pieces, Simple Orbs and Tower Mana as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

While the Wealthy Werewarrior Mouse can be attracted by standard cheese, it has a distinct preference for Moon Cheese or Crescent Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Shadow traps are very effective against the Wealthy Werewarrior Mouse. Arcane traps are of normal effectiveness.
The Wealthy Werewarrior Mouse can only be encountered during the Midnight, Pitch, and Utter Darkness phases at Nighttime in Fort Rox.
