# 📍 Floating Islands

> **Wilayah / Region**: Hollow Heights  
> **Pangkat Minimal**: Archduke/Archduchess  
> **Fasilitas Lokal**: Cheese ShoppeTrapsmithCharm ShoppeGeneral StoreCartographer

## 🌍 Gambaran & Atmosfer Lingkungan

Floating Islands is a Location located within the Hollow Heights Region, requiring a minimum Rank of Archduke/Archduchess and the Dirigible and Flight License Map Piece to access.

Floating Islands makes use of 8 different Power Types, nearly every available one in the game, namely:

- Arcane
- Forgotten
- Hydro
- Shadow
- Draconic
- Law
- Physical
- Tactical

Mice here are able to pillage Standard Cheese, Points and Gold if one fails to capture them.

Hunters first collect Cloud Curd to purchase Cloud Cheesecake while at the Launch Pad. All 8 Power Types are effective here.
Subsequently, Hunters fly towards specific Power Type Islands to hunt mice using their Sky Map.
There are 2 versions of each Island, Low Altitude and High Altitude: Hunters must first clear a minimum of 4 Low Altitude Islands and defeat 4 different Wardens to unlock a single High Altitude Island.
While hunting on the islands, mice will drop loot used to upgrade your Skyfarer's Oculus, customise your Dirigible and obtain new Traps.

When Hunters have completed the Explore the Floating Islands Adventure, they can proceed to purchase the High Altitude Flight License from the Cartographer.
Together with 500 Sky Glass and 500 Sky Ore, Hunters can then attach Rocket Boosters onto their Dirigible.
These boosters enable Paragons, Pirates and Richard the Rich in both Low Altitude and High Altitude islands to drop Storm Cells.
Once the Hunter has collected 50 Storm Cells, they can be spent on the Launch Console while at the Launch Pad to journey to the Empyrean Sky Palace instead of a regular island.
The Empyrean Sky Palace consists of different Power Type Vaults which have mice that drop greater amounts of loot as compared to those obtained on regular islands.

> "Welcome to the Floating Islands where you can finally see the world from Ronza's perspective!

Now that you've got your flight license, the sky is the limit! The King has commissioned the construction of this luxurious Launch Pad (it took a lot of tariffs to pull this one off!) as a home for hunters who are taking to the skies to capture the mice above the clouds!

Here you will find a new type of building: the Workshop. It can be used to upgrade your Skyfarer's Oculus and customize your dirigible. You can also find the usual assortment of shops which will be crucial in providing much needed bait, trap upgrades, and some special supplies for sailing the skies!

The Cheese Shoppe has been stocked with a delicious new cheesy treat that is so light and fluffy that it defies the laws of physics! I suggest loading up on at least 75 pieces for your first trip to the islands.

The Charm Shoppe has some pretty powerful charms for sale so it's worth a visit for the veteran Floating Islander.

The Trapsmith should be one of your first stops so you can see what new wonders of the mouse catching world are available for purchase as well as what resources they require from the various islands above.

Now, before you go rushing off to hit that shiny new "Start" button, let me share the intel we've gathered so far about how the islands seem to work.

Upon opening your Sky Map, you will see the current layout of islands available to you as well as Markers indicating unique spots that are located on each one. Each island is also home to a specific group of mice that are weak to the power type indicated on the axis of the Sky Map grid. Remember, the winds of change are constantly blowing, after each island you explore, the Sky Map will change and a new set of islands will appear so don't take this decision lightly!

An island with a shrine on it will also be home to one of the four Wardens. These Wardens prevent travel to the High Altitude islands which are home to the most powerful mice and rarest resources. You will need to capture all four Wardens before your Sky Map will reveal the High Altitude islands. Make sure to gather as many resources as possible along the way so that you will be well prepared to battle the powerful guardians of the High Altitude islands: the Paragons!

After selecting an island and pressing "Launch", you will set sail in your dirigible and land at your destination. Upon landing, make sure to adjust your trap setup to the power type of that island. To make this easier, your dirigible has been equipped with a brand new Trap Library that you can use to save your complete setup for each power type. Once you've saved it, that setup will equip automatically every time you visit an island with that power type! This fancy new feature should help to make your island voyages much smoother! Once you've landed and equipped your setup, you can begin exploring these miraculous places! Be careful though, you only have 75 hunts before the island floats too far away from the Launch Pad thus preventing a safe return. If you exceed this limit, your crew will automatically pack everything up and take you back to the Launch Pad. So, make every hunt count!

Each successful catch on the island will progress your exploration bar but each time you fail to catch, the enemy mice on the other side of the island will make progress towards you!
If you are successful enough to enter a special area of the island, you will immediately gain its bonus for the remainder of your hunts. However, the mice are exploring it as well but they are looking for you! If you meet up with that enemy hoard and there is a shrine on the island, it will be the Warden that leads the charge! Once you meet a Warden, you won't be able to attract any other mice until you either catch it or are forced off the island!

Fully exploring an area of the island will fortify its location which means the opposing mice cannot push you back any further.

Well, I guess that's it -- oh wait, I almost forgot! There have been reports of Sky Pirates roaming the skies in this region so be extra vigilant in your expeditions!

The rest is up to you. Good luck, hunter!

Wow... what a view! To think, I almost missed out on this due to my crippling fear of heights!"
