# Barracuda Mouse

The Barracuda Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Sunken City. It is known to drop Brined Curd as loot

## Cheese Preference

The Barracuda Mouse has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Barracuda Mouse is susceptible only to Hydro weapons. It can be found in the Feeding Grounds zone while on a Dive in the Sunken City.


## Event Participation

- The Barracuda Mouse drops the Underwater Predator Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

The Barracuda Mouse was released 2 July 2014 as part of the Sunken City content.
