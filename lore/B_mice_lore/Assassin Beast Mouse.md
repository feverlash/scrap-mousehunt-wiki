# Assassin Beast Mouse

The Assassin Beast Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Burroughs Rift. It is known to drop Rift Circuitry as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Assassin Beast Mouse is only attracted to Polluted Parmesan.


## Hunting Strategy

Assassin Beast Mouse can only be attracted when the Mist Meter is in Red Zone.

Rift power type is effective against Assassin Beast Mouse while all others are less effective.


## History and Trivia

- 9 September 2014: The Assassin Beast Mouse was released as part of the Burroughs Rift location.
