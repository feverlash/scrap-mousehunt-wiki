| These two hardworking brothers have dedicated their lives to collecting, cataloguing, and interpreting much of what the mice of today know as the staples of murine folklore. Their most famous works include: "Little Red Riding Mouse", "The Rat Prince", "Mouserella", and "Rodentstiltskin". ## Cheese Preference

Brothers Grimmaus is only attracted to Standard Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Brothers Grimmaus is only available when the stage is Not writing.

Forgotten power type is effective against Brothers Grimmaus.
All other types are ineffective.


## Loot

Brothers Grimmaus can drop:

- Draft Derby Curd

With Leather Bound Cover, the following loot is added to the pool:

- Cleverness Clam
- Crop Coin
- Ingenuity Grub
- Inspiration Ink
- Mythical Mulch
- Pond Penny
- Papyrus Seed
- Parable Papyrus
