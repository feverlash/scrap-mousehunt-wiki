## Cheese Preference

Cheat Sheet Conjurer Mouse is only attracted to Standard Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Cheat Sheet Conjurer Mouse can only be attracted during the Shadow phase of the Final Exam.

Shadow power type is very effective against Cheat Sheet Conjurer Mouse.
Arcane power type is effective against Cheat Sheet Conjurer Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
