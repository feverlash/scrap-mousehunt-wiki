# 🛡️ Dragon's Breath Opal Refractor Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 3,000 Power | 35 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Viceroy  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 24,000,000 gold1 Mythical Dragon Heart20,000 Dragon's Breath Opal

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Extraordinary advancements in energy refraction technology have allowed the Trapsmith at the Draconic Depths to create this exceptional new iteration in the line of refractor bases. By harnessing the enormous energy of the Mythical Dragon Heart and focussing it through one massive Dragon's Breath Opal, this base is capable of delivering a power so tremendous and terrifying that it rivals even the mightiest of dragons.
> In addition to its extraordinary mouse-catching power, this miracle of modern science has the following special effects:
> Floating Islands: Chance to add +1 to area-specific loot drops
> Bountiful Beanstalk: Doubles Fabled Fertilizer loot drops
> School of Sorcery: Adds +1 Spell Force
> Doubles Dragonhide Sliver loot drops (Draconic Depths: Replicart™ compatible!)

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Dragon's Breath Opal Refractor Base can be purchased from the Trapsmith in Draconic Depths for:

- 24,000,000 gold
- 1 Mythical Dragon Heart
- 20,000 Dragon's Breath Opal

It requires the Hunter to own the Sorcerer's Sapphire Refractor Base and complete the Vanquish the Mythical Dragon Emperor adventure to purchase.

It cannot be refunded.
