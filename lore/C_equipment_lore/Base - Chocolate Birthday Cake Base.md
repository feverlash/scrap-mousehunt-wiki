# 🛡️ Chocolate Birthday Cake Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 175 Power | 1 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Apprentice  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 13,000

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Three of Gnawnia's finest bakers collaborated to produce this amazing cake, which also doubles as a decorative base. With double chocolate icing, three layers of triple chocolate cake, and an astounding, super rich filling of quadruple chocolate fudge delight, this is one of the most chocolate-y bases ever! This cake is tasty and filling for hunters, but if a mouse takes more than a few bites, it will lapse into a sugary coma.
> A flavour first savoured during MouseHunt's 2nd birthday!

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Chocolate Birthday Cake Base can be obtained during the annual MouseHunt Birthday event.
