## Preferred Cheese

The Rainmancer Mouse has been found to be especially attracted to Rainy Cheese cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.

## Hunting Strategy

Only Shadow, Arcane and Draconic type traps are effective against the Rainmancer Mouse, all other power types are ineffective.

The Rainmancer Mouse is only available between 80%-100% rain intensities in Moussu Picchu. It is the only mouse available at 100% rain intensity.
