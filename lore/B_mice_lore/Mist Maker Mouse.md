The Mist Maker Mouse is a breed of mouse found on the Floating Islands, on a Hydro Hideaway.

## Cheese Preference

The Mist Maker Mouse is only attracted to Cloud Cheesecake.


## Hunting Strategy

Only Hydro traps are effective against the Mist Maker Mouse, all other power types are ineffective. The Mist Maker Mouse can only be encountered on a Hydro Hideaway.


## Loot


### Floating Islands's Island Based Loot

When found on a Low Altitude Island or a High Altitude Island, depending on the buffs present, the Mist Maker Mouse can drop the following loot:


| Item | Condition |
