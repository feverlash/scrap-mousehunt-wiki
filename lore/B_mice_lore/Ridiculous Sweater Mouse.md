## History and Trivia

- 19 December 2012: The Ridiculous Sweater Mouse was released as part of the Great Winter Snowball Fight event.
- The image of the Ridiculous Sweater Mouse includes a sweater with a picture of a cat saying "MEOW!" and a tag that says "LOVE, GRANDMA".
- 13 December 2016: The points/gold for Ridiculous Sweater Mouse were changed.
