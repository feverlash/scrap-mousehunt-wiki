# Leviathan Mouse

> "Leviathan" redirects here. For other uses, see The Leviathan Skin.

The Leviathan Mouse is a breed of mouse found on the ship the S.S. Huntington IV. They are known to drop Simple Orbs as loot. They are also known to drop Mesh Netting as loot, but only while hunters are in the process of completing the Zurreal Trap Research Assignment.
