## Cheese Preference

The Captain Cloudkicker is solely attracted to Cloud Cheesecake.


## Hunting Strategy

Only Tactical traps are effective against the Captain Cloudkicker, all other power types are ineffective. The Captain Cloudkicker can only be encountered on a Tactical Castle.


## Loot


### Floating Islands's Island Based Loot

When found on a Low Altitude Island or a High Altitude Island, depending on the buffs present, the Captain Cloudkicker can drop the following loot:


| Item | Condition |

| Corsair's Curd | Drops when a Pirate Den is present Can be boosted by Loot Caches |
