# Rancid Bog Beast Mouse

The Rancid Bog Beast Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Burroughs Rift. It is not known to drop any loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Rancid Bog Beast Mouse is only attracted to Polluted Parmesan Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Rancid Bog Beast Mouse can be attracted when the Mist Meter is in Green and Red Zone.

Rift power type is effective against Rancid Bog Beast Mouse while all others are less effective.


## History and Trivia

- 9 September 2014: The Rancid Bog Beast Mouse was released as part of the Burroughs Rift location.
