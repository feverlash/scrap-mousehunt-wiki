# Cannonball Mouse

> "Cannonball" redirects here. For other uses, see Cannonball (disambiguation).

The Cannonball Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Gnawnian Express Station. It can only be attracted and caught during the Raider River phase of a train journey. They are known to drop Copper Beads and Raider-B-Gone Mouse Repellents as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Cannonball mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Law type traps such as S.L.A.C. are very effective against the Cannonball Mouse. While an Automice encounter is guaranteed during a Trap Check, it is also possible to attract this mouse during normal hunting.


## Event Participation

- The Cannonball Mouse drops the Gnawnian Express Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

- Released on 26 September 2013 as part of the Gnawnian Express Station content.
