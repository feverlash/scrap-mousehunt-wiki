## Preferred Cheese

Arcane Summoner Mouse can be attracted by any Standard Cheese as well as Crescent Cheese and Moon Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Arcane Summoner Mouse is available during 4 sub-phases of Night:

- Midnight
- Pitch
- Utter Darkness
- First Light

Arcane power type is very effective against this mouse. Shadow type is of normal effectiveness.
All other types are ineffective.


## History and Trivia

- 3 November 2016: Arcane Summoner Mouse was release as a part of Fort Rox location.
- 15 November 2022: Arcane Summoner Mouse started dropping Tower Mana with the New Dawn balance update to Fort Rox.
