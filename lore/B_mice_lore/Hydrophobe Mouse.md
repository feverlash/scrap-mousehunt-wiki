# Hydrophobe Mouse

The Hydrophobe Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Rodentia region. It can be attracted and caught only during the Spring Season. They are not known to drop any loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Hydrophobe Mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Strong Physical weapons are recommended in order to catch this mouse. Alternatively, hunters may equip their best Tactical trap.


## History and Trivia

- Hydrophobe Mice were introduced on 17 August 2010 with the release of the Seasonal Garden location.
