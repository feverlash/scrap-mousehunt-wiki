# Hypnotized Gunslinger Mouse

The Hypnotized Gunslinger Mouse is a breed of mouse found in Fort Rox. They are known to drop Bloodstone, Fool's Gold, and Howlite as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Hypnotized Gunslinger Mouse have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Arcane traps are very effective against the Hypnotized Gunslinger Mouse. Shadow traps are of normal effectiveness.
The Hypnotized Gunslinger Mouse can only be encountered during the Midnight, Pitch, Utter Darkness, and First Light phases at Nighttime in Fort Rox.


## History and Trivia

- 3 November 2016: The Hypnotized Gunslinger Mouse was release as a part of Fort Rox update.
