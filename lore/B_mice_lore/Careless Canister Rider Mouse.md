## Cheese Preference

Careless Canister Rider Mouse is only attracted to Sky Pirate Swiss or Sky Raider Romano.


## Hunting Strategy

The Careless Canister Rider Mouse can only be attracted during an Atmospheric shipment.

Law power type is effective against Careless Canister Rider Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
