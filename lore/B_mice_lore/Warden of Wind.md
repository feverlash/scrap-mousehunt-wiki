| This swirling tempest of unknown origin believes that no earthbound mortal is worthy to soar among his winds. He approaches airships with a subtle breeze and gently guides them into his center without their knowledge. Once the ship is within his grasp, this turbulent terror rends it apart from bow to stern with punishing gale-force gusts and a flurry of cyclones. He is relentless in his pursuit of trespassers and as such, none have survived his blustering assaults with their airships intact. |  |  |  |

## Island Info

- To encounter the Warden of Wind, the Hunter must pick a Low Altitude Island with a Shrine of Wind present on the Sky Map.
- After catching the Warden of Wind, the Shrine of Wind will not be available on subsequent Sky Maps until ​the Hunter has left a High Altitude Island.
