# Monstrous Abomination Mouse

The Monstrous Abomination Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Burroughs Rift. It is known to drop Burroughs Rift Theme Scrap II and Rift Circuitry as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Monstrous Abomination Mouse can be attracted using any Standard String Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Monstrous Abomination Mouse can only be attracted when the Mist Meter is in Red Zone.

Rift power type is effective against Monstrous Abomination Mouse while all others are less effective.


## History and Trivia

- 9 September 2014: The Monstrous Abomination Mouse was released as part of the Burroughs Rift location.
