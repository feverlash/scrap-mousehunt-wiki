# Serpentine Mouse

The Serpentine Mouse is a breed of mouse that can be found in the Sand Crypts, and is known to drop Aleth Essences, Ber Essences, Cynd Essences and Lunaria Petals as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Serpentine mice have been found to be only attracted to Graveblossom Camembert.


## Hunting Strategy

The Serpentine mouse is a member of the Sand Dunes mouse group. As such all power types except Shadow weapons are ineffective against it.


## History and Trivia

- 14 November 2012: The Serpentine Mouse was released as a part of the Living Garden update.
