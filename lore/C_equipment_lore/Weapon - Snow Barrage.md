# ⚔️ Snow Barrage

> **Classification**: Tactical Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 1,450 Power | 28 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Initiate  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 140,600

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Looks can be deceiving. Many mice fail to even recognize that a snowman could be a trap! Mice who make this mistake and underestimate the power of this bazooka's 3-snowball-per-second firing rate will very quickly find themselves buried in a pile of their own yellow snow. The King has allowed this weapon to be sold for a limited time only.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Snow Barrage is a Limited Edition weapon and can no longer be acquired by Hunters. It could be purchased for 140,600 gold from Ronza's Traveling Shoppe during her visit before Christmas 2008. It refunded for 25,308 gold at the same location.
