# 🛡️ Enerchi Induction Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 100 Power | 10 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Grand Duke/Grand Duchess  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 2,399,000 Gold1 Rift Circuitry1 Chi Claw Heirloom1 Chi Fang Heirloom1 Chi Belt Heirloom

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> This base is comprised of a system of inductive power coils that convert mystical Enerchi directly into usable power via the process of induction. Through a complicated system of coils, magnetic fields and some transformers for good measure, it uses Enerchi to create even more Enerchi!
> This base will generate 2 Enerchi whenever a mouse drops Enerchi as loot or when some is generated from an Enerchi Charm.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Enerchi Induction Base can be purchased from the Trapsmith in Furoma Rift for

- 2,399,000 Gold
- 1 Rift Circuitry
- 1 Chi Claw Heirloom
- 1 Chi Fang Heirloom
- 1 Chi Belt Heirloom

It cannot be refunded.
