## History and Trivia

- 10 December 2013: The Snow Sorceress was released as part of the Great Winter Snowball Fight event.
- When first released, if a Winter Charm was equipped, a Snowflake mouse could be attracted instead of a Snow Sorceress, causing the hunter to miss the Snow Sorceress' large Snow Block drop if caught. This was quickly fixed.
- The description for the Snow Sorceress contains references to the Elsa character from the animated film Frozen.
- 13 December 2016: The points/gold for Snow Sorceress were changed.
