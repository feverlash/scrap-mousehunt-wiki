## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Brute mice rely on raw force to keep hunters from progressing. And how do you deal with an unstoppable force? With an immovable object!When Brute mice try to knock a hunter back with a powerful blow, this spiky, saw-toothed plank drives itself firmly into the ground, preventing any backwards movement.

The Spiked Base can be purchased from Trapsmith in Slushy Shoreline and Iceberg for:

- 6 Bottled Cold Fusion
- 3 Wire Spool
- 3 Frosty Metal
