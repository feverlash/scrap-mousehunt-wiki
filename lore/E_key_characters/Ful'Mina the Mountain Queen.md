## 📜 Lore & Persona

> "Resting high above Moussu Picchu among the clouds, Ful'Mina is the true queen of the mountain and ruler over the skies and storms. As the Moussu Picchu Inhabitants below battle endlessly for dominance of the weather, the resulting stalemate allows the queen to slumber for generations.<br>Once, long ago, she would appear high above the land anywhere a lightning cloud hovered, visiting the many realms of mice across Gnawnia, her tail smacking the ground like lightning as she plucked treasures with it to add to her hoard. Now, countless decades pass in between sightings of the Mountain Queen, so much so that her very existence has gone from folklore, to legend, to mere myth...<br>But now, MouseHunters from the Kingdom of Gnawnia have found their way through the mountain pass, and their presence has brought a shift to the power struggle in these ruins, as they always do. Hunters have begun to harness the powers of both the Wind Wanderer and Rain Roamer tribes of mice in the area, ironically bringing unity between the two after endless centuries of feuding.<br>As the winds grow stronger, the rains fall heavier, and the storms begin to mix and clash, great bone-shaking growls can be heard from above the thickest of clouds, where the queen is said to reside. Is this thunder? Or is it the great Mountain Queen being rudely awakened from her slumber? Slowly but surely, disturbed by the intensifying battle in the lands below and the low rumble of impending thunderstorms in the clouds upon which she rests, she begins to rouse from her deep sleep...<br>Soon, and without warning, Ful'Mina, the Mountain Queen, will descend upon the lands with great fury to bring order to the chaos through the destructive force of her indomitable lightning storms."
