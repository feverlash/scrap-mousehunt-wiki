# ⚔️ Multi-Crystal Laser

> **Classification**: Rift Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 1,000 Power | 3 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Duke/Duchess  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 1,500,000 Gold1 Crystal Tower50 Rift Circuitry

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Take a smashed Crystal Tower, add a healthy collection of high technology scavenged from the most powerful Burroughs Rift mice, sprinkle with a dollop of Digby ingenuity, and what do you get? Terrifying Crystal Laser technology that brings Rift Power traps to the next level!This variant of Crystal Laser technology splits the laser into three separate beams, useful for covering more area, and ensuring the smaller, less powerful Rift mice don't sneak through. That, plus the 3 Luck makes this trap great for catching the majority of Rift mice, but less effective against the tougher bosses.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Multi-Crystal Laser can be purchased from the Trapsmith in Burroughs Rift for:

- 1,500,000 Gold
- 1 Crystal Tower
- 50 Rift Circuitry

It requires the Hunter to own 1 Null Gauntlet to purchase.

It cannot be refunded.
