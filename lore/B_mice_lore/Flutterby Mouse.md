# Flutterby Mouse

The Flutterby Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Crystal Library. It is not known to drop any loot.

## History and Trivia

- 9 August 2011: The Flutterby Mouse was introduced with the release of the Crystal Library location.
- 23 September 2011: The inspiration for the Flutterby Mouse was revealed by the artist (in a forum post) to be The Powerpuff Girls.
