## Island Info

- To encounter the Paragon of Dragons, the Hunter must pick a Draconic Sanctuary on the Sky Map.
- After catching the Paragon of Dragons, the Draconic Sanctuary will not be available on subsequent Sky Maps until ​the Hunter has defeated all 4 Sky Wardens.


## Loot

- Depending on the buff present, the Paragon of Dragons will drop the corresponding High Altitude Loot:

## History and Trivia
