## Preferred Cheese

The Rain Wallower Mouse has been found to be especially attracted to Rainy Cheese cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.

## Hunting Strategy

Only Shadow, Arcane and Draconic type traps are effective against the Rain Wallower Mouse, all other power types are ineffective.

The Rain Wallower Mouse is only available between 0%-34% rain intensities in Moussu Picchu.
