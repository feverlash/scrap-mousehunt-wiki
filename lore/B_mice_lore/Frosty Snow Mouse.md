## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Frosty Snow Mouse is attracted only to SUPER|brie+.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Frosty Snow Mouse.

## Locations

Hunters can encounter the Frosty Snow Mouse in the Mountain location.


## Loot

The Frosty Snow Mouse is not known to drop any loot.


## History and Trivia

- The Frosty Snow Mouse's name is a reference to Frosty the Snowman
- 22 December 2009: The Frosty Snow Mouse was released as part of The Great Winter Hunt '09.
- 24 December 2009: The Frosty Snow Mouse appeared in Ronza's Traveling Shoppe.
- 5 January 2010: The Frosty Snow Mouse began appearing in the Mountain.
- 25 June 2013: The Frosty Snow Mouse gained a vulnerability to Law power type traps, with the release of Varmint Valley.
- 25 March 2014: The Frosty Snow Mouse gained a vulnerability to Rift power type traps, with the introduction of the Rift Plane.
- 17 October 2018: The Frosty Snow Mouse changed mouse groups from Indigenous Mice to Mountain Mice.
