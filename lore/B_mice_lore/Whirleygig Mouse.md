# Whirleygig Mouse

The Whirleygig Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Rodentia region. It can be attracted and caught only during the Fall Season. They are not known to drop any loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Whirleygig Mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Strong Shadow weapons such as Clockapult of Time are recommended in order to catch this mouse. Alternatively, hunters may equip their best Tactical trap.


## History and Trivia

- Whirleygig Mice were introduced on 17 August 2010 with the release of the Seasonal Garden location.
- They began dropping the Antique Blue Argyle Egg as loot, on 16 April 2011, for the Spring Egg Hunt 2011 event.
