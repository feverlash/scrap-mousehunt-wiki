# Dunehopper Mouse

The Dunehopper Mouse is a breed of mouse that can be found in the Sand Dunes, and is known to drop Aleth Essences, Ber Essences, Cynd Essences and Duskshade Petals as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Dunehopper mice have been found to be only attracted to Dewthief Camembert.


## Hunting Strategy

The Dunehopper mouse is a member of the Sand Dunes mouse group. As such all power types except Shadow weapons are ineffective against it.


## History and Trivia

- Dunehopper mice were released on 14 November 2012, as part of the Living Garden update.
