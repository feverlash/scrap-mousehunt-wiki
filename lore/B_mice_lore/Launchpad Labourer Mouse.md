## Cheese Preference

The Launchpad Labourer Mouse can be attracted by using Standard Cheeses and Cloud Cheesecake.


## Hunting Strategy

All traps except Rift and Parental traps are effective against the Launchpad Labourer Mouse, all other power types are ineffective. The Launchpad Labourer Mouse can only be encountered on the Launch Pad.


## History and Trivia

- 08 September 2020: The Launchpad Labourer Mouse was introduced with the release of the Floating Islands.
