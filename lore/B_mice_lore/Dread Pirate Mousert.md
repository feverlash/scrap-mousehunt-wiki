# Dread Pirate Mousert

The Dread Pirate Mousert is a breed of mouse found in the Sunken City. It is known to drop Barnacles and Sand Dollars as loot.

## Cheese Preference

The Dread Pirate Mousert has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Dread Pirate Mousert is susceptible only to Hydro weapons. It can be found in the Shipwreck zone while on a Dive in the Sunken City.


## Event Participation


### Spring Egg Hunt

- The Dread Pirate Mousert drops the Barnacle Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

- The Dread Pirate Mousert Mouse was released 2 July 2014 as part of the Sunken City content.
- This mouse's name and description are references to The Princess Bride.
