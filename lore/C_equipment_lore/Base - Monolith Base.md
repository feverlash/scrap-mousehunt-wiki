# 🛡️ Monolith Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 500 Power | 0 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Master  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 0

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Mice become brutal and vicious in the presence of the Monolith Base, reverting to a feral state. Hunters, too, feel its pull as a sense of overwhelming dread. The ominous feel of this base comes from the fact that this base was found perfectly formed from the lost elemental material Ohnoesium. This extremely rare ore can only be found in small, hard-to-reach deposits deep beneath the earth at Digby, although most hunters who have come into contact with Ohnoesium ore agree that it should probably stay in the ground.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Monolith Base is available only as loot dropped by the Big Bad Burroughs Mouse in the Town of Digby.
