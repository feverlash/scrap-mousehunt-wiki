# ⚔️ Soul Catcher

> **Classification**: Shadow Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 1,500 Power | 5 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Master  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 190,000 gold1 Soulstone

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Harnessing the power of a Soulstone, this trap pierces the darkness and seeks out weaknesses in Shadow mice.
> This shard of Soulstone pulsates slowly, as if longing to be alive. It almost seems to morph and bulge within its platinum setting as it hangs suspended in its web. Perhaps it can be improved if imbued with another powerful stone...
> The haunting hum of the Soulstone gives this trap a luck bonus exclusively at the Gloomy Greenwood.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Soul Catcher is a Limited Edition weapon and can no longer be acquired by Hunters. It was available at the Trapsmith in the Haunted Terrortories for 190,000 gold plus 1 Soulstone during the Halloween at the Haunted Terrortories event. It could be refunded for 34,000 gold at the same location.

The Soul Catcher (if unintentionally smashed) can be reassembled from Soul Catcher Parts.
