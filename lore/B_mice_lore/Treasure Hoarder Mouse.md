# Treasure Hoarder Mouse

The Treasure Hoarder Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Sunken City. It is known to drop a variety of loot.

## Cheese Preference

The Treasure Hoarder Mouse has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Treasure Hoarder Mouse is susceptible only to Hydro weapons. It can be found in the Sunken Treasure zone while on a Dive in the Sunken City.


## Event Participation


### Spring Egg Hunt

- The Treasure Hoarder Mouse drops the Deep, Deep, Deep Egg, the Sand Dollar Egg and the Sunken Treasure Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

The Treasure Hoarder Mouse was released 2 July 2014 as part of the Sunken City content.
