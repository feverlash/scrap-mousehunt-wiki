# Dark Magi Mouse

The Dark Magi Mouse is a breed of mouse that can be found in the Cursed City. It is known to drop Ancient Relic, Dark Chest, and Living Garden Theme Scrap 2 as loot.

## Hunting Strategy

Dark Magi Mouse can only be attracted with Graveblossom Camembert and Shattering Charm armed.

It is a member of the Lost City Mice group. As such all power types except Arcane weapons are ineffective against it.

It is only available in Curses Lifted state in the Fear, Darkness, and Mist Curses Minigame.


## History and Trivia

- 14 January 2013: The Dark Magi Mouse was introduced as part of the Living Garden expansion.
