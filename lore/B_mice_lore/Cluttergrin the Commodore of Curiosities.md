## Cheese Preference

Cluttergrin the Commodore of Curiosities is only attracted to Aurora Bocconcini.


## Hunting Strategy

The Cluttergrin the Commodore of Curiosities can only be attracted while docked.

Law power type is very effective against Cluttergrin the Commodore of Curiosities.
All other types are ineffective.
