# 📍 Balack's Cove

> **Wilayah / Region**: Tribal Isles  
> **Pangkat Minimal**: Knight  
> **Fasilitas Lokal**: Cheese ShoppeTrapsmithCharm ShoppeGeneral Store

## 🌍 Gambaran & Atmosfer Lingkungan

Balack's Cove is a hunting location in Tribal Isles region.

It requires the rank of Knight and Balack's Lantern to access.

Mouse pool here is determined by Tide and cheese used.

Catching a Riptide Mouse transfers the Hunter to Jungle of Dread, unless an Anchor Charm variant is armed.
