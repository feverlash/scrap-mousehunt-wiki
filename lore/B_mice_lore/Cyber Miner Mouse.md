# Cyber Miner Mouse

The Cyber Miner Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Burroughs Rift. It is known to drop Mist Canister and Polluted Parmesan Potion as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Cyber Miner Mouse is only attracted to Terre Ricotta Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Cyber Miner Mouse can be attracted when the Mist Meter is in Yellow and Green Zone.

Rift power type is effective against Cyber Miner Mouse while all others are less effective.


## History and Trivia

- 9 September 2014: The Cyber Miner Mouse was released as part of the Burroughs Rift location.
