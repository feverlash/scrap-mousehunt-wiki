## Cheese Preference

The Gemstone Worshipper Mouse can only be attracted by using Glowing Gruyere Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Gemstone Worshipper Mouse has Forgotten as its primary weakness and Hydro as its secondary weakness.
