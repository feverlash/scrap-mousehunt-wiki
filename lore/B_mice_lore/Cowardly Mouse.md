# Cowardly Mouse

The Cowardly Mouse is a breed of mouse found in various locations in Meadow and Town of Gnawnia.

## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Cowardly Mouse can be attracted using Standard Cheese.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Cowardly Mouse.

## History and Trivia

- 30 June 2010: The Cowardly Mouse was introduced.
- 25 June 2013: The Cowardly Mouse gained a vulnerability to Law power type traps, with the release of Varmint Valley.
- 25 March 2014: The Agitated Gentle Giant Mouse was introduced as the Rift counterpart of the Cowardly Mouse.
- 25 March 2014: The Cowardly Mouse gained a vulnerability to Rift power type traps, with the release of Rift Plane.
