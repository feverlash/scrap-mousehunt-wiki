## History and Trivia

- 4 March 2013: Over the course of three days, the Travel map showed images of the Super FighterBot MegaSupreme emerging from the sea near the Harbour and approaching the eventual event site.
- 7 March 2013: The Super FighterBot MegaSupreme was released as part of MouseHunt's Fifth Birthday event.
- 7 March 2013: The Super FighterBot MegaSupreme could not be encountered until a Hunter obtained and armed the Ultra MegaMouser MechaBot Trap from Ronza's Traveling Shoppe Trapsmith.
- The Super FighterBot MegaSupreme is based on the Mighty Morphin Power Rangers cartoon.
- 4 March 2014: During MouseHunt's Sixth Birthday, capturing the Super FighterBot MegaSupreme also left a message in the Hunter's Journal:

> The Super FighterBot MegaSupreme may be a colossal menace under normal circumstances, but its huge size and uncoordinated moves don't do it any favours on the dance floor!
