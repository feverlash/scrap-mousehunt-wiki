# 🛡️ Furoma Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 100 Power | 10 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Novice  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 0

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> A lovely glass case with a perfectly feng shui display setup inside for maximum meditative enlightenment.
> This base has a chance of looting an extra Student Token in the Dojo or Chi Token in the Furoma Rift.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Furoma Base is a Limited Edition base not sold by any Trapsmith. It may only be obtained from opening a Season 6 Treasure Chest.

Each Season 6 Relic Hunter Treasure Chest has a chance of containing the Furoma Base. The more Season 6 chests you open, the greater your chance of finding the Limited Edition base will be!
There are two Limited Edition bases in Season 6, After opening 29 chests, you're guaranteed to either 
1) find both bases in your 30th (if it is a rare chest) if you have not already found them
2) find the first base in your 30th and the second base in your 31st (if it is a normal chest) if you have not already found them.
