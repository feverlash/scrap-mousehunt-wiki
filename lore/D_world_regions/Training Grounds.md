# 📍 Training Grounds

> **Wilayah / Region**: Furoma  
> **Pangkat Minimal**: Grandmaster  
> **Fasilitas Lokal**: Cheese ShoppeTrapsmithCharm ShoppeGeneral Store

## 🌍 Gambaran & Atmosfer Lingkungan

The Training Grounds is a location in the Furoma region requiring a minimum rank of Grandmaster and the Shredded Furoma Map Piece to enter.
The Shredded Furoma Map Piece can be obtained by successfully catching a Mousevina von Vermin in the Mousoleum.
Mice in this location may steal multiple pieces of cheese (but not points or gold) if not captured.
