# 🛡️ Ancient Booster Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 300 Power | 10 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Initiate  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 0

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Built from the same jade and gold materials, this magical base shares an affinity with Ancient Relics. Upon contact with a Relic Hunter Mouse, this base will attempt to consume one Ancient Relic to generate extra Ancient Charms.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Ancient Booster Base is a Limited Edition base not sold by any Trapsmith. It may only be obtained from opening a Season 5 Treasure Chest.

Each Season 5 Relic Hunter Treasure Chest has a chance of containing the Ancient Booster Base. The more Season 5 chests you open, the greater your chance of finding the Limited Edition trap will be! After opening 29 chests, you're guaranteed to find the trap in your 30th if you have not already found it.
