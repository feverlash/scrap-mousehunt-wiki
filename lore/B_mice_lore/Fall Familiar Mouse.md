# Fall Familiar Mouse

The Fall Familiar Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Rodentia region. It can be attracted and caught only during the Fall Season.

## Preferred Cheese

Fall Familiar mice have been found to be especially attracted to SUPER|brie+ cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.


## Hunting Strategy

Strong Shadow weapons such as Clockapult of Time are recommended in order to catch this mouse. Alternatively, hunters may equip their best Tactical trap.


## History and Trivia

- Fall Familiar Mice were introduced on 17 August 2010 with the release of the Seasonal Garden location.
- On 27 March 2011 they began to drop the Sandtail Call to Arms for the first time, following the release of the Fiery Warpath location on 22 March 2011.
- Fall Familiar Mice began dropping Ancient Relic as loot on 29 August 2012.
- On 29 July 2015 they stopped dropping the Sandtail Call to Arms as loot with the introduction of the Adventure Book.
