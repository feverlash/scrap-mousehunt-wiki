## Cheese Preference

Colossal Canister Collector Mouse is only attracted to Sky Pirate Swiss or Sky Raider Romano.


## Hunting Strategy

The Colossal Canister Collector Mouse can only be attracted during an Atmospheric shipment.

Law power type is effective against Colossal Canister Collector Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
