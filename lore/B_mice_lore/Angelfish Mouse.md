## Cheese Preference

The Angelfish Mouse has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Angelfish Mouse is susceptible only to Hydro weapons. It can be found in the Coral Castle, Deep Oxygen Stream, Magma Flow and Murky Depths zones while on a Dive in the Sunken City.


## Event Participation


### Spring Egg Hunt

- The Angelfish Mouse drops the Bubbling Egg, the Coral Egg, the Deep, Deep, Deep Egg, the Oxygen Egg, and the Salty Sea Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

The Angelfish Mouse was released 2 July 2014 as part of the Sunken City content.
