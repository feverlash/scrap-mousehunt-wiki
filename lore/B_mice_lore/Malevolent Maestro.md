## Cheese Preference

Malevolent Maestro can be attracted with any Standard Cheese, Beanster Cheese, Lavish Beanster Cheese or Royal Beanster Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Malevolent Maestro can only be attracted after planting a Medium Vine to the Ballroom Floor and with Noise Level 400.

Physical power type is very effective against Malevolent Maestro.
All other types are ineffective.

Catching the Malevolent Maestro will cause the Hunter to be moved to back to the Beanstalk.
