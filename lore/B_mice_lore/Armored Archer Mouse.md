# Armored Archer Mouse

The Armored Archer Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Furoma Rift. It is the Rift counterpart of Archer Mouse.

## Preferred Cheese

The Armored Archer Mouse has been found to be especially attracted to String Cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.


## Hunting Strategy

Only Rift type traps are effective against Armored Archer Mouse, all other power types are ineffective.
