# 🛡️ Glowing Golem Guardian Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 500 Power | 10 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Journeyman  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 150,000 Gold5 Snowflake Sugar Cookies5 Ornament Sugar Cookies5 Nutcracker Sugar Cookies5 Toboggan Sugar Cookies6 Iceberg Sculptor's Picks

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> A magical sheet of ice sheered from the iceberg formed between the collision of the Festive Comet and the poor, poor S.S. Huntington III. While flying through the air, it captured some glowing festive spirit that entwined with the Gnawnian Aurora Borealis. Also makes a great base for a Christmas Tree!
> Provides a bonus of +6 Luck when armed with the Golem Guardian Trap.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Glowing Golem Guardian Base could be obtained during the Great Winter Hunt 2018 event.
