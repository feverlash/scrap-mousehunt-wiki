## Cheese Preference

Sly Skulking Scrapper Mouse is only attracted to Sky Pirate Swiss or Sky Raider Romano.


## Hunting Strategy

The Sly Skulking Scrapper Mouse can only be attracted while docked.

Law power type is effective against Sly Skulking Scrapper Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
