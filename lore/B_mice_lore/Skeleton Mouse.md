# Skeleton Mouse

The Skeleton Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Catacombs. It is not known to drop any loot.

## Hunting Strategy

Arcane traps are very effective against the Skeleton Mouse. Forgotten and Shadow traps are of normal effectiveness.

The Antiskele Charm can be used to repel the Skeleton Mouse from your trap.
