## Cheese Preference

Loutish Loach Mouse is only attracted to Allegory Anari.


## Hunting Strategy

The Loutish Loach Mouse can only be attracted while on the rapids before the waterfall in Epilogue Falls.

Hydro power type is effective against Loutish Loach Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
