# Stratocaster Mouse

The Stratocaster Mouse is a breed of mouse found on the Floating Islands, on a Shadow Island or a Shadow Stronghold.

## Cheese Preference

The Stratocaster Mouse is only attracted to Cloud Cheesecake.


## Hunting Strategy

Only Shadow traps are effective against the Stratocaster Mouse, all other power types are ineffective. The Stratocaster Mouse can only be encountered on a Shadow Island or a Shadow Sanctuary.


## Loot


### Floating Islands's Island Based Loot

When found on a Low Altitude Island or a High Altitude Island, depending on the buffs present, the Stratocaster Mouse can drop the following loot:


| Item | Condition |
