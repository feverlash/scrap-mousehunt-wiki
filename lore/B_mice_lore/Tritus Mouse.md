## Cheese Preference

The Tritus Mouse has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Tritus Mouse is susceptible only to Hydro weapons. It can be found in the Monster Trench and Lair of the Ancients zones while on a Dive in the Sunken City.


## Event Participation


### Spring Egg Hunt

- The Tritus Mouse drops the Deep, Deep, Deep Egg, the Tritus Egg and the Underwater Predator Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.
