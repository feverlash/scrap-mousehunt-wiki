## Cheese Preference

Sultry Saxophonist Mouse is only attracted to Beanster Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Sultry Saxophonist Mouse can only be attracted after planting a Medium Vine to the Ballroom Floor.

Physical power type is effective against Sultry Saxophonist Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.


## Loot

Sultry Saxophonist Mouse can drop:

- Golden Harp String
- Royal Ruby Bean
