# Scarlet Revenger Mouse

The Scarlet Revenger Mouse is a breed of mouse found on the Floating Islands, on any island with 2 Sky Pirate Den present and active.

## Cheese Preference

The Scarlet Revenger Mouse is only attracted to Sky Pirate Swiss Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

All powertypes except Rift and Parental are effective against the Scarlet Revenger Mouse. The Scarlet Revenger Mouse can only be encountered on an island with 2 Sky Pirate Dens present and active.


## Loot


### Floating Islands's Sky Pirate Loot

Scarlet Revenger Mouse has a chance to drop Storm Cell if Rocket Boosters have been attached on the Hunter's Dirigible and the following loot depending on the buffs present:


| Item | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| Sky Glass | Always drops Boosted by Sky Glass Formations and Loot Caches |
| Sky Ore | Always drops Boosted by Sky Ore Deposit and Loot Caches |
| Sky Pirate Seal | Always Drops The amount a mouse drops depends on the mouse caught. |
| Cloud Curd | Drops when a Cloud Curd Bonus is present Can be boosted by Loot Caches |


Additionally, the Scarlet Revenger Mouse can drop the following loots as well:

- Bottled Wind
- Cyclone Stone
- Flawed Orb
