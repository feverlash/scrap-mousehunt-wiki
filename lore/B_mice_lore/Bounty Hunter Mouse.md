## Hunting Strategy

Law type traps such as S.L.A.C. are very effective against the Bounty Hunter Mouse.
The Bounty Hunter Mouse can only be encountered if the Hunter is not doing a Wanted Poster.
The Bounty Hunter Mouse is guaranteed to drop a Sealed Wanted Poster upon capture.
Hunters with a Sheriff's Badge Charm equipped are guaranteed to catch the next Bounty Hunter Mouse they encounter and loot 2 Sealed Wanted Posters, even without using a Law trap. The mouse attraction rate remains the same.

If the Hunter already has the maximum limit of 6 Sealed Wanted Poster, the Bounty Hunter Mouse will not drop one, with the following message appearing in the Hunter's Journal:

> The Bounty Hunter Mouse didn't leave me a Sealed Wanted Poster as I have the maximum allowed by the Marshall of Varmint Valley.


## History and Trivia

- 25 June 2013: The Bounty Hunter Mouse was released as part of the Claw Shot City content.
- 14 June 2022: Sealed Wanted Posters are now a guaranteed loot drop and the Bounty Hunter Mouse can now be encountered even when Hunters have Sealed Wanted Posters in their inventory. The message that used to be displayed in the Hunter's Journal upon capturing the Bounty Hunter Mouse was removed:

> I've caught a Bounty Hunter! He was carrying a Sealed Wanted Poster, which are special bounties issued from the Marshall of Varmint Valley! I should unfurl the Wanted Poster, bring a friend along as my Deputy, and start hunting down one of the Wild Bunch mouse gangs!
While I'm actively hunting down a Wild Bunch gang, the Bounty Hunter Mouse will avoid my trap until he can find a new poster for me.
