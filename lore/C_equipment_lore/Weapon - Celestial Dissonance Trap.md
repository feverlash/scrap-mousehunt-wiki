# ⚔️ Celestial Dissonance Trap

> **Classification**: Rift Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 3,500 Power | 10 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Archduke/Archduchess1,000,000,001 points  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 13,600,000 Gold20 Core of the Eclipse

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> A powerful model replica of the Valour Rift Tower derived from its original blueprints. This replica tower uses the power of the Eclipse Core to recombine itself around the mouse, sending it into its own version of an infinite tower in another dimension.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Celestial Dissonance Trap can be purchased from the Trapsmith in Valour Rift for:

- 13,600,000 Gold
- 20 Core of the Eclipse

It requires the Hunter to own the Timesplit Dissonance Trap to purchase.

It cannot be refunded.

The Celestial Dissonance Trap (if unintentionally smashed) can be reassembled from Celestial Dissonance Trap Parts.
