# Mystic Rook Mouse

The Mystic Rook Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Rodentia region. They are known to drop the Crystal Library Card.

## Preferred Cheese

Mystic Rook mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Mystic Rook mouse is a typical member of the Wizard's Pieces group, and as such, Tactical weapons are of normal effectiveness against it, while all others are ineffective. They are only available to attract after 2 Mystic Bishop mice have been caught.

Arming a Rook Crumble Charm gives the Hunter's weapon a 300% Power Bonus when encountering a Mystic Rook.


## Event Participation

- The Mystic Rook Mouse drops the Zugzwang's Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

- During the Spring Egg Hunt 2011, Mystic Rook mice began dropping a bonus Zugzwang's Egg as loot for hunters who had obtained the Rocket Rabbit Mallet. They dropped the same loot the following year during the Spring Egg Hunt 2012 event for hunters who had obtained the Eggscavator.
- Mystic Rook mice began to drop the Crystal Library Card on 9 August 2011 with the release of the Crystal Library location.
