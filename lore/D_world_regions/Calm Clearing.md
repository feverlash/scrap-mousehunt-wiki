# 📍 Calm Clearing

> **Wilayah / Region**: Whisker Woods  
> **Pangkat Minimal**: Recruit  
> **Fasilitas Lokal**: Cheese ShoppeTrapsmithCharm ShoppeGeneral Store

## 🌍 Gambaran & Atmosfer Lingkungan

Calm Clearing is a hunting location in Whisker Woods region.
It requires the rank of Journeyman/Journeywoman and the Calm Clearing Map Piece to access.

The main goal here is to hunt for Cyclops for the Great Gnarled Tree map piece to progress further into the region.
