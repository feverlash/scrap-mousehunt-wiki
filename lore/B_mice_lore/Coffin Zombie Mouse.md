## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

While the Coffin Zombie Mouse can be attracted by Undead Emmental, Undead String Emmental and Radioactive Blue, it has a distinct preference for Crimson Cheese.

## Hunting Strategy

Shadow traps are very effective against the Coffin Zombie Mouse.
The Coffin Zombie Mouse is only available when there is no barricade.
