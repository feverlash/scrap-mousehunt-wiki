## History and Trivia

- 7 March 2013: The Force Fighter Blue was first released for the MouseHunt's Fifth Birthday event.
- During MouseHunt's Fifth Birthday, the Force Fighter Blue could not be attracted when the Limited Edition Ultra MegaMouser MechaBot Trap was equipped.
- The appearance and description of Force Fighter Blue, like all of the Force Fighters, is a play on the popular children's show Mighty Morphin Power Rangers. Force Fighter Blue is also based on the title character of the Japanese animated show Sailor Moon.
