## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Gorgon Mouse is attracted to Ancient Cheese and Runic Cheese.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Gorgon Mouse.

## Locations

Hunters can encounter the Gorgon Mouse in the Acolyte Realm and the Forbidden Grove location.


## Loot

The Gorgon Mouse drops the following loot in the Acolyte Realm location:

- Nuclear Garlic


## History and Trivia

- 4 March 2009: The Gorgon Mouse is released with the introduction of the Acolyte Realm and Forbidden Grove.
- 18 April 2011: The Gorgon Mouse began dropping the Antique Wavy Purple Egg as loot as part of the Spring Egg Hunt 2011 event.
- 14 October 2014: The Gorgon Mouse was given new art. The costumed version appeared during the Halloween 2014 event and the new uncostumed version appeared when the event ended on 4 November 2014.
