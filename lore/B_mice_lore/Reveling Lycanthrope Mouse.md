# Reveling Lycanthrope Mouse

The Reveling Lycanthrope is a breed of mouse found in Fort Rox. They are not known to drop any loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Reveling Lycanthrope Mouse have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Shadow traps are very effective against the Reveling Lycanthrope Mouse. Arcane traps are of normal effectiveness.
The Reveling Lycanthrope Mouse can only be encountered during the Twilight, Midnight, Pitch, and Utter Darkness phases at Nighttime in Fort Rox.
