The Battle Cleric Mouse can be found in Zokor. It is known to drop Plates of Fealty.

## Preferred Cheese

The Battle Cleric Mouse prefers Glowing Gruyere, but can be attracted with Standard Cheeses.


## Hunting Strategy

Forgotten weapons are very effective against the Battle Cleric Mouse. Arcane weapons are of normal effectiveness.

The Battle Cleric Mouse can be found in the Outer Fealty Shrine, the Inner Fealty Temple, and the Templar's Sanctum.


## Loot

- Labyrinth Theme Scrap III and Plates of Fealty


## History and Trivia

> 9 September 2015: The Battle Cleric Mouse was introduced as part of the Hollow Heights release.
