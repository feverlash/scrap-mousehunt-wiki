## Island Info

- To encounter the Paragon of Water, the Hunter must pick a Hydro Hideaway on the Sky Map.
- After catching the Paragon of Water, the Hydro Hideaway will not be available on subsequent Sky Maps until ​the Hunter has defeated all 4 Sky Wardens.


## Loot

- Depending on the buff present, the Paragon of Water will drop the corresponding High Altitude Loot:


| Buff | High Altitude Loot |
| --- | --- |
| Paragon Bangle Shrine | Cloudstone Bangle |
| Paragon Silk Shrine | Skysoft Silk |
| Paragon Sprocket Shrine | Sky Sprocket |

## History and Trivia

- 08 September 2020: The Paragon of Water was introduced with the release of the Floating Islands.
