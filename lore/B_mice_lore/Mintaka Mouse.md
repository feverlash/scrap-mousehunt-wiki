# Mintaka Mouse

The Mintaka Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Tribal Isles region. They are known to drop Delicious Stones in Derr Dunes as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Mintaka mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## History and Trivia

- Mintaka, Alnitak and Alnilam are the names of the three stars in Orion's belt.
- Mintaka mice began dropping the Antique Wavy Purple Egg as loot, on 18 April 2011, for the Spring Egg Hunt 2011 event.
