## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Scavenger Mouse is attracted to both Ancient Cheese and Radioactive Blue cheese.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Scavenger Mouse.


## Power Type Weaknesses


| VeryEffective | Effective | Less Effective |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arcane | ShadowForgotten | None |



## Locations

Hunters can encounter the Scavenger Mouse in the Catacombs and the Forbidden Grove locations.


## Loot

The Scavenger Mouse drops the following loot in the Catacombs location:

- Ancient Potion
- Coal
- Corrupted Radioactive Blue Potion
- Scrap Metal

The Scavenger Mouse drops the following loot in the Forbidden Grove location:

- Ancient Potion
- Rune
- Scrap Metal


## History and Trivia

- 4 February 2009: The Scavenger Mouse was introduced with the release of the Catacombs location.
- 19 March 2009: The Scavenger Mouse began dropping Ancient Potion as loot.
- 12 January 2011: The Scavenger Mouse was found at Ronza's Traveling Shoppe where it dropped Rockforth cheese as loot.
- 16 April 2011: The Scavenger Mouse began dropping the Antique Blue Argyle Egg as loot in the Catacombs as part of the Spring Egg Hunt 2011 event.
- 4 April 2012: The Scavenger Mouse began dropping a bonus Scavenger Egg as loot as part of the Spring Egg Hunt 2012 event.
- The description for the Scavenger Mouse has changed since its release. The original description read as follows:

> The Catacombs are a dangerous place where mouse and hunter alike meet their fate. Scavenger mice sneak into the labyrinth that is the Catacombs collecting various bits of discarded cheese, gold and equipment. Scavengers are very protective of their findings and those who get in their way often find themselves at the wrong end of a shovel!

- 29 July 2015: The quantity of Scrap Metal dropped by Scavenger Mouse is increased.
