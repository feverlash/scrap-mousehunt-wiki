# Master Exploder Mouse

The Master Exploder Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Burroughs Rift. It is known to drop Mist Canisters and Polluted Parmesan Potion as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Master Exploder Mouse is only attracted to Terre Ricotta Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Master Exploder Mouse can only be attracted when the Mist Meter is in Green Zone.

Rift power type is effective against Master Exploder Mouse while all others are less effective.


## History and Trivia

- 9 September 2014: The Master Exploder Mouse was released as part of the Burroughs Rift location.
