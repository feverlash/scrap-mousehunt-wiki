## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Fuzzy Drake Mouse is primarily attracted to Bland Queso, although it is attracted to a lesser extent to Mild Queso, Medium Queso, and Hot Queso.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Fuzzy Drake Mouse.


## Hunting Strategy

Only Draconic traps are effective against the Fuzzy Drake Mouse, all other power types are ineffective. The Fuzzy Drake Mouse can only be encountered in Queso Geyser while gathering Cork Bark.

Dragonbane Charm, Super Dragonbane Charm, Extreme Dragonbane Charm, and Ultimate Dragonbane Charm provide a power bonus against the Fuzzy Drake Mouse.


## History and Trivia
