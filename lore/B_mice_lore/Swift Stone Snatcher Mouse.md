## Cheese Preference

Swift Stone Snatcher Mouse is only attracted to Sky Pirate Swiss or Sky Raider Romano.


## Hunting Strategy

The Swift Stone Snatcher Mouse can only be attracted during a Mining shipment.

Law power type is effective against Swift Stone Snatcher Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
