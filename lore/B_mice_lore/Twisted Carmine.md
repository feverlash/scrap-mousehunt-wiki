# Twisted Carmine

The Twisted Carmine is a breed of mouse that can be found in the Twisted Garden, and is known to drop Living Chests as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Twisted Carmine is only attracted to Lunaria Camembert.


## Hunting Strategy

The Twisted Carmine is a member of the Living Garden mouse group. As such all power types except Hydro weapons are ineffective against it.


## History and Trivia

- 14 November 2012: The Twisted Carmine was released as a part of the Living Garden update.
