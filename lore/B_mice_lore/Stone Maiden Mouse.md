## Cheese Preference

The Stone Maiden Mouse can only be attracted by using Glowing Gruyere Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Stone Maiden Mouse has Forgotten as its primary weakness and Hydro as its secondary weakness.


## History and Trivia

- 12 May 2015: The Stone Maiden Mouse was released as part of the Fungal Cavern location.
