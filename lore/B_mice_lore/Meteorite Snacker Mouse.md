## Preferred Cheese

Meteorite Snacker Mouse can be attracted by any Standard Cheese as well as Crescent Cheese and Moon Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Meteorite Snacker Mouse is only available during Day phase.

Law power type is very effective against this mouse. Arcane and Shadow are less effective.
All other types are ineffective.


## History and Trivia

- 3 November 2016: Meteorite Snacker Mouse was release as a part of Fort Rox location.
- 15 November 2022: Meteorite Snacker Mouse started dropping Moon Cheese with the New Dawn balance update to Fort Rox.
