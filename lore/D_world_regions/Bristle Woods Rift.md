## 🌍 Gambaran & Atmosfer Lingkungan

The Bristle Woods Rift is located in the Rift Plane region requiring a minimum rank of Grand Duke/Duchess and the Rift Detector to enter.
The Rift Detector can be purchased at the Living Garden Cartographer for 120,000 gold and 5 Dol Essences.
Leaving the Acolyte Chamber or any other chamber will not reset a Hunter's progress.
