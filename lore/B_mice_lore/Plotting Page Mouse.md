## Cheese Preference

Plotting Page Mouse is only attracted to Standard Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Plotting Page Mouse can only be attracted after planting a Tall Vine to the Great Hall Floor.

Physical power type is effective against Plotting Page Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.


## Loot

Plotting Page Mouse can drop:

- Golden Goose Egg
