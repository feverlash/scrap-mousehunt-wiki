## Cheese Preference

Nyagarha the Falls Guardian is only attracted to Symbolic Sirene.


## Hunting Strategy

The Nyagarha the Falls Guardian can only be attracted within the waterfall or hidden grotto of Epilogue Falls.

Hydro power type is effective against Nyagarha the Falls Guardian.
All other types are ineffective.
