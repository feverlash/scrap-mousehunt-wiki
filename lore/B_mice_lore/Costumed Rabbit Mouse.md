## History and Trivia

- 10 February 2011: The Costumed Rabbit Mouse was released as part of the Heart of the Rabbit Party event.
- The amount of points (1,888) and gold (888) awarded on catching a Costumed Rabbit Mouse reflects the belief of the number eight being considered "lucky" in Chinese culture; a sequence of a "lucky" number is considered even more favorable!
