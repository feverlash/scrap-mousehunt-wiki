# ⚔️ Dragon Lance

> **Classification**: Draconic Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 4,950 Power | 12 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Knight  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 8,900,000

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Fighting fire with fire! The Dragon Lance taps into heat from Gnawnia's magmatic underplating which passes through and focuses within the tip of the specialized anti-dragon lance! The trap is created from 100% organic dragon bones and the lance is made with dragon bone infused metal, the only material able to contain the immense power and heat generated by this trap.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Dragon Lance is a Limited Edition weapon and can no longer be acquired by Hunters. It was available to be purchased for 8,900,000 gold from Ronza's Traveling Shoppe during Ronza's May 2014 visit. It could be refunded for 1,602,000 gold at the same location.
