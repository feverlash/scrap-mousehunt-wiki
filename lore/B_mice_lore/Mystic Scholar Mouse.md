# Mystic Scholar Mouse

The Mystic Scholar Mouse can be found in the Labyrinth and Zokor. It is known to drop Scholar Scrolls in Zokor.

## Preferred Cheese

The Mystic Scholar Mouse prefers Glowing Gruyere in both the Labyrinth and Zokor, but can be attracted with Standard Cheeses.


## Hunting Strategy

Forgotten weapons are very effective against the Mystic Scholar Mouse. Arcane weapons are of normal effectiveness.

- Labyrinth

> The Mystic Scholar Mouse can be found in Epic Scholar Hallways.

- Zokor

> The Mystic Scholar Mouse can be found in the Dark Library.


## Loot

- Zokor

> Labyrinth Theme Scrap II and Scholar Scrolls
