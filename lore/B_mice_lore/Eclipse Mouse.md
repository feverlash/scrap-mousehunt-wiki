## Hunting Strategy

Eclipse Mouse is only attracted to Gauntlet Cheese Tier 8.


### Power Type Weaknesses

Though several power types are shown as very effective, Forgotten is relatively much more effective against Eclipse Mouse.

## Loot

Eclipse Mouse can drop Ancient Relic as loot.


## History and Trivia

- The "Stealth Mouse" in the 1 April 2010 news item was the silhouetted version of the Eclipse Mouse.
- 8 June 2010: Eclipse Mouse was released as part of King's Gauntlet location.
- 9 July 2010: The King granted permission to use the Gauntlet Potion Tier 8, allowing Hunters to hunt the Eclipse Mouse.
- 29 August 2012: The Eclipse Mouse began dropping Ancient Relic as loot.
