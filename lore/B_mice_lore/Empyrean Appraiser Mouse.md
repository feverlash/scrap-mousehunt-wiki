## Vault Info

- The Empyrean Appraiser Mouse can only be encountered in a vault that has at least 3 Ore and Glass Deposits active.
- The Empyrean Appraiser Mouse's attraction is increased in a vault that has 4 Ore and Glass Deposits active.
