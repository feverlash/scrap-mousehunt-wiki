## Cheese Preference

Wily Weevil Mouse can be attracted with any Standard Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Wily Weevil Mouse is available when at least 2 plants are growing.

Tactical power type is effective against Wily Weevil Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.


## Loot

Wily Weevil Mouse can drop:

- Condensed Creativity
- Crop Coin
