## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Smoldersnap Mouse is only attracted to Medium Queso, Hot Queso or Flamin' Queso.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Smoldersnap Mouse.


## Hunting Strategy

Only Draconic traps are effective against the Smoldersnap Mouse, all other power types are ineffective. The Smoldersnap Mouse can only be encountered in Queso Geyser while either a Tiny Eruption, Small Eruption, Medium Eruption, Large Eruption or a Epic Eruption is triggered.

Dragonbane Charm, Super Dragonbane Charm, Extreme Dragonbane Charm, and Ultimate Dragonbane Charm provide a power bonus against the Smoldersnap Mouse.


## History and Trivia

- 30 April 2019: The Smoldersnap Mouse was introduced, with the release of the Queso Geyser area.
