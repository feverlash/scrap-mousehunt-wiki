# 🛡️ Festive Winter Hunt Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 100 Power | 10 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Apprentice  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 127,0001 Nugget of Nougat

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Winter Hunt in a box! Compress your Winter Hunt tree into this lovely crystal box that doubles as a base! You can now rememeber it all year round just as you left it.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Festive Winter Hunt Base is a base that was first made available during the Great Winter Hunt 2017 event. It could be purchased from the Great Winter Hunt 2017 Trapsmith in exchange for 127,000 gold and 1 Nugget of Nougat.
