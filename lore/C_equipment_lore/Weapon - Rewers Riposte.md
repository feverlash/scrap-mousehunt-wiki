# ⚔️ Rewers Riposte

> **Classification**: Tactical Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 2,900 Power | 25 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Journeyman  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 605,000

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> These droids have been specially trained by one of the kingdom's most devoted hunters. The droids are quick and agile, allowing them to easily parry an incoming mouse and corral them under a massive net.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
Rewers Riposte is a Limited Edition weapon and can no longer be acquired by Hunters. It was available to be purchased for 605,000 gold from Ronza's Traveling Shoppe during Ronza's January 2011 visit. It could be refunded for 121,000 gold at the same location.
