# M400

The M400 is a breed of mouse found when working the final phase of the M400 Hunting Research Assignment. It is known to drop a large variety of loot.

## Hunting Strategy

To catch M400, the Hunter must do the following Library Assignments:

- 1: M400 Bait Research Assignment to obtain Fusion Fondue
- 2: M400 Hunting Research Assignment to track down M400 itself

The mouse requires Fusion Fondue in a designate location when the Hunter is at Step 10 of the Hunting Research Assignment.
Only Physical and Tactical power types work against this mouse.
EMP400 Charm provides a bonus of 25,000 Power against M400.

Fusion Fondue is automatically disarmed upon catching this mouse.

In addition to the normal message about completing a Library Assignment, catching an M400 also leaves this message in the Hunter's Journal:

> Just as it was captured, the M400 mouse fired an emergency EMP blast, disarming my bait and warning other M400 Mice in the area!
I will have to start another research assignment to locate where the M400 are now hiding.

## History and Trivia

- The M400 was intended to be the 400th mouse introduced to the game, but its introduction was delayed.
- 26 February 2014: M400 was released as part of the expansion to Library Assignment.
- 16 April 2024: The M1000 mouse was released, whose name is an homage to the M400.
