# Romeno Mouse

The Romeno Mouse is part of the Indigenous Mice found in Town of Gnawnia.

It was previously an Event Mouse that was found during the annual Valentine's Day or Lunar New Year events.

## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Romeno Mouse is attracted only to Gilded Cheese.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Romeno Mouse.

## History and Trivia

- 9 February 2010: The Romeno Mouse is introduced as part of the Heart of the Tiger event.
