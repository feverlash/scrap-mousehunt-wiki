## Cheese Preference

The Skydiver Mouse can be attracted by using Standard Cheeses and Cloud Cheesecake.


## Hunting Strategy

All traps except Rift and Parental traps are effective against the Skydiver Mouse, all other power types are ineffective. The Skydiver Mouse can only be encountered on the Launch Pad.


## History and Trivia

- 08 September 2020: The Skydiver Mouse was introduced with the release of the Floating Islands.
