# Chipper Mouse

The Chipper Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Slushy Shoreline and Iceberg locations. It is known to drop a variety of loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Chipper Mouse has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

In the Slushy Shoreline, the Chipper Mouse is known to drop War Scrap as loot.
The Chipper Mouse can be found in the Treacherous Tunnels, Bombing Run, and The Mad Depths zones of Iceberg. The encounter rate for the Chipper Mouse can be increased by arming the Iceberg Boiler Base, Magnet Base, or Ultimate Iceberg Base.


## History and Trivia

- 7 May 2012:: The Chipper Mouse was released in Slushy Shoreline. It was found to drop War Supplies as loot until 14 May 2012.
- Between 14 May 2012 and 24 May 2012: The Chipper Mouse dropped Interlocking Hull Fragments.
- 1 June 2012: The Chipper Mouse began to drop loot in the Iceberg.
