## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Rambunctious Rain Rumbler Mouse is only attracted to Flamin' Queso.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Rambunctious Rain Rumbler Mouse.


## Hunting Strategy

Only Draconic traps are effective against the Rambunctious Rain Rumbler Mouse, all other power types are ineffective. The Rambunctious Rain Rumbler Mouse can only be encountered in Queso Geyser while gathering Cork Bark.

Dragonbane Charm, Super Dragonbane Charm, Extreme Dragonbane Charm, and Ultimate Dragonbane Charm provide a power bonus against the Rambunctious Rain Rumbler Mouse.


## History and Trivia

- 30 April 2019: The Rambunctious Rain Rumbler Mouse was introduced, with the release of the Queso Geyser area.
