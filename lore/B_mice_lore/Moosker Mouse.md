## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Moosker Mouse can be attracted when hunting with Standard Cheese.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Moosker Mouse.

## Locations

Hunters can encounter the Moosker Mouse in the Calm Clearing and Great Gnarled Tree locations.


## Loot

The Moosker Mouse is not known to drop any loot.


## History and Trivia

- 31 March 2009: The Moosker Mouse was released with the introduction of the Calm Clearing.
- 25 June 2013: The Moosker Mouse gained a vulnerability to Law traps, with the release of Varmint Valley.
- The Moosker Mouse, for a time, was also found at the Lagoon.
- The original appearance of the Moosker Mouse was similar to the White Mouse.
