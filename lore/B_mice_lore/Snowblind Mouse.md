# Snowblind Mouse

The Snowblind Mouse is a breed of mouse found in The Mad Depths zone of the Iceberg. It is known to drop Hunting Research, Satchel of Gold, Sticky Charms, and Wax Charms as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Snowblind Mouse has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

In the Iceberg, a Wax Charm will increase the distance of a successful catch, and a Sticky Charm will reduce the distance of a miss.


## History and Trivia

- 24 May 2012: The Snowblind Mouse was released as a part of the Iceberg.
- 1 June 2012: The Snowblind Mouse began to drop loot.
