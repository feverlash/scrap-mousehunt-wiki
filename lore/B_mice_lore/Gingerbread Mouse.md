# Gingerbread Mouse

> "Gingerbread" redirects here. For more uses, see Gingerbread (disambiguation).

## History and Trivia

- 10 December 2012: The Gingerbread Mouse was released as part of the Great Winter Hunt 2012 event. However, the mouse was "locked" and unavailable to be caught by Hunters.
- 17 December 2012: The Gingerbread Mouse was "unlocked" and Hunters were then able to attract and capture it.
- 13 December 2016: The points for Gingerbread Mouse were changed.
