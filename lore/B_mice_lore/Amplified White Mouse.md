# Amplified White Mouse

The Amplified White Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Burroughs Rift. It is known to drop Mist Canisters as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Amplified White Mouse can be attracted using any Standard String Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Amplified White Mouse can be attracted when the Mist Meter is in No Mist Zone.

Rift power type is effective against Amplified White Mouse while all others are less effective.


## History and Trivia

- 9 September 2014: The Amplified White Mouse was released as part of the Burroughs Rift location.
