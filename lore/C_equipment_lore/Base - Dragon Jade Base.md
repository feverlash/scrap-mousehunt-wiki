# 🛡️ Dragon Jade Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 300 Power | 10 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Grandmaster  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 445,000

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> To celebrate the Year of the Dragon Festival, Ronza sought out a Trapsmith capable of carving jade to create the Dragon Jade Base! The ornate carvings carry with them a special enchantment to bring extra luck to your trap!Only those possessing a Dragon Seal, proving they have braved a victorious encounter with the Costumed Dragon Mouse, may handle the dragon power channeled through this dragon base.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Dragon Jade Base was available for purchase for 445,000 gold and 1 Jade Base from Ronza's Traveling Shoppe during her January-February 2012 visit if they had a Dragon Seal. It refunded for 65,000 gold at the same location. It can no longer be obtained.
