## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Golem Mouse will be attracted when hunting with Ancient cheese, Radioactive Blue and Runic cheeses.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Golem Mouse.

## Locations

Hunters can encounter the Golem Mouse in the Acolyte Realm, the Forbidden Grove and the Catacombs locations.
