# Riptide Mouse

The Riptide Mouse is a breed of mouse found in Balack's Cove.

Riptide mice cannot be found during the low and medium tides in Balack's Cove; they only appear during high tides. Upon capturing this mouse, hunters are transported to Jungle of Dread.

## Preferred Cheese

Riptide mice have been found to be attracted to both Vanilla Stilton cheese and Vengeful Vanilla Stilton cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Capturing a Riptide Mouse will cause Hunters to be washed out of Balack's Cove and into the Jungle of Dread.

- During Low Tide and Medium Tide the Riptide Mouse cannot be encountered.
- During High Tide the Riptide Mouse is the only mouse that can be encountered with its preferred cheese.

Arming any of the various Anchor Charms will prevent a Hunter from being washed out when capturing a Riptide Mouse.


## History and Trivia
