## Preferred Cheese

Red-Eyed Watcher Owl mice have been found to be especially attracted to String Cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.


## Hunting Strategy

The Red-Eyed Watcher Owl Mouse is weak to Rift Traps. It can only be attracted by String Cheese.

The Red-Eyed Watcher Owl Mouse is only available when rage in the Crazed Clearing section in Whisker Woods Rift is between 25-49.


## Event Participation


### Spring Egg Hunt

- The Red-Eyed Watcher Owl Mouse drops the Enlarged Rift Egg and the Rift Mist Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

- Red-Eyed Watcher Owl mice were introduced on 28 January 2015, with the introduction of the Whisker Woods Rift.
- Red-Eyed Watcher Owl mice are the Rift version of Eagle Owl mice.
