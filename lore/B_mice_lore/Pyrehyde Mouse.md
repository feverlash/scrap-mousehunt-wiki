## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Pyrehyde Mouse is only attracted to Flamin' Queso.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Pyrehyde Mouse.


## Hunting Strategy

Catching a Pyrehyde Mouse while building pressure in Queso Geyser increases the amount of pressure by 100 kPa.

Only Draconic traps are effective against the Pyrehyde Mouse, all other power types are ineffective. The Pyrehyde Mouse can only be encountered while building pressure in Queso Geyser, after a cork has been constructed.

Dragonbane Charm, Super Dragonbane Charm, Extreme Dragonbane Charm, and Ultimate Dragonbane Charm provide a power bonus against the Pyrehyde Mouse.
