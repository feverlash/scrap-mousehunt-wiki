# Sludge Swimmer Mouse

The Sludge Swimmer Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Toxic Spill, for hunters of rank Grand Duke/Grand Duchess and above.

## Preferred Cheese

Sludge Swimmer mice are attracted to Magical Rancid Radioactive Blue at every spill level and to Rancid Radioactive Blue when spill level is for Grand Duke/Grand Duchess and above.


## Hunting Strategy

Hydro type traps such as Oasis Water Node Trap or Phantasmic Oasis Trap are effective against the Sludge Swimmer Mouse.


## Event Participation

- The Sludge Swimmer Mouse drops the Toxic Spill Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

- Released on 12 February 2014 as part of the second Toxic Spill content.
