| Few mice enjoy digging in the dirt as much as this opportunistic scrounger. The Root Rummager has a distinct taste for seeds that have already begun to germinate and therefore has a penchant for digging them out of the ground: roots, stalk, and all! These mice tend to be easier to track due to the trail of dirt that follows them wherever they go but the seeds they carry often require a bit of trimming and untangling before they can be reused. |  |  |  |

## Cheese Preference

Root Rummager Mouse can be attracted with any Standard Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Root Rummager Mouse is available when 0-1 plant is growing.

Tactical power type is effective against Root Rummager Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.


## Loot

Root Rummager Mouse can drop:

- Papyrus Seed
