## Locations

Hunters can encounter the Eagle Owl Mouse in the Calm Clearing, Great Gnarled Tree, and the Lagoon locations.


## Loot

The Eagle Owl Mouse drops Cherry Potions in the Calm Clearing location.

The Eagle Owl Mouse is not known to drop any loot in the Great Gnarled Tree or Lagoon locations.


## History and Trivia

- 31 March 2009: The Eagle Owl Mouse was released with the introduction of the Calm Clearing.
- 14 April 2011: The Eagle Owl Mouse began dropping a bonus Whisker Woods Egg as loot as part of the annual Spring Egg Hunt.
- 16 April 2011: The Eagle Owl Mouse began dropping the Antique Blue Argyle Egg as loot in the Great Gnarled Tree as part of the Spring Egg Hunt 2011 event.
- 25 June 2013: The Eagle Owl Mouse gained a vulnerability to Law traps with the release of Varmint Valley.
