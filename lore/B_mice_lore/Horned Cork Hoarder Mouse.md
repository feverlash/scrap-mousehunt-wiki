## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Horned Cork Hoarder Mouse is only attracted to Hot Queso or Flamin' Queso.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Horned Cork Hoarder Mouse.


## Hunting Strategy

Only Draconic traps are effective against the Horned Cork Hoarder Mouse, all other power types are ineffective. The Horned Cork Hoarder Mouse can only be encountered in Queso Geyser while gathering Cork Bark.

Dragonbane Charm, Super Dragonbane Charm, Extreme Dragonbane Charm, and Ultimate Dragonbane Charm provide a power bonus against the Horned Cork Hoarder Mouse.


## History and Trivia
