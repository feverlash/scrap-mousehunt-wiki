## Cheese Preference

Menacing Medusozoa Mouse is only attracted to Metaphor Manchego.


## Hunting Strategy

The Menacing Medusozoa Mouse can only be attracted while on the rapids before the waterfall in Epilogue Falls.

Hydro power type is effective against Menacing Medusozoa Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
