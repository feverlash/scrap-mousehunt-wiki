# Puffer Mouse

The Puffer Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Sunken City. It is known to drop a variety of loot.

## Cheese Preference

The Puffer Mouse has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Puffer Mouse is susceptible only to Hydro weapons. It can be found in the Oxygen Stream, School of Mice, Sea Floor, and Shallow Shoals zones while on a Dive in the Sunken City.


## Event Participation


### Spring Egg Hunt

- The Puffer Mouse drops the Bubbling Egg, the Mouse Scale Egg, the Oxygen Egg, and the Salty Sea Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

The Puffer Mouse was released 2 July 2014 as part of the Sunken City content.
