# Scruffy Mouse

The Scruffy Mouse is a breed of mouse found in various locations in Gnawnia, and in King's Arms.

## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Scruffy Mouse can be attracted using Standard Cheese.

The Scruffy Mouse will not be attracted when hunting with Grilled Cheese in the Windmill location.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Scruffy Mouse.

## Loot

The Scruffy Mouse drops Flawed Orb in all locations, and additionally Packet of Flour in the Windmill location.


## History and Trivia

- 30 June 2010: The Scruffy Mouse was introduced as part of the Windmill update.
- 25 January 2011: The Scruffy Mouse began to drop Flawed Orb as loot.
- 25 June 2013: The Scruffy Mouse gained a vulnerability to Law power type traps with the release of Varmint Valley.
- 25 March 2014: The Scruffy Mouse gained a vulnerability to Rift power type traps with the introduction of the Rift Plane.
- 2 September 2014: The Scruffy Mouse began dropping Packets of Flour at the Windmill.
