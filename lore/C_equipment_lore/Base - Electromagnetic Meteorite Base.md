# 🛡️ Electromagnetic Meteorite Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 350 Power | 8 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Baron  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 0

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Composed of a solar panel, battery pack, and strange, almost alien technology, this base has the ability to attract chunks of meteorite after charging up enough energy. It only seems to activate when the sun goes down and the charging cycle has completed, causing the glyphs etched into its surface to glow, and something from deep within begins to hum...
> Use this base during the night in Fort Rox for a chance to find Meteorite Pieces.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Electromagnetic Meteorite Base is a Limited Edition base not sold by any Trapsmith. It may only be obtained from opening a Season 7 Treasure Chest.

Each Season 7 Relic Hunter Treasure Chest has a chance of containing the Electromagnetic Meteorite Base. The more Season 7 chests you open, the greater your chance of finding the Limited Edition base will be!
There are two Limited Edition bases in Season 7, After opening 29 chests, you're guaranteed to either 
1) find both bases in your 30th (if it is a rare chest) if you have not already found them
2) find the first base in your 30th and the second base in your 31st (if it is a normal chest) if you have not already found them.
