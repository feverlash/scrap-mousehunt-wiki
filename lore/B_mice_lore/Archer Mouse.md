## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Archer Mouse is attracted to Standard Cheese in the Dojo and Training Grounds

In the Dojo location, the Archer Mouse will also be attracted when hunting with Maki Cheese.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Archer Mouse.

## Locations

Hunters can encounter the Archer Mouse in the Training Grounds and Dojo locations.

Hunters must capture the Archer Mouse in the Training Grounds location to obtain the Ticket to the Burroughs Bazaar and gain access to the Bazaar location.


## Loot

The Archer Mouse drops the following loot:

- Training Grounds

> Satchel of Gold (250)
Ticket to the Burroughs Bazaar

- Dojo

> Satchel of Gold (250)


## History and Trivia

- The visual appearance of the Archer Mouse is based on common incarnations of the Robin Hood legend.
- 10 October 2008: The Archer Mouse was released , accompanied by Kung Fu and Samurai mice.
- 27 November 2008: The Archer Mouse's habitat expanded to the Dojo when hunters accessed the Furoma Pagoda locations for the first time.
- 11 December 2008: The Archer Mouse began to drop Satchels of Gold worth 250 gold.
