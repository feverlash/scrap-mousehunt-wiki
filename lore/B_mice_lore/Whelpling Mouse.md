# Whelpling Mouse

The Whelpling Mouse is a breed of mouse found in Dracano. They are known to drop Delicious Stones, Savoury Vegetables, Seashells, Dragon Embers and Satchels of Gold worth 1,000 gold. They are also known to drop the Draconic Book, but only while hunters are in the process of completing the Zurreal Trap Research Assignment.

## Preferred Cheese

The Whelpling mice have been found to be particularly attracted to Inferno Havarti, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.


## History and Trivia

- The Whelpling Mouse is markedly similar to illustrations of Max in Maurice Sendak's book Where the Wild Things Are.
- On 23 August 2011 they began to drop the Draconic Book as loot.
