## Cheese Preference

Adventure Weaver is attracted to Plotting Paneer or Storied Saga or Deus Ex Maconnais.


## Hunting Strategy

The Adventure Weaver can only be attracted while writing a postscript to a story.

Only Law power types are effective against Adventure Weaver.
All other types are ineffective.
