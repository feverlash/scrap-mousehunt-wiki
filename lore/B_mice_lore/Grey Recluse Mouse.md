## History and Trivia

- 14 October 2014: The Grey Recluse Mouse is released as part of the Halloween 2014 event.
- 13 October 2016: The descrition was changed for the Halloween 2016 event. The old description read:

> The definition of creepy and crawly! The Grey Recluse is one to watch out for while trickily traversing the Haunted Terrortories. Even after being captured, his sticky webs have a way to tie up travellers and keep them from advancing.\n\nCatching this mouse has its consequences for he will tie you up and drag you back one space in the Haunted Terrortories.
