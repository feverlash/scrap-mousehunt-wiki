# ⚔️ Storm Wrought Ballista Trap

> **Classification**: Draconic Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 6,000 Power | 15 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Grand Duke/Grand Duchess  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 1 Dragonvine Ballista Trap3 Ful'Mina's Tooth

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> A vortex of thunderous bolts fire endlessly from this Storm Wrought Ballista Trap. Enhanced by dark dragon magic drawn from Ful'Mina's powerful tooth, its powerful storm vortex can rip an additional Dragon Scale from the clutches of its victims.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Storm Wrought Ballista Trap can be purchased from the Trapsmith in Moussu Picchu for:

- 1 Dragonvine Ballista Trap
- 3 Ful'Mina's Tooth

It can be refunded for 340,000 Gold at the same location.

The Storm Wrought Ballista Trap (if unintentionally smashed) can be reassembled from Storm Wrought Ballista Trap Parts.
