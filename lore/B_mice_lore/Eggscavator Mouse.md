## History and Trivia

- 4 April 2012: The Eggscavator Mouse was released as part of the Spring Egg Hunt 2012 celebration.
- The name of the Eggscavator Mouse is a play on the word "excavator".
- The description of the Eggscavator Mouse references a line from the film The Dark Knight
