# Maps and Keys

As hunters travel around the world of MouseHunt, they will occasionally find maps and keys while catching mice. These inventory items grant hunters access to new locations. The difficulty in acquiring a particular map or key depends on the difficulty of the mouse relative to their trap power. As only some mice hold them, hunters will usually need to catch at least a few before finding what they want. Some map pieces must be crafted, or purchased from a Cartographer.

In many cases, it is possible for a hunter to obtain a map piece while ranked with a lower Title than what is required to travel to the locations unlocked by that map piece.

The map pieces owned by a hunter can be found on the Map Pieces tab under the Travel section. Additionally, each Hunter's Profile includes a status of how many map pieces a hunter owns, as one of the lists on the Items tab.


## Gnawnia


### Gnawnia Map Piece

You start the game with this basic map piece, which gives you access to the Meadow, Town of Gnawnia, the Windmill, the Harbour and the Mountains.

The Gnawnia Map Piece grants hunters access to all locations within the Gnawnia region. All hunters start with the map already in their inventory.

This is some kind of strange and intricate device made entirely of stone. It seems to depict a large maze carved into a miniaturized mountain range. Some hunters have discovered a relief in the deepest depths of the Fungal Cavern that when deposited, causes the walls to shift, open up and reveal an entrance to the Labyrinth.

## Folklore Forest


### Legendary Looking Glass

This extraordinary looking glass emanates a bizarre and captivating power of mythical proportions. It was constructed from three unique pieces (a Liquid Lens, an Antique Frame, and a Tactical Grip) that could have only originated from a place where the line between reality and fantasy is blurred.

Once the three pieces were assembled and imbued with the awesome, otherworldly power of the Adorned Empyrean Jewel, this exquisite oddity transformed from a simple, ornate hand mirror into a window from our world into a place where imagination is the ultimate power. By gazing into its mystical lens, a hunter can see themselves in this place that is neither here nor there, neither fact nor fiction, neither reality nor fantasy; a place that puts the story in history.

A small, nearly illegible inscription on the back of the frame hints that there is a specific spot in Gnawnia where the veil between perception and imagination is very thin. If an intrepid hunter were to take this Legendary Looking Glass to that one spot, open their mind, forgo all preconceptions as to the nature of their reality, and look very, very deeply into its lens then perhaps they might just find themselves on the other side of that reflection.

Only hunters ranked Archduke/Archduchess and above can gaze into this mystical reflection...

The Legendary Looking Glass grants access to Foreword Farm, Prologue Pond, and Table of Contents.

It can be purchased from the Cartographer in Floating Islands for:

- 1,000,000 gold

### Mythweaver's Guide to World Domination

This complete how-to guide contains detailed, step-by-step instructions for any mouse looking to achieve their goals of total world domination. There is no listed author or publisher so the origin of this extremely powerful and oddly specific book is a mystery.

This appears to be the playbook that the Mythweaver has been using all this time to enact his nefarious plans in the Folklore Forest.

The first chapter describes rewriting folklore at the Table of Contents, the second chapter details how to write oneself into the Bountiful Beanstalk, and so on. It's all here! Everything the Mythweaver has done so far is laid out in clearly written instructions accompanied by detailed diagrams, full colour illustrations, and even a few pie charts.

Each chapter appears to have been checked off... except for the last one. The final chapter in this book is titled "How to Write the Ultimate Conclusion to the Kingdom of Gnawnia in 3 Easy Steps!". Skimming through this chapter, you're able to determine the final stages of the Mythweaver's plan:

Step 1: Corrupt the Infinitree at the Afterword Acres
Step 2: Discover the hidden wisdom of the Epilogue Falls
Step 3: Write the end of the story at the Conclusion Cliffs

This is it! You have what you need to stop the Mythweaver before he finishes his ultimate plan. It's not going to be easy but with this book, you've got a fighting chance!

Only hunters ranked Viceroy and above can read from these enchanted pages...

The Mythweaver's Guide to World Domination will grant access to the Afterword Acres and the Epilogue Falls.

There are 2 ways to obtain this map piece:

- Loot from Mythweaver between 19 August 2025 to 2 September 2025
- Draconic Depths Cartographer after 2 September 2025

This map piece depicts an area of the Rift Plane within the Valour Region. The Bristle Woods Rift Cartographer requires information that can be gathered by collecting 2000 Rift Distortions from Rift Plane mice.

Purchase a Rift Chronometer from the Bristle Woods Rift to begin your adventure and discover the Valour Rift.

This area of the Rift Plane can only be explored by hunters ranked Archduke/Archduchess or above.

Although the entrance to this Rift has been pinpointed, it is not yet stable enough to traverse.

The Valour Rift Map Piece grants access to the Valour Rift. It can be purchased for 2,000 Rift Distortions at the Bristle Woods Rift Cartographer, with no wait time and no additional requirements.
