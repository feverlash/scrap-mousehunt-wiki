## Cheese Preference

Baroque Dancer Mouse is only attracted to Standard Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Baroque Dancer Mouse can only be attracted after planting a Medium Vine to the Ballroom Floor.

Physical power type is effective against Baroque Dancer Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.


## Loot

Baroque Dancer Mouse can drop:

- Golden Harp String
- Royal Ruby Bean
