## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> A soft-glowing bed of fast-growing moss sleeps soundly on this otherwordly base. The strangely-glowing shards radiate warmth into the dirt. This warmth is used in accelerating the growth rate of flora that comes in contact with the base.
> The Living Base will increase petal and herb drops from mice in the Living Garden, Lost City, Sand Dunes, and their twisted counterparts!
