## Cheese Preference

The Saltwater Axolotl Mouse has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Saltwater Axolotl Mouse is susceptible only to Hydro weapons. It can be found in the Pearl Patch and Sand Dollar Sea Bar zones while on a Dive in the Sunken City.


## Event Participation

- The Saltwater Axolotl Mouse drops the Sand Dollar Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

The Saltwater Axolotl Mouse was released 2 July 2014 as part of the Sunken City content.
