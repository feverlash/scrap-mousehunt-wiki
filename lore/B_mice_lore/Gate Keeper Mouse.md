## Cheese Preference

Gate Keeper Mouse is only attracted to Lavish Beanster Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Gate Keeper Mouse can only be attracted after planting a Short Vine to the Dungeon Floor.

Physical power type is effective against Gate Keeper Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.


## Loot

Gate Keeper Mouse can drop:

- Ancient Relic
- Lavish Lapis Bean
