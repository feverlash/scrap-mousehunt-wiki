# Blacksmith Mouse

The Blacksmith Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Sandtail Desert region. They are known to drop Papyrus, Scrap Metal and Simple Orb as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Blacksmith mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese, but can only be attracted when a hunter has an Artisan Charm armed.


## History and Trivia

- Blacksmith Mice were introduced on 2 June 2011 with the release of the Muridae Market location.
- Along with the Mage Weaver mouse, it was the first mouse to be introduced that required a specific charm to be armed in order to attract it.
- On 25 June 2013, they gained a vulnerability to Law power type traps, with the release of Varmint Valley.
