# ⚔️ Obelisk of Incineration Trap

> **Classification**: Arcane Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 2,900 Power | 6 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Legendary  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 45,529 Gold2 Scrap Metal1 Coal1 Obelisk of Slumber Trap

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> This magical obelisk fuses super concentrated carbon material with supernatural heat forces to produce an unmeasurably hot flame. The resulting thermal explosion quickly seeks out the nearest object with somewhat limited accuracy. A Nose peg is suggested when using this hunting gear.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Obelisk of Incineration Trap can be purchased from Trapsmith in Catacombs, Forbidden Grove, Acolyte Realm, Balack's Cove, and Fort Rox for:

- 45,529 Gold
- 2 Scrap Metal
- 1 Coal
- 1 Obelisk of Slumber Trap

It cannot be refunded.
