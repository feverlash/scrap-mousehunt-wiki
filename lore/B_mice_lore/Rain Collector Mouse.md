## Preferred Cheese

The Rain Collector Mouse has been found to be especially attracted to Rainy Cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.

## Hunting Strategy

Only Shadow, Arcane and Draconic type traps are effective against the Rain Collector Mouse, all other power types are ineffective.

The Rain Collector Mouse is only available between 0%-34% rain intensities in Moussu Picchu.


## History and Trivia

- 6 September 2017: The Rain Collector Mouse was introduced as part of the Moussu Picchu release.
