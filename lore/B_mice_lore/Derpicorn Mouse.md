# Derpicorn Mouse

> "Derpicorn" redirects here. For other uses, see Your Tiny Derpicorn.

The Derpicorn Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Seasonal Garden during Spring. They are not known to drop any loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Derpicorn Mouse has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Strong Physical weapons are recommended in order to catch this mouse. Alternatively, hunters may equip their best Tactical trap.
