# 🛡️ Denture Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 100 Power | 0 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Grand Duke/Grand Duchess  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 20,000,000 Gold

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> A special membrane designed to channel the thunderous storm energy that resides within Ful'Mina's incredible teeth. By carefully smashing Ful'Mina's Teeth into smaller toothlets, they can be affixed within the specially designed sockets that line the edge of the base.
> With each successful hunt, the base is empowered by a sweet release of lightning! However, it can occasionally cause a toothlet to decay.
> So long as at least one toothlet remains in your inventory to power the base, its stats will be boosted to:
> • 1500 Power
> • 25% Power Bonus
> • 25% Attraction Bonus
> • 20 Luck

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Denture Base is available for purchase for 20,000,000 Gold from Ronza's Shoppe.

It cannot be refunded.
