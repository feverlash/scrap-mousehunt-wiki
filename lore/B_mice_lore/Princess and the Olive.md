## Cheese Preference

Princess and the Olive is only attracted to First Draft Derby Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Princess and the Olive is only available when the stage is writing.

Forgotten power type is effective against Princess and the Olive.
All other types are ineffective.


## Loot

Princess and the Olive can drop:

- Draft Derby Curd

With Leather Bound Cover, the following loot is added to the pool:

- Cleverness Clam
- Crop Coin
- Ingenuity Grub
- Inspiration Ink
- Mythical Mulch
- Pond Penny
- Papyrus Seed
- Parable Papyrus
