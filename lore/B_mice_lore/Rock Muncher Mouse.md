## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Rock Muncher Mouse is attracted only to Limelight Cheese.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Rock Muncher Mouse.

Arming the Mining Charm provides a power boost against Digby Dirt Dwellers.

## Locations

Hunters can encounter the Rock Muncher Mouse only in the Town of Digby.


## Loot

The Rock Muncher Mouse is not known to drop any loot.
