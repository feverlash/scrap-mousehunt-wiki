# Alnitak Mouse

The Alnitak Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Tribal Isles region. They are known to drop Seashells in Elub Shore.

## Preferred Cheese

Alnitak mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## History and Trivia

- Alnitak, Alnilam and Mintaka are the names of the three stars in Orion's belt.
- Alnitak mice began dropping the Antique Blue Argyle Egg as loot, on 16 April 2011, for the Spring Egg Hunt 2011 event.
