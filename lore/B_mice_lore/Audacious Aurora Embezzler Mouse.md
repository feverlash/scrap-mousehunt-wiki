## Cheese Preference

Audacious Aurora Embezzler Mouse is only attracted to Sky Pirate Swiss or Sky Raider Romano.


## Hunting Strategy

The Audacious Aurora Embezzler Mouse can only be attracted during a Spice shipment.

Law power type is effective against Audacious Aurora Embezzler Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
