## Cheese Preference

Classroom Disrupter Mouse is only attracted to Master Mimolette.


## Hunting Strategy

The Classroom Disrupter Mouse can only be attracted while enrolled in Shadow Sciences or during the Shadow phase of the Final Exam.

Shadow power type is very effective against Classroom Disrupter Mouse.
Arcane power type is effective against Classroom Disrupter Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
