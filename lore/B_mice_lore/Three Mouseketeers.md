## Cheese Preference

Three Mouseketeers is only attracted to Deus Ex Maconnais.


## Hunting Strategy

The Three Mouseketeers can only be attracted while writing a story.

Only Law power types are effective against Three Mouseketeers.
All other types are ineffective.
