# Frostbite Mouse

The Frostbite Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Rodentia region. It can be attracted and caught only during the Winter Season. They are not known to drop any loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Frostbite Mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Strong Hydro weapons such as Ancient Spear Gun and Heat Bath are recommended in order to catch this mouse. Alternatively, hunters may equip their best Tactical trap.


## Event Participation

- The Frostbite Mouse drops the Seasonal Garden Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

- Frostbite Mice were introduced on 17 August 2010 with the release of the Seasonal Garden location.
- During the Spring Egg Hunt 2011, Frostbite mice began dropping a bonus Seasonal Garden Egg as loot for hunters who had obtained the Rocket Rabbit Mallet. They dropped the same loot the following year during the Spring Egg Hunt 2012 event for hunters who had obtained the Eggscavator.
