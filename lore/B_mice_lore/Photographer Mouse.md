# Photographer Mouse

The Photographer Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Gnawnian Express Station. They are known to drop a Minuscule Photo Album as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Photographer mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Law type traps such as S.L.A.C. are very effective against the Photographer Mouse.
