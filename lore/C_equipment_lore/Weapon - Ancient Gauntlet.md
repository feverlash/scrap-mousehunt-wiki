> **Classification**: Physical Weapon

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> This long-forgotten trap can lend much-needed aid to hunters attempting the long climb up the King's Gauntlet. While the statues may look dormant and docile, one of them has been secretly replaced with an incredibly patient hunter droid. Let's see if the mice notice!Tier 1 mice caught with this trap will be so frightened, they'll drop two Tier 2 Gauntlet potions!
