# Plankrun

The first ever Mouse Hunter; Plankrun was commissioned by the King to investigate the strange behaviors of the Mice appearing in the region of Gnawnia. His fate is, as of yet, unknown. However, pages torn from his journal have been found across the region, detailing his brave escapades. The page read by the Scribe Mouse mentions that he was known as Sir Plankrun.
