# Winter Mage Mouse

The Winter Mage Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Rodentia region. It can be attracted and caught only during the Winter Season. It is known to drop Winter Key Shard, and occasionally an Ancient Relic, as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

'Winter Mage Mice' have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Strong Hydro weapons such as Ancient Spear Gun and Heat Bath are recommended in order to catch this mouse. Alternatively, hunters may equip their best Tactical trap.


## History and Trivia

- Winter Mage Mice were introduced on 17 August 2010 with the release of the Seasonal Garden location.
- They began dropping Ancient Relic as loot on 29 August 2012.
