# Super Mega Mecha Ultra RoboGold Mouse

The Super Mega Mecha Ultra RoboGold is a breed of mouse found in the Burroughs Rift. It is not known to drop any loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Super Mega Mecha Ultra RoboGold Mouse is only attracted to Polluted Parmesan Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Super Mega Mecha Ultra RoboGold Mouse can be attracted when the Mist Meter is in Green and Red Zone.

Rift power type is effective against Super Mega Mecha Ultra RoboGold Mouse while all others are less effective.


## History and Trivia

- 9 September 2014: The Super Mega Mecha Ultra RoboGold Mouse was released as part of the Burroughs Rift location.
