## Preferred Cheese

The Absolute Acolyte Mouse has been found to be especially attracted to Runic String Cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.

## Hunting Strategy

Only Rift type traps are very effective against Absolute Acolyte Mouse, all other power types are ineffective.

Absolute Acolyte Mouse can only be attracted upon fully charging the Timesplit Obelisk in the Acolyte Chamber and can only be caught after fully draining the Acolyte’s sand.

Completing the Frozen Alcove will prevent the Absolute Acolyte from pillaging the Hunter's trap and it will add additional loot to the Absolute Acolyte when caught.
