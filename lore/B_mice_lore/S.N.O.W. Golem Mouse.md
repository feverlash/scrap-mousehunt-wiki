## History and Trivia

- 10 December 2013: The S.N.O.W. Golem Mouse was introduced as part of the Great Winter Hunt 2013 event.

> If a hunter had not constructed a Snow Fort yet, but had used the required number of Snowball Bocconcini, then a different mouse is attracted and a message is left in the Hunter's Journal:
The S.N.O.W. Golem Mouse has shown his face, but I cannot defeat him without a Snow Fort.
As soon as I have enough Snow Blocks, I must BUILD my Snow Fort if I want to stand a chance against this snowy monster!

- 13 December 2016: The points/gold for S.N.O.W. Golem Mouse were changed.
