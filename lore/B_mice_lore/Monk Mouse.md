## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Monk Mouse is attracted to Standard Cheese in the Training Grounds location.
The Monk Mouse is attracted to Standard Cheese and Maki Cheese in the Dojo location.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Monk Mouse.

## Locations

Hunters can encounter the Monk Mouse in the Training Grounds and Dojo locations.


## Loot

The Monk Mouse drops the following loot in the Training Grounds and Dojo locations:

- Plankrun's Dojo Notes
- Satchel of Gold (750)

The Monk Mouse will drop Luscious Lumber in the Training Grounds location when Hunters are working the Zurreal Trap Research Assignment.
6 Luscious Lumber are required to construct the Zurreal's Folly weapon.


## History and Trivia

- 22 December 2008: The Monk Mouse is released for the first time.
- 23 August 2011: The Monk Mouse began to drop Luscious Lumber as loot in the Training Grounds.
