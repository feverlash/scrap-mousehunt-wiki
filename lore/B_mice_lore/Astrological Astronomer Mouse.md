## Cheese Preference

The Astrological Astronomer Mouse is attracted to Cloud Cheesecake and Standard Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Only Shadow traps are effective against the Astrological Astronomer Mouse, all other power types are ineffective. The Astrological Astronomer Mouse can only be encountered on a Shadow Island or a Shadow Sanctuary.


## Loot


### Floating Islands's Island Based Loot

When found on a Low Altitude Island or a High Altitude Island, depending on the buffs present, the Astrological Astronomer Mouse can drop the following loot:
