## Cheese Preference

The Sky Highborne Mouse is only attracted to Cloud Cheesecake.


## Hunting Strategy

Only Arcane traps are effective against the Sky Highborne Mouse, all other power types are ineffective. The Sky Highborne Mouse can only be encountered on an Arcane Keep.


## Loot


### Floating Islands's Island Based Loot

When found on a High Altitude Island, depending on the buffs present, the Sky Highborne Mouse can drop the following loot:


| Item | Condition |
