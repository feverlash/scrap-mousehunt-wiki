# Corrupt Mouse

The Corrupt Mouse is a breed of mouse that can be found in the Cursed City. It is not known to drop any loot.

## Hunting Strategy

The Corrupt Mouse is only attracted to Graveblossom Camembert.

It is a member of the Lost City Mice group. As such all power types except Arcane are ineffective against it.

During the Fear, Darkness, and Mist Curses Minigame, the Corrupt Mouse cannot be attracted in the first three hunts in Cursed City after all three curses have been lifted. If the Hunter is using a Shattering Charm, the Corrupt Mouse cannot be encountered in the first five hunts instead.


## History and Trivia

- 14 November 2012: The Corrupt Mouse was introduced with the release of the Cursed City location.
- Prior to 14 January 2013, it was possible to have three hunts without attracting a Corrupt Mouse after any one curse was lifted, rather than after all three curses.
