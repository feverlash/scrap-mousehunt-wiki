# ⚔️ Goldfrost Crossbow Trap

> **Classification**: Shadow Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 5,000 Power | 20 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Baron  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 5,510,000 1 Nugget of Nougat

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> A gilded Crossbow Trap with a beautiful patterned design etched into the body. The terrible cold allows the significantly cheaper ice bolt ammunition to survive the impressive heat and speeds of being fired cross-dimensionally with such power!
