# Mage Weaver Mouse

The Mage Weaver Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Sandtail Desert region. They are known to drop Flawless Orb, Ionized Salt, Papyrus and Simple Orb as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Mage Weaver mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese, but can only be attracted when a hunter has an Artisan Charm armed.


## Event Participation


### Spring Egg Hunt

- The Mage Weaver Mouse drops the Mage Weaver Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

- Mage Weaver Mice were introduced on 2 June 2011 with the release of the Muridae Market location.
- Along with the Blacksmith mouse, it was the first mouse to be introduced that required a specific charm to be armed in order to attract it.
- On 25 June 2013, they gained a vulnerability to Law power type traps, with the release of Varmint Valley.
