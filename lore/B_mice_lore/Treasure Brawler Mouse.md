# Treasure Brawler Mouse

The Treasure Brawler Mouse can be found in the Labyrinth and Zokor. It is known to drop Plates of Fealty, Scholar Scrolls, and Tech Power Cores in Zokor.

## Preferred Cheese

The Treasure Brawler Mouse prefers Glowing Gruyere in both the Labyrinth and Zokor, but can be attracted with Standard Cheeses.


## Hunting Strategy

Forgotten weapons are very effective against the Treasure Brawler Mouse. Arcane weapons are of normal effectiveness.

- Labyrinth

> The Treasure Brawler Mouse can be found in Superior Treasury Hallways.

- Zokor

> The Treasure Brawler Mouse can be found in the Treasure Vault.


## Loot

- Zokor

> Labyrinth Theme Scrap IV, Plates of Fealty, Scholar Scrolls, and Tech Power Cores
