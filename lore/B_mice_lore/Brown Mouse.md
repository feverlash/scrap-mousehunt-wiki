# Brown Mouse

> "Brown" redirects here. For more uses, see Brown (disambiguation).

## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Brown Mouse prefers Standard Cheese, but will avoid traps baited with White Cheddar Cheese.

In the Mountain location, the Brown Mouse will not be encountered when hunting with White Cheddar Cheese, Chedd-Ore Cheese, or Abominable Asiago.
In the Windmill location, the Brown Mouse will not be encountered when hunting with White Cheddar Cheese or Grilled Cheese.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Brown Mouse.

## Loot

The Brown Mouse drops various loot in the following locations:

- Meadow and Town of Gnawnia

> Marble Cheese

- Windmill

> Packet of Flour

The Brown Mouse is not known to drop any loot in the Harbour, King's Arms, or Mountain locations.


## History and Trivia

- 7 March 2008: The Brown Mouse was introduced with the release of MouseHunt.
- 25 June 2013: The Brown Mouse gained a vulnerability to Law power type traps, with the release of Varmint Valley.
- 25 March 2014: The Riftweaver Mouse was introduced as the Rift counterpart of the Brown Mouse.
- 25 March 2014: The Brown Mouse gained a vulnerability to Rift power type traps with the introduction of the Rift Plane.
- 2 September 2014: The Brown Mouse began dropping Packet of Flour at the Windmill.
