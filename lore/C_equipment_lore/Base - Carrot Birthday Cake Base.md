# 🛡️ Carrot Birthday Cake Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 175 Power | 3 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Initiate  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 40,000

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> This base is actually a slab of cake sneakily sliced from the carrot cake originally concocted specifically to attract the Dinosuit Mouse. In addition to attracting mice, it also attracts the occasional rabbit, whose passing feet have lent this base a +3 luck bonus.
> A flavour first savoured during MouseHunt's 4th birthday!

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Carrot Birthday Cake Base can be obtained during the annual MouseHunt Birthday event.
