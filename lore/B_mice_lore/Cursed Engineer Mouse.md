# Cursed Engineer Mouse

The Cursed Engineer Mouse is a breed of mouse that can be found in the Cursed City, and is known to drop Aleth Essences, Ber Essences and Plumepearl Herbs as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Cursed Engineer mice have been found to be only attracted to Graveblossom Camembert.


## Hunting Strategy

The Cursed Engineer mouse is a member of the Lost City mouse group. As such all power types except Arcane weapons are ineffective against it.


## History and Trivia

- 14 November 2012: The Cursed Engineer Mouse was released as a part of the Living Garden update.
