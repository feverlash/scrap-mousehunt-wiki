## Island/Vault Info

- To encounter the Admiral Cloudbeard, the Hunter must pick an island or vault with at least 2 Sky Pirate Dens on the Sky Map or Launch Console respectively.


## Loot


### Floating Islands's Sky Pirate Loot

Admiral Cloudbeard has a chance to drop Storm Cell if Rocket Boosters have been attached on the Hunter's Dirigible and the following loot depending on the buffs present:


| Item | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| Sky Glass | Always drops Boosted by Sky Glass Formations and Loot Caches |
| Sky Ore | Always drops Boosted by Sky Ore Deposit and Loot Caches |
| Sky Pirate Seal | Always Drops The amount a mouse drops depends on the mouse caught. |
| Cloud Curd | Drops when a Cloud Curd Bonus is present Can be boosted by Loot Caches |


- Additionally, the Admiral Cloudbeard can drop the following loot as well:
Bottled Wind
Chrome Charm
Cyclone Stone
Rift Tarnished Charm
Storm Cell
Tarnished Charm
Wealth Charm
