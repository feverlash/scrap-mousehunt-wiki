## Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Zombie Mouse. Arming Brain Charm or Super Brain Charm increases the chance of encountering the Zombie Mouse.


## Power Type Weaknesses

It is recommended that Hunters use a trap that will be effective against the main mouse population in their hunting location.

## History and Trivia

- 8 June 2010: The Zombie Mouse began dropping the Tattered Mousoleum Map in the Town of Digby instead of the Laboratory.
- 17 April 2011: The Zombie Mouse began dropping the Antique Stripy Red Egg as loot in the Forbidden Grove as part of the Spring Egg Hunt 2011 event.
- 19 April 2011: The Zombie Mouse began dropping the Antique Scalloped Pink Egg as loot in the Bazaar as part of the Spring Egg Hunt 2011 event.
- 25 June 2013: The Zombie Mouse gained a vulnerability to Law power type traps with the release of Varmint Valley.
- 25 March 2014: The Zombie Mouse gained a vulnerability to Rift power type traps with the introduction of the Rift Plane.
- 17 October 2018: the Zombie Mouse was removed from the Laboratory.
