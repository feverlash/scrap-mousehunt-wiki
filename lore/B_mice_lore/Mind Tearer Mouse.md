# Mind Tearer Mouse

The Mind Tearer Mouse can be found in the Labyrinth and Zokor. It is known to drop Plates of Fealty in Zokor.

## Preferred Cheese

The Mind Tearer Mouse prefers Glowing Gruyere in both the Labyrinth and Zokor, but can be attracted with Standard Cheeses.


## Hunting Strategy

Forgotten weapons are very effective against the Mind Tearer Mouse. Arcane weapons are of normal effectiveness.

- Labyrinth

> The Mind Tearer Mouse can be found in Superior and Epic Fealty Hallways.

- Zokor

> The Mind Tearer Mouse can be found in the Inner Fealty Temple and the Templar's Sanctum.


## Loot

- Zokor

> Labyrinth Theme Scrap III and Plates of Fealty
