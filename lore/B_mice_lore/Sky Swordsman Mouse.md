# Sky Swordsman Mouse

The Sky Swordsman Mouse is a breed of mouse found on the Floating Islands, on a Physical Island or a Physical Palisade.

## Cheese Preference

The Sky Swordsman Mouse is attracted to Cloud Cheesecake.


## Hunting Strategy

Only Physical traps are effective against the Sky Swordsman Mouse, all other power types are ineffective. The Sky Swordsman Mouse can only be encountered on a Physical Island or a Physical Palisade.


## Loot


### Floating Islands's Island Based Loot

When found on a Low Altitude Island or a High Altitude Island, depending on the buffs present, the Sky Swordsman Mouse can drop the following loot:


| Item | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| Sky Glass | Always drops Boosted by Sky Glass Formations and Loot Caches |
| Sky Ore | Always drops Boosted by Sky Ore Deposit and Loot Caches |

| Corsair's Curd | Drops when a Pirate Den is present Can be boosted by Loot Caches |
