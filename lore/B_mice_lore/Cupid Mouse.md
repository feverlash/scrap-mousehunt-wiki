## History and Trivia

- 10 February 2009: The Cupid Mouse was released as part of the Valentine's Giveaway event.
- Prior to 2012, the Cupid Mouse was found to be especially attracted to SUPER|brie+ cheese.
- 10 February 2014: Prior to the Valentine's population shift, the Cupid Mouse could only be found in the Iceberg and Acolyte Realm phases of Calamity Carl's Cozy Cruise.
- The description of the Cupid Mouse has been shortened since its release. The original description was:

> Love is in the air, and so is this mouse! Firing their heart-tipped arrows, these mice are responsible for a slight increase in the mouse population during February. Hunters who get caught in the crossfire may have second thoughts about using their deadly traps on the little mice. Hey, even zombie mice deserve some love! This mouse is available around Valentine's Day.

- 19 March 2024: The Cupid Mouse was relocated to the Training Grounds at the beginning of the Spring Egg Hunt 2024 event. Prior to this relocation, the Cupid Mouse was available in the Valentine's Day or Lunar New Year events.
