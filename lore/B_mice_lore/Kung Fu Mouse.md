## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Kung Fu Mouse is attracted to Standard Cheese in the Training Grounds location.
The Kung Fu Mouse is attracted to Standard Cheese and Maki Cheese in the Dojo location.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Kung Fu Mouse.

## Locations

Hunters can encounter the Kung Fu Mouse in the Training Grounds and Dojo locations.


## Loot

The Kung Fu Mouse drops the following loot in the Dojo location:

- Combat Research (750 points)
- Satchel of Gold (500 gold)

The Kung Fu Mouse will drop Luscious Lumber in the Training Grounds location when Hunters are working the Zurreal Trap Research Assignment.
6 Luscious Lumber are required to construct the Zurreal's Folly weapon.

The Kung Fu Mouse is not known to drop any other loot in the Training Grounds location.


## History and Trivia

- 10 October 2008: The Kung Fu Mouse was released in the Training Grounds, accompanied by Archer and Samurai mice.
- 27 November 2008: The Kung Fu Mouse's habitat expanded to the Dojo when Hunters accessed the Furoma Pagoda locations for the first time.
- 11 December 2008: The Kung Fu Mouse began to drop Satchels of Gold worth 500 gold as additional loot.
- 23 August 2011: The Kung Fu Mouse began to drop Luscious Lumber as loot in the Training Grounds as part of the Zurreal Trap Research Assignment
- 29 July 2015: The Kung Fu Mouse stopped dropping Ticket to the Burroughs Bazaar.
