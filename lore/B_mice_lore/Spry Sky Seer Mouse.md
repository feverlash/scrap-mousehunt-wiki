# Spry Sky Seer Mouse

The Spry Sky Seer Mouse is a breed of mouse found on the Floating Islands, on a Forgotten Island or a Forgotten Fortress.

## Cheese Preference

The Spry Sky Seer Mouse is attracted to Cloud Cheesecake.


## Hunting Strategy

Only Forgotten traps are effective against the Spry Sky Seer Mouse, all other power types are ineffective. The Spry Sky Seer Mouse can only be encountered on a Forgotten Island or a Forgotten Fortress.


## Loot


### Floating Islands's Island Based Loot

When found on a Low Altitude Island or a High Altitude Island, depending on the buffs present, the Spry Sky Seer Mouse can drop the following loot:


| Item | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| Sky Glass | Always drops Boosted by Sky Glass Formations and Loot Caches |
| Sky Ore | Always drops Boosted by Sky Ore Deposit and Loot Caches |
| Cloud Curd | Drops when a Cloud Curd Bonus is present Can be boosted by Loot Caches |
| Corsair's Curd | Drops when a Pirate Den is present Can be boosted by Loot Caches |
