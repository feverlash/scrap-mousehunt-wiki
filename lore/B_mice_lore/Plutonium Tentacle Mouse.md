# Plutonium Tentacle Mouse

The Plutonium Tentacle is a breed of mouse found in the Burroughs Rift. It is known to drop Rift Circuitry as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Plutonium Tentacle Mouse is only attracted to Polluted Parmesan Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Plutonium Tentacle Mouse can only be attracted when the Mist Meter is in Red Zone.

Rift power type is effective against Plutonium Tentacle Mouse while all others are less effective.


## History and Trivia

- 9 September 2014: The Plutonium Tentacle Mouse was released as part of the Burroughs Rift location.
