## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Lycan Mouse is attracted only to Moon Cheese, Crescent Cheese and Radioactive Blue Cheese in all three locations except Mousoleum, where it is not attracted to Radioactive Blue Cheese.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Lycan Mouse.

## Locations

Hunters can encounter the Lycan Mouse in the Catacombs, Forbidden Grove, and Mousoleum locations.


## Loot

The Lycan Mouse drops the following loot:

- Catacombs

> Ancient Relic
Stale Cheese

- Forbidden Grove

> Ancient Relic

- Mousoleum

> Ancient Relic

Hunters may be required to catch the Lycan Mouse to obtain Nuclear Garlic while working an M400 Bait Research Assignment.
Nuclear Garlic is required to craft Fusion Fondue which is used to attract the M400 Mouse.


## History and Trivia

- The name and visual appearance of the Lycan Mouse is based on common incarnations of the lycanthrope legend.
- 10 October 2008: The Lycan Mouse is released in the Mousoleum and drops Shredded Furoma Map Piece, allowing access to the Training Grounds
- January 2009: For a brief period, the Lycan Mouse was also attracted to Cheddar Cheese in the Mousoleum.
- June 2010: The Lycan Mouse was removed from the population of mice available in the Training Grounds. This explains why the Lycan Mouse drops the Furoma map piece. In the Training Grounds, the Lycan Mouse was known to drop Radioactive Blue Cheese as loot.
- 14 April 2011: The Lycan Mouse began dropping the Antique Green Plaid Egg in the Catacombs as part of the Spring Egg Hunt 2011 event.
- 18 April 2011: The Lycan Mouse began dropping the Antique Wavy Purple Egg in the Mousoleum as part of the Spring Egg Hunt 2011 event.
- 29 July 2015: The strength of the Lycan Mouse was reduced and its chance of encounter was increased in the Mousoleum.
- 17 October 2018: The Lycan Mouse stops dropping Shredded Furoma Map Piece in the Mousoleum as part of the Burroughs area overhaul.
