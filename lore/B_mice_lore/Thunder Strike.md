## Preferred Cheese

The Thunder Strike has been found to be especially attracted to Dragonvine Cheese cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.

## Hunting Strategy

Only Draconic type traps are effective against the Thunder Strike, all other power types are ineffective.

Dragonbane Charms, Super Dragonbane Charms and Extreme Dragonbane Charms provide a power bonus against the Thunder Strike.

The Thunder Strike is only available when either wind or rain intensities are between 0%-34% in Moussu Picchu.
