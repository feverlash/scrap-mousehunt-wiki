## Cheese Preference

Herbaceous Bravestalk is only attracted to SUPER|brie+.


## Hunting Strategy

Physical power type is effective against Herbaceous Bravestalk.
All other types are ineffective.


## Loot

Herbaceous Bravestalk can drop:

- Condensed Creativity
- Magic Bean
