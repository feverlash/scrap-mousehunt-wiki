# 📍 Bountiful Beanstalk

> **Entity**: Location | **Region**: Folklore Forest

> **Wilayah / Region**: Folklore Forest  
> **Pangkat Minimal**: Recruit  
> **Fasilitas Lokal**: Cheese ShoppeTrapsmithCharm ShoppeGeneral StoreCartographer

## 🌍 Gambaran & Atmosfer Lingkungan

Bountiful Beanstalk is a hunting location in the Folklore Forest region.

Hunters require a minimum Rank of Viceroy and the Tale of the Bountiful Beanstalk to access.

Hunters start off by looting Magic Beans on the Beanstalk to purchase Beanster Cheese. After catching Vinneus Stalkhome and looting 1 Fabled Fertilizer, Hunters can plant a vine up the beanstalk to enter The Castle in the Clouds.
There are 3 floors within the Castle, the Dungeon Floor, the Ballroom Floor, and the Great Hall Floor, each of which contain rooms that provide different primary loots.
Hunters are able to primarily loot Magic Beans and Lavish Lapis Beans in the Dungeon Floor, Royal Ruby Beans and Golden Harp Strings in the Ballroom Floor, and Golden Goose Eggs in the Great Hall Floor.
Hunters are able to collect Fabled Fertilizers from multiple Vinneus Stalkhome catches, and Giants at the end of each Castle floor.

Golden Harp Strings, Magic Beans, Lavish Lapis Beans, and Royal Ruby Beans earned within the rooms can be used to purchase different type of cheese in Bountiful Beanstalk.
Fabled Fertilizers earned can be used to plant vines to the Dungeon Floor, Ballroom Floor, or Great Hall Floor.

# 📍 Floating Islands

> **Entity**: Location | **Region**: Hollow Heights

> **Wilayah / Region**: Hollow Heights  
> **Pangkat Minimal**: Archduke/Archduchess  
> **Fasilitas Lokal**: Cheese ShoppeTrapsmithCharm ShoppeGeneral StoreCartographer

## 🌍 Gambaran & Atmosfer Lingkungan

Floating Islands is a Location located within the Hollow Heights Region, requiring a minimum Rank of Archduke/Archduchess and the Dirigible and Flight License Map Piece to access.

Floating Islands makes use of 8 different Power Types, nearly every available one in the game, namely:

- Arcane
- Forgotten
- Hydro
- Shadow
- Draconic
- Law
- Physical
- Tactical

Mice here are able to pillage Standard Cheese, Points and Gold if one fails to capture them.

Hunters first collect Cloud Curd to purchase Cloud Cheesecake while at the Launch Pad. All 8 Power Types are effective here.
Subsequently, Hunters fly towards specific Power Type Islands to hunt mice using their Sky Map.
There are 2 versions of each Island, Low Altitude and High Altitude: Hunters must first clear a minimum of 4 Low Altitude Islands and defeat 4 different Wardens to unlock a single High Altitude Island.
While hunting on the islands, mice will drop loot used to upgrade your Skyfarer's Oculus, customise your Dirigible and obtain new Traps.

When Hunters have completed the Explore the Floating Islands Adventure, they can proceed to purchase the High Altitude Flight License from the Cartographer.
Together with 500 Sky Glass and 500 Sky Ore, Hunters can then attach Rocket Boosters onto their Dirigible.
These boosters enable Paragons, Pirates and Richard the Rich in both Low Altitude and High Altitude islands to drop Storm Cells.
Once the Hunter has collected 50 Storm Cells, they can be spent on the Launch Console while at the Launch Pad to journey to the Empyrean Sky Palace instead of a regular island.
The Empyrean Sky Palace consists of different Power Type Vaults which have mice that drop greater amounts of loot as compared to those obtained on regular islands.

Well, I guess that's it -- oh wait, I almost forgot! There have been reports of Sky Pirates roaming the skies in this region so be extra vigilant in your expeditions!

Because trains use the same scheduling mechanic as Tournaments, Hunters must form (or join) a team (though they do not need to add any team members) to board trains. Hunters may not be in a Tournament at the same time they are aboard a train. Hunters may only schedule 1 train at a time. This also means that Hunters are limited to scheduling no more than 1 train and 2 Tournaments at any time.

Amplifier modifies power and luck, thus greatly affects the Hunter's ability to catch mice here.

Each trip can be a single or double run. After each run, the Hunter need to charge the amplifier in Seasonal Garden again before doing the next run.

Hunters must complete the Capture the Mystic and Technic Kings Adventure to obtain the Sandtail Call to Arms, which is necessary to gain access to the Fiery Warpath.

Hunters wishing to travel to the Crystal Library location must obtain a Crystal Library Card from the mice found in this location.
