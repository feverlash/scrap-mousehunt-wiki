# 📜 Adventure Book

> **Entity**: Adventure_lore

The Adventure Book is a new way to see what big challenges you've accomplished and which still await you!

The Adventure Book is a feature added to the game on 29 July 2015. It contains various objectives/quests, called "Adventures", a hunter can embark on that focus on catching a powerful mouse, unlocking a new area, or unlocking new traps in specific areas. As a hunter progresses through the various ranks in the game, they will unlock more Adventures. Adventures do not have to be started as you can work on all the Adventures at the same time, but once one is completed and you claim the reward you can not restart that Adventure. The Adventure Book is completely optional and has no negative effects on the game if not done. 

Each major step will be checked off as you complete it to track which steps of the Adventure you've become experienced in. Within the Adventure details you'll also see what sort of power types are suggested, the length of the Adventure, the purpose of the adventure, and a description hinting at possible rewards and strategy. All adventures award Gold and Points with some awarding King's Credit when completed.

When completing a step on an Adventure, the following message appears in the Hunter's Journal:

> I made progress on an adventure!
[Adventure Name]
[Adventure Step]

Upon claiming a reward for completing an Adventure, the following message appears in the Hunter's Journal:

> I claimed a reward for completing [Adventure Name]!
I earned:
[Rewards]

The Adventure Book can be opened by clicking the banner at the top of your hunter's journal (if it is not available, the settings should be checked to ensure that the Hide Adventure Book option has been disabled). In the book you'll see of a list of adventures spanning the entire Kingdom organized by the Hunter's Title required to start each one. If you're unable to start an adventure, you'll see a message explaining what you need to do in order to unlock the adventure. An Adventure can not be started unless the hunter meets the rank requirement or has a certain map key. Towards the top-right corner of each uncompleted adventure is a small star icon. Click the star icon to select it as your current adventure. Whichever adventure you have chosen to "star" will always be displayed above your journal no matter where you travel.
