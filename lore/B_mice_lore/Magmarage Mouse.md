# Magmarage Mouse

The Magmarage Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Fiery Warpath location. It can be attracted and caught only during Wave Three. They are known to drop Heatproof Mage Cloth.

## Preferred Cheese

Magmarage Mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## History and Trivia

- Magmarage Mice were introduced on 22 March 2011 with the release of the Sandtail Desert region.
- Magmarage Mice began dropping the Antique Stripy Red Egg as loot, on 17 April 2011, for the Spring Egg Hunt 2011 event.
