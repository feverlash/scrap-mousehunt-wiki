## Cheese Preference

Obstinate Oboist Mouse is only attracted to Beanster Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Obstinate Oboist Mouse can only be attracted after planting a Medium Vine to the Ballroom Floor.

Physical power type is effective against Obstinate Oboist Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.


## Loot

Obstinate Oboist Mouse can drop:

- Golden Harp String
- Royal Ruby Bean
