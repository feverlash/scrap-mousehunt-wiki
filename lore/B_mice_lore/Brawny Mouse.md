# Brawny Mouse

The Brawny Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Gnawnia Rift and Furoma Rift.

## Preferred Cheese

In Gnawnia Rift, Brawny mice have been found to be especially attracted to String cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.

In Furoma Rift, the Brawny Mouse is only attracted to Marble String or Swiss String Cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.
