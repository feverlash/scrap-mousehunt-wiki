# 🛡️ Bacon Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 200 Power | 0 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Initiate  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 70,000

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> This odd little base was granted to Ronza for her help in defeating a Nether Pig plaguing a small village in a far off land. Thankfully it's not actually made of real meat, but rather a cleverly painted polycarbonate slab.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Bacon Base was available for purchase for 70,000 gold from Ronza's Traveling Shoppe during Ronza's January 2011 visit. It refunded for 14,000 gold at the same location. It can no longer be obtained.
