# ⚔️ New Year's Fireworks Trap

> **Classification**: Physical Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 6,000 Power | 23 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Knight  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 600,000

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Grand, gilded and jam-packed with a colourful array of highly-explosive fireworks! Every time the trap is triggered, a whole glorious fireworks display is created.
> Now you can celebrate 2017 with every catch!

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The New Year's Fireworks Trap is a Limited Edition weapon and can no longer be acquired by Hunters. It was available for sale in the Great Winter Hunt 2016 Trapsmith for 600,000 gold and required Hunters to collect 100 Deployed Festive New Year's Fireworks. The fireworks were not spent when purchasing the trap.
