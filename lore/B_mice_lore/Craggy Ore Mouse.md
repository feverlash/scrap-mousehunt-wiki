## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Craggy Ore Mouse can only be attracted with Chedd-Ore Cheese.

Chedd-Ore Cheese is obtained by smashing boulders in the Mountain location.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Craggy Ore Mouse.

## Locations

Hunters can encounter the Craggy Ore Mouse only in the Mountain location.


## Loot

The Craggy Ore Mouse drops Iced Curd as loot which is used to craft Abominable Asiago Cheese. It also drops Power Charm as loot.


## History and Trivia

- 7 November 2017: The Craggy Ore Mouse was introduced as part of the Mountain overhaul.
- 17 October 2018: The Craggy Ore Mouse changed mouse groups from Indigenous Mice to Mountain Mice.
