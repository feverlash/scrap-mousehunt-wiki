# Dehydrated Mouse

> "Dehydrated" redirects here. For similar uses, see Dehydration (disambiguation).

The Dehydrated Mouse is a breed of mouse that can be found in the Twisted Garden, and is known to drop Cynd Essence, Dol Essence and Graveblossom Petals as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Dehydrated Mouse is only attracted to Duskshade Camembert.


## Hunting Strategy

The Dehydrated Mouse is a member of the Living Garden mouse group. As such all power types except Hydro weapons are ineffective against it.

It can only be attracted during the Red and Yellow Dewdrop Minigame.


## History and Trivia

- 14 November 2012: The Dehydrated Mouse was released as a part of the Living Garden update.
