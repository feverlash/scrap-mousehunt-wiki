# Old One Mouse

The Old One Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Sunken City. It is known to drop Mouse Scales and Sand Dollars as loot.

## Cheese Preference

The Old One Mouse has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Old One Mouse is susceptible only to Hydro weapons. It can be found in the Lost Ruins zone while on a Dive in the Sunken City.


## Event Participation


### Spring Egg Hunt

- The Old One Mouse drops the Deep, Deep, Deep Egg and the Mouse Scale Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

The Old One Mouse was released 2 July 2014 as part of the Sunken City content.
