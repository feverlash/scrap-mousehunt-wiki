# Vanguard Mouse

The Vanguard Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Fiery Warpath location. It can be attracted and caught only during the Wave One. They are not known to drop any loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Vanguard Mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## History and Trivia

- Vanguard Mice were introduced on 22 March 2011 with the release of the Sandtail Desert region.
- Vanguard Mice began dropping the Antique Green Plaid Egg as loot, on 14 April 2011, for the Spring Egg Hunt 2011 event.
