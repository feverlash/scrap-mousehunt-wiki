## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> To all mice who approach, the wise well will offer them a proposition: "Answer the riddle I propose and all this cheese will be yours to hold!" The trick is that the well acknowledges every answer as correct. As the mouse climbs onto the bucket of delicious cheese to collect their prize, the bucket quickly plunges into a bottomless pit.
> The wisest one knows that there is no such thing as "free cheese".

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Well of Wisdom Trap is a Limited Edition weapon. It can be purchased for 600,000 gold from Ronza's Traveling Shoppe 2018.
