## History and Trivia

- 2 December 2011: The Destructoy Mouse was painted live during an 'All about the art' Feedback Friday. Ideas and suggestions for how the mouse should look were provided by the audience at the time of painting.
- 7 December 2011: The Destructoy Mouse was released as part of the Great Winter Hunt 2011.
- 13 December 2016: The points/gold for Destructoy Mouse were changed.
