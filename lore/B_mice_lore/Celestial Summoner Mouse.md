## Cheese Preference

Celestial Summoner Mouse is only attracted to Apprentice Ambert.


## Hunting Strategy

The Celestial Summoner Mouse can only be attracted during the Shadow phase of the Final Exam.

Shadow power type is very effective against Celestial Summoner Mouse.
Arcane power type is effective against Celestial Summoner Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
