## Cheese Preference

M1000 is only attracted to Thousandth Draft Derby Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The M1000 is only available when the stage is writing. Catching it will add 1,066 words to the book. With both the Silver Quill and Golden Quill upgrades, combined with using Condensed Creativity, catching the M1000 will yield 4000 words, enough to go directly to the Encyclopedia stage of writing.

All power types are effective against M1000 except Parental.
