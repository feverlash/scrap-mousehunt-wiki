# 🛡️ Soiled Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 400 Power | 6 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Baron/Baroness  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 1 Fel Essence1 Gur Essence1 Hix Essence3 Fresh Twisted Garden Soil1 Dewthief Petal1 Duskshade Petal1 Graveblossom Petal1 Lunaria Petal

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> This base is comprised of a full level of soil, carefully lifted from the Living Garden. It's soft underfoot and relaxing to mice venturing upon it, but contains many living roots that can wrap around tiny mice feet, holding them in place for traps to snap shut!

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Soiled Base can be purchased from Trapsmith in Living Garden, Twisted Garden, Lost City, Cursed City, Sand Dunes, or Sand Crypts for:

- 1 Fel Essence
- 1 Gur Essence
- 1 Hix Essence
- 3 Fresh Twisted Garden Soil
- 1 Dewthief Petal
- 1 Duskshade Petal
- 1 Graveblossom Petal
- 1 Lunaria Petal

It cannot be refunded.

The Soiled Base (if unintentionally smashed) can be reassembled from Soiled Base Parts.
