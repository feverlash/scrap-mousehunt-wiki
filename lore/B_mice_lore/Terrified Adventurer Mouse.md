## Floor Type Info

- The Terrified Adventurer Mouse can be found on All Floors except Eclipse.
Capturing the Terrified Adventurer Mouse advances the Hunter up the Valour Rift tower 2X steps, where X is the Hunter's Speed level.
Capturing the Terrified Adventurer Mouse with Champion's Fire enabled advances the Hunter up the tower by 2X+2 steps.
The String Stepping augmentation doubles the progress made when capturing the Terrified Adventurer Mouse:
Capturing the Terrified Adventurer Mouse with String Stepping but not Champion's Fire enabled advances the Hunter up the tower by 4X steps.
Capturing the Terrified Adventurer Mouse with both String Stepping and Champion's Fire enabled advances the Hunter up the tower by 4X+4 steps.
- The Terrified Adventurer Mouse has a Minimum Floor of 1.


## Loot
