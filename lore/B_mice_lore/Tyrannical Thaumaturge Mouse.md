## Cheese Preference

Tyrannical Thaumaturge Mouse is only attracted to Master Mimolette.


## Hunting Strategy

The Tyrannical Thaumaturge Mouse can only be attracted during the Arcane phase of the Final Exam.

Arcane power type is very effective against Tyrannical Thaumaturge Mouse.
Shadow power type is effective against Tyrannical Thaumaturge Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
