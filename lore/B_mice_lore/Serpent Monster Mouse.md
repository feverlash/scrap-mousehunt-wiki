# Serpent Monster Mouse

The Serpent Monster Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Sunken City. It is known to drop Brined Curd, Flawless Orb and Super Power Charm as loot.

## Cheese Preference

The Serpent Monster Mouse has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Serpent Monster Mouse is susceptible only to Hydro weapons. It can be found in the Carnivore Cove and Monster Trench zones while on a Dive in the Sunken City.


## Event Participation


### Spring Egg Hunt

- The Serpent Monster Mouse drops the Deep, Deep, Deep Egg and the Underwater Predator Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

The Serpent Monster Mouse was released 2 July 2014 as part of the Sunken City content.
