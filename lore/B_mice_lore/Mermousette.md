# Mermousette

The Mermousette is a breed of mouse found in the Sunken City. Is known to drop the Dinglehopper collectible as loot.

## Cheese Preference

The Mermousette has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Mermousette is susceptible only to Hydro weapons. It can be found in the Mermouse Den zone while on a Dive in the Sunken City.


## Event Participation

- The Mermousette drops the Mouse Scale Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

The Mermousette was released 2 July 2014 as part of the Sunken City content.
