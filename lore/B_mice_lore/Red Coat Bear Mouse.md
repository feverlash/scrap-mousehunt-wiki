## Preferred Cheese

Red Coat Bear mice have been found to be especially attracted to String Cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.


## Hunting Strategy

The Red Coat Bear Mouse is weak to Rift Traps. It can only be attracted by String Cheese.

The Red Coat Bear Mouse is only available when rage in the Gigantic Gnarled Tree section in Whisker Woods Rift is between 25-49.


## Event Participation


### Spring Egg Hunt

- The Red Coat Bear Mouse drops the Enlarged Rift Egg and the Rift Mist Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

- Red Coat Bear mice were introduced on 28 January 2015, with the introduction of the Whisker Woods Rift.
- Red Coat Bear mice are the Rift version of Bear mice.
