# Ethereal Thief Mouse

The Ethereal Thief Mouse is a breed of mouse that can be found in the Lost City, and is known to drop Aleth Essences, Ber Essences and Dreamfluff Herbs.

## Preferred Cheese

Ethereal Thief mice have been found to be only attracted to Dewthief Camembert.


## Hunting Strategy

The Ethereal Thief mouse is a member of the Lost City mouse group. As such all power types except Arcane weapons are ineffective against it.


## History and Trivia

- 14 November 2012: The Ethereal Thief Mouse was released as a part of the Living Garden update.
