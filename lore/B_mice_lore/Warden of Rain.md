| A tsunami of power embodied within a cumulonimbus of rage. She began as a single, fallen tear from a scorned mouse over the steep edge of Moussu Picchu. The tear was hurled up into the atmosphere at tremendous speeds by the region's intensely shifting winds and was charged with supernatural fury by passing through the magical storms at the mountain's peak. The tear grew into the epicenter of this incredible anomaly of rain and wrath whose sole purpose is to enact a watery vengeance upon Gnawnian hunters who attempt to invade the islands. |  |  |  |

## Island Info

- To encounter the Warden of Rain, the Hunter must pick a Low Altitude Island with a Shrine of Rain present on the Sky Map.
- After catching the Warden of Rain, the Shrine of Rain will not be available on subsequent Sky Maps until ​the Hunter has left a High Altitude Island.
