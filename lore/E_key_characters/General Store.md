# General Store

> General Stores sell items that are used for crafting. Each General Store is unique to its location, and sells different items.

General Stores are located in several locations throughout the kingdom. Players may purchase items for crafting there. At times, Ronza's Traveling Shoppe has also carried supplies in a General Store.
