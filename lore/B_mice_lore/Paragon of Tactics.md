## Island Info

- To encounter the Paragon of Tactics, the Hunter must pick a Tactical Castle on the Sky Map.
- After catching the Paragon of Tactics, the Tactical Castle will not be available on subsequent Sky Maps until ​the Hunter has defeated all 4 Sky Wardens.


## Loot

- Depending on the buff present, the Paragon of Tactics will drop the corresponding High Altitude Loot:


| Buff | High Altitude Loot |

| Paragon Silk Shrine | Skysoft Silk |

## History and Trivia

- 08 September 2020: The Paragon of Tactics was introduced with the release of the Floating Islands.
