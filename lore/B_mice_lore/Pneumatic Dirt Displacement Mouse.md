# Pneumatic Dirt Displacement Mouse

The Pneumatic Dirt Displacement Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Burroughs Rift. It is not known to drop any loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Pneumatic Dirt Displacement Mouse is only attracted to Terre Ricotta Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Pneumatic Dirt Displacement Mouse can be attracted when the Mist Meter is in Yellow and Green Zone.

Rift power type is effective against Pneumatic Dirt Displacement Mouse while all others are less effective.


## History and Trivia

- 9 September 2014: The Pneumatic Dirt Displacement Mouse was released as part of the Burroughs Rift location.
