## Cheese Preference

Quarrelsome Quartermaster Mouse is only attracted to Sky Raider Romano.


## Hunting Strategy

The Quarrelsome Quartermaster Mouse can be attracted at any point in the area.

Law power type is very effective against Quarrelsome Quartermaster Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
