# Surgeon Bot Mouse

The Surgeon Bot Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Burroughs Rift. It is known to drop Mist Canisters and Optic Receiver as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Surgeon Bot Mouse can be attracted using any Standard String Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Surgeon Bot Mouse can be attracted when the Mist Meter is in No Mist Zone.

Rift power type is effective against Surgeon Bot Mouse while all others are less effective.


## History and Trivia

- 9 September 2014: The Surgeon Bot Mouse was released as part of the Burroughs Rift location.
