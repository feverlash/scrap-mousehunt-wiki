## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Mild Spicekin Mouse is only attracted to Mild Queso, Medium Queso, or Hot Queso.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Mild Spicekin Mouse.


## Hunting Strategy

Only Draconic traps are effective against the Mild Spicekin Mouse, all other power types are ineffective. The Mild Spicekin Mouse can only be encountered in Queso Geyser while either a Tiny Eruption, Small Eruption, Medium Eruption, Large Eruption or a Epic Eruption is triggered.

Dragonbane Charm, Super Dragonbane Charm, Extreme Dragonbane Charm, and Ultimate Dragonbane Charm provide a power bonus against the Mild Spicekin Mouse.


## History and Trivia

- 30 April 2019: The Mild Spicekin Mouse was introduced, with the release of the Queso Geyser area.
