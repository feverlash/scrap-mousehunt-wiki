## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> The Soap Dispensing Pollution Disposal Unit, a.k.a the Scum Scrubber, is the Laboratory's way of fighting back and cleaning up the mess that was made. Twin sudsidizers produce the soapy liquid that is shot out of the Pressure-MASTER™ 3000 at 3000 PSI of pure cleansing power! The Clean-Claw™ holds mice still while they get the shower of their life! The claw collects leftover toxins and residue and properly* disposes it.*Disposal method carried out in accordance with Section 45, Line 452 of the King's Safety Act of 1999.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Scum Scrubber can be purchased from the Trapsmith in Toxic Spill for:

- 1 Scum Scrubber Blueprints
- 400 Pollutinum
- 20 Scrap Metal
- 40 Soapy Suds
- 4 Canister Ring

It cannot be refunded.

The Scum Scrubber (if unintentionally smashed) can be reassembled from Scum Scrubber Parts.
