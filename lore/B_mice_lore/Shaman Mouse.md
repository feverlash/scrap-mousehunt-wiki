## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Shaman Mouse is attracted mainly to Standard Cheese.

In the Calm Clearing location, the Shaman Mouse will also be attracted when hunting with Cherry Cheese.
In the Great Gnarled Tree location, the Shaman Mouse will also be attracted when hunting with Gnarled Cheese.
In the Lagoon location, the Shaman Mouse will not be attracted when hunting with Gnarled Cheese or Wicked Gnarly Cheese.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Shaman Mouse.

## Locations

Hunters can encounter the Shaman Mouse in the Calm Clearing, Great Gnarled Tree, andLagoon locations.


## Loot

The Shaman Mouse drops Splintered Wood in the Calm Clearing, Great Gnarled Tree, andLagoon locations.


## History and Trivia

- 31 March 2009: The Shaman Mouse was released with the introduction of the Calm Clearing.
- 14 April 2011: The Shaman Mouse began dropping the Antique Green Plaid Egg as loot as part of the Spring Egg Hunt 2011 event.
- 25 June 2013: The Shaman Mouse gained a vulnerability to Law traps with the release of Varmint Valley.
