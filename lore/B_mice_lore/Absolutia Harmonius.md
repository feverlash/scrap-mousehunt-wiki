## Cheese Preference

Absolutia Harmonius is only attracted to Elemental Emmental.


## Hunting Strategy

The Absolutia Harmonius can only be attracted while hunting in an Elemental Dragons' Den.

Only Draconic power types are effective against Absolutia Harmonius.
All other types are ineffective.
