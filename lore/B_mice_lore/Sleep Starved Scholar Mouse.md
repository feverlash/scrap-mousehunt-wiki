## Cheese Preference

Sleep Starved Scholar Mouse is only attracted to Standard Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Sleep Starved Scholar Mouse can only be attracted during the Arcane phase of the Final Exam.

Arcane power type is very effective against Sleep Starved Scholar Mouse.
Shadow power type is effective against Sleep Starved Scholar Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
