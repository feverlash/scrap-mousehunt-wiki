## Cheese Preference

Pernicious Prince Mouse is only attracted to Royal Beanster Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Pernicious Prince Mouse can only be attracted after planting a Tall Vine to the Great Hall Floor.

Physical power type is very effective against Pernicious Prince Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.


## Loot

Pernicious Prince Mouse can drop:

- Ancient Relic
- Golden Goose Egg
