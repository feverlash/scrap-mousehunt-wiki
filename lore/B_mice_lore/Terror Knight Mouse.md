# Terror Knight Mouse

The Terror Knight Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Catacombs. It is known to drop Scrap Metal as loot.

## Hunting Strategy

Arcane traps are very effective against the Terror Knight Mouse. Forgotten and Shadow traps are of normal effectiveness.
