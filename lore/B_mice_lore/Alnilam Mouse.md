# Alnilam Mouse

The Alnilam Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Tribal Isles region. They are known to drop Savoury Vegetables in Nerg Plains.

## Preferred Cheese

Alnilam mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Alnilam is a typical member of The Nerg Tribe group. As such Tactical weapons are very effective against it, and all other power types are ineffective.


## History and Trivia

- Alnilam, Alnitak and Mintaka are the names of the three stars in Orion's belt.
- Alnilam mice began dropping the Antique Stripy Red Egg as loot, on 17 April 2011, for the Spring Egg Hunt 2011 event.
