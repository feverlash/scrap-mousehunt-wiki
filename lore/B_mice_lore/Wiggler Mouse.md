## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Wiggler Mouse is attracted only to Standard Cheese.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Wiggler Mouse.

## Locations

Hunters can encounter the Wiggler Mouse in the Calm Clearing and Great Gnarled Tree locations.


## Loot

The Wiggler Mouse is not known to drop any loot.


## History and Trivia

- 31 March 2009: The Wiggler Mouse was released with the introduction of the Calm Clearing.
- 14 April 2011: The Wiggler Mouse began dropping a bonus Whisker Woods Egg as loot as part of the annual Spring Egg Hunt.
- 25 June 2013: The Wiggler Mouse gained a vulnerability to Law traps, with the release of Varmint Valley.
- Previously the Wiggler Mouse was also found at the Lagoon.
