# Ethereal Enchanter Mouse

The Ethereal Enchanter Mouse is a breed of mouse that can be found in the Lost City, and are known to drop Aleth Essences and Dreamfluff Herbs as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Ethereal Enchanters have been found to be only attracted to Dewthief Camembert.


## Hunting Strategy

Ethereal Enchanter is a member of the Lost City mouse group. As such all power types except Arcane weapons are ineffective against it.


## History and Trivia

- 14 November 2012: The Ethereal Enchanter Mouse was released as a part of the Living Garden update.
