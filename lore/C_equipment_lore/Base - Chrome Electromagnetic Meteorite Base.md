# 🛡️ Chrome Electromagnetic Meteorite Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 1250 Power | 25 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Archduke  
> **Acquisition Cost**: #Obtained Via

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> This base was originally composed of a solar panel, battery pack, and strange, almost alien technology that had the ability to attract chunks of meteorite after charging up enough energy. It only seemed to activate when the sun went down and the charging cycle had completed, which caused the glyphs etched into its surface to glow, and something from deep within to hum...
> However, the original design blueprints that contained the secrets to this base's extraordinary effects was lost to the Trapsmiths of Gnawnia... until now! It turns out that the villainous Captain Crook discovered and then stole these lost blueprints during her raid of Fort Rox so that she could make her own pirated version. Now that the design secrets have been recovered, the Trapsmith at the Cerulean Skyport is able to forge this new, stronger, and shinier version using the miraculous combination of Cerulean Chrome that has been charged with the energy from Adorned Empyrean Jewels!
> Use this base during the night in Fort Rox for a chance to find Meteorite Pieces.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Chrome Electromagnetic Meteorite Base can be purchased from the Trapsmith in Cerulean Skyport for:

- 5,000,000 Gold
- 20 Chrome Electromagnetic Meteorite Blueprint Scrap
- 200 Cerulean Chrome Ingot
- 3 Adorned Empyrean Jewel

It requires the Hunter to own the Chrome Base and complete the Liberate the Cerulean Skyport from Captain Crook adventure to purchase.

It cannot be refunded.
