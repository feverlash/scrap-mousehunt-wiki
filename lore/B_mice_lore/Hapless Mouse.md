## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Hapless Mouse is attracted only to Standard Cheese in the Meditation Room, Dojo, and Pinnacle Chamber locations.
The Hapless Mouse is not attracted to SUPER|brie+ in the Dojo location.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Hapless Mouse.

## Locations

Hunters can encounter the Hapless Mouse in the Meditation Room, Dojo, and Pinnacle Chamber locations.


## Loot

The Hapless Mouse drops the Ambush Trap Blueprints in the Meditation Room and Pinnacle Chamber locations.
The Ambush Trap Blueprints are required to craft the Ambush Trap.

The Hapless Mouse is not known to drop any loot in the Dojo location.


## History and Trivia

- The Hapless Mouse was attracted to Combat, Glutter, and Susheese cheeses when first released.
- 26 November 2008: The Hapless Mouse is released
- 2 December 2008: The Hapless Mouse began to drop Ambush Trap Blueprints as loot.
- 19 April 2010: The Hapless Mouse began dropping the Antique Scalloped Pink Egg as loot in the Pinnacle Chamber as part of the Spring Egg Hunt 2011 event.
