# Magmatic Crystal Thief Mouse

The Magmatic Crystal Thief is a breed of mouse found in the Gnawnian Express Station. It can only be attracted and caught during the Daredevil Canyon phase and only with Magmatic Crystal Charms armed. They are known to drop Fuel Nuggets and Tin Scraps as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Magmatic Crystal Thief mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese, but can only be attracted when a hunter has a Magmatic Crystal Charm armed.


## Hunting Strategy

Law type traps such as S.L.A.C. are very effective against the Magmatic Crystal Thief Mouse.


## Event Participation

- The Magmatic Crystal Thief Mouse drops the Gnawnian Express Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

- Released on 26 September 2013 as part of the Gnawnian Express Station content.
