## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Steel Mouse is attracted to Standard Cheese.

In the Mountain location, the Steel Mouse is not attracted to Chedd-Ore Cheese or Abominable Asiago Cheese.
In the Bazaar and Town of Gnawnia locations, the Steel Mouse is also attracted to Gilded Cheese.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Steel Mouse.

## Loot

The Steel Mouse drops Marble Cheese and Satchel of Gold (500) in the Meadow.

The Steel Mouse has a chance to drop Scrap Metal in all locations but if captured with the Digby Drillbot or the Limited Edition Chrome DrillBot it drops Scrap Metal every time.


## History and Trivia

- 10 August 2010: The Steel Mouse started to drop Scrap Metal as loot when captured using the Digby DrillBot or the Chrome DrillBot.
- 15 December 2010: The Steel Mouse began dropping Seasoned Gouda Potions as part of the Great Winter Hunt 2010 event.
- 25 June 2013: The Steel Mouse gained a vulnerability to Law power type traps with the release of Varmint Valley.
- 25 March 2014: The Steel Mouse gained a vulnerability to Rift power type traps with the introduction of the Rift Plane.
- 25 March 2014: The Spiritual Steel Mouse was introduced as the Rift counterpart of the Steel Mouse.
- 14 October 2014: The Steel Mouse was given new art. The costumed version appeared during the Halloween 2014 event and the new uncostumed version appeared when the event ended on 4 November 2014.
- 17 October 2018: The Steel Mouse was removed from the Laboratory.
- 22 March 2022: The Steel Mouse got a chance to drop Scrap Metal in every location it is available at irrespective of the trap.
