## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Devilishly intelligent hunters have found an incredible use for, and an excellent recycling opportunity by harnessing the power of the dragon embers to melt and fuse the excess pieces of scrap metal. Gears of platinum churn the massive quantity of compressed molten metal which provides a significant power bonus. Any attempts to record this design upon a blueprint, quickly result in ashes, making this base's design especially secret.
