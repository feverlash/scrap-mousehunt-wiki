## History and Trivia

- 10 December 2012: Greedy Al was released as part of the Great Winter Hunt 2012 event, but remaine locked and was unavailable for Hunters to catch.
- 25 December 2012: Greedy Al was unlocked and could be caught by Hunters only after the construction of a Wrapped Gift Trap. When caught, Greedy Al wrote the following message in the Hunter's Journal:

> I gave Greedy Al his childhood presents!
A single tear rolled down his mousey cheek and he smiled at me.
He was so thrilled that he decided to remain behind and hand out presents to any mice who visit my trap! He thanked me for everything I had done and for renewing his spirit for the season!

- Greedy Al is listed as Naughty on Naughty List Printing Press Base image.
