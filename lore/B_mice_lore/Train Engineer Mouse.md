# Train Engineer Mouse

The Train Engineer Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Gnawnian Express Station. It can only be attracted and caught during the Daredevil Canyon phase. They are known to drop Dusty Coal Charm, Fuel Nuggets and Tin Scraps as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Train Engineer mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Law type traps such as S.L.A.C. are very effective against the Train Engineer Mouse.


## Event Participation

- The Train Engineer Mouse drops the Gnawnian Express Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

- Released on 26 September 2013 as part of the Gnawnian Express Station content.
