# 🛡️ Living Grove Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 450 Power | 8 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Baron/Baroness  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 3,500,000 Gold

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> A Soiled Base with a special topical fungal agent seeded with Nightshade. This base absorbs moisture from its environment and once it has absorbed enough, it will produce a Cavern Fungus or a Nightshade.
> When armed, it has a chance to produce Cavern Fungus or Nightshade when catching mice anywhere in the Kingdom.
> When armed in the Fungal Cavern, its localized ecosystem stimulates the growth of the flora causing it to produce Cavern Fungus or Nightshade on every successful catch!
> The Living Grove Base also benefits from arming Growth and Wild Growth Charms. Equipping a Growth or Wild Growth charm increases the base's Luck, Power, Power Bonus and Attraction rate!

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Living Grove Base is not sold by any Trapsmith. It can only be crafted given the proper ingredients.
