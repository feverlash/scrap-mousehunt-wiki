## Island Info

- To encounter the Paragon of Forgotten, the Hunter must pick a Forgotten Fortress on the Sky Map.
- After catching the Paragon of Forgotten, the Forgotten Fortress will not be available on subsequent Sky Maps until ​the Hunter has defeated all 4 Sky Wardens.


## Loot

- Depending on the buff present, the Paragon of Forgotten will drop the corresponding High Altitude Loot:


| Buff | High Altitude Loot |
| --- | --- |
| Paragon Bangle Shrine | Cloudstone Bangle |
| Paragon Silk Shrine | Skysoft Silk |
| Paragon Sprocket Shrine | Sky Sprocket |
| Paragon Wing Shrine | Enchanted Wing |


- Additionally, the Paragon of Forgotten can also drop the following loot:
Ancient Relic
Cyclone Stone
Divine Orb
Storm Cell


## History and Trivia

- 08 September 2020: The Paragon of Forgotten was introduced with the release of the Floating Islands.
