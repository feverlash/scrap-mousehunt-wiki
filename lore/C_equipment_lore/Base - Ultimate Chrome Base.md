# 🛡️ Ultimate Chrome Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 1250 Power | 25 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Archduke  
> **Acquisition Cost**: #Obtained Via

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> By combining the remarkable powers of all the many Chrome bases that have been discovered, the Trapsmith at the Cerulean Skyport has finally created one Chrome base to rule them all... the Ultimate Chrome Base!
> This incredible base infuses massive quantities of Cerulean Chrome with an enormous amount of energy harnessed from Adorned Empyrean Jewels in order to power its long list of special effects.
> The Ultimate Chrome Base has the following special effects while using it:
> • Anywhere: Spawns a Chrome Skull every 300 hunts
> • Anywhere: Provides a 100% chance to roll up any armed Chrome Charm into its next, more powerful version
> • In the Tribal Isles: Increases quantity range for drops of Seashells, Savoury Vegetables, Delicious Stones, and all Seeds
> • In the Tribal Isles: Spawns a pepper that matches whatever seed a mouse drops
> • In the Sandtail Desert: Consumes a Flameshard every hunt, mice drop bonus loot when caught
> • In all Living Garden related areas: Chance to generate a random essence
> • In all Living Garden related areas: Increases petal and herb drops
> • In the Sand Crypts: Adds +1 to the salt level upon catching a mouse with any salt charm
> • In the Sand Crypts: Automatically attracts the King Grub Mouse when the salt level reaches 50
> • In the Lost/Cursed City: 50% chance to break any curse upon catching a mouse
> • In Fort Rox (Night/Dawn Phase): Chance to find Meteorite Pieces
> • In the Queso Canyon: Consumes 5 Solidified Amber Queso every hunt, mice drop bonus loot when caught
> • In Zokor: Activate Stealth Mode to provide a 50% chance to not lose Stealth
> • In Zokor: Activate Heist Mode to double Stealth lost but also double Fealty, Tech, and Scholar district loot gained
> • In Moussu Picchu: Activate Forecasting Mode to double weather intensity gained on every catch based on cheese armed
> • In Moussu Picchu: Activate Condensing Mode to prevent the weather intensity from increasing

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Ultimate Chrome Base can be purchased from the Trapsmith in Cerulean Skyport for:

- 5,000,000 Gold
- 200 Cerulean Chrome Ingot
- 3 Adorned Empyrean Jewel

It requires the Hunter to own:

- Chrome Base
- Chrome Tribal Vegetation Kaboom Base
- Chrome Desert Heater Base
- Chrome Essential Living Garden Base
- Chrome Electromagnetic Meteorite Base
- Chrome Queso Cannonstorm Base
- Chrome Thief Base
- Chrome Stormcaster Base

And to complete the Liberate the Cerulean Skyport from Captain Crook adventure to purchase.

It cannot be refunded.
