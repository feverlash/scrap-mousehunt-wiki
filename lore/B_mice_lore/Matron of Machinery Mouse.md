# Matron of Machinery Mouse

The Matron of Machinery Mouse can be found Zokor. It is known to drop Tech Power Cores and a variety of Orbs.

## Preferred Cheese

The Matron of Machinery Mouse prefers Glowing Gruyere, but can be attracted with Standard Cheeses.


## Hunting Strategy

Forgotten weapons are very effective against the Matron of Machinery Mouse. Arcane weapons are of normal effectiveness.

The Matron of Machinery Mouse can be found in the Tech Research Center, and the Manaforge.


## Loot

- Divine Orb, Flawed Orb, Flawless Orb, Labyrinth Theme Scrap I, Simple Orb, and Tech Power Cores.


## Event Participation

- The Matron of Machinery Mouse drops the Zokor Tech Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

> 9 September 2015: The Matron of Machinery Mouse was introduced as part of the Hollow Heights release.
