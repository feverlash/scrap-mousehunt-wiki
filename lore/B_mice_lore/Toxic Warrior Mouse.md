# Toxic Warrior Mouse

The Toxic Warrior Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Toxic Spill, for hunters of rank Duke/Duchess and above.

## Preferred Cheese

Toxic Warrior mice are attracted to Magical Rancid Radioactive Blue at every spill level and to Rancid Radioactive Blue when spill level is for Duke/Duchess and above.


## Hunting Strategy

Hydro type traps such as Oasis Water Node Trap or Phantasmic Oasis Trap are effective against the Toxic Warrior Mouse.


## Event Participation

- The Toxic Warrior Mouse drops the Toxic Spill Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

- Released on 21 January 2014 as part of the Toxic Spill content.
