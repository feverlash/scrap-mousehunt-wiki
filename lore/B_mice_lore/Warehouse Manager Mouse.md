# Warehouse Manager Mouse

The Warehouse Manager Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Gnawnian Express Station. It can only be attracted and caught during the Supply Depot phase. They are known to drop Iron Pellets and Royal Supply Crates as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Warehouse Manager mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Law type traps such as S.L.A.C. are very effective against the Warehouse Manager Mouse. This mouse is attracted only when a Supply Schedule Charm is armed and a Supply Rush is not in effect. Catching this mouse starts a Supply Rush for all members of the Hunting Team on the same train, where the Supply Hoarder Mouse is available for the next 5 hunts each. During the Supply Rush, the Supply Schedule Charm offers no additional benefits.


## Event Participation

- The Warehouse Manager Mouse drops the Gnawnian Express Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.
