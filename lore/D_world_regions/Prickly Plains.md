# 📍 Prickly Plains

> **Wilayah / Region**: Queso Canyon  
> **Pangkat Minimal**: Recruit  
> **Fasilitas Lokal**: Cheese ShoppeTrapsmithCharm ShoppeGeneral StoreCartographer

## 🌍 Gambaran & Atmosfer Lingkungan

Prickly Plains is a hunting location in Queso Canyon region.
It requires the rank of Count/Countess and the Queso Dowsing Rod to access.

In this location, the Hunter can loot various types of Spice Leaves to craft high-tier Queso.
