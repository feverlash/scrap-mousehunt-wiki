## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> The image of the mighty tiger carved into this base imbues it with its powerful strength, energy, and ferocity.

The Tiger Jade Base was a reward for completing the Celebrate Lunar New Year 2022 adventure during the Lunar New Year 2022 event.
