## Preferred Cheese

Tome Sprite mice have been found to be especially attracted to SUPER|brie+, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.


## History and Trivia

- Tome Sprite Mice were introduced on 9 August 2011 with the release of the Crystal Library location.
- One of the pages ripped by the Tome Sprite Mouse contains a picture of a Flameshard, as well as a tic-tac-toe game.
