# Carnivore Mouse

The Carnivore Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Sunken City. It is known to drop Brined Curd as loot

## Cheese Preference

The Carnivore Mouse has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Carnivore Mouse is susceptible only to Hydro weapons. It can be found in the Carnivore Cove and Monster Trench zones while on a Dive in the Sunken City.


## Event Participation


### Spring Egg Hunt

- The Carnivore Mouse drops the Deep, Deep, Deep Egg and the Underwater Predator Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

The Carnivore Mouse was released 2 July 2014 as part of the Sunken City content.
