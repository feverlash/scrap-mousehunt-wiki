# 🛡️ Tidal Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 800 Power | 10 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Count/Countess  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 2,500,000 Gold150 Sand Dollar1 Aqua Base30 Barnacle30 Mouse Scale30 Damaged Coral Fragment

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> A miniature ocean habitat encompassed in tempered molten glass. This base harnesses the force created by its environment's tidal waves for additional power.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Tidal Base can be purchased from the Trapsmith in Sunken City for:

- 2,500,000 Gold
- 150 Sand Dollar
- 1 Aqua Base
- 30 Barnacle
- 30 Mouse Scale
- 30 Damaged Coral Fragment

It can be refunded for 450,000 Gold at the same location.
