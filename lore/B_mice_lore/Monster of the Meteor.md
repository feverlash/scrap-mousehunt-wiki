## Preferred Cheese

Monster of the Meteor mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Arcane traps are very effective against the Monster of the Meteor. Shadow traps are of normal effectiveness.
Monster of the Meteor mice can only be encountered during the dawn phase in Fort Rox.

Upon catching the Monster of the Meteor, the following message is displayed in the Hunter's Journal:
Capturing the Monster of the Meteor enraged him and his company of guards.
From now on, any mouse I fail to capture will do x00% damage to my fort wall!

With every catch the % damage will increase with an additional 100%, and the HUD will display a different level of irritation with each catch:


### Wall Damage

When Hunters reach the Dawn phase, each catch of a Monster of the Meteor will multiply the damage effects to the wall by an additional 100%. Any FTC of a Monster of the Meteor or Dawn Guardian will result in greater damage to the Hunter's wall. Damage from encountering a Battering Ram Mouse is not multiplied.
