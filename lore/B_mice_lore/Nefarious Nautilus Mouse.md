## Cheese Preference

Nefarious Nautilus Mouse is only attracted to Clamembert Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Hydro power type is effective against Nefarious Nautilus Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.


## Loot

Nefarious Nautilus Mouse can drop:

- Condensed Creativity

Additionally, it can drop the following loot depending on Chum status:

- Pond Penny (when Brainstorm Chum off)
- Inspiration Ink (when Brainstorm Chum on)
