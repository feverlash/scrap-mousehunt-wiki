# ⚔️ Zugzwang's Last Move

> **Classification**: Tactical Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 2,200 Power | 7 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Grandmaster  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 310,000

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Throughout the years Wizard Zugzwang's creations have been spread across the Gnawnia Kingdom, being traded between experienced hunters and trapsmith. Zugzwang enchanted the pieces of his chess set to create a trap weapon capable of combating the clever mice in his tower. The pieces of this weapon come to life when a mouse approaches the trap's bait, the battle that follows is quick and decisive.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
Zugzwang's Last Move can be purchased for 310,000 Gold from the Trapsmith in the following locations:

- Bazaar
- Training Grounds
- Dojo
- Meditation Room
- Pinnacle Chamber
- Seasonal Garden
- Zugzwang's Tower
- Crystal Library

It can be refunded for 55,800 Gold at the same locations.

Zugzwang's Last Move (if unintentionally smashed) can be reassembled from Chess Pieces.
