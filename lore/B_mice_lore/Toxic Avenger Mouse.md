# Toxic Avenger Mouse

The Toxic Avenger Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Burroughs Rift. It is not known to drop any loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Toxic Avenger Mouse is only attracted to Polluted Parmesan Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Toxic Avenger Mouse can be attracted when the Mist Meter is in Green and Red Zone.

Rift power type is effective against Toxic Avenger Mouse while all others are less effective.


## History and Trivia

- 9 September 2014: The Toxic Avenger Mouse was released as part of the Burroughs Rift location.
