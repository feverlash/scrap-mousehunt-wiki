## Cheese Preference

Dastardly Duchess Mouse is only attracted to Lavish Beanster Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Dastardly Duchess Mouse can only be attracted after planting a Tall Vine to the Great Hall Floor.

Physical power type is effective against Dastardly Duchess Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.


## Loot

Dastardly Duchess Mouse can drop:

- Ancient Relic
- Golden Goose Egg
