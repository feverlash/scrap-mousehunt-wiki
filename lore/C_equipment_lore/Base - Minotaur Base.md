# 🛡️ Minotaur Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 1,000 Power | 15 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Duke/Duchess  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 16,000,000 Gold6 Really, Really Shiny Precious Gold700 Plate of Fealty700 Scholar Scroll700 Tech Power Core

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Stacks on stacks of Really, Really Shiny Precious Gold tightly bound together using the minotaur's own collections of fine leather.
> Mice will be sure to give you little resistance when traveling with a print of the minotaur on your base.
> Using this base inside of the Labyrinth has a chance of preventing a Dead End Clue from confusing your Labyrinth Compass during an unsuccessful hunt.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Minotaur Base can be purchased from Trapsmith in Labyrinth and Zokor for:

- 16,000,000 Gold
- 6 Really, Really Shiny Precious Gold
- 700 Plate of Fealty
- 700 Scholar Scroll
- 700 Tech Power Core

It cannot be refunded.
