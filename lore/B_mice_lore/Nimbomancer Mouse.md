# Nimbomancer Mouse

The Nimbomancer Mouse is a breed of mouse found on the Floating Islands, on a Hydro Island or Hydro Hideaway.

## Cheese Preference

The Nimbomancer Mouse is primarily attracted to Cloud Cheesecake, however it also can be attracted to Standard Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Only Hydro traps are effective against the Nimbomancer Mouse, all other power types are ineffective. The Nimbomancer Mouse can only be encountered on a Hydro Island or Hydro Hideaway.


## Loot


### Floating Islands's Island Based Loot

When found on a Low Altitude Island or a High Altitude Island, depending on the buffs present, the Nimbomancer Mouse can drop the following loot:


| Item | Condition |

| Sky Ore | Always drops Boosted by Sky Ore Deposit and Loot Caches |

| Corsair's Curd | Drops when a Pirate Den is present Can be boosted by Loot Caches |
