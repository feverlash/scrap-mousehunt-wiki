## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Kalor'ignis of the Geyser is only attracted to Wildfire Queso.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Kalor'ignis of the Geyser.


## Hunting Strategy

Only Draconic traps are effective against the Kalor'ignis of the Geyser, all other power types are ineffective. The Kalor'ignis of the Geyser can only be encountered in Queso Geyser while an Epic Eruption is triggered.

Dragonbane Charm, Super Dragonbane Charm, Extreme Dragonbane Charm, and Ultimate Dragonbane Charm provide a power bonus against the Kalor'ignis of the Geyser.


## History and Trivia

- 30 April 2019: The Kalor'ignis of the Geyser was introduced, with the release of the Queso Geyser area.
