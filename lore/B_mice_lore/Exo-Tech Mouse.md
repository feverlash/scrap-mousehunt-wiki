The Exo-Tech Mouse can be found in Zokor. It is known to drop Tech Power Cores.

## Preferred Cheese

The Exo-Tech Mouse prefers Glowing Gruyere, but can be attracted with Standard Cheeses.


## Hunting Strategy

Forgotten weapons are very effective against the Exo-Tech Mouse. Arcane weapons are of normal effectiveness.

The Exo-Tech Mouse can be found in the Tech Foundry Outskirts, the Tech Research Center, and the Manaforge.


## Loot

- Labyrinth Theme Scrap I and Tech Power Cores


## Event Participation

- The Exo-Tech Mouse drops the Zokor Tech Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

> 9 September 2015: The Exo-Tech Mouse was introduced as part of the Hollow Heights release.
