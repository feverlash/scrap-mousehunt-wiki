# ⚔️ The Holiday Express Trap

> **Classification**: Law Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 2,100 Power | 8 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Initiate

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> The Holiday Express has come to town bringing gifts to all the good and lawful people of Gnawnia. Any do-badders caught by the Holiday Express will have their name permanently recorded in the BAD section of the train's delivery manifest so that they'll never be delivered a gift ever again!
> Has a chance to deliver a Gift Wrapped Charm to the hunter when a mouse is caught!

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The The Holiday Express Trap can be purchased for 90,000 Gold, 40 Nutcracker Sugar Cookies, 10 Ornament Sugar Cookies, and 10 Snowflake Sugar Cookies at Great Winter Hunt 2021.
