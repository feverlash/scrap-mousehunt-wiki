# Stinger Mouse

The Stinger Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Rodentia region. It can be attracted and caught only during the Summer Season. They are not known to drop any loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Stinger Mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Tactical weapons such as Zugzwang's First Move should be used in order to catch this mouse.


## Event Participation

- The Stinger Mouse drops the Seasonal Garden Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

- Stinger Mice were introduced on 17 August 2010 with the release of the Seasonal Garden location.
- During the Spring Egg Hunt 2011, Stinger mice began dropping a bonus Seasonal Garden Egg as loot for hunters who had obtained the Rocket Rabbit Mallet. They dropped the same loot the following year during the Spring Egg Hunt 2012 event for hunters who had obtained the Eggscavator.
