## Cheese Preference

Covetous Canister Thief Mouse is only attracted to Sky Pirate Swiss or Sky Raider Romano.


## Hunting Strategy

The Covetous Canister Thief Mouse can only be attracted during an Atmospheric shipment.

Law power type is effective against Covetous Canister Thief Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
