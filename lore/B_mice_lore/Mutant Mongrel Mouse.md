The Mutant Mongrel Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Toxic Spill, for hunters of rank Duke/Duchess and above.


## Preferred Cheese

Mutant Mongrel mice are attracted to Magical Rancid Radioactive Blue at every spill level and to Rancid Radioactive Blue when spill level is for Duke/Duchess and above.


## Hunting Strategy

Hydro type traps such as Oasis Water Node Trap or Phantasmic Oasis Trap are effective against the Mutant Mongrel Mouse.


## Event Participation


### Spring Egg Hunt

- The Mutant Mongrel Mouse drops the Mutant Mongrel Egg and the Toxic Spill Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.
