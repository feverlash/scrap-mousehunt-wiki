The Isle Idol Stakeshooter Trap Skin can be obtained by opening the Isle Idol Box Set, which was available to be purchased for 1,250,000 gold from Ronza's Traveling Shoppe during Ronza's March 2013 visit. The box could be refunded for 750,000 gold at the same location.
