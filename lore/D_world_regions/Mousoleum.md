# 📍 Mousoleum

> **Wilayah / Region**: Burroughs  
> **Pangkat Minimal**: Master  
> **Fasilitas Lokal**: Cheese ShoppeTrapsmithCharm ShoppeGeneral StoreCartographer

## 🌍 Gambaran & Atmosfer Lingkungan

The Mousoleum is a location in the Burroughs region requiring a minimum rank of Master and the Tattered Mousoleum Map Piece to access.
In the Mousoleum, Hunters are tasked to build a Barricade so as to lure out mice that drop the necessary ingredients required for crafting cheese that will attract the boss mouse in the location.
The Barricade gets damaged as the Hunter hunts in the Mousoleum and will eventually become completely destroyed if not repaired. It must then be rebuilt by the Hunter.
