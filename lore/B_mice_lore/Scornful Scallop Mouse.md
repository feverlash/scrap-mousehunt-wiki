## Cheese Preference

Scornful Scallop Mouse is only attracted to Standard Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Scornful Scallop Mouse can only be attracted while on the shore or the rapids before the waterfall in Epilogue Falls.

Hydro power type is effective against Scornful Scallop Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
