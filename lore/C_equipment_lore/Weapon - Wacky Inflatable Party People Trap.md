# ⚔️ Wacky Inflatable Party People Trap

> **Classification**: Rift Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 1,100 Power | 4 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Count/Countess  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 10,000 Gold3 Golden Punch Ticket

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> A seemingly lifeless pair of balloons lay dormant until an unsuspecting party person triggers this stellar celebration! Once a mouse gets too close, the music starts, confetti fills the air and the balloons begin to inflate with powerful Rift Mist to bring some out-of-this-world party people to life!
> Armed with their unpredictable dance moves, this trap is incredibly hard to dodge when limbs come firing out of the thick and billowing coloured mist!
