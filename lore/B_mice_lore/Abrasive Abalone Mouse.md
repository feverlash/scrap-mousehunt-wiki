## Cheese Preference

Abrasive Abalone Mouse is only attracted to Standard Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Abrasive Abalone Mouse can only be attracted while on the shore or the rapids before the waterfall in Epilogue Falls.

Hydro power type is effective against Abrasive Abalone Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
