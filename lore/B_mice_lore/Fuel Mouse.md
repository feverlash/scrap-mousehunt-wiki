# Fuel Mouse

The Fuel is a breed of mouse found in the Gnawnian Express Station. It can only be attracted and caught during the Daredevil Canyon phase. They are known to drop Fuel Nuggets and Tin Scraps as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Fuel mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Law type traps such as S.L.A.C. are very effective against the Fuel Mouse.


## Event Participation

- The Fuel Mouse drops the Gnawnian Express Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

- Released on 26 September 2013 as part of the Gnawnian Express Station content.
