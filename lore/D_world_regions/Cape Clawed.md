## 🌍 Gambaran & Atmosfer Lingkungan

Cape Clawed is a hunting location in Tribal Isles region.
It requires the rank of Legendary and the Ocean Navigation Kit to access.

While it is not required to hunt in this location to progress further in the region, the Hunter can find loot to upgrade certain weapons here.
