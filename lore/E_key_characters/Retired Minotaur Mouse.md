## 📜 Lore & Persona

> "Once young and lively, this minotaur loved racing through the hallways of the Labyrinth, chasing down hunters and mice alike, howling and bellowing with delight as they scurried at the mere sound of his hooves on the cobbled floors. But now, it seems he has run his last lap of the maze, and has settled down into cozy end-life routines. Donning his fuzzy slippers in his favourite easy chair (which is a giant sack of gold), this retired beast has had his pension paid out in full and has relocated to his secret inner chamber to enjoy his retirement in peace and quiet.<br>The same shenanigans that used to bring him joy now only cause him grief, and he can be seen shaking his fist from atop his tower at the other mice hustling and bustling in the city. All that noise, all that racket! What a grumpy old grump this once playful beast has become...<br>But even in his twilight and seemingly endless misery, he still manages to let a small grin spread across his lips whenever he causes a mouse to jump in fear at the sound of his snort or the clipping of his hooves.<br>No one has broken into his private chambers and lived to tell the tale. Rumor has it that he stores many great and valuable items on opulent displays. Perhaps a resourceful hunter can find a way in and nab one of these rare treasures before being caught!"
