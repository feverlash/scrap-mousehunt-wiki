# Essence Guardian Mouse

The Essence Guardian Mouse is a breed of mouse that can be found in the Cursed City, and is known to drop Cynd Essence and Plumepearl Herbs as loot.

## Hunting Strategy

The Essence Guardian Mouse is only attracted to Graveblossom Camembert.

It is a member of the Lost City Mice group. As such all power types except Arcane weapons are ineffective against it.

It is only available in Curses Lifted state in the Fear, Darkness, and Mist Curses Minigame.


## History and Trivia

- 14 November 2012: The Essence Guardian Mouse was introduced with the release of the Cursed City location.
