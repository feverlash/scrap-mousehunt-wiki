> **Classification**: Tactical Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 10,000 Power | 35 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Viceroy

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> This fully-functional, fully-automated, sustainable sawmill is the epitome of efficiency! The custom-built Lumberdroids that operate the mill are second-to-none at sawing, chopping, and replanting. The engineers that built these Lumberdroids were determined to program them to work with three primary objectives: diligence, efficiency, and safety. However, due to time and budget constraints, they were only able to program two objectives.
