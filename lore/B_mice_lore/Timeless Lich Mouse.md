# Timeless Lich Mouse

The Timeless Lich Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Bristle Woods Rift. It is the Rift counterpart of Lich Mouse.

## Preferred Cheese

The Timeless Lich Mouse has been found to be especially attracted to Ancient String Cheese and Runic String Cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.

## Hunting Strategy

Only Rift type traps are very effective against Timeless Lich Mouse, all other power types are ineffective.
