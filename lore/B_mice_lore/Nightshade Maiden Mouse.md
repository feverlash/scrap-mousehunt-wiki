## Preferred Cheese

The Nightshade Maiden Mouse has been found to be especially attracted to SUPER|brie+ cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.

## Hunting Strategy

Only Shadow, Arcane and Draconic type traps are effective against Nightshade Maiden Mouse, all other power types are ineffective.
