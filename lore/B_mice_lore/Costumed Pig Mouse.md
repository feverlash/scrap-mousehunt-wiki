## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Costumed Pig Mouseis especially attracted to Dumpling Cheese or Nian Gao'da Cheese to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.


### Charms

No Charms are required to attract the Costumed Pig Mouse.
