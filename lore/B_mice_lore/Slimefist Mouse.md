# Slimefist Mouse

The Slimefist Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Toxic Spill, for hunters of rank Duke/Duchess and above.

## Preferred Cheese

Slimefist mice are attracted to Magical Rancid Radioactive Blue at every spill level and to Rancid Radioactive Blue when spill level is for Duke/Duchess and above.


## Hunting Strategy

Hydro type traps such as Oasis Water Node Trap or Phantasmic Oasis Trap are effective against the Slimefist Mouse.


## Event Participation

- The Slimefist Mouse drops the Toxic Spill Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.
