## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

In the Calm Clearing location, the Foxy Mouse is only attracted when hunting with SUPER|brie+.
In the Great Gnarled Tree location, the Foxy Mouse will be attracted when hunting with Standard Cheese and Gnarled Cheese.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Foxy Mouse.

## Locations

Hunters can encounter the Foxy Mouse in the Calm Clearing location (though it is extremely rare) and in the Great Gnarled Tree location.


## Loot

The Foxy Mouse drops Swiss Cheese as loot in the Calm Clearing and Great Gnarled Tree locations.


## History and Trivia

- 31 March 2009: The Foxy Mouse was released with the introduction of the Calm Clearing.
- 8 June 2010: With the MouseHunt 3.0 update, the Foxy Mouse gained a preference for SUPER|brie+ in the Calm Clearing.
- 25 June 2013: The Foxy Mouse gained a vulnerability to Law traps with the release of Varmint Valley.
