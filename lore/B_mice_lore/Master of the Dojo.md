## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Master of the Dojo is attracted only to Rumble Cheese.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Master of the Dojo.

## Locations

Hunters can encounter the Master of the Dojo only in the Pinnacle Chamber location.


## Loot

The Master of the Dojo drops Onyx Stones and Onyx Mallet Blueprints as loot in the Pinnacle Chamber location.


## History and Trivia

- 26 November 2008: The Master of the Dojo was released.
- 30 November 2009: The Master of the Dojo was chosen as the "Monday Mystery Mouse". For this day only, there was a 50% chance that it could drop up to 10 pieces of SUPER|brie+ cheese as loot.
- 22 March 2022: The Master of the Dojo started dropping Onyx Mallet Blueprints.
