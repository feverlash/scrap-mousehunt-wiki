## Locations

Hunters can encounter the Elven Princess Mouse in the Calm Clearing, Great Gnarled Tree, and the Lagoon locations.


## Loot

The Elven Princess Mouse is not known to drop any loot in the Calm Clearing, Great Gnarled Tree or Lagoon locations.


## History and Trivia

- The Elven Princess Mouse was a suggestion by hunters in the first months of MouseHunt.
- 31 March 2009: The Elven Princess Mouse was released with the introduction of the Calm Clearing location.
- 9 April 2009: The Elven Princess Mouse began appearing in the Great Gnarled Tree location.
- 22 April 2009: The Elven Princess Mouse began appearing in the Lagoon location.
- 4 April 2012: The Elven Princess Mouse began dropping a bonus Whisker Woods Egg as loot as part of the Spring Egg Hunt 2012 event.
- 25 June 2013: The Elven Princess Mouse gained a vulnerability to Law power type traps, with the release of Varmint Valley.
