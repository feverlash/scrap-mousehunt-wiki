| With hard work, commitment to using only the finest ingredients, and an impressive sense of balance, the Dumpling Chef Mouse makes its living cooking dumplings for the bewhiskered masses. It serves thousands of dumplings a day to hungry travelers, filled with seasoned grass, wheat seeds, and, plenty of cheese, of course. ## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Dumpling Chef Mouse is attracted only to SUPER|brie+.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Dumpling Chef Mouse.

## Locations

Hunters can encounter the Dumpling Chef Mouse only in the Training Grounds location.


## Loot

The Dumpling Chef Mouse is not known to drop any loot.


## History and Trivia

- 9 February 2010: The Dumpling Chef Mouse was released as part of the Heart of the Tiger event.
- 22 February 2010: The Dumpling Chef Mouse was reclassified from a Rare Breed to the Followers of Furoma when it began appearing in the Training Grounds.
- The transformation of the Dumpling Chef Mouse from a Rare Breed into a regular breed resulted in a glitch whereby the total number of regular breeds available to catch increased (from 140 to 141), but the actual catches were counted in the Rare Breeds section. This was quickly corrected by the developers.
