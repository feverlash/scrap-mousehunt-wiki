# Lab Technician Mouse

The Lab Technician Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Toxic Spill, for hunters of rank Hero and above. It is known to drop a variety of loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Lab Technician Mouse is attracted to Magical Rancid Radioactive Blue and Rancid Radioactive Blue at every spill level. Additionally, these mice are only encountered when Rotten or Super Rotten Charms are in use.


## Hunting Strategy

Hydro type traps such as Oasis Water Node Trap or Phantasmic Oasis Trap are effective against the Lab Technician Mouse.


## Event Participation


### Spring Egg Hunt

- The Lab Technician Mouse drops the Clean Egg and Toxic Spill Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

- 21 January 2014: The Lab Technician Mouse was released as part of the Toxic Spill location.
- 15 April 2014: The Lab Technician Mouse began dropping the Clean Egg and Toxic Spill Egg as part of the Spring Egg Hunt 2014 event.
