# Wight Mouse

The Wight Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Acolyte Realm. It is not known to drop any loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Wight Mouse has been found to be especially attracted to Runic cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.


## Hunting Strategy

Arcane weapons are very effective against the Wight mouse.


## History and Trivia

- 6 March 2009: The Wight Mouse was released for the first time..
- 18 April 2011: The Wight Mouse began dropping the Antique Wavy Purple Egg as loot as part of the Spring Egg Hunt 2011 event.
