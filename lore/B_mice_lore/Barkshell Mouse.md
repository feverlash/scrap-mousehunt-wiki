# Barkshell Mouse

The Barkshell Mouse is a breed of mouse that can be found in the Twisted Garden, and is known to drop Aleth Essences and Graveblossom Petals as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Barkshell Mouse is only attracted to Duskshade Camembert.


## Hunting Strategy

The Barkshell Mouse is a member of the Living Garden mouse group. As such all power types except Hydro weapons are ineffective against it.


## History and Trivia

- 14 November 2012: The Barkshell Mouse was released as a part of the Living Garden update.
