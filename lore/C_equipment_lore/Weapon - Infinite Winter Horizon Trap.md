# ⚔️ Infinite Winter Horizon Trap

> **Classification**: Arcane Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 6,000 Power | 26 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Duke  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 10,700,000 1 Nugget of Nougat

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> The centre of this Horizon Trap is a glowing portal full of winter wonder. Each mouse caught within its gravitational pull get sucked into its centre and whisked away into its own snow galaxy, an entire galaxy consisting of only a single house and perpetual snow. Like a snow globe! Forever!

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Infinite Winter Horizon Trap is not sold by any Trapsmith. It can only be crafted given the proper ingredients.
