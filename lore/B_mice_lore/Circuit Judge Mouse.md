# Circuit Judge Mouse

Circuit Judge Mouse, also known as "Mr. Crumbs", is a mouse found in the Claw Shot City.

## Hunting Strategy

Circuit Judge Mouse is only available when the Hunter has cleared all Accomplices in a Corrupt Cartel Wanted Poster.
Law power type is very effective against this mouse. All other types are ineffective.


## History and Trivia

- 25 June 2013: Circuit Judge Mouse was released as part of the Claw Shot City, but couldn't be attracted yet.
- 8 July 2013: Circuit Judge Mouse was made available with the Corrupt Cartel Wanted Poster.
