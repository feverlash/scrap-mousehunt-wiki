## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The White Mouse prefers Standard Cheese, but will avoid traps baited with White Cheddar Cheese.

In the Mountain location, the White Mouse will not be encountered when hunting with White Cheddar Cheese, Chedd-Ore Cheese, or Abominable Asiago.
In the Windmill location, the White Mouse will not be encountered when hunting with White Cheddar Cheese or Grilled Cheese.
In the Training Grounds location, the White Mouse will not be encountered when hunting with SUPER|brie+.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the White Mouse.

## Loot

The White Mouse drops various loot in the following locations:

- Meadow and Town of Gnawnia

> Marble Cheese

- Training Grounds

> Brie Cheese
Swiss Cheese

The White Mouse is not known to drop any loot in the Harbour, King's Arms, or Mountain locations.


## History and Trivia

- 7 March 2008: The White Mouse was introduced with the release of MouseHunt.
- 12 January 2011: The White Mouse was found again at Ronza's Traveling Shoppe where it dropped Rockforth Cheese as loot.
- 16 March 2012: The White Mouse dropped the Seven Leaf Clover Cap during St. Patrick's Day weekend.
- 25 June 2013: The White Mouse gained a vulnerability to Law power type traps with the release of Varmint Valley.
- 17 March 2014: The White Mouse dropped the Seven Leaf Clover Cap during St. Patrick's Day weekend.
- 25 March 2014: The White Mouse gained a vulnerability to Rift power type traps with the introduction of the Rift Plane.
- 25 March 2014: The Brawny Mouse was introduced as the Rift counterpart of the White Mouse.
- 2 September 2014: The White Mouse began dropping Packets of Flour at the Windmill.
