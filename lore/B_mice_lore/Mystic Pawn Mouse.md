## Preferred Cheese

Mystic Pawn mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Mystic Pawn mouse is a typical member of the Wizard's Pieces group, and as such Tactical weapons are of normal effectiveness against it, while all others are ineffective. Additionally, it has been found that this mouse is resistant to Technic Pawn Pincher, and vulnerable to Mystic Pawn Pincher.
