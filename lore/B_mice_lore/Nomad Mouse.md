## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

In the Great Gnarled Tree location, the Nomad Mouse will only be attracted when hunting with SUPER|brie+.
In the Lagoon location, the Nomad Mouse will only be attracted when hunting with Gnarled Cheese and Wicked Gnarly Cheese.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Nomad Mouse.

## Locations

Hunters can encounter the Nomad Mouse in the Great Gnarled Tree and Lagoon locations.


## Loot

The Nomad Mouse is not known to drop any loot.


## History and Trivia

- 9 April 2009: The Nomad Mouse was released with the introduction of the Great Gnarled Tree.
- 14 April 2011: The Nomad Mouse began dropping a bonus Whisker Woods Egg as loot as part of the annual Spring Egg Hunt.
- 25 June 2013: The Nomad Mouse gained a vulnerability to Law traps with the release of Varmint Valley.
- The Nomad Mouse could also be found in S.S. Huntington II until mid-August 2010.
