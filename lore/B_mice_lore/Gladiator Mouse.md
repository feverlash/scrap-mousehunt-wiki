# Gladiator Mouse

The Gladiator Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Derr Dunes location. It is known to drop Red Pepper Seeds as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Gladiator Mouse has been found to be especially attracted to Crunchy cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.


## History and Trivia
