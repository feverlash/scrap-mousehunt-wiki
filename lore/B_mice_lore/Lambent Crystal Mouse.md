## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Lambent Crystal Mouse is attracted only to Limelight Cheese.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Lambent Crystal Mouse.

Arming the Mining Charm provides a power boost against Digby Dirt Dwellers.

## Locations

Hunters can encounter the Lambent Crystal Mouse only in the Town of Digby.


## Loot

The Lambent Crystal Mouse drops Living Shards as loot.


## History and Trivia

- From the description of the Lambent Crystal Mouse, NVMRC stands for Non-Violent Mouse Removal Charity.
- 24 March 2010: The Lambent Crystal Mouse is released.
- 9 September 2014: The Lambent Mouse was introduced as the Rift counterpart of the Lambent Crystal Mouse.
