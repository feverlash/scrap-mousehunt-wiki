## Preferred Cheese

Technic King mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.

## Hunting Strategy

The weaknesses of the Technic King Mouse differ slightly from most others in the Wizard's Pieces group. Tactical weapons are very effective against it, while all the others are less effective. It is only available to attract after a Technic Queen mouse has been caught.

Upon capturing a Technic King mouse, the hunter is automatically transported back to the Seasonal Garden unless they are using either Checkmate Cheese or a Stalemate Charm. By using either Checkmate Cheese or a Stalemate Charm a hunter is able to remain in Zugzwang's Tower and attempt to capture additional mice, including the Chess Master.

Upon capturing a Technic King mouse with either Checkmate Cheese or a Stalemate Charm armed, the mouse population changes. If Checkmate Cheese is armed, the available mice will be the Chess Master, Technic Rook and whichever Mystic mice have been unlocked. If Standard Cheese is armed, the available mice will be the Technic Pawn and whichever Mystic mice have been unlocked.

## Event Participation

### Spring Egg Hunt

- The Technic King Mouse drops the Technic King Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.

## History and Trivia

- Catching the Technic King Mouse became one requirement for traveling to the Fiery Warpath when it was released on 22 March 2011. Hunters who had already caught this mouse did not have to catch another one.
- Technic King mice began dropping the Antique Wavy Purple Egg as loot, on 18 April 2011, for the Spring Egg Hunt 2011 event.
- 29 July 2012: Catching the Technic King Mouse is no longer a requirement for gaining the Sandtail Call to Arms
