## Cheese Preference

Hefty Hulking Hauler Mouse is only attracted to Sky Pirate Swiss or Sky Raider Romano.


## Hunting Strategy

The Hefty Hulking Hauler Mouse can only be attracted while docked.

Law power type is effective against Hefty Hulking Hauler Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
