## Cheese Preference

Belligerent Berghia Mouse is only attracted to Metaphor Manchego.


## Hunting Strategy

The Belligerent Berghia Mouse can only be attracted while on the rapids before the waterfall in Epilogue Falls.

Hydro power type is effective against Belligerent Berghia Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
