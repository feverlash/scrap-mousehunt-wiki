# ⚔️ Smoldering Stone Sentinel Trap

> **Classification**: Physical Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 12,000 Power | 25 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Count/Countess  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 15,000,000 Gold2,500 Solidified Amber Queso300 Nachore3 Geyser Smolder Stone

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> A lumbering elemental built from powerful Geyser Smolder Stones, this massive weapon sits still until the thermal signature of a nearby mouse comes near. Driven by its craving for heat, the trap snatches up mice and consumes them to keep its core hot.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Smoldering Stone Sentinel Trap can be purchased from the Trapsmith in Queso Geyser for:

- 15,000,000 Gold
- 2,500 Solidified Amber Queso
- 300 Nachore
- 3 Geyser Smolder Stone

It requires that the Hunter has completed the Slay the Geyser Dragon adventure prior to purchase.

It cannot be refunded.
