# 🛡️ Ox Jade Base

> **Classification**: General Base

> **Title Requirement**: Grandmaster

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Diligent and indomitable. Carved into this base is the Mighty Ox with its infallible fighting spirit backed by an unbreakable strength of will.
> Those who completed the 2021 Lunar New Year adventure were awarded with this intricately carved Ox Jade Base!

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Ox Jade Base was a reward for completing the Celebrate Lunar New Year 2021 adventure during the Lunar New Year 2021 event.
