## Preferred Cheese

Technic Pawn mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Technic Pawn mouse is a typical member of the Wizard's Pieces group, and as such Tactical weapons are of normal effectiveness against it, while all others are ineffective. Additionally, it has been found that this mouse is somewhat resistant to Mystic Pawn Pincher, and vulnerable to Technic Pawn Pincher.
