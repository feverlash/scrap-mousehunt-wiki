## Cheese Preference

Shimmerdread the Sovereign of Spice is only attracted to Aurora Bocconcini.


## Hunting Strategy

The Shimmerdread the Sovereign of Spice can be only attracted during a Spice shipment.

Law power type is very effective against Shimmerdread the Sovereign of Spice.
All other types are ineffective.
