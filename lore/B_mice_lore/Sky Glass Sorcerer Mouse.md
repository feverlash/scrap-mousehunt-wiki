## Cheese Preference

The Sky Glass Sorcerer Mouse is primarily attracted to Cloud Cheesecake, however it also can be attracted to Standard Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Only Arcane traps are effective against the Sky Glass Sorcerer Mouse, all other power types are ineffective. The Sky Glass Sorcerer Mouse can only be encountered on an Arcane Island.


## Loot


### Floating Islands's Island Based Loot

When found on a Low Altitude Island or a High Altitude Island, depending on the buffs present, the Sky Glass Sorcerer Mouse can drop the following loot:


| Item | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| Sky Glass | Always drops Boosted by Sky Glass Formations and Loot Caches |
| Sky Ore | Always drops Boosted by Sky Ore Deposit and Loot Caches |
| Cloud Curd | Drops when a Cloud Curd Bonus is present Can be boosted by Loot Caches |
| Corsair's Curd | Drops when a Pirate Den is present Can be boosted by Loot Caches |
