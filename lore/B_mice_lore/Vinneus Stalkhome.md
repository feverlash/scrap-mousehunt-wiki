## Cheese Preference

Vinneus Stalkhome can be attracted with any Standard Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Physical power type is very effective against Vinneus Stalkhome.
All other types are ineffective.


## Loot

Vinneus Stalkhome can drop:

- Ancient Relic
- Condensed Creativity
- Fabled Fertilizer
- Magic Bean
