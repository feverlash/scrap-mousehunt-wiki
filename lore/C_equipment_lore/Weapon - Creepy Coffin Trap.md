# ⚔️ Creepy Coffin Trap

> **Classification**: Shadow Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 1,800 Power | 7 Luck

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> A bed for the dead! Undead mice see this as one big bed and breakfast. Armed with a delicious bait, mood enhancing fog, old musty wooden scent and topped with a beautiful display of gnarled trees, who could resist!?
> Unbeknownst to potential visitors, this coffin has a permanent resident confined within its 6 walls and it doesn't take kindly to house guests...
> As an added bonus, it produces an extra Cemetery Slat when one is dropped within the Mousoleum.
