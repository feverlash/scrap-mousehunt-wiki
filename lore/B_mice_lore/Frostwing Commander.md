# Frostwing Commander

The Frostwing Commander is a breed of mouse found in the later zones of the Iceberg: Icewing's Lair and Hidden Depths. They are known to drop Drill Charge, Hunting Research, Satchels of Gold, Sticky Charms, War Scraps, Wax Charms, and Well-sealed Canister as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Frostwing Commander has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## History and Trivia

- 24 May 2012: The Frostwing Commander was released as a part of the Iceberg.
- 1 June 2012: The Frostwing Commander began to drop additional loot.


## Hunting Strategy

The Frostwing Commander can only be encountered when in Icewing's Lair (only after 425 hunts beyond a hunter's first defeat of an Iceberg Invasion) or the Hidden Depths.


## History and Trivia

- 24 May 2012: The Frostwing Commander was released as a part of the Iceberg.
- 1 June 2012: The Frostwing Commander began to drop additional loot.
- On its original release, the Frostwing Commander could be encountered in Icewing's Lair without having to exceed 425 hunts; hunters were required to have Steam Laser Mk. I or any other Hydro weapon (excluding Steam Laser Mk. II and Steam Laser Mk. III) armed in order to be able to encounter and catch it. This was changed to the current strategy on 14 June 2012.
