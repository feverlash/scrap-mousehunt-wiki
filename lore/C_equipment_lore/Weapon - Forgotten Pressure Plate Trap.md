# ⚔️ Forgotten Pressure Plate Trap

> **Classification**: Forgotten Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 3,500 Power | 2 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Legendary  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 400,000 Gold

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> This simple Forgotten trap is designed to blend in with the architecture found in the Acolyte Realm so as to avoid arising suspicion, and features a small, poisonous blowgun hidden within a simple stone column.
> Once a mouse steps on the pressure plate to collect the cheese, a volley of poisonous rune-tipped darts fire. Lethal or non-lethal poisons can be used depending on the will of the hunter.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Forgotten Pressure Plate Trap can be purchased from the Trapsmith in Catacombs, Forbidden Grove, Acolyte Realm, Balack's Cove, and King's Gauntlet for:

- 400,000 Gold
- 6 Rune

It can be refunded for 72,000 Gold at the same locations.
