## Cheese Preference

Arrogant Aurora Connoisseur Mouse is only attracted to Sky Pirate Swiss or Sky Raider Romano.


## Hunting Strategy

The Arrogant Aurora Connoisseur Mouse can only be attracted during a Spice shipment.

Law power type is effective against Arrogant Aurora Connoisseur Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
