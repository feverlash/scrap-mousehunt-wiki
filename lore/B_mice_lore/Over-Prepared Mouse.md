# Over-Prepared Mouse

The Over-Prepared Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Rodentia region. It can be attracted and caught only during the Winter Season. They are not known to drop any loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Over-Prepared Mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Strong Hydro weapons such as Ancient Spear Gun and Heat Bath are recommended in order to catch this mouse. Alternatively, hunters may equip their best Tactical trap.

- Over-Prepared Mice were introduced on 17 August 2010 with the release of the Seasonal Garden location.
- They began dropping the Antique Stripy Red Egg as loot, on 17 April 2011, for the Spring Egg Hunt 2011 event.
