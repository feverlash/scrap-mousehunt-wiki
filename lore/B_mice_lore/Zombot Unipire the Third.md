# Zombot Unipire the Third

The Zombot Unipire the Third is a breed of mouse found in the Burroughs Rift. It is known to drop Terre Ricotta Potion and Brain Bits as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Zombot Unipire the Third can be attracted using any Standard String Cheese or Undead String Emmental.


## Hunting Strategy

Zombot Unipire the Third can be attracted by using either:

- Standard String Cheese when the Mist Meter is in Green Zone.
- Undead String Emmental

Using Brain Charm or Super Brain Charm increases the attraction rate of Zombot Unipire the Third.

Rift power type is effective against Zombot Unipire the Third while all others are less effective.


## History and Trivia

- 9 September 2014: The Zombot Unipire the Third was released as part of the Burroughs Rift location.
- 13 January 2015: The Zombot Unipire the Third gained its preference to Brain Charm and Super Brain Charm with the start of Zombie Invasion Week 2015.
