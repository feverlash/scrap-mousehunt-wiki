# Coral Cuddler Mouse

The Coral Cuddler Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Sunken City. It is known to drop Damaged Coral Fragments and Sand Dollars as loot.

## Cheese Preference

The Coral Cuddler Mouse has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Coral Cuddler Mouse is susceptible only to Hydro weapons. It can be found in the Coral Reef zone while on a Dive in the Sunken City.


## Event Participation


### Spring Egg Hunt

- The Coral Cuddler Mouse drops the Coral Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

- The Coral Cuddler Mouse was released 2 July 2014 as part of the Sunken City content.
- On the Facebook fan page, the artwork for the Coral Cuddler was previewed on 30 June 2014.
