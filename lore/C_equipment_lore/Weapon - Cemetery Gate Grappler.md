# ⚔️ Cemetery Gate Grappler

> **Classification**: Draconic Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 5,100 Power | 10 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Knight  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 0

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> A section of a cemetery gate repurposed with magical Draconic bindings and pure iron harpoons. Dragons captured with this trap get sealed within the gates. The magical bindings prevent the dragon from being able to escape.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Cemetery Gate Grappler is an Limited Edition weapon and can no longer be acquired by Hunters. It was not sold by any Trapsmith. It could only be obtained by collecting 800 Spooky Ship Parts during the 2019 Halloween event.
