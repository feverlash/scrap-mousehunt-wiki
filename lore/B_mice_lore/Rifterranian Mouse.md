# Rifterranian Mouse

The Rifterranian Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Burroughs Rift. It is known to drop Polluted Parmesan Potion and Crystallized Fossil as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Rifterranian Mouse is only attracted to Terre Ricotta Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Rifterranian Mouse can be attracted when the Mist Meter is in Yellow and Green Zone.

Rift power type is effective against Rifterranian Mouse while all others are less effective.


## History and Trivia
