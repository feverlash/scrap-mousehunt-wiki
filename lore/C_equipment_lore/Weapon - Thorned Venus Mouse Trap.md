# ⚔️ Thorned Venus Mouse Trap

> **Classification**: Tactical Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 3,400 Power | 14 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Legendary  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 550,000 Gold1 Thorned Vine1 Venus Mouse Trap Husk

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Adorned with razor-sharp thorns grown in the Jungle of Dread, this Venus Mouse Trap uses its vines to trap any mice unfortunate enough to wander near.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Thorned Venus Mouse Trap can be purchased from Trapsmith in Cape Clawed and Nerg Plains for:

- 550,000 Gold
- 1 Thorned Vine
- 1 Venus Mouse Trap Husk

It cannot be refunded.

The Thorned Venus Mouse Trap (if unintentionally smashed) can be reassembled from Thorned Venus Mouse Trap Husk.
