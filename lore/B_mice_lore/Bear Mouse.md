## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Bear Mouse is attracted mainly to Standard Cheese.

In the Calm Clearing location, the Bear Mouse will also be attracted when hunting with Cherry Cheese.
In the Great Gnarled Tree location, the Bear Mouse will not be attracted when hunting with Gnarled Cheese.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Bear Mouse.

## Locations

Hunters can encounter the Bear Mouse in the Calm Clearing and Great Gnarled Tree locations.


## Loot

The Bear Mouse drops Gnarled Potions in the Great Gnarled Tree location.

The Bear Mouse is not known to drop any loot in the Calm Clearing location.


## History and Trivia

- 31 March 2009: The Bear Mouse was released with the introduction of the Calm Clearing.
- 25 June 2013: The Bear Mouse gained a vulnerability to Law traps, with the release of Varmint Valley.
- 1 June 2021: The Bear Mouse started dropping Teddy Bear as loot.
