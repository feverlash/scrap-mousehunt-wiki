## Hunting Strategy

Cardshark Mouse is only available when the Hunter has cleared all Accomplices in a Scamping Scammers Wanted Poster.
Law power type is very effective against this mouse. All other types are ineffective.


## History and Trivia

- 25 June 2013: Cardshark Mouse was released as part of the Claw Shot City, but couldn't be attracted yet.
- 1 July 2013: Cardshark Mouse was made available with the Scamping Scammers Wanted Poster.
