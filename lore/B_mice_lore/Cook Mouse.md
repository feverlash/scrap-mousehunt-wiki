# Cook Mouse

The Cook Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the S.S. Huntington IV. It is known to drop Ancient Relic as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Cook Mouse has been found to be particularly attracted to SUPER|brie+ cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.


## History and Trivia

- 14 April 2011: The Cook Mouse began dropping the Antique Green Plaid Egg as loot for the Spring Egg Hunt 2011 event.
- 14 April 2011: The Cook Mouse began dropping a bonus Cook Egg as loot for the Spring Egg Hunt 2011 event.
- 29 August 2012: The Cook Mouse began dropping the Ancient Relic as loot.
