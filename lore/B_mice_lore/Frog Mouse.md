## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Frog Mouse is only attracted to Standard Cheese.

In the Calm Clearing location, the Frog Mouse will not be attracted when hunting with Cherry Cheese.
In the Great Gnarled Tree location, the Frog Mouse will not be attracted when hunting with Gnarled Cheese.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Frog Mouse.

## Locations

Hunters can encounter the Frog Mouse in the Calm Clearing and Great Gnarled Tree locations.


## Loot

The Frog Mouse drops Gnarled Potions as loot in the Great Gnarled Tree.

The Frog Mouse is not known to drop any loot in the Calm Clearing location.


## History and Trivia

- 31 March 2009: The Frog Mouse was released with the introduction of the Calm Clearing.
- 9 April 2009: The Frog Mouse began dropping Gnarled Potion as loot with its introduction in the Great Gnarled Tree location.
- 25 June 2013: The Frog Mouse gained a vulnerability to Law traps, with the release of Varmint Valley.
