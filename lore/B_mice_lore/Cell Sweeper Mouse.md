## Cheese Preference

Cell Sweeper Mouse is only attracted to Beanster Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Cell Sweeper Mouse can only be attracted after planting a Short Vine to the Dungeon Floor.

Physical power type is effective against Cell Sweeper Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.


## Loot

Cell Sweeper Mouse can drop:

- Lavish Lapis Bean
