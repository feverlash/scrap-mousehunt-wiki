# ⚔️ Surprise Party Trap

> **Classification**: Law Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 2,000 Power | 4 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Legendary  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 1,0003 Golden Punch Tickets

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> A birthday surprise party where the mice bring themselves right to the law enforcement! No need to serve fancy warrants or send elaborate SWAT (Special Weapons and Traps) teams! Just let the criminals walk right in!
> Inside you will find a lovely Birthday Cake, a squad of droids who sing you your rights, a fun game of "put the cuffs on the criminal", surprise boxes of evidence, and some lucky mice even get to leave with a cool orange party jumpsuit!
> This trap has a rare chance to drop a Party Charm when a mouse is caught!

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Surprise Party Trap can be purchased for 1,000 gold and 3 Golden Punch Tickets at The SUPER|brie+ Factory Trapsmith.
