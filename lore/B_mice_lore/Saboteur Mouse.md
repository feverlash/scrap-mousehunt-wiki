# Saboteur Mouse

The Saboteur Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Slushy Shoreline and Bombing Run zone of the Iceberg. They are known to drop Heating Oil as loot in the Slushy Shoreline, and Hunting Research, Satchel of Gold, Sticky Charms, and Wax Charms in the Iceberg.

## Preferred Cheese

The Saboteur Mouse has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Saboteur Mouse can be encountered in the Slushy Shoreline only when a hunter has Softserve Charms armed. In the Iceberg, catching a Saboteur Mouse with the Iceberg Boiler Base, Remote Detonator Base, or Ultimate Iceberg Base armed will provide an additional advance through the Iceberg.


## History and Trivia

- 14 May 2012: The Saboteur Mouse was released in Slushy Shoreline.
- Between 14 May 2012 and 24 May 2012: The Saboteur Mouse dropped Interlocking Hull Fragments.
- 1 June 2012: The Saboteur Mouse began to drop loot in the Iceberg.
