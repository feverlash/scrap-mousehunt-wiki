## Cheese Preference

Boulder and Pebble is only attracted to Sky Pirate Swiss or Sky Raider Romano.


## Hunting Strategy

The Boulder and Pebble can only be attracted during a Mining shipment.

Law power type is effective against Boulder and Pebble.
All other types are ineffective.
