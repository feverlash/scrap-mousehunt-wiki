# Elub Lich Mouse

The Elub Lich Mouse is a breed of mouse found in Balack's Cove. It is known to drop Flawless Orb and Pinch of Annoyance as loot. It is also known to drop the Lich Jewel as loot, but only while hunters are in the process of completing the Zurreal Trap Research Assignment.

## Preferred Cheese

The Elub Lich Mouse has been found to be particularly attracted to Vengeful Vanilla Stilton and, to a lesser extent, Vanilla Stilton cheeses.


## History and Trivia

- 17 May 2010: The Elub Lich Mouse is released with the introduction of Balack's Cove.
- 14 April 2011: The Elub Lich Mouse began dropping a bonus Cove Egg as loot during the Spring Egg Hunt 2011 event.
- 23 August 2011: The Elub Lich Mouse began to drop the Lich Jewel as loot.
- 6 February 2014: The Elub Lich Mouse began to drop Flawless Orbs as loot.
- 22 March 2022: The Elub Lich Mouse began to drop Scrap Metal as loot.
