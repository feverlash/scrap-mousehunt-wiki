# Juliyes Mouse

The Juliyes Mouse is part of the Indigenous Mice found in Town of Gnawnia.
It was previously an Event Mouse that was found during the annual Valentine's Day or Lunar New Year events.

## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Juliyes Mouse is attracted only to Gilded Cheese.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Juliyes Mouse.

## History and Trivia

- 10 February 2011: The Juliyes Mouse was released as part of the Heart of the Rabbit Party event.
- 19 March 2024: The Juliyes Mouse became a permanent addition to the Town of Gnawnia on the start of the Spring Egg Hunt 2023 event. Its gold were adjusted to be appropriate for its new difficulties:
Gold: 999 -> 9,999
