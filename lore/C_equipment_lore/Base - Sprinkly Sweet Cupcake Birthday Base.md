# 🛡️ Sprinkly Sweet Cupcake Birthday Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 300 Power | 9 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Journeyman  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 0

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> A sprinkly sweet batch of super sweet sprinkles are contained in this special sprinkle container! Quite the sparkly spectacle for mice looking to sprinkle up their spoils during MouseHunt's 9th Birthday!
> The Head Trapsmith Chef is at it again with this year's sprinkly surprise: Sprinkles that get jostled out during attempted cupcake catchery turn into Sprinkly Sweet Cupcake Charms! Also, with the well-established included lifetime warranty, all lost sprinkles are covered under an immediate replacement plan, so your base is never missing one of its colourful specs of sugar!
> This base has a rare chance to drop a Sprinkly Sweet Cupcake Charm when a mouse is caught!
> A flavour first savoured during MouseHunt's 9th birthday!

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
This base can be obtained during the annual MouseHunt Birthday event. The requirements for obtaining this base may change from year to year.
