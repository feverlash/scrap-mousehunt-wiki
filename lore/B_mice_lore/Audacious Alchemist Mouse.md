## Cheese Preference

Audacious Alchemist Mouse is only attracted to Apprentice Ambert.


## Hunting Strategy

The Audacious Alchemist Mouse can only be attracted while enrolled in Shadow Sciences or during the Shadow phase of the Final Exam.

Shadow power type is very effective against Audacious Alchemist Mouse.
Arcane power type is effective against Audacious Alchemist Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
