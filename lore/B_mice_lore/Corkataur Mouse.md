## Preferred Cheese

The Corkataur Mouse is only attracted to Wildfire Queso.


## Hunting Strategy

Only Draconic traps are effective against the Corkataur Mouse, all other power types are ineffective. The Corkataur Mouse can only be encountered in Queso Geyser while gathering cork bark.

Dragonbane Charm, Super Dragonbane Charm, Extreme Dragonbane Charm, and Ultimate Dragonbane Charm provide a power bonus against the Corkataur Mouse.


## History and Trivia

- 30 April 2019: The Corkataur Mouse was introduced, with the release of the Queso Geyser area.
