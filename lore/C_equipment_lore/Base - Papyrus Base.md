# 🛡️ Papyrus Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 400 Power | 10 Luck

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Gathered together in this base is a stack of extremely thin papyrus notes revealing many mouse secrets and weaknesses. Having this knowledge stacked with your trap will surely grant you the upper hand against many foes.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Papyrus Base can be purchased from the Trapsmith in Muridae Market for:

- 1,296,000 Gold
- 98 Papyrus
- 35 Scrap Metal

It requires 1 Rebuild Trapsmith to purchase.

It can be refunded for 233,280 Gold at the same location.
