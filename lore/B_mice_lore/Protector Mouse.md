# Protector Mouse

The Protector Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Tribal Isles region. They are known to drop Blue Pepper Seed in Elub Shore.

Protector mice have been found to be especially attracted to Shell cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.
