# Camoflower Mouse

The Camoflower Mouse is a breed of mouse that can be found in the Living Garden, and is known to drop Aleth Essences and Dewthief Petals as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Camoflower Mouse is attracted to Standard Cheese and Duskshade Camembert.


## Hunting Strategy

The Camoflower Mouse is a member of the Living Garden mouse group. As such all power types except Hydro weapons are ineffective against it.


## History and Trivia

- 14 November 2012: The Camoflower Mouse was released as a part of the Living Garden update.
