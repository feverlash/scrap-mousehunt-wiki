# 🛡️ Desert Heater Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 500 Power | 10 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Baron  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 25,000 Gold40 Ornament Sugar Cookies20 Snowflake Sugar Cookies

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> A repurposed holiday heater redesigned to turn up the heat on mice! Fueled by Flameshards, using this base in the Sandtail Desert will turn the heat up so high that mice will drop more loot in an attempt to quickly shed layers and escape!
> While armed within the Sandtail Desert:
> • This base will consume a Flameshard on every hunt
> • Mice will drop bonus loot when caught

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Desert Heater Base can be purchased for 25,000 Gold, 40 Ornament Sugar Cookies and 20 Snowflake Sugar Cookies at Great Winter Hunt 2019.
