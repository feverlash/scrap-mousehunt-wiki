# 📍 Great Gnarled Tree

> **Wilayah / Region**: Whisker Woods  
> **Pangkat Minimal**: Recruit  
> **Fasilitas Lokal**: Cheese ShoppeTrapsmithCharm ShoppeGeneral StoreCartographer

## 🌍 Gambaran & Atmosfer Lingkungan

Great Gnarled Tree is a hunting location in Whisker Woods region.
It requires the rank of Master and the Great Gnarled Tree Map Piece to access.

The Hunter's main goal here is to catch Fairy to get Map of the Lagoon.
Gnarled Potion and Wicked Gnarly Potion needed for Lagoon can be obtained here as well.
