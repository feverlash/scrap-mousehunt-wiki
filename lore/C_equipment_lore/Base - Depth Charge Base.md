# 🛡️ Depth Charge Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 450 Power | 10 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Legendary  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 200,000

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> An old, antique depth charge found washed ashore and repurposed as a trap base. Perfectly safe while on land but activates as soon as you go below sea level.
> This base receives an additional 1,000 Power when diving at the Sunken City!

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Depth Charge Base could only be bought from the Extreme Toboggan Challenge Trapsmith during Great Winter Hunt 2015. It cost 200,000 gold and could not be refunded.
