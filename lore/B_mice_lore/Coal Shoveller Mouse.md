## Hunting Strategy

Law power type is very effective against Coal Shoveller Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.

- In Claw Shot City, this mouse is available when the Hunter is working on a Thugs Gang Wanted Poster or Dirt Dealers Wanted Poster.
- In Gnawnian Express Station, this mouse is available during Daredevil Canyon phase.


## History and Trivia

- 25 June 2013: Coal Shoveler Mouse was released as part of the Claw Shot City.
- 26 September 2013: Coal Shoveller Mouse was added to Gnawnian Express Station.
It was renamed from Coal Shoveler Mouse to Coal Shoveller Mouse.
It also received a longer description.
