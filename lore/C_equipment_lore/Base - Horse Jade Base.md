# 🛡️ Horse Jade Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 325 Power | 10 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Grandmaster  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 525,000

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Only hunters who have helped Carl complete a full cruise and saved up sufficient Cruise Bux will be able to have their Jade Base carved into this attractive and fantastic base!

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Horse Jade Base was available for purchase for 525,000 gold, 1 Jade Base, and 1000 Cruise Bux from Calamity Carl's Cozy Cruise Trapsmith during the Year of the Horse Festival once the Captain's Cavalry Sword was obtained. It could not be refunded and can no longer be obtained.
