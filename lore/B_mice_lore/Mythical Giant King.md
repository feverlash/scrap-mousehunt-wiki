## Hunting Strategy

Mythical Giant King is available at the end of the giant chase phase on Great Hall Floor in Bountiful Beanstalk.
It can be attracted by Standard Cheese, Beanster, Lavish Beanster, Leaping Lavish Beanster, and Royal Beanster.

Physical power type is very effective against Mythical Giant King.
All other types are ineffective.

Catching Mythical Giant King will cause the Hunter to be moved to back to the Beanstalk.
