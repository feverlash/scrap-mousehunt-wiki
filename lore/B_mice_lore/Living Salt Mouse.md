# Living Salt Mouse

The Living Salt Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Iceberg. They are known to drop Hunting Research, Satchel of Gold, Sticky Charms, and Wax Charms as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Living Salt Mouse has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Living Salt Mouse is uncommon in the first four areas of the Iceberg (Treacherous Tunnels, Brutal Bulwark, Bombing Run, and the Mad Depths) before 425 hunts, but is abundant in the Hidden Depths, or throughout the Iceberg (before Icewing's Lair) after 425 hunts are exhausted.


## History and Trivia

- 1 June 2012: The Living Salt Mouse was released in Iceberg.
- On its original release, the Living Salt Mouse could only be encountered when a hunter had exhausted 425 hunts and not yet entered Icewing's Lair in the Iceberg. These requirements were relaxed to the current strategy on 18 June 2012 and the second paragraph of their description was also changed from the following, original paragraph:

> These mice will only appear after all the chests have been smashed and the Iceberg has reached Gnawnia's shores.
