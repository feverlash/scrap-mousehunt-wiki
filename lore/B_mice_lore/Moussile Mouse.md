## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Moussile Mouse is attracted only to Empowered SUPER|brie+, Rockforth Cheese and SUPER|brie+.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Moussile Mouse.

More information on how to locate and catch the Moussile Mouse can be found on the current Ronza location page.

## History and Trivia

- 19 March 2014: The Moussile Mouse was released as part of MouseHunt's Sixth Birthday event.
- The first capture of the Moussile Mouse awarded the hunter with the Limited Edition collectible Inert Riftwood, along with this bonus message:

> Capturing a Moussile Mouse revealed another clue!
I found a fragment of material that has faint traces of the same type of energy now being produced at the power generator.
The metal seems to fade in and out, as though it's from another plane of existence. Could this be evidence of the twisted world spilling further into our own? Could this be some sort of a rift opening?!
Clearly, the Moussiles are to blame for the generator sabotage!
Convinced I've learned all there is to know for now, the King gave me a reward!
30 King's Credits have been added to my inventory.

- Hunters that obtained the Inert Riftwood had the option of purchasing the Rift Detector map piece early at the Vacant Lot Cartographer.
- 25 March 2014: The Moussile Mouse became the first Event Mouse to remain available in the Vacant Lot after an event ended.

> After MouseHunt's Sixth Birthday event ended, the Moussile Mouse dropped Inert Riftwood in the Vacant Lot but no longer dropped other loot.
