## History and Trivia

- 10 December 2012: The Triple Lutz Mouse was released as part of the Great Winter Hunt 2012 event. Hunters had to catch a Confused Courier with the Letter to Santa, Age 6 to attract this mouse..
- 28 April 2013: Even though the Triple Lutz Mouse was not available during the Great Gnawnian Games, it was assigned to this sub-group rather than the Great Winter Hunt sub-group when sub-groups were added.
- 8 October 2013: The Triple Lutz Mouse was reassigned to the Great Winter Hunt sub-group.
- 13 December 2016: The points for Triple Lutz Mouse were changed.
