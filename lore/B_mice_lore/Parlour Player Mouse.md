## Hunting Strategy

Law power type is very effective against Parlour Player Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.

- In Claw Shot City, this mouse is available when the Hunter is working on a Thugs Gang Wanted Poster, Thieves Gang Wanted Poster, or Scamping Scammers Wanted Poster.
- In Gnawnian Express Station, this mouse is always available.


## History and Trivia

- 25 June 2013: Parlour Player Mouse was released as part of the Claw Shot City.
- 26 September 2013: Parlour Player Mouse was added to Gnawnian Express Station.
