## History and Trivia

- 10 December 2012: The Confused Courier Mouse was released with the introduction of the MegaBuy Mart. It could only be caught once at the beginning of each phase of the Great Winter Hunt event, with phases beginning on the 10th, 12th, 14th, 17th, 19th, and 21st of December.
- The artwork includes an image of a broken compass, with all directions labeled N.
- 13 December 2016: The points/gold for Confused Courier Mouse were changed.
- 8 December 2023: The Confused Courier Mouse drops a Passing Parcel if the hunter does not already own a parcel.
