# ⚔️ Ambush Trap

> **Classification**: Tactical Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 3,000 Power | 12 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Grandmaster  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 63,410 Gold1 Master's Seal1 Ambush Trap Blueprints

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> This peaceful setting for tea and a light snack appears to be the perfect spot for war-hardened mice to pause for some peaceful rest and meditation. Yet behind a thin paper wall lies a robot ambush of epic proportions! The loyal droids follow the will of their controlling hunter to either capture and exile mice or use their high speed reflexes for a heavy battle that is sure to erupt into various explosions and montages.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Ambush Trap can be purchased from Trapsmith in Dojo, Meditation Room, and Pinnacle Chamber for:

- 63,410 Gold
- 1 Master's Seal
- 1 Ambush Trap Blueprints

It cannot be refunded.

The Ambush Trap (if unintentionally smashed) can be reassembled from Ambush Trap Parts.
