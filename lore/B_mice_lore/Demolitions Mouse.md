## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Demolitions Mouse is attracted only to Limelight Cheese.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Demolitions Mouse.

Arming the Mining Charm provides a power boost against Digby Dirt Dwellers.

Hunters can encounter the Demolitions Mouse only in the Town of Digby.


## Loot

The Demolitions Mouse is not known to drop any loot.
