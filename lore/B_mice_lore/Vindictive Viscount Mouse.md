## Cheese Preference

Vindictive Viscount Mouse is only attracted to Beanster Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Vindictive Viscount Mouse can only be attracted after planting a Tall Vine to the Great Hall Floor.

Physical power type is effective against Vindictive Viscount Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.


## Loot

Vindictive Viscount Mouse can drop:

- Golden Goose Egg
