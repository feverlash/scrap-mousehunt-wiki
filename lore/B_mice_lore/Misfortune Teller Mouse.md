## Cheese Preference

Misfortune Teller Mouse is only attracted to Standard Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Misfortune Teller Mouse can only be attracted while enrolled in Arcane Arts or during the Arcane phase of the Final Exam.

Arcane power type is very effective against Misfortune Teller Mouse.
Shadow power type is effective against Misfortune Teller Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
