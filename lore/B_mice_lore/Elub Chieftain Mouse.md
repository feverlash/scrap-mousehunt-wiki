# Elub Chieftain Mouse

The Elub Chieftain Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Elub Shore. It is known to drop Blue Pepper Seeds as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Elub Chieftain Mouse has been found to be especially attracted to Shell cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.


## History and Trivia

- 21 July 2009: The Elub Chieftain Mouse was introduced with the release oif the Tribal Isles region.
- 14 April 2011: The Elub Chieftain Mouse began dropping a bonus Elub Egg as loot during the Spring Egg Hunt 2011 event.
