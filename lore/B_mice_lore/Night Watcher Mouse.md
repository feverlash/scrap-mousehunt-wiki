# Night Watcher Mouse

The Night Watcher is a breed of mouse found in Fort Rox. They are known to drop Bloodstone as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Night Watcher Mouse have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Arcane traps are very effective against the Night Watcher Mouse. Shadow traps are of normal effectiveness.
The Night Watcher Mouse can only be encountered during the Midnight, Pitch, Utter Darkness, and First Light phases at Nighttime in Fort Rox.


## History and Trivia

- 3 November 2016: The Night Watcher Mouse was release as a part of Fort Rox update.
