# Mischievous Meteorite Miner Mouse

The Mischievous Meteorite Miner is a breed of mouse found in Fort Rox. They are known to drop Meteorite Pieces as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Mischievous Meteorite Miner Mouse mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Law type traps are very effective against the Mischievous Meteorite Miner Mouse. Arcane and Shadow power types are less effective.
The Mischievous Meteorite Miner Mouse can only be encountered during the daytime phase in Fort Rox.


## History and Trivia

- 3 November 2016: The Mischievous Meteorite Miner Mouse was release as a part of Fort Rox update.
