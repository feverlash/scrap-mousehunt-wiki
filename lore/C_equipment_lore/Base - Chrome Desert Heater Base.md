# 🛡️ Chrome Desert Heater Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 1250 Power | 25 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Archduke  
> **Acquisition Cost**: #Obtained Via

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> This base was originally a repurposed holiday heater redesigned to turn up the heat on mice! Fueled by Flameshards, using this base in the Sandtail Desert will turn the heat up so high that mice will drop more loot in an attempt to quickly shed layers and escape!
> However, the original design blueprints that contained the secrets to this base's extraordinary effects was lost to the Trapsmiths of Gnawnia... until now! It turns out that the villainous Captain Crook discovered and then stole these lost blueprints during her raid of the Fiery Warpath so that she could make her own pirated version. Now that the design secrets have been recovered, the Trapsmith at the Cerulean Skyport is able to forge this new, stronger, and shinier version using the miraculous combination of Cerulean Chrome that has been charged with the energy from Adorned Empyrean Jewels!
> While armed within the Sandtail Desert:
> • This base will consume a Flameshard on every hunt
> • Mice will drop bonus loot when caught

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Chrome Desert Heater Base can be purchased from the Trapsmith in Cerulean Skyport for:

- 5,000,000 Gold
- 20 Chrome Desert Heater Blueprint Scrap
- 200 Cerulean Chrome Ingot
- 3 Adorned Empyrean Jewel

It requires the Hunter to own the Chrome Base and complete the Liberate the Cerulean Skyport from Captain Crook adventure to purchase.

It cannot be refunded.
