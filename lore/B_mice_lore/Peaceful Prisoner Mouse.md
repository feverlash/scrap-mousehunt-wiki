## Cheese Preference

Peaceful Prisoner Mouse can be attracted with any Standard Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Physical power type is effective against Peaceful Prisoner Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.


## Loot

Peaceful Prisoner Mouse can drop:

- Lavish Lapis Bean
