## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Bearded Elder Mouse is only attracted to Mild Queso, Medium Queso, Hot Queso or Flamin' Queso.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Bearded Elder Mouse.


## Hunting Strategy

Only Draconic traps are effective against the Bearded Elder Mouse, all other power types are ineffective. The Bearded Elder Mouse can only be encountered in Queso Geyser while a Medium Eruption is triggered.

Dragonbane Charm, Super Dragonbane Charm, Extreme Dragonbane Charm, and Ultimate Dragonbane Charm provide a power bonus against the Bearded Elder Mouse.


## History and Trivia

- 30 April 2019: The Bearded Elder Mouse was introduced, with the release of the Queso Geyser area.
