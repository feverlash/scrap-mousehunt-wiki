## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Ignatia Mouse is only attracted to Hot Queso or Flamin' Queso.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Ignatia Mouse.


## Hunting Strategy

Only Draconic traps are effective against the Ignatia Mouse, all other power types are ineffective. The Ignatia Mouse can only be encountered in Queso Geyser while either a Tiny Eruption, Small Eruption, Medium Eruption, Large Eruption or an Epic Eruption is triggered.

Dragonbane Charm, Super Dragonbane Charm, Extreme Dragonbane Charm, and Ultimate Dragonbane Charm provide a power bonus against the Ignatia Mouse.


## History and Trivia

- 30 April 2019: The Ignatia Mouse was introduced, with the release of the Queso Geyser area.
