## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

In the Acolyte Realm and Forbidden Grove locations, the Mutated Brown Mouse is attracted only to Radioactive Blue Cheese.
In the Bazaar location, the Mutated Brown Mouse is attracted to Standard Cheese and Gilded Cheese. 
In the Laboratory location, the Mutated Brown Mouse is attracted to Radioactive Blue Cheese, Rancid Radioactive Blue Cheese and Magical Rancid Radioactive Blue Cheese.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Mutated Brown Mouse.


## Power Type Weaknesses

The Mutated Brown Mouse is weak to all Power Types (except Draconic, Parental and Rift). However, in the Forbidden Grove, and Acolyte Realm locations Shadow or Arcane weapons will be effective against a larger group of the available mice.
It is recommended that Hunters use a trap that will be effective against the main mouse population in their hunting location.

## Loot

The Mutated Brown Mouse drops Stale Cheese as loot in the Forbidden Grove and Acolyte Realm locations.

The Mutated Brown Mouse is not known to drop any loot in the Laboratory or Bazaar locations.

## History and Trivia

- 17 October 2013: The Mutated Brown Mouse was released at the start of the Halloween 2013 event.
- 17 October 2013: The Mutated Brown Mouse was released to all the same locations as the Mutated White Mouse and Mutated Grey Mouse.
- 25 March 2014: The Mutated Brown Mouse gained a vulnerability to Rift power type traps, with the introduction of the Rift Plane.
- 9 September 2014: The Amplified Brown Mouse mouse was introduced as the Rift counterpart of the Mutated Brown Mouse.
- 17 October 2018: The Mutated Brown Mouse changed mouse groups from Indigenous Mice to Lab Experiments and also was removed from the Mousoleum. It is also no longer attracted to Standard Cheese in Laboratory.
