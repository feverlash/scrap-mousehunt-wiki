# Black Powder Thief Mouse

The Black Powder Thief is a breed of mouse found in the Gnawnian Express Station. It can only be attracted and caught during the Daredevil Canyon phase and only with Black Powder Charms armed. They are known to drop Fuel Nuggets, Magmatic Crystal Charms, and Tin Scraps as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Black Powder Thief mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese, but can only be attracted when a hunter has a Black Powder Charm armed.


## Hunting Strategy

Law type traps such as S.L.A.C. are very effective against the Black Powder Thief Mouse.


## Event Participation

- The Black Powder Thief Mouse drops the Gnawnian Express Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

- Released on 26 September 2013 as part of the Gnawnian Express Station content.
