# Heavy Blaster Mouse

The Heavy Blaster Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Bombing Run zone of the Iceberg. It is known to drop Hunting Research, Satchel of Gold, Sticky Charms, and Wax Charms as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Heavy Blaster Mouse has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Catching a Heavy Blaster Mouse with the Iceberg Boiler Base, Remote Detonator Base, or Ultimate Iceberg Base armed will provide an additional advance through the Iceberg.


## History and Trivia

- 24 May 2012: The Heavy Blaster Mouse was released as a part of the Iceberg.
- 1 June 2012: The Heavy Blaster Mouse began to drop loot.
