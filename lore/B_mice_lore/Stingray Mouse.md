The Stingray Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Sunken City. It is known to drop a variety of loot.

## Cheese Preference

The Stingray Mouse has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Stingray Mouse is susceptible only to Hydro weapons. It can be found in the Deep Oxygen Stream, Murky Depths, Oxygen Stream, Sea Floor and Shipwreck zones while on a Dive in the Sunken City.


## Event Participation


### Spring Egg Hunt

- The Stingray Mouse drops the Barnacle Egg, the Bubbling Egg, the Deep, Deep, Deep Egg, the Oxygen Egg, and the Salty Sea Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

The Stingray Mouse was released 2 July 2014 as part of the Sunken City content.
