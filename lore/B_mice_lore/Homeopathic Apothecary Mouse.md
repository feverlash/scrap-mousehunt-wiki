## Preferred Cheese

The Homeopathic Apothecary Mouse has been found to be especially attracted to Glowing Gruyere Cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.

## Hunting Strategy

Only Shadow, Arcane and Draconic type traps are effective against Homeopathic Apothecary Mouse, all other power types are ineffective.
