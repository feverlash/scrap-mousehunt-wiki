## Preferred Cheese

The Cycloness Mouse has been found to be especially attracted to Windy Cheese cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.

## Hunting Strategy

Only Shadow, Arcane and Draconic type traps are effective against the Cycloness Mouse, all other power types are ineffective.

The Cycloness Mouse is only available between 35%-99% wind intensities in Moussu Picchu.
