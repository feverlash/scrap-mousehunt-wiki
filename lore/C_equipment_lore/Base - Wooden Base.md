# 🛡️ Wooden Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 35 Power | 0 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Novice  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 300 Gold

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> A standard wood base to attach to your weapon.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Wooden Base is given to Hunter's by Larry during their introduction to MouseHunt.

The Wooden Base can be purchased for 300 Gold from the Trapsmith in Town of Gnawnia.

It refunds for 60 Gold at the same location.
