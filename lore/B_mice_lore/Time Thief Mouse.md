Pouring over decades of designs and patterns, this inquisitive mouse began his illustrious career in the world of cloth and thread as a humble tailor, weaving beautiful garments for the mousey elite, inspired by history's influence over fashion.<br>The more he explored the history of fashion, the more he learned its secrets.
