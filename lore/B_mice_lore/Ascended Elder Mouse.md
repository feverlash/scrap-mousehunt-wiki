# Ascended Elder Mouse

The Ascended Elder Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Furoma Rift. It is the Rift version of Hapless Mouse.

## Preferred Cheese

The Ascended Elder Mouse has been found to be especially attracted to Ascended Cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.


## Hunting Strategy

Only Rift type traps are very effective against Ascended Elder Mouse, all other power types are ineffective.
