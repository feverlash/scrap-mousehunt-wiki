## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Goblin Mouse is attracted only when hunting with Gnarled Cheese or Wicked Gnarly Cheese.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Goblin Mouse.


## Power Type Weaknesses


| VeryEffective | Effective | Less Effective |

## Locations

Hunters can encounter the Goblin Mouse only in the Lagoon location.


## Loot

The Goblin Mouse drops Flawless Orbs and Scrap Metal in the Lagoon location.


## History and Trivia

- The original image of the Goblin Mouse was edited to remove a piece of the loincloth that created confusion over what was being depicted.
- 22 April 2009: The Goblin Mouse was released with the introduction of the Lagoon.
- 25 January 2011: The Goblin Mouse began to drop Flawless Orb as loot.
- 18 April 2011: The Goblin Mouse began dropping the Antique Wavy Purple Egg as loot as part of the Spring Egg Hunt 2011 event.
- 29 July 2015: The Goblin Mouse began dropping Scrap Metal as loot.
