## Cheese Preference

The Clownfish Mouse has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Clownfish Mouse is susceptible only to Hydro weapons. It can be found in the Coral Reef, Feeding Grounds, Rocky Outcrop, School of Mice and Shallow Shoals zones while on a Dive in the Sunken City.


## Event Participation


### Spring Egg Hunt

- The Clownfish Mouse drops the Barnacle Egg, the Coral Egg, the Mouse Scale Egg, the Salty Sea Egg, and the Underwater Predator Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

The Clownfish Mouse was released 2 July 2014 as part of the Sunken City content.
