# Seadragon Mouse

The Seadragon Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Sunken City. It is known to drop Damaged Coral Fragments and Sand Dollars as loot.

Fairly small in size, the Seadragon Mouse enjoys life along the bottom of most sea banks, mostly floating and drifting, using their tails to anchor themselves in the more interesting parts of the ocean.They have long, leaf-like appendages which help them blend in with the local flora. ## Cheese Preference

The Seadragon Mouse has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Seadragon Mouse is susceptible only to Hydro weapons. It can be found in the Coral Garden and Coral Reef zones while on a Dive in Sunken City.


## Event Participation


### Spring Egg Hunt

- The Seadragon Mouse drops the Coral Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

The Seadragon Mouse was released 2 July 2014 as part of the Sunken City content.
