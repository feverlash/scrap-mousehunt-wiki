# ⚔️ Chrome Nannybot

> **Classification**: Shadow Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 1,200 Power | 6 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Grandmaster  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 508,000 gold

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> No shenanigans will go unpunished while the Chrome Nannybot guards your trap base! With a little ingenuity and and the help of the Digby scientists, you've built the deluxe factory model, complete with anti-sass-mouth scanners, advanced gum-in-hair removal matrixes, and a stern yet sensible nagging voice available in thirty-eight languages. Now mice who visit can receive a much-needed lesson in table manners before being frozen for your convenience.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Chrome Nannybot is a Limited Edition weapon and can no longer be acquired by Hunters. It was not sold by any Trapsmith. It can only be crafted given the proper ingredients.
