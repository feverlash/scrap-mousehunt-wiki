# 🛡️ 2026 New Year's Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 226 Power | 10 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Novice  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 25 Firework Sugar Cookie

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Happy New Year! This party-powered base celebrates 2025 all year long!
> During all of 2025, while this base is equipped, mice have a chance to drop 2025 Charms when caught. The charm-generating powers of this base will cease to work on January 1, 2026.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The 2026 New Year's Base were available during Great Winter Hunt 2025 event from 30 December 2025.

It could be purchased for 25 Firework Sugar Cookie from the Trapsmith in:

- Cinnamon Hill
- Golem Workshop
- Ice Fortress

It cannot be refunded.
