# Aged Mouse

The Aged Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Tribal Isles region. They are known to drop Ancient Relic, Rhino Horn and Stale SUPER|brie+ in Cape Clawed.

## Preferred Cheese

Aged Mice have been found to be especially attracted to Crunchy cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.


## Hunting Strategy

The Aged Mouse is a typical member of The Derr Tribe group, and as such Physical weapons are of normal effectiveness against it. All the other weapons are ineffective.
