# Drudge Mouse

The Drudge Mouse can be found in the Labyrinth and Zokor. It is known to drop Plates of Fealty in Zokor.

## Preferred Cheese

The Drudge Mouse prefers Glowing Gruyere in both the Labyrinth and Zokor, but can be attracted with Standard Cheeses.


## Hunting Strategy

Forgotten weapons are very effective against the Drudge Mouse. Arcane weapons are of normal effectiveness.

- Labyrinth

> The Drudge Mouse can be found in Plain, Superior, and Epic Fealty Hallways.

- Zokor

> The Drudge Mouse can be found in the Outer Fealty Shrine, the Inner Fealty Temple, and the Templar's Sanctum.


## Loot

- Zokor

> Labyrinth Theme Scrap III and Plates of Fealty
