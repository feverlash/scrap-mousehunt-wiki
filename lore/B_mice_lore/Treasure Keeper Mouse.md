## Cheese Preference

The Treasure Keeper Mouse has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Treasure Keeper Mouse is susceptible only to Hydro weapons. It can be found in the Sunken Treasure zone while on a Dive in the Sunken City.


## Event Participation


### Spring Egg Hunt

- The Treasure Keeper Mouse drops the Deep, Deep, Deep Egg, the Sand Dollar Egg and the Sunken Treasure Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

The Treasure Keeper Mouse was released 2 July 2014 as part of the Sunken City content.
