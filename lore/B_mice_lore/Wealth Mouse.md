# Wealth Mouse

The Wealth Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Gnawnia Rift and Furoma Rift. It is not known to drop any loot.

## Preferred Cheese

In Gnawnia Rift, the Wealth Mouse has been found to be especially attracted to Riftiago cheese, although Swiss String, Brie String, and Magical String cheeses will also suffice.

In Furoma Rift, the Wealth Mouse is only attracted to String Cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.
