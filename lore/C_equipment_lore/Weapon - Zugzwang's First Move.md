# ⚔️ Zugzwang's First Move

> **Classification**: Tactical Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 4,200 Power | 17 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Lord/Lady  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 615,000 Gold1 Zugzwang's Last Move1 Magic Feather

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> The chess pieces of Zugzwang's Last Move have been reborn with the help of a magic feather, becoming a powerful knight who will defend your cheese to the death. Using strategy gleaned from his origins as a chess set, and a stubbornness that can only come from being made of stone, he will fight off any rodent attacker in the name of his King or Queen.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Zugzwang's First Move can be purchased from Trapsmith in Seasonal Garden, Zugzwang's Tower, and Crystal Library for:

- 615,000 Gold
- 1 Zugzwang's Last Move
- 1 Magic Feather

It cannot be refunded.
