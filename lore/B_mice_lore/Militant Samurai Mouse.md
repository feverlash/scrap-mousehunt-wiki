# Militant Samurai Mouse

The Militant Samurai Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Furoma Rift. It is the Rift counterpart of Samurai Mouse.

## Preferred Cheese

The Militant Samurai Mouse has been found to be attracted to String Cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice. The Militant Samurai Mouse avoids Marble String outside the Grand Pagoda.


## Hunting Strategy

Only Rift type traps are very effective against Militant Samurai Mouse, all other power types are ineffective.


## History and Trivia

- 18 May 2016: Militant Samurai Mouse was released with the release of Furoma Rift.
