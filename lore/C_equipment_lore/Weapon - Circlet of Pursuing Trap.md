# ⚔️ Circlet of Pursuing Trap

> **Classification**: Arcane Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 9,000 Power | 35 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Archduke/Archduchess  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 13,000,000 Gold4 Sky Sprocket4 Skysoft Silk4 Enchanted Wing4 Cloudstone Bangle65 Adorned Empyrean Jewel

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> A significantly more powerful enchantment imbued by an ancient jewel transforms this winged crystal into a sentient familiar capable of strategic thinking. It can predict the movements of its quarry with remarkable accuracy which allows it to launch its golden circlet with impeccable timing and absolute precision. The Arcane power within this crystal is so potent that it can absorb the captured mouse's essence from a great distance which negates the need for its mighty and beautiful wings. However, now that the crystal is sentient, it chooses to keep the wings for reasons that can only be described as vanity.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Circlet of Pursuing Trap can be purchased from the Trapsmith in Floating Islands for:

- 13,000,000 Gold
- 4 Sky Sprocket
- 4 Skysoft Silk
- 4 Enchanted Wing
- 4 Cloudstone Bangle
- 65 Adorned Empyrean Jewel

It requires the hunter to own the Circlet of Seeking Trap to purchase.

It cannot be refunded.

The Circlet of Pursuing Trap (if unintentionally smashed) can be reassembled from Circlet of Pursuing Trap Parts.
