> **Classification**: Physical Weapon

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Swiss Army Mouse Trap can be purchased for 55,000 Gold from the Trapsmith in the following locations:

- Town of Gnawnia
- Windmill
- Harbour
- Mountain
- Calm Clearing
- Great Gnarled Tree
