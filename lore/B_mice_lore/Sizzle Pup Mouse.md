## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Sizzle Pup Mouse is only attracted to Mild Queso, Medium Queso, Hot Queso or Flamin' Queso.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Sizzle Pup Mouse.


## Hunting Strategy

Only Draconic traps are effective against the Sizzle Pup Mouse, all other power types are ineffective. The Sizzle Pup Mouse can only be encountered in Queso Geyser while a Small Eruption is triggered.

Dragonbane Charm, Super Dragonbane Charm, Extreme Dragonbane Charm, and Ultimate Dragonbane Charm provide a power bonus against the Sizzle Pup Mouse.


## History and Trivia

- 30 April 2019: The Sizzle Pup Mouse was introduced, with the release of the Queso Geyser area.
