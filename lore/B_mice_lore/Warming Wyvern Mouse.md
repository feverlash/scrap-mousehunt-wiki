## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Warming Wyvern Mouse is only attracted to Medium Queso, Hot Queso or Flamin' Queso.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Warming Wyvern Mouse.


## Hunting Strategy

Catching a Warming Wyvern Mouse while building pressure in Queso Geyser increases the amount of pressure by 15 kPa.

Only Draconic traps are effective against the Warming Wyvern Mouse, all other power types are ineffective. The Warming Wyvern Mouse can only be encountered while building pressure in Queso Geyser, after a cork has been constructed.

Dragonbane Charm, Super Dragonbane Charm, Extreme Dragonbane Charm, and Ultimate Dragonbane Charm provide a power bonus against the Warming Wyvern Mouse.
