## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Master of the Cheese Claw Mouse is attracted only to Susheese Cheese.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Master of the Cheese Claw Mouse.


## Power Type Weaknesses

## Locations

Hunters can encounter the Master of the Cheese Claw Mouse only in the Meditation Room location.


## Loot

The Master of the Cheese Claw Mouse drops the following loot in the Meditation Room location:

- Master Claw Shard
- Satchel of Gold (1,000 gold)


## History and Trivia

- 26 November 2008: The Master of the Cheese Claw Mouse was released.
- 10 February 2011: The Master of the Cheese Claw Mouse began to drop the Ox Mask as loot in the Meditation Room as part of the Heart of the Rabbit Party.
- 17 April 2011: The Master of the Cheese Claw Mouse began dropping the Antique Stripy Red Egg as loot as part of the Spring Egg Hunt 2011 event.
