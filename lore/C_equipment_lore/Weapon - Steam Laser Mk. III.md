# ⚔️ Steam Laser Mk. III

> **Classification**: Hydro Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 4,800 Power | 18 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Lord/Lady  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 1 Steam Laser Mk. II Parts1 Steam Nine

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> The ultimate in steam laser technology, the Steam Laser Mk. III is truly a marvel of engineering. Able to hyper-heat steam beyond normal (or safe) levels, this trap is truly the pinnacle of anti-Iceberg weaponry.This combination of hot and hydro works especially well on the Slushy Shoreline and in the Iceberg.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Steam Laser Mk. III can be purchased from Trapsmith in Slushy Shoreline and Iceberg for:

- 1 Steam Laser Mk. II Parts
- 1 Steam Nine

It cannot be refunded.
