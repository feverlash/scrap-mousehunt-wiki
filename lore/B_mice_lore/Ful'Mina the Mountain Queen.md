## Preferred Cheese

Ful'Mina, The Mountain Queen is only attracted to Dragonvine Cheese.

## Hunting Strategy

Only Draconic type traps are effective against Ful'Mina, The Mountain Queen, all other power types are ineffective.

Dragonbane Charm, Super Dragonbane Charm, Extreme Dragonbane Charm, and Ultimate Dragonbane Charm provide a power bonus against the Ful'Mina the Mountain Queen.

Ful'Mina, The Mountain Queen is only available when both wind and rain intensities are at least 80% in Moussu Picchu.
