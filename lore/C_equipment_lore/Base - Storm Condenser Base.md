# 🛡️ Storm Condenser Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 250 Power | 8 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Grand Duke/Duchess  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 0

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Constructed from repurposed hailstone, this base houses a powerful coil and fan capable of capturing and neutralizing stormy weather.
> While this base is equipped, the weather intensity at Moussu Picchu will never increase.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
This base could only be obtained during Great Winter Hunt 2024 event.

After sending 25 Golems, the Hunter could claim this base from the Reward Track.
