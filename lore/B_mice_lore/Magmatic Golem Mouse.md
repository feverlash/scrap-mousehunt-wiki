# Magmatic Golem Mouse

The Magmatic Golem Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Gnawnian Express Station. It can only be attracted and caught during the Daredevil Canyon phase and only with Magmatic Crystal Charms armed. They are known to drop Fuel Nuggets and Tin Scraps as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Magmatic Golem mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese, but can only be attracted when a hunter has a Magmatic Crystal Charm armed.


## Hunting Strategy

Law type traps such as S.L.A.C. are very effective against the Magmatic Golem Mouse.


## Event Participation


### Spring Egg Hunt

- The Magmatic Golem Mouse drops the Gnawnian Express Egg and the Magmatic Golem Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.
