## Cheese Preference

Peevish Piccoloist Mouse is only attracted to Beanster Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Peevish Piccoloist Mouse can only be attracted after planting a Medium Vine to the Ballroom Floor.

Physical power type is effective against Peevish Piccoloist Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.


## Loot

Peevish Piccoloist Mouse can drop:

- Golden Harp String
- Royal Ruby Bean
