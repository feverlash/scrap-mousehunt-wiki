## History and Trivia

- 10 December 2013: Reinbo was released as part of the Great Winter Snowball Fight event.
- Initially, when a Winter Charm was equipped, a Snowflake mouse could be attracted instead of Reinbo, causing the hunter to miss the Reinbo's large Snow Block drop if caught. This was quickly fixed.
- The Reinbo's name, appearance, and description are references to the character Rambo.
- 13 December 2016: The points/gold for Reinbo were changed.
