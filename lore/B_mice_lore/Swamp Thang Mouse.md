## History and Trivia

- 17 October 2012: The Swamp Thang Mouse was introduced as part of the Halloween 2012 event.
- 26 October 2012: The Swamp Thang Mouse began dropping the Witch's Brew Trap Skin as loot.
- 13 October 2016: A reference to the Haunted Terrortories was replaced with a reference to the Spooky Sandcastle in the description.
- The name and appearance of the Swamp Thang Mouse are a reference to the Swamp Thing comic book character.
- 13 October 2021: Gold was changed for the Master the Magic of the Boiling Cauldrons event.
