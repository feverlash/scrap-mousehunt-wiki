## Cheese Preference

Magical Multitasker Mouse is only attracted to Master Mimolette.


## Hunting Strategy

The Magical Multitasker Mouse can only be attracted while enrolled in Shadow Sciences or during the Shadow phase of the Final Exam.

Shadow power type is very effective against Magical Multitasker Mouse.
Arcane power type is effective against Magical Multitasker Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
