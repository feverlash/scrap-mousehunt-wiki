## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

In the Great Gnarled Tree location, the Goldleaf Mouse will be attracted when hunting with Standard Cheese, with a strong preference for SUPER|brie+, and Gnarled Cheese.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Goldleaf Mouse.

## Locations

Hunters can encounter the Goldleaf Mouse only in the Great Gnarled Tree location.


## Loot

The Goldleaf Mouse drops the Doobers collectible as loot in the Great Gnarled Tree location.

## History and Trivia

- 12 September 2012: The Goldleaf Mouse was introduced to help promote the now defunct Magic Pets game from HitGrab Inc.
- 25 June 2013: The Goldleaf Mouse gained a vulnerability to Law traps with the release of Varmint Valley.
- While Magic Pets was active, catching a Goldleaf Mouse left this additional message in the Hunter's Journal:

> Rumor has it the Goldleaf mouse comes from a far off land called Elunia...
Play HitGrab's newest game Magic Pets now!

- The Goldleaf Mouse's description has been shortened since its release. The original read as follows:

> Goldleaves are known for their striking ears. They change color based on the hue of the leaves they eat each season.Rumor has it this mouse comes from a far off land called Elunia...Play HitGrab's newest game Magic Pets now!
