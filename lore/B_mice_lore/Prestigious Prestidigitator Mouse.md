## Cheese Preference

Prestigious Prestidigitator Mouse is only attracted to Apprentice Ambert.


## Hunting Strategy

The Prestigious Prestidigitator Mouse can only be attracted while enrolled in Shadow Sciences or during the Shadow phase of the Final Exam.

Shadow power type is very effective against Prestigious Prestidigitator Mouse.
Arcane power type is effective against Prestigious Prestidigitator Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
