# Spear Fisher Mouse

The Spear Fisher Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Sunken City. It is known to drop Brined Curd as loot.

## Cheese Preference

The Spear Fisher Mouse has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Spear Fisher Mouse is susceptible only to Hydro weapons. It can be found in the Carnivore Cove and Feeding Grounds zones while on a Dive in the Sunken City.


## Event Participation

- The Spear Fisher Mouse drops the Underwater Predator Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

The Spear Fisher Mouse was released 2 July 2014 as part of the Sunken City content.
