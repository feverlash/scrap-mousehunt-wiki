## Cheese Preference

Shadow Master Sorcerer is attracted to Standard Cheese, Apprentice Ambert, and Master Mimolette.


## Hunting Strategy

The Shadow Master Sorcerer can only be attracted at the end of each course level while enrolled in Shadow Sciences.

Shadow power type is very effective against Shadow Master Sorcerer.
Arcane power type is effective against Shadow Master Sorcerer.
All other types are ineffective.
