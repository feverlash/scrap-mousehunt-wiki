# ⚔️ High Tension Spring

> **Classification**: Physical Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 75 Power | 2 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Novice  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 600

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Razor sharp barbs adorn a crushing bar held with over 1000 lbs. of tension.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
If Hunters choose a different weapon from the King when they first start playing, they can purchase the High Tension Spring for 600 Gold from the Trapsmith in the following locations:

- Town of Gnawnia
- Catacombs
- Forbidden Grove
- Acolyte Realm
- Dracano

It can be refunded for 110 Gold at the same locations.

The High Tension Spring Trap (if unintentionally smashed) can be reassembled from High Tension Spring Parts.
