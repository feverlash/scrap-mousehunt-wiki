# 🛡️ 2022 New Year's Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 222 Power | 10 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Novice  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 202,022 Gold30 Firework Sugar Cookies

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Happy New Year! This party-powered base celebrates 2022 all year long!
> During all of 2022, while this base is equipped, mice have a chance to drop 2022 Charms when caught. The charm-generating powers of this base will cease to work on January 1, 2023.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The 2022 New Year's Base could be obtained from Festive Comet Trapsmith during the Great Winter Hunt 2021 event.
