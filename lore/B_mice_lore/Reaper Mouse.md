## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Reaper Mouse is attracted only to Ancient Cheese.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Reaper Mouse.

## Locations

Hunters can encounter the Reaper Mouse only in the Forbidden Grove location.


## Loot

The Reaper Mouse drops the following loot in the Forbidden Grove location:

- Ancient Relic


## History and Trivia

- 4 March 2009: The Reaper Mouse is released with the introduction of the Forbidden Grove.
- 12 January 2011: The Reaper Mouse was found at Ronza's Traveling Shoppe where it dropped Rockforth cheese as loot. It also began to drop Power Charm as loot in this location, as well as in the Forbidden Grove area.
- 29 August 2012: The Reaper Mouse began dropping Ancient Relic as loot.
