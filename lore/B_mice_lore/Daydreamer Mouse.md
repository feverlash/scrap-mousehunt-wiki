# Daydreamer Mouse

The Daydreamer Mouse is a breed of mouse found on the Floating Islands. It can be encountered on any Low Altitude Island with either Cloud Cheesecake or Standard Cheese, and on any High Altitude Island when using Standard Cheese

## Cheese Preference

The Daydreamer Mouse is attracted to Cloud Cheesecake and Standard Cheese.

## Hunting Strategy

All types except Rift and parental are effective against the Daydreamer Mouse.
