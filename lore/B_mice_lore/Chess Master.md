# Chess Master

The Chess Master is a breed of mouse found in the Zugzwang's Tower. They are known to drop Ancient Relic, Master Bindings and occasionally, Zugzwang's Left Sock, as loot. Hunters are transported to the Seasonal Garden upon catching the Chess Master.

## Preferred Cheese

The Chess Master is only attracted to Checkmate Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Chess Master can be attracted using Checkmate Cheese once the Hunter catches either a Mystic King or a Technic King and stays in the tower (by either using Checkmate Cheese or Stalemate Charm).

Tactical traps are very effective against the Chess Master, while all others are ineffective.

Catching the Chess Master will cause the Hunter to be moved to the Seasonal Garden unless Stalemate Charm is also armed. If the Hunter manages to stay in Zugzwang's Tower after catching the Chess Master (by using Stalemate Charm), they can attempt to catch more mice, including the Chess Master itself.

Catching a Chess Master with a Uncharged Scholar Charm armed will add a Scholar Charm to the Hunter's inventory. This charm can be used to attract Zurreal the Eternal in Crystal Library.


## History and Trivia

- 26 August 2010: The Chess Master was released as part of the Zugzwang's Tower.
- The Chess Master is pictured wearing one sock on the right foot; its mate is Zugzwang's Left Sock dropped as loot.
- 29 August 2012: The Chess Master began dropping Ancient Relic as loot.
