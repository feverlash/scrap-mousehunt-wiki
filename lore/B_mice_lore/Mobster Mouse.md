# Mobster Mouse

The Mobster Mouse is a breed of mouse found in all locations. It drops SUPER|brie+ as loot, known quantities are 25, 50, 75, 100, 250, 500 and 1000 pieces of SUPER|brie+. During a King's Giveaway, they can drop King's Prize Keys as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

As of 18 November 2015, the Mobster Mouse does not consume cheese or charms when attracted.


## Hunting Strategy

All power types are of normal effectiveness against Mobster Mouse with exception of Parental which is ineffective.
The current details regarding hunting strategy can be found here.


## History and Trivia

- 1 April 2008: The Mobster Mouse was released to replace the iTunes Mouse. The first Mobster Mouse to be caught was christened "Fat Tony" via a forum poll.
- 16 August 2009: The developers announced in a video that going forward roughly 100 Mobsters would be released daily, each worth 25 SUPER|brie+.
- 8 June to 20 October 2010: The Mobster Mouse was not available due to the rebuilding of the prize system in the MouseHunt version 3.0.
- During the King's Giveaway 2010, failure to catch a Mobster Mouse resulted in an increase of one Prize Power.
- 18 November 2015: Prize Mice no longer consume cheese or charms when attracted.
