# ⚔️ Polar Vortex Trap

> **Classification**: Tactical Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 8,000 Power | 30 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Baron/Baroness  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 0

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> This severe swirling snowstorm is a large-scale extratropical cyclone that occurs when the raging forces contained within Cyclone Stones are released into an atmospheric zone that has a high concentration of holiday magic. These festive forces produce an inescapable vortex that pull mice into its centre and trap them forever. Once inside, most mice are quite content to be trapped because the eye of this storm is actually a calm and cheerful place where the snow is always ideal for packing and the surrounding rushing winds sound like merry melodies.
> This trap adds +1 to area-specific loot drops and consumes 1 Cyclone Stone per catch when used in the Floating Islands.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Polar Vortex Trap could only be obtained during Great Winter Hunt 2025 event.

After sending 25 Golems, the Hunter could claim this weapon from the Reward Track.
