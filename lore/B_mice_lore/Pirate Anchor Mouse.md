## Cheese Preference

The Pirate Anchor Mouse has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Pirate Anchor Mouse is susceptible only to Hydro weapons. It can be found in the Haunted Shipwreck and Shipwreck zones while on a Dive in the Sunken City.


## Event Participation


### Spring Egg Hunt

- The Pirate Anchor Mouse drops the Barnacle Egg and the Deep, Deep, Deep Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

The Pirate Anchor Mouse was released 2 July 2014 as part of the Sunken City content.
