## Cheese Preference

Jovial Jailor Mouse is only attracted to Beanster Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Jovial Jailor Mouse can only be attracted after planting a Short Vine to the Dungeon Floor.

Physical power type is effective against Jovial Jailor Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.


## Loot

Jovial Jailor Mouse can drop:

- Lavish Lapis Bean
