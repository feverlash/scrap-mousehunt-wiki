## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Stormsurge the Vile Tempest is only attracted to Mild Queso, Medium Queso, Hot Queso or Flamin' Queso.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Stormsurge the Vile Tempest.


## Hunting Strategy

Only Draconic traps are effective against the Stormsurge the Vile Tempest, all other power types are ineffective. The Stormsurge the Vile Tempest can only be encountered in Queso Geyser while a Epic Eruption is triggered.

Dragonbane Charm, Super Dragonbane Charm, Extreme Dragonbane Charm, and Ultimate Dragonbane Charm provide a power bonus against the Stormsurge the Vile Tempest.


## History and Trivia

- 30 April 2019: The Stormsurge the Vile Tempest was introduced, with the release of the Queso Geyser area.
