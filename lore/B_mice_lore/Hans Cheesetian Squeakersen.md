## Cheese Preference

Hans Cheesetian Squeakersen is only attracted to Standard Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Hans Cheesetian Squeakersen is only available when the stage is Not writing.

Forgotten power type is effective against Hans Cheesetian Squeakersen.
All other types are ineffective.


## Loot

Hans Cheesetian Squeakersen can drop:

- Draft Derby Curd

- Cleverness Clam

- Inspiration Ink
