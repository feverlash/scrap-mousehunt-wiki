# Swarm of Pygmy Mice

The Swarm of Pygmy Mice are a breed of mice found in the Jungle of Dread. They are known to drop Vanilla Beans as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Swarm of Pygmy Mice have been found to be especially attracted to Gouda, SUPER|brie+, and Vanilla Stilton cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Shadow traps are very effective against the Swarm of Pygmy Mice. All other power types are ineffective.


## History and Trivia

- 8 June 2010: The Swarm of Pygmy Mice were originally named the Pigmy Swarm Mouse. The name was changed to Swarm of Pigmy Mice with the release of MouseHunt Version 3.0.
- 17 August 2010: The Swarm of Pygmy Mice acquired its current spelling.
- 14 April 2011: The Swarm of Pygmy Mice began dropping a bonus Pygmy Swarm Egg as loot as part of the Spring Egg Hunt 2011 event.
