# Summer Mage Mouse

The Summer Mage Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Rodentia region. It can be attracted and caught only during the Summer Season. It is known to drop Summer Key Shard, and occasionally an Ancient Relic, as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Summer Mage Mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Tactical weapons such as Zugzwang's First Move should be used in order to catch this mouse.


## History and Trivia

- Summer Mage Mice were introduced on 17 August 2010 with the release of the Seasonal Garden location.
- They began dropping Ancient Relic as loot on 29 August 2012.
