## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Sitting at a full -100 degrees Celsius, this base is dangerous to handle and requires protective gear even to arm cheese safely. Given the extreme cold, it can destabilize most traps, severely impairing their effectiveness. However, certain traps in a certain area may in fact benefit from the intense cold it emits, even perhaps using it as a stabilizing agent at a critical point.
