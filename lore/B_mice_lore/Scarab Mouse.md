# Scarab Mouse

> "Scarab" redirects here. For other uses, see Scarab (disambiguation).

The Scarab Mouse is a breed of mouse that can be found in the Sand Crypts, and is known to drop Ber Essences, Cynd Essences and Lunaria Petals as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Scarab mice have been found to be only attracted to Graveblossom Camembert.


## Hunting Strategy

The Scarab mouse is a member of the Sand Dunes mouse group. As such all power types except Shadow weapons are ineffective against it.


## History and Trivia

- 14 November 2012: The Scarab Mouse was released as a part of the Living Garden update.
