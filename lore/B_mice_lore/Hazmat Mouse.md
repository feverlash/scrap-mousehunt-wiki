# Hazmat Mouse

The Hazmat Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Toxic Spill, for hunters of rank Hero and above. It is known to drop a variety of loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Hazmat Mouse is attracted to Magical Rancid Radioactive Blue and Rancid Radioactive Blue at every spill level. Additionally, these mice are only encountered when Super Rotten Charms are in use.


## Hunting Strategy

Hydro type traps such as Oasis Water Node Trap or Phantasmic Oasis Trap are effective against the Hazmat Mouse.


## History and Trivia

- 21 January 2014: The Hazmat Mouse was released as part of the Toxic Spill location.
- 15 April 2014: The Hazmat Mouse began dropping the Clean Egg and Toxic Spill Egg as part of the Spring Egg Hunt 2014 event.
