# 🛡️ Chrome Essential Living Garden Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 1250 Power | 25 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Archduke  
> **Acquisition Cost**: #Obtained Via

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Over the years, many important bases were designed to aid hunters who were looking to tame and cultivate the Living and Twisted Gardens, break the curses of the Lost and Cursed Cities, and survive the harsh climates of the Sand Dunes and Crypts.
> One contained the power of an aurora at any time of the year, at any time of the day, and localized entirely within the base. Another contained a lovely flowerbed of poinsettias kept fresh inside a fully sealed and functional hothouse. Another was made from a highly unstable metallic alloy that produced a bizarre chemical reaction when exposed to massive amounts of sodium chloride. And another harnessed mystical energies that warded against heinous curses.
> Each of these miraculous bases had their own unique effects and unfortunately, the designs for each of them have long been lost to the Trapsmiths of Gnawnia... until now! It turns out that the villainous Captain Crook discovered and then stole these lost blueprints during her raid of the Living Garden so that she could make her own pirated versions. However, she got the blueprints for each all mixed up and couldn't tell them apart so she combined them into something new that has the effects of all four!
> Now that the design secrets have been recovered, the Trapsmith at the Cerulean Skyport is able to forge this new, stronger, and shinier version using the miraculous combination of Cerulean Chrome that has been charged with the energy from Adorned Empyrean Jewels!
> While hunting in the Living/Twisted Garden, Lost/Cursed City, or Sand Dunes/Crypts, this base has the following effects:
> • Chance to generate a random essence
> • Increases petal and herb drops
> • Adds +1 to the salt level in the Sand Crypts upon catching a mouse with any salt charm
> • Automatically attracts the King Grub Mouse in the Sand Crypts when the salt level reaches 50
> • 50% chance to break any curse in the Lost/Cursed City upon catching a mouse

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Chrome Essential Living Garden Base can be purchased from the Trapsmith in Cerulean Skyport for:

- 5,000,000 Gold
- 20 Chrome Essential Living Garden Blueprint Scrap
- 200 Cerulean Chrome Ingot
- 3 Adorned Empyrean Jewel

It requires the Hunter to own the Chrome Base and complete the Liberate the Cerulean Skyport from Captain Crook adventure to purchase.

It cannot be refunded.
