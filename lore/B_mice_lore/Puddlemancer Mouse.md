# Puddlemancer Mouse

The Puddlemancer Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Rodentia region. It can be attracted and caught only during the Spring Season.

## Preferred Cheese

Puddlemancer Mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Strong Physical weapons are recommended in order to catch this mouse. Alternatively, hunters may equip their best Tactical trap.


## Event Participation

- The Puddlemancer Mouse drops the Seasonal Garden Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

- Puddlemancer Mice were introduced on 17 August 2010 with the release of the Seasonal Garden location.
- Puddlemancer Mice began to drop Sandtail Call to Arms on 22 March 2011 with the release of the Fiery Warpath location.
- During the Spring Egg Hunt 2011, Puddlemancer Mice began dropping a bonus Seasonal Garden Egg as loot for hunters who had obtained the Rocket Rabbit Mallet. They dropped the same loot the following year during the Spring Egg Hunt 2012 event for hunters who had obtained the Eggscavator.
- On 29 July 2015 they stopped dropping the Sandtail Call to Arms as loot with the introduction of the Adventure Book.
