## Preferred Cheese

The Nightfire Mouse have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Arcane traps are very effective against the Nightfire Mouse. Shadow traps are of normal effectiveness.
The Nightfire Mouse can only be encountered during the Pitch, Utter Darkness, and First Light phases at Nighttime in Fort Rox.
Once a hunter has upgraded the Fort Rox fort to the Energy Cannon, it is no longer possible to miss a Nightfire Mouse.
