# Grubling Herder Mouse

The Grubling Herder Mouse is a breed of mouse that can be found in the Sand Dunes, and is known to drop Aleth Essences, Ber Essences and Duskshade Petals.

## Preferred Cheese

Grubling Herder mice have been found to be only attracted to Dewthief Camembert.


## Hunting Strategy

The Grubling Herder mouse is a member of the Sand Dunes mouse group. As such all power types except Shadow weapons are ineffective against it.


## History and Trivia

- Grubling Herder mice were released on 14 November 2012, as part of the Living Garden update.
- The phrases "Muddy'Deeb" and "The Grublings must flow!" in the description are a reference to the SF novel Dune by Frank Herbert.
