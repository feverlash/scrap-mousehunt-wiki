## Cheese Preference

Scheming Squire Mouse is only attracted to Standard Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Scheming Squire Mouse can only be attracted after planting a Tall Vine to the Great Hall Floor.

Physical power type is effective against Scheming Squire Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.


## Loot

Scheming Squire Mouse can drop:

- Golden Goose Egg
