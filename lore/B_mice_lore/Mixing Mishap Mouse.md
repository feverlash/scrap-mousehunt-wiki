## Cheese Preference

Mixing Mishap Mouse is only attracted to Standard Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Mixing Mishap Mouse can only be attracted while enrolled in Shadow Sciences or during the Shadow phase of the Final Exam.

Shadow power type is very effective against Mixing Mishap Mouse.
Arcane power type is effective against Mixing Mishap Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
