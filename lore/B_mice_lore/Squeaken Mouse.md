# Squeaken Mouse

The Squeaken Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Rodentia region. They are known to drop Simple Orbs as loot. They are also known to drop Mesh Netting as loot, but only while hunters are in the process of completing the Zurreal Trap Research Assignment.
