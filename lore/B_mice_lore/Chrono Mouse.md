## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Chrono Mouse is attracted only to Runic Cheese.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Chrono Mouse.


## Power Type Weaknesses


| VeryEffective | Effective | Less Effective |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Locations

Hunters can encounter the Chrono Mouse only in the Acolyte Realm location.


## Loot

The Chrono Mouse drops the following loot in the Acolyte Realm location:

- Ancient Potion
- Ancient Relic
- Rune
- Runic Potion
- Satchel of Gold (50,000)


## Event Participation


### Spring Egg Hunt

- The Chrono Mouse drops the Chrono Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.

## History and Trivia

- 12 July 2012: The Chrono Mouse was painted live and is based on a design which won a Grand Prize in an "Official Design a Mouse Art Contest" that was held via the Discussion Boards.
- 20 July 2012: The Chrono Mouse was was released for the first time.
- 29 August 2012: The Chrono Mouse began dropping Ancient Relic as loot.
