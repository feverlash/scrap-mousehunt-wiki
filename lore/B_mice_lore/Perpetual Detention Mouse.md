## Cheese Preference

Perpetual Detention Mouse is only attracted to Standard Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Perpetual Detention Mouse can only be attracted while enrolled in Arcane Arts or during the Arcane phase of the Final Exam.

Arcane power type is very effective against Perpetual Detention Mouse.
Shadow power type is effective against Perpetual Detention Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
