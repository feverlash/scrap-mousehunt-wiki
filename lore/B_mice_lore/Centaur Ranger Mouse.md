## Preferred Cheese

Centaur Ranger mice have been found to be especially attracted to String Cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.


## Hunting Strategy

The Centaur Ranger Mouse is weak to Rift Traps. It can only be attracted by String Cheese.

The Centaur Ranger Mouse is only available when rage in the Gigantic Gnarled Tree section in Whisker Woods Rift is at 50.

Catching a Centaur Ranger Mouse will reset the rage levels of all three factions in the Whisker Woods Rift unless a Taunting Charm is armed.


## Event Participation


### Spring Egg Hunt

- The Centaur Ranger Mouse drops the Enlarged Rift Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

- Centaur Ranger mice were introduced on 28 January 2015, with the introduction of the Whisker Woods Rift.
- Centaur Ranger mice are the Rift version of Centaur mice.
