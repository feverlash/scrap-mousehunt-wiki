# ⚔️ Crystal Tower

> **Classification**: Rift Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 900 Power | 0 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Count/Countess  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 4,900,000 Gold1 Raw Rift Crystal

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> By utilizing raw Rift crystals and advanced technology, intrepid hunters have attempted to create a trap capable of harnessing the indomitable powers of the Rift. Being such a foreign power, the trap is barely able to hold itself together, but thankfully it retains enough control to not immediately destroy itself and instead allows the hunter to focus its power to retain a hunting edge within this dangerous alien landscape.When smashed with the Hunter's Hammer, you'll be able to recover a delicate but powerful Shattered Rift Crystal, which you can use to build one of two new amazing Rift Power traps!

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Crystal Tower can be purchased from the Trapsmith in Gnawnia Rift for:

- 4,900,000 Gold
- 1 Raw Rift Crystal

It cannot be refunded.

The Crystal Tower (if unintentionally smashed) can be reassembled from a Shattered Rift Crystal.
