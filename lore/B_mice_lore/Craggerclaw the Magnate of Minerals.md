## Cheese Preference

Craggerclaw the Magnate of Minerals is only attracted to Aurora Bocconcini.


## Hunting Strategy

The Craggerclaw the Magnate of Minerals can be only attracted during a Mining shipment.

Law power type is very effective against Craggerclaw the Magnate of Minerals.
All other types are ineffective.
