# 🛡️ Refined Pollutinum Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 500 Power | 10 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Hero  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 2,200,000 Gold450 Pollutinum1 Polluted Base

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> A significant allotment of Refined Pollutinum has been forged, folded and heat treated for maximum strength and durability. The refining process has given it a natural ability to absorb and nullify toxins and radiation.
> The Refined Pollutinum Base will react with Polluted Charms, increasing its core radiation and using that energy to add extra Luck!

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Refined Pollutinum Base can be purchased from the Trapsmith in Toxic Spill for:

- 2,200,000 Gold
- 450 Pollutinum
- 1 Polluted Base

It cannot be refunded.
