# ⚔️ S.L.A.C.

> **Classification**: Law Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 650 Power | 0 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Lord/Lady  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 150,000 Gold

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> The Spring-Loaded Attack Cactus (S.L.A.C.) is an attempt to harness the lawless wilds of the Old West itself in order to tame the Wild Bunch mice.
> As the mice nibble away at the piece of cheese, they feel at ease in their natural environment. Most mice in Varmint Valley enjoy eating their meals by the shade of a cactus plant, and are mostly unaware that the S.L.A.C. is more than just a cactus.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The S.L.A.C. can be purchased from the Trapsmith in Claw Shot City and Gnawnian Express Station for 150,000 Gold.

It can be refunded for 75,000 Gold at the same locations.
