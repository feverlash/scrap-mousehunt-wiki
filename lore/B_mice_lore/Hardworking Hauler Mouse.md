# Hardworking Hauler Mouse

The Hardworking Hauler is a breed of mouse found in Fort Rox. They are known to drop Meteorite Pieces as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Hardworking Hauler Mouse have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Law type traps are very effective against the Hardworking Hauler Mouse. Arcane and Shadow power types are less effective.
The Hardworking Hauler Mouse can only be encountered during the daytime phase in Fort Rox.


## History and Trivia

- 3 November 2016: The Hardworking Hauler Mouse was release as a part of Fort Rox update.
