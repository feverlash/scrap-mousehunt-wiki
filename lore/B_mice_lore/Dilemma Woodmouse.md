## Cheese Preference

Dilemma Woodmouse is only attracted to Deus Ex Maconnais.


## Hunting Strategy

The Dilemma Woodmouse can only be attracted while writing a story.

Only Law power types are effective against Dilemma Woodmouse.
All other types are ineffective.
