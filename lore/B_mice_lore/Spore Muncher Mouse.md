The Spore Muncher Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Fungal Cavern.

| Spore Muncher mice have a poor sense of sight that is offset by the glowing spores that grow along their backs. This symbiotic relationship allows these mice to find other spores. This is also partly self-preservation, as they tend to ignore any blue-hued fungus, thinking it could just be another Spore Muncher. |  |  |  |

## Cheese Preference

The Spore Muncher Mouse can be attracted using any Standard Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Spore Muncher Mouse has Hydro as its primary weakness and Forgotten as its secondary weakness.
