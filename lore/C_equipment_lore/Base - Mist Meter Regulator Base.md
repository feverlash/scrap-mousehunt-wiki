# 🛡️ Mist Meter Regulator Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 300 Power | 6 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Count/Countess  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 3,945,000 Gold

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> An industrial mist quality regulator made in the laboratories found within the Burroughs. Powered by mist canisters, it can regulate the environment, keeping things locked in a healthy green range.
> While in the green zone, the Mist Meter Regulator Base will consume two Mist Canisters per hunt and the Mist Meter will remain steady at the current mist level.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Mist Meter Regulator Base could be purchased for 3,945,000 Gold at Ronza's Traveling Shoppe during her 2022 visit.

It cannot be refunded.
