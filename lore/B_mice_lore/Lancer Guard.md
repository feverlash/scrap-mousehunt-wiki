The Lancer Guard is a breed of mouse found on the Floating Islands, on a Draconic Island or a Draconic Sanctuary.

## Cheese Preference

The Lancer Guard is attracted to Cloud Cheesecake and Standard Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Only Draconic traps are effective against the Lancer Guard, all other power types are ineffective. The Lancer Guard can only be encountered on a Draconic Island or a Draconic Sanctuary.

Dragonbane Charms, Super Dragonbane Charms and Extreme Dragonbane Charms provide a power bonus against the Lancer Guard.
