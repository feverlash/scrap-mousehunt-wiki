## Cheese Preference

The Cavern Crumbler Mouse can only be attracted by using Glowing Gruyere Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Cavern Crumbler Mouse has Forgotten as its primary weakness and Hydro as its secondary weakness.

Gemology Base removes this mouse from the attraction pool.
