## Cheese Preference

Shifty Shrimp Mouse is only attracted to Allegory Anari.


## Hunting Strategy

The Shifty Shrimp Mouse can only be attracted while on the rapids before the waterfall in Epilogue Falls.

Hydro power type is effective against Shifty Shrimp Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
