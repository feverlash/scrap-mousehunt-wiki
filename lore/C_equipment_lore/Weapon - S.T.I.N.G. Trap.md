# ⚔️ S.T.I.N.G. Trap

> **Classification**: Law Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 3,500 Power | 10 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Archduke/Archduchess  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 1,500,000 Gold1 Sky Sprocket1 Skysoft Silk1 Enchanted Wing1 Cloudstone Bangle

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> The Surprise Transport Incarceration Nuisance Grabber trap is baited with costume jewelry, fake gold, and worthless glass reproductions of valuable gems. It uses these cheap but convincing imitations to lure unsuspecting criminals into the incarceration module. Once the mouse is inside, the iron door snaps shut and the trap promptly shuttles the offender off to prison.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The S.T.I.N.G. Trap can be purchased from the Trapsmith in Floating Islands for:

- 1,500,000 Gold
- 1 Sky Sprocket
- 1 Skysoft Silk
- 1 Enchanted Wing
- 1 Cloudstone Bangle

It cannot be refunded.
