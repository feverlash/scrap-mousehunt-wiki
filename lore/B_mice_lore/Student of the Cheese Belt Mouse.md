## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

Maki Cheese has an attraction rate for the Student of the Cheese Belt Mouse of about twice that of SUPER|brie+ and four times that of Standard Cheeses.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Student of the Cheese Belt Mouse.

## Locations

Hunters can encounter the Student of the Cheese Belt Mouse only in the Dojo location.


## Loot

The Student of the Cheese Belt Mouse drops the following loot in the Dojo location:

- Plankrun's Dojo Notes
- Satchel of Gold (750 gold)
- Token of the Cheese Belt


## History and Trivia

- 26 November 2008: The Student of the Cheese Belt Mouse is released.
- 27 November 2008: The Student of the Cheese Belt Mouse begins dropping the Token of the Cheese Belt as loot.
- 16 April 2011: The Student of the Cheese Belt Mouse began dropping the Antique Blue Argyle Egg as loot as part of the Spring Egg Hunt 2011 event.
- 14 April 2011: The Student of the Cheese Belt Mouse began dropping a bonus Dojo Student Egg as loot during the Spring Egg Hunt 2011 event.
- The description for the Student of the Cheese Belt Mouse has changed since its release. The original description read as follows:

> Students of the Cheese Belt may look peaceful and docile but don't let that catch you off guard. They are fueled by their hunger which is limitless, a trap will hardly be able to get between this mouse and your cheese! The masters of these students apparently have learned to control their cravings but tend to have a rough time refining the young students to their ways.
