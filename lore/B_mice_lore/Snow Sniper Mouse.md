# Snow Sniper Mouse

The Snow Sniper Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Slushy Shoreline. They are known to drop War Scrap as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Snow Sniper Mouse has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Snow Sniper Mouse can only be encountered in the Slushy Shoreline when a hunter has Softserve Charms armed.


## History and Trivia

- 14 May 2021: The Snow Sniper Mouse was released in Slushy Shoreline.
- Between 14 May 2012 and 24 May 2012: The Snow Sniper Mouse dropped Interlocking Hull Fragments.
