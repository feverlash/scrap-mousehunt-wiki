# Crabolia Mouse

The Crabolia Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Sunken City. It is known to drop Barnacles and Sand Dollars as loot.

## Cheese Preference

The Crabolia Mouse has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Crabolia Mouse is susceptible only to Hydro weapons. It can be found in the Rocky Outcrop and Shipwreck zones while on a Dive in the Sunken City.


## Event Participation


### Spring Egg Hunt

- The Crabolia Mouse drops the Barnacle Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

The Crabolia Mouse was released 2 July 2014 as part of the Sunken City content.
