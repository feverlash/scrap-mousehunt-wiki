# ⚔️ Derelict Airship Trap

> **Classification**: Rift Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 2,000 Power | 3 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Count/Countess  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 6,500,000 Gold

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> The result of a failed prototype from the first and only time Ronza tried to create an airship that could withstand the harsh elements of the Rift. Luckily she was able to salvage some of the design and create this formidable trap instead.
> This trap has a chance to create a Rift Tarnished Charm on a successful catch.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Derelict Airship Trap is available for purchase for 6,500,000 Gold from Ronza's Shoppe.

It cannot be refunded.
