# 🛡️ Chrome Queso Cannonstorm Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 1250 Power | 25 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Archduke  
> **Acquisition Cost**: #Obtained Via

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> This base was originally a repurposed Hailstone Cannon that had been permanently set in a bed of hardened amber queso making it portable and allowing it to be used outside of the Great Winter Taiga. When used in the Queso Canyon region, this augmented cannon fired Solidified Amber Queso at any mouse that approached its trap and each successful hit exploded into bonus Queso Canyon loot!
> However, the original design blueprints that contained the secrets to this base's extraordinary effects was lost to the Trapsmiths of Gnawnia... until now! It turns out that the villainous Captain Crook discovered and then stole these lost blueprints during her raid of the Queso Geyser so that she could make her own pirated version. Now that the design secrets have been recovered, the Trapsmith at the Cerulean Skyport is able to forge this new, stronger, and shinier version using the miraculous combination of Cerulean Chrome that has been charged with the energy from Adorned Empyrean Jewels!
> While armed within the Queso Canyon Region:
> • This base will destroy 5 Solidified Amber Queso on every hunt
> • Bonus Queso Canyon loot will be found in the rubble after catching a mouse

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Chrome Queso Cannonstorm Base can be purchased from the Trapsmith in Cerulean Skyport for:

- 5,000,000 Gold
- 20 Chrome Queso Cannonstorm Blueprint Scrap
- 200 Cerulean Chrome Ingot
- 3 Adorned Empyrean Jewel

It requires the Hunter to own the Chrome Base and complete the Liberate the Cerulean Skyport from Captain Crook adventure to purchase.

It cannot be refunded.
