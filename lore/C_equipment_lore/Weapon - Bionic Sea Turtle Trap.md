# ⚔️ Bionic Sea Turtle Trap

> **Classification**: Hydro Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 15,000 Power | 36 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Viceroy  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 24,000,000 Gold800 Pearl of Wisdom

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> This serene, sophisticated, sea turtle is the latest advancement in aquatic automaton technology. It swims through the seas with unprecedented speed and grace and is able to effortlessly outmaneuver even the most agile of marine mice. Due to the Pearls of Wisdom used in its construction, it is not only swift and powerful but also extremely intelligent and programmed with an unlimited supply of clever idioms, poignant metaphors, and even helpful life advice. Some mice who encounter this miraculous contraption give themselves up willingly to it because they're so mystified by its insightful guidance to do so.
