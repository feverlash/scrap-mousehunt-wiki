# Essence Collector Mouse

The Essence Collector Mouse is a breed of mouse that can be found in the Lost City, and are known to drop Ber Essences, Cynd Essences and Dreamfluff Herbs as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Essence Collector mice have been found to be only attracted to Dewthief Camembert.


## Hunting Strategy

Essence Collector is a member of the Lost City mouse group. As such all power types except Arcane weapons are ineffective against it.

It can only be attracted during the Lost Curse Minigame.


## History and Trivia

- 14 November 2012: The Essence Collector Mouse was released as a part of the Living Garden update.
