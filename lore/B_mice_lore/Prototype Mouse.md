# Prototype Mouse

The Prototype is a breed of mouse found in the Burroughs Rift. It is known to drop Terre Ricotta Potions as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Prototype Mouse can be attracted using any Standard String Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Prototype Mouse can be attracted when the Mist Meter is in Yellow and Green Zone.

Rift power type is effective against Prototype Mouse while all others are less effective.


## History and Trivia

- 9 September 2014: The Prototype Mouse was released as part of the Burroughs Rift location.
