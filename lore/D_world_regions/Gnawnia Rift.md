# 📍 Gnawnia Rift

> **Wilayah / Region**: Rift Plane  
> **Pangkat Minimal**: Count/Countesss  
> **Fasilitas Lokal**: Cheese ShoppeTrapsmithCharm ShoppeGeneral StoreCartographer

## 🌍 Gambaran & Atmosfer Lingkungan

Gnawnia Rift is a hunting location in Rift Plane region.

It requires the minimum rank of Count/Countess and the Rift Detector to access.

This is the first area to make use of Rift power type in the game.

The Hunter need to use String Cheese to attract mice that carry Riftiago Potion. Riftiago Cheese brew from that Potion can attract mice that drop materials to craft Resonator Cheese, which is required to attract the Goliath Field Mouse. The Raw Rift Crystal dropped by that mouse can be used to buy a Rift weapon from the Trapsmith.

A Rift trap is far more effective against mice in Gnawnia Rift as well as later areas in Rift Plane compared to other power type.
