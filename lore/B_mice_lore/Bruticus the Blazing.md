## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Bruticus the Blazing is only attracted to Flamin' Queso.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Bruticus the Blazing.


## Hunting Strategy

Only Draconic traps are effective against the Bruticus the Blazing, all other power types are ineffective. The Bruticus the Blazing can only be encountered in Queso Geyser while either a Tiny Eruption, Small Eruption, Medium Eruption, Large Eruption or a Epic Eruption is triggered.

Dragonbane Charm, Super Dragonbane Charm, Extreme Dragonbane Charm, and Ultimate Dragonbane Charm provide a power bonus against the Bruticus the Blazing.


## History and Trivia

- 30 April 2019: The Bruticus the Blazing was introduced, with the release of the Queso Geyser area.
