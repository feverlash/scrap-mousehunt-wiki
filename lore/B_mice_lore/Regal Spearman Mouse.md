## Cheese Preference

The Regal Spearman Mouse is only attracted to Cloud Cheesecake.


## Hunting Strategy

Only Draconic traps are effective against the Regal Spearman Mouse, all other power types are ineffective. The Regal Spearman Mouse can only be encountered on a Draconic Sanctuary.

Dragonbane Charms, Super Dragonbane Charms and Extreme Dragonbane Charms provide a power bonus against the Regal Spearman Mouse.
