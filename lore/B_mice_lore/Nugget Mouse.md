# Nugget Mouse

The Nugget Mouse is an extremely rare Mouse, the Digby Drillbot Parts are an extremely rare loot drop, getting the Digby Drillbot Parts from a Nugget Mouse should not be considered a viable way of getting them.

## Cheese and Charm Preference


### Cheese

The Nugget Mouse is attracted only to Limelight Cheese.


### Charm

No Charms are required to attract the Nugget Mouse.

Arming the Mining Charm provides a power boost against Digby Dirt Dwellers.

## Locations

Hunters can encounter the Nugget Mouse only in the Town of Digby.


## Loot

The Nugget Mouse drops the following loot:

- Ancient Relic
- Digby DrillBot Parts (Extremely rare)
- Living Shard
- Onyx Stone
- Satchel of Gold (500)
- Satchel of Gold (1,000)
- Scrap Metal


## History and Trivia

- 24 March 2010: The Nugget Mouse is released for the first time.
- 29 August 2012: The Nugget Mouse began dropping Ancient Relics as loot.
