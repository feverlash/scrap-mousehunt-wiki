# Mammoth Mouse

The Mammoth Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Brutal Bulwark zone of the Iceberg. It is known to drop Hunting Research, Satchel of Gold, Sticky Charms, and Wax Charms as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Mammoth Mouse has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

When a Mammoth Mouse is encountered in the Iceberg, the hunter will lose progress regardless of whether the mouse is caught or missed, unless the Iceberg Boiler Base, Spiked Base, or Ultimate Iceberg Base is armed.


## History and Trivia

- 24 May 2012: The Mammoth Mouse was released as a part of the Iceberg.
- 1 June 2012: The Mammoth Mouse began to drop loot.
