# Harvester Mouse

> "Harvester" redirects here. For other uses, see Harvester (disambiguation).

The Harvester Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Rodentia region. It can be attracted and caught only during the Fall Season. It is known to drop Fall Key Shard.

## Preferred Cheese

Harvester Mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Strong Shadow weapons such as Clockapult of Time are recommended in order to catch this mouse. Alternatively, hunters may equip their best Tactical trap.


## History and Trivia

- Harvester Mice were introduced on 17 August 2010 with the release of the Seasonal Garden location.
