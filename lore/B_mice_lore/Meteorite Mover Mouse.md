# Meteorite Mover Mouse

The Meteorite Mover is a breed of mouse found in Fort Rox. They are known to drop Meteorite Pieces as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Meteorite Mover Mouse mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Law type traps are very effective against the Meteorite Mover Mouse. Arcane and Shadow power types are less effective.
The Meteorite Mover Mouse can only be encountered during the daytime phase in Fort Rox.


## History and Trivia

- 3 November 2016: The Meteorite Mover Mouse was release as a part of Fort Rox update.
