## Cheese Preference

Angry Aphid Mouse can be attracted with any Standard Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Angry Aphid Mouse is available when at least 1 plant is growing.

Tactical power type is effective against Angry Aphid Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.


## Loot

Angry Aphid Mouse can drop:

- Condensed Creativity
- Crop Coin
