# Grand Master of the Dojo Mouse

The Grand Master of the Dojo Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Furoma Rift. It is the Rift counterpart of Master of the Dojo Mouse.

## Preferred Cheese

The Grand Master of the Dojo Mouse has been found to be especially attracted to Rift Rumble cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.


## Hunting Strategy

Only Rift type traps are very effective against Grand Master of the Dojo Mouse, all other power types are ineffective.


## Event Participation


### Spring Egg Hunt

- The Grand Master of the Dojo Mouse drops the Grand Master of the Dojo Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.
