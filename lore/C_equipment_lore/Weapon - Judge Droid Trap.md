# ⚔️ Judge Droid Trap

> **Classification**: Law Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 2,400 Power | 6 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Count/Countess  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 470,000 Gold

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> With the scales of justice forever tipped in their favour, this self-appointed court of droids enforces their own laws within the courtroom. Among them you will find: falsified evidence, fake witnesses, a biased judge, and a stenographer that only writes down incriminating words and ignores the defense. This trap finds all mice guilty until proven guiltier. Once judged, the bailiff brings all offenders to the King's Stockade to rot.
> The mice are real, the cases are real, the rulings are final. This is Judge Droid.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Judge Droid Trap can be purchased for 470,000 Gold from the Trapsmith in:

- Queso River
- Prickly Plains
- Cantera Quarry

It can be refunded for 84,600 Gold at the same locations.
