# Artillery Commander Mouse

The Artillery Commander Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Fiery Warpath. They are known to drop the Artillery Strike Launch Box as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Artillery Commander Mouse has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Artillery Commander Mouse can be encountered and attracted in the Artillery Commander's Tent, but is immune to capture until five Theurgy Warden Mice have been caught. Using an Ultimate Charm will override this immunity.

Arcane weapons are the most effective for capturing the Artillery Commander Mouse but Physical, Tactical or Hydro weapons can also be used to catch it.

The Artillery Commander Mouse mice can only be encountered by entering the Artillery Commander's Tent using a Warpath Portal Projector during Wave 4 in the Fiery Warpath.

Upon catching the Artillery Commander Mouse, the following message is displayed in the Hunter's Journal:
As I captured Artillery Commander Mouse, a strange metallic suitcase clunked onto the ground. Flipping it open, I was faced with an odd electronic interface which is used to call down an artillery strike against waves of mice in the Fiery Warpath!
