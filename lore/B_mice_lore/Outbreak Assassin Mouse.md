# Outbreak Assassin Mouse

The Outbreak Assassin Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Toxic Spill, for hunters of rank Duke/Duchess and above.

## Preferred Cheese

Outbreak Assassin mice are attracted to Magical Rancid Radioactive Blue at every spill level and to Rancid Radioactive Blue when spill level is for Duke/Duchess and above.


## Hunting Strategy

Hydro type traps such as Oasis Water Node Trap or Phantasmic Oasis Trap are effective against the Outbreak Assassin Mouse.


## Event Participation

- The Outbreak Assassin Mouse drops the Toxic Spill Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

- Released on 21 January 2014 as part of the Toxic Spill content.
