## Preferred Cheese

The Charming Chimer Mouse has been found to be especially attracted to Windy Cheese cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.

## Hunting Strategy

Only Shadow, Arcane and Draconic type traps are effective against the Charming Chimer Mouse, all other power types are ineffective.

The Charming Chimer Mouse is only available between 0%-34% wind intensities in Moussu Picchu.
