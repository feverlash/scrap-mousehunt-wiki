# Sludge Mouse

> "Sludge" redirects here. For other uses, see Sludge (disambiguation).

The Sludge Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Toxic Spill with spill level for hunters of rank Hero to Duke/Duchess.

## Preferred Cheese

Sludge mice are attracted to Magical Rancid Radioactive Blue and Rancid Radioactive Blue when the spill level at the Toxic Spill is for Hero to Duke/Duchess.


## Hunting Strategy

Hydro type traps such as Oasis Water Node Trap or Phantasmic Oasis Trap are effective against the Sludge Mouse.
