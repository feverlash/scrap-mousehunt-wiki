## Cheese Preference

The Crystal Observer Mouse can only be attracted by using Gemstone Cheese, and to a lesser extent, Mineral Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Crystal Observer Mouse can only be caught using Forgotten power type.


## History and Trivia

- 12 May 2015: The Crystal Observer Mouse was released as part of the Fungal Cavern location.
