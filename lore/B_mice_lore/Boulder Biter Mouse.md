# Boulder Biter Mouse

The Boulder Biter Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Burroughs Rift. It is known to drop Mist Canisters and Polluted Parmesan Potion as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Boulder Biter Mouse is only attracted to Terre Ricotta Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Boulder Biter Mouse can only be attracted when the Mist Meter is in Green Zone.

Rift power type is effective against Boulder Biter Mouse while all others are less effective.


## History and Trivia

- 9 September 2014: The Boulder Biter Mouse was released as part of the Burroughs Rift location.
