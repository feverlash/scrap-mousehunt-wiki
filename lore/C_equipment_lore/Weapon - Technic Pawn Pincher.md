# ⚔️ Technic Pawn Pincher

> **Classification**: Tactical Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 60 Power | 0 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Lord/Lady  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 300,000 gold

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> With all of the gizmos, gadgets and mechanical gewgaws placed on the field by the Technic Team, one more coal engine isn't going to arouse much notice. In fact, this trap fits in so seamlessly with the Technic defensive line that Technic Pawns try to check it for repairs on a regular basis. When they do, the claw picks them up, and either flings them away, or drops them in the engine for three free hours of running time. Efficiency at its best!

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Technic Pawn Pincher can be purchased from Trapsmith in Seasonal Garden and Zugzwang's Tower for 300,000 gold.

It can be refunded for 54,000 gold at the same locations.
