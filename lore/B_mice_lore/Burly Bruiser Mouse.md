## Cheese Preference

The Burly Bruiser Mouse is only attracted to Medium Queso, Hot Queso or Flamin' Queso.


## Hunting Strategy

Only Draconic traps are effective against the Burly Bruiser Mouse, all other power types are ineffective. The Burly Bruiser Mouse can only be encountered in Queso Geyser while gathering Cork Bark.

Dragonbane Charm, Super Dragonbane Charm, Extreme Dragonbane Charm, and Ultimate Dragonbane Charm provide a power bonus against the Burly Bruiser Mouse.


## History and Trivia
