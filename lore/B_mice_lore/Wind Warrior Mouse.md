## Preferred Cheese

The Wind Warrior Mouse has been found to be especially attracted to Windy Cheese cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.

## Hunting Strategy

Only Shadow, Arcane and Draconic type traps are effective against the Wind Warrior Mouse, all other power types are ineffective.

The Wind Warrior Mouse is only available between 80%-100% wind intensities in Moussu Picchu. It is the only mouse available at 100% Wind intensity.
