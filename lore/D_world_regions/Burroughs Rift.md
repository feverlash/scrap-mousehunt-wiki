## 🌍 Gambaran & Atmosfer Lingkungan

Burroughs Rift is a hunting location in Rift Plane region.

It requires the minimum rank of Duke/Duchess and the Rift Detector to access.

Mouse attraction pool is affected by Mist level and cheese used.

The main goal of this location is to catch Menace of the Rift to obtain a Null Gauntlet.
