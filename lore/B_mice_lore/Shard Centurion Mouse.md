# Shard Centurion Mouse

The Shard Centurion Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Gnawnia Rift location. It is known to drop Rift Dust as loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Shard Centurion Mouse has been found to be especially attracted to Riftiago cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.


## Event Participation


### Spring Egg Hunt

- The Shard Centurion Mouse drops the Enlarged Rift Egg and the Gnawnia Rift Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

- 25 March 2014: The Shard Centurion Mouse was introduced as part of the Rift Plane.
- The Shard Centurion Mouse is the Rift version of the Granite Mouse.
