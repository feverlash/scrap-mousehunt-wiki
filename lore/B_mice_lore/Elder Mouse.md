# Elder Mouse

> For other uses of "Elder", see Elder (disambiguation).

The Elder Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Tribal Isles region. They are known to drop Ancient Relic and Ancient Spears in Cape Clawed.

## Preferred Cheese

Elder mice have been found to be especially attracted to Shell cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.
