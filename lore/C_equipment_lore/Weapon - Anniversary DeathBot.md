## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> The origins of the very first DeathBot were unknown when it was discovered 10 years ago. Until now! It seems it somehow found its way into the past and we've finally now caught up to its inception point. However, it seems the recent timeloops have allowed some improvements to be made over time.
> A high-tech robot with advanced designs borrowed from the future. The Anniversary DeathBot comes equipped with a bank of high speed rapid fire laser cannons, an automated, targeting shoulder-mounted plasma caster and housed with enough armour to withstand the heaviest of assaults.
> Designed for dealing with an overwhelming number of mice simultaneously. Sometimes it allows a mouse to escape with cheese in order to track down larger quantities of mice when the escaped vermin inevitably bring back more friends. This is done with a quick psychological and bio-analysis of the mouse type to determine its likeliness of return.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Anniversary DeathBot can be purchased for 25,640 gold, 10 Time Threads, 10 Pausing Pins, 10 Spacetime Spools, 50 Paradox Patterns and 10 Fabrics of Time.
The completion of the Adventure Restore Gnawnia's 10-Year Timeline is a requirement to purchase Anniversary DeathBot.
