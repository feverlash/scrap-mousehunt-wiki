## History and Trivia

- 9 December 2014: The Miser Mouse was released as part of the Great Winter Hunt 2014 event, but could not be attracted yet.
- 18 December 2014: Using Winter Miser charms obtained from a Feedback Friday link, hunters had a chance at attracting a Miser Mouse prior to the general release.
- 22 December 2014: The Miser Mouse became available as part of the third week of the Great Winter Hunt 2014.
- 13 December 2016: The points/gold for Miser Mouse were changed.
