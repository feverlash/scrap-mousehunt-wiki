## Cheese Preference

Bookworm Mouse is only attracted to Standard Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Bookworm Mouse can only be attracted while enrolled in Shadow Sciences or during the Shadow phase of the Final Exam.

Shadow power type is very effective against Bookworm Mouse.
Arcane power type is effective against Bookworm Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.
