# 🛡️ Gemology Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 350 Power | 10 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Duke/Duchess  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 0

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> This glittering slab unearthed from the depths below the Fungal Cavern is an ancient composition of mineral, gemstone, and diamond. The Fungal Fiends go absolutely wild for the precious stones hidden within the veins of this base.
> While equipped at the Fungal Cavern, hunters will encounter more mice that drop higher quantities of Minerals, Gemstones, and Diamonds.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
Gemology Base can only be obtained from opening Season 9 Treasure Chests. These chests are labeled (Season 9) in their names.

The chance of a chest containing this base increases with every chest opened.

The 30th Season 9 chest is guaranteed to contain the Gemology Base if it is not yet owned by the Hunter.
