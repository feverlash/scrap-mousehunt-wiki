# Firebreather Mouse

The Firebreather Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Rodentia region. It can be attracted and caught only during the Summer Season.

## Preferred Cheese

Firebreather Mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Tactical weapons such as Zugzwang's First Move should be used in order to catch this mouse.


## History and Trivia

- Firebreather Mice were introduced on 17 August 2010 with the release of the Seasonal Garden location.
- Firebreather Mice began dropping Sandtail Call to Arms on 22 March 2011 with the release of the Fiery Warpath location.
- On 29 July 2015 they stopped dropping the Sandtail Call to Arms as loot with the introduction of the Adventure Book.
