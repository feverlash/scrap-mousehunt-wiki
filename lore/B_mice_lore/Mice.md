# Mice

> "Mouse" redirects here. For other uses, see Mouse (disambiguation).

Catching the army of mice that are invading the Kingdom of Gnawnia is the main goal in MouseHunt!


## Mouse Attributes

Each mouse has the following attributes:

- Mouse Power

> The strength of a mouse. It is reflected as a comparison against your current Trap Power and Power Type.
In ascending order, it is given as one of the following: "effortless", "easy", "moderate", "challenging", "difficult", "overpowering", "near impossible", or "impossible".

- Mouse Group

> One of 48 Mouse Groups: Indigenous Mice, Dock Dwellers, Mountain Mice, Forest Guild, Lab Experiments, The Shadow Clan, Digby Dirt Dwellers, Followers of Furoma, The Forgotten Mice, Aquatic Order, The Elub Tribe, The Nerg Tribe, The Derr Tribe, The Dreaded Horde, Draconic Brood, Balack's Banished, Gauntlet Gladiators, Seasonal Soldiers, Wizard's Pieces, Zurreal's Breed, Icewing's Invasion, Wild Bunch, Train Robbers, Meteorite Miners, The Marching Flame, Muridae Market Mice, Living Garden Mice, Lost City Mice, Sand Dunes Mice, Queso Canyoneers, Deep Sea Dwellers, Fungal Fiends, Citizens of Zokor, Moussu Picchu Inhabitants, Floating Islanders, Foreword Farmers, Prologue Pond Prowlers, Storytellers, Beanstalkers, Sorcery Apprentices, Dragons of the Depths, Afterword Anti-Arborists, Epilogue Ecosystem, Conclusion Cliffhangers, Rift Walkers, Rift Stalkers, The Polluted or Event Mice.

- Gold Value

> The amount of gold received for each catch. Mice of the same breed always award the same amount of gold.

- Points Value

> The amount of points received for each catch. Mice of the same breed always award the same amount of points.

- Loot

> What the mouse might drop when caught. Drops are usually specific to a location. Not all mice drop loot.

- Preferred Cheese

> Penchant for a particular cheese. When using a preferred cheese, the mouse is encountered more often than usual. Not all mice have a preference. Additionally, not all have equally strong preferences - some mice are simply more likely to be encountered, while some are only encountered with one specific cheese. Cheese preferences listed in the table below, as well as on individual mouse pages, are listed alphabetically, not in order of preference.

- Mouse Weight

> The heaviness of a mouse, expressed in pounds and ounces. The weight has no impact on gameplay currently, historically, the gold and points of a mouse were varied depending on the weight of the mouse, with heavier mice giving more gold but less points.
