# Grandfather Mouse

The Grandfather Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Tribal Isles region. They are known to drop Ancient Relic and Thorned Vine in Cape Clawed.

## Preferred Cheese

Grandfather mice have been found to be especially attracted to Gumbo cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.
