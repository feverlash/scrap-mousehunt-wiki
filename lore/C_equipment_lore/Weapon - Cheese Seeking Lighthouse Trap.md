# ⚔️ Cheese Seeking Lighthouse Trap

> **Classification**: Shadow Weapon

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> A light in the dark, a beacon of safety.
> This trap illuminates cheese drawing mice to it, inviting them to rest with the soothing ocean sounds. When the mice let down their guard the light goes out, and creatures emerge from the shadows.
> No one ever sees the mice after that, no one knows what lurks in the shadows.
