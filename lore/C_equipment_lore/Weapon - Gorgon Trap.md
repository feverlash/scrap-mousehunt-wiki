# ⚔️ Gorgon Trap

> **Classification**: Shadow Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 2,000 Power | 7 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Legendary  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 700,000 Gold

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> Made from the eye of the Gorgon, a hideous monster with hair made of snakes, this trap will literally scare mice stiff, turning them into living statues. Hunters with visions of figurine collections dancing in their head may be disappointed that the perfect little mouse statues only last for a few hours before they thaw out, but that’s certainly long enough to throw them into a sack and send them to a safe location.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Gorgon Trap can be purchased for 700,000 Gold from the Trapsmith in following locations:

- Cape Clawed
- Jungle of Dread
- Balack's Cove
- King's Gauntlet
- Fort Rox

It can be refunded for 126,000 Gold at the same locations.
