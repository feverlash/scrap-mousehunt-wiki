## Preferred Cheese

Medicine mice have been found to be especially attracted to String Cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.


## Hunting Strategy

The Medicine Mouse is weak to Rift Traps. It can only be attracted by String Cheese.

The Medicine Mouse is only available when rage in the Deep Lagoon section in Whisker Woods Rift is between 25-49.


## Event Participation


### Spring Egg Hunt

- The Medicine Mouse drops the Enlarged Rift Egg and the Rift Mist Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

- Medicine mice were introduced on 28 January 2015, with the introduction of the Whisker Woods Rift.
- Medicine mice are the Rift version of Shaman mice.
