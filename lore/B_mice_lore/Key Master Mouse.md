## Cheese Preference

Key Master Mouse is only attracted to Lavish Beanster Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Key Master Mouse can only be attracted after planting a Short Vine to the Dungeon Floor.

Physical power type is effective against Key Master Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.


## Loot

Key Master Mouse can drop:

- Ancient Relic
- Lavish Lapis Bean
