## Cheese Preference

Whimsical Waltzer Mouse is only attracted to Standard Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Whimsical Waltzer Mouse can only be attracted after planting a Medium Vine to the Ballroom Floor.

Physical power type is effective against Whimsical Waltzer Mouse.
All other types are ineffective.


## Loot

Whimsical Waltzer Mouse can drop:

- Golden Harp String
- Royal Ruby Bean
