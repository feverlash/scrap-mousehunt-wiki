# 📍 Jungle of Dread

> **Wilayah / Region**: Tribal Isles  
> **Pangkat Minimal**: Hero  
> **Fasilitas Lokal**: Cheese ShoppeTrapsmithCharm ShoppeGeneral Store

## 🌍 Gambaran & Atmosfer Lingkungan

Jungle of Dread is a hunting location in Tribal Isles region.

It requires the rank of Hero and the Ocean Navigation Kit to access.

The Hunter can use Havarti Cheeses here to catch mice that drop Fire Salt to craft Inferno Havarti to use in Dracano.
The Hunter can use Standard Cheese here to catch mice that drop Vanilla Bean to craft Vanilla Stilton and Vengeful Vanilla Stilton to use in Balack's Cove.

Catching a Riptide Mouse without an Anchor Charm variant in Balack's Cove transfers the Hunter to this location.
