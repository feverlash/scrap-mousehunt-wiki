# Pinchy Mouse

The Pinchy Mouse is a breed of mouse found in Cape Clawed,the Elub Shore, and aboard the S.S. Huntington IV. It is not known to drop any loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Pinchy Mouse has no specific preference for any kind of cheese. It is known to have an aversion to Gouda and SUPER|brie+ in Elub Shore.
