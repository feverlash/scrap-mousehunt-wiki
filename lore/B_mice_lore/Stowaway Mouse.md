# Stowaway Mouse

The Stowaway Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Gnawnian Express Station. They are known to drop Fool's Gold.

## Preferred Cheese

While Stowaway mice can be attracted by standard cheese, they have a distinct preference for SUPER|brie+.


## Hunting Strategy

Law type traps such as S.L.A.C. are very effective against Stowaway Mouse.


## Event Participation

- The Stowaway Mouse drops the Gnawnian Express Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.
