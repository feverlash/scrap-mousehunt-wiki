# ⚔️ Warpath Thrasher

> **Classification**: Physical Weapon  
> **Power / Luck**: 4,500 Power | 6 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Knight  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 2,150,000

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> The Warpath Thrasher, often referred to as the "angry flailing croissant", is as dangerous as it is weird looking. Designed to take on the army of the Marching Flame, this trap barrels into the waves, daring to take them all on at once.
> This trap is extremely powerful at the beginning of each wave, boasting an impressive 9000 power and 30 luck, however; it begins to rapidly lose power after the initial onslaught.

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
The Warpath Thrasher is a Limited Edition weapon and can no longer be acquired by Hunters. It was available for purchase for 2,150,000 gold from Ronza's Traveling Shoppe during her January-February 2012 visit. It refunded for 380,000 gold at the same location.
