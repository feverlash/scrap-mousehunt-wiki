# Portal Plunderer Mouse

The Portal Plunderer Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Bristle Woods Rift. It is the Rift counterpart of Scavenger Mouse.

## Preferred Cheese

The Portal Plunderer Mouse has been found to be especially attracted to String Cheese, Ancient String Cheese and Runic String Cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.

## Hunting Strategy

Only Rift type traps are effective against Portal Plunderer Mouse, all other power types are ineffective.
