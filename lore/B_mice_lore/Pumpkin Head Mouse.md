# Pumpkin Head Mouse

The Pumpkin Head Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Rodentia region. It can be attracted and caught only during the Fall Season. They are not known to drop any loot.

## Preferred Cheese

Pumpkin Head Mice have no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Strong Shadow weapons such as Clockapult of Time are recommended in order to catch this mouse. Alternatively, hunters may equip their best Tactical trap.
