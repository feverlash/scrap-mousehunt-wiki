# 🛡️ Aurora Base

> **Classification**: General Base  
> **Power / Luck**: 250 Power | 6 Luck  
> **Title Requirement**: Knight  
> **Acquisition Cost**: 12,500,000 gold

## 📜 Lore & Rekayasa Perangkap

> AURORA BOREALIS?! At this time of year? At this time of day? In this part of the Kingdom? Localized entirely within your base!? Yes!
> This captured phenomenon from the Living Garden is a swirling vortex of light and energy which is where Living Garden essences grow from.
> The Aurora Base has a chance to generate a random essence when hunting in the Living Garden, Lost City, Sand Dunes, and their twisted counterparts!

## 🔨 Asal-Usul & Perakitan (Crafting/Shoppe)
Aurora Base could be purchased for 12,500,000 gold from the Trapsmith in Ronza's Traveling Shoppe during her 2019 visit.

It cannot be refunded.
