## Preferred Cheese

The Retired Minotaur Mouse prefers Glowing Gruyere, but can be attracted with Standard Cheeses.


## Hunting Strategy

Forgotten weapons are very effective against the Retired Minotaur Mouse. Arcane weapons are of normal effectiveness.

The Retired Minotaur Mouse can only be found in the Lair of the Minotaur. While it can be encountered at any time within the Lair of the Minotaur after the Hunter has caught any one mouse, it is more easily attracted and caught as its state (as indicated in the HUD) moves closer to Reckless. Hunters may only catch one Retired Minotaur Mouse per visit to the Lair of the Minotaur.

Hunters are returned to the beginning of the Labyrinth upon catching a Retired Minotaur Mouse.
