## Cheese Preference

The Sand Dollar Diver Mouse has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

The Sand Dollar Diver Mouse is susceptible only to Hydro weapons. It can be found in the Sand Dollar Sea Bar zone while on a Dive in the Sunken City.


## Event Participation

- The Sand Dollar Diver Mouse drops the Sand Dollar Egg during the annual Spring Egg Hunt.


## History and Trivia

The Sand Dollar Diver Mouse was released 2 July 2014 as part of the Sunken City content.
