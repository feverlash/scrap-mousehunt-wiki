## Preferred Cheese

The Cloud Collector Mouse has been found to be especially attracted to Standard Cheese and Glowing Gruyere Cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.

## Hunting Strategy

Only Shadow, Arcane and Draconic type traps are effective against Cloud Collector Mouse, all other power types are ineffective.
