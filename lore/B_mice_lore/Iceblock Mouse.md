# Iceblock Mouse

The Iceblock Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Brutal Bulwark, Bombing Run, and The Mad Depths zones of the Iceberg. It is known to drop Hunting Research, Satchel of Gold, Sticky Charms, and Wax Charms as loot. Additionally, it is known to drop Frozen Sealed Bottle in the Brutal Bulwark zone of the Iceberg.

## Preferred Cheese

The Iceblock Mouse has no specific preference for any kind of cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

In the Iceberg, a Wax Charm will increase the distance of a successful catch, and a Sticky Charm will reduce the distance of a miss.


## History and Trivia

- 24 May 2012: The Iceblock Mouse was released as a part of the Iceberg.
- 1 June 2012: The Iceblock Mouse began to drop loot.
- 18 June 2012: The Iceblock Mouse began to drop Frozen Sealed Bottle.
