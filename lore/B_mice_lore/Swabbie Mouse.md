# Swabbie Mouse

The Swabbie Mouse is a breed of mouse found aboard the S.S. Huntington IV. It is not known to drop any loot.

## Preferred Cheese

The Swabbie Mouse has no specific preference for any kind of cheese. It is known to have an aversion to SUPER|brie+.


## History and Trivia

- 23 June 2009: The Swabbie Mouse was released.
- 12 January 2011: The Swabbie Mouse could be found at Ronza's Traveling Shoppe where it dropped Power Charm as loot.
- The description for the Swabbie Mouse has changed since its release. The original description read as follows:

> Forced to cleanup after their shipmates, the Swabbie mouse is an under-appreciated pirate wannabe. This poor, hapless servant faces daily humiliation from his fellow shipmates.
