# Tiny Dragonfly Mouse

The Tiny Dragonfly Mouse is a breed of mouse found on the Floating Islands, on a Draconic Island or a Draconic Sanctuary.

## Cheese Preference

The Tiny Dragonfly Mouse is attracted to Cloud Cheesecake and Standard Cheese.


## Hunting Strategy

Only Draconic traps are effective against the Tiny Dragonfly Mouse, all other power types are ineffective. The Tiny Dragonfly Mouse can only be encountered on a Draconic Island or a Draconic Sanctuary.

Dragonbane Charms, Super Dragonbane Charms and Extreme Dragonbane Charms provide a power bonus against the Tiny Dragonfly Mouse.


## Loot


### Floating Islands's Island Based Loot

When found on a Low Altitude Island or a High Altitude Island, depending on the buffs present, the Tiny Dragonfly Mouse can drop the following loot:
