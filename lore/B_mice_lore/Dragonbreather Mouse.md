## Cheese Preference

The Dragonbreather Mouse is only attracted to Cloud Cheesecake.

- On a Draconic Island or a Draconic Sanctuary, the Dragonbreather Mouse is only attracted to Cloud Cheesecake.
- In a Draconic Hoard, the Dragonbreather Mouse is only attracted to Extra Rich Cloud Cheesecake.


## Hunting Strategy

Only Draconic traps are effective against the Dragonbreather Mouse, all other power types are ineffective. The Dragonbreather Mouse can only be encountered on a Draconic Island, a Draconic Sanctuary, or a Draconic Hoard.

Dragonbane Charms, Super Dragonbane Charms and Extreme Dragonbane Charms provide a power bonus against the Dragonbreather Mouse.
