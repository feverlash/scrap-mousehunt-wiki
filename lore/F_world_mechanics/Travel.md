# Travel

Travel is a feature that lets hunters access different locations in MouseHunt. The primary means of traveling is through the travel page.

Alternative ways of traveling include:

- Acolyte Realm: Catching a Realm Ripper in Forbidden Grove when it is closed
- Jungle of Dread: Catching a Riptide Mouse in Balack's Cove without Balack's Cove Charms
- Seasonal Garden: Completing a Zugzwang's Tower run by either catching Mystic King or Technic King without Checkmate Cheese, or Chess Master
- Slushy Shoreline: Ending an Iceberg run by catching a Frostwing Commander, Icewing, Deep or exploding a Cold Fusion Bomb
- Twisted Garden: Catching Carmine the Apothecary in Living Garden
- Living Garden/Lost City/Sand Dunes: Hunting with any cheese except Duskshade, Graveblossom and Lunaria Camembert in Twisted Garden/Cursed City/Sand Crypts
- Laboratory: Remaining in Toxic Spill after a spill has been cleaned up
- Zokor: Choosing a district door
- Labyrinth: Running out of stealth or catching the Retired Minotaur Mouse
- Vacant Lot: Remaining in an event location after it ends

Each time travel occurs (including automatic travel), an event is logged in the Hunter's Journal.

### Larry's Travel

Prior to 29 July, 2015, Larry could take hunters back to the Meadow for no charge.

The feature was removed when all travel became free of charge on 29 July, 2015.


### Tools

Direct travel between locations is not always the least expensive route. For example, if you travel from the Harbour directly to the Laboratory, the cost is 2,500 gold. But if you instead follow Larry back to the Meadow for free and travel to the Laboratory from there, it will cost you just 2,010 gold, saving 490 gold. There are other examples that save a more significant amount of gold.

The tools below are designed to assist hunters in planning the most efficient travel itinerary:

- Travel Map of the Kingdom of Gnawnia shows the travel paths between locations, which can be useful in determining where you can stop on the way between locations without incurring extra costs.
