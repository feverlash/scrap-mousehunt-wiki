# Timelost Thaumaturge Mouse

The Timelost Thaumaturge Mouse is a breed of mouse found in the Bristle Woods Rift. It is the Rift counterpart of Sorcerer Mouse.

## Preferred Cheese

The Timelost Thaumaturge Mouse has been found to be especially attracted to Ancient String Cheese and Runic String Cheese, to the point that any other kind of cheese will simply not suffice.

## Hunting Strategy

Only Rift type traps are very effective against Timelost Thaumaturge Mouse, all other power types are ineffective.
