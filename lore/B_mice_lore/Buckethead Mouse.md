## History and Trivia

- 4 March 2011: The Buckethead mouse was released as part of MouseHunt's 3rd Birthday Celebration
- The Buckethead, Pintail, and Sleepwalker mice are references to the Three Blind Mice nursery rhyme.
- 29 February 2012: As part of MouseHunt's Fourth Birthday Celebration the bucket on the Buckethead mouse's head was updated and given a dented appearance to tie-in with the Dent loot,
